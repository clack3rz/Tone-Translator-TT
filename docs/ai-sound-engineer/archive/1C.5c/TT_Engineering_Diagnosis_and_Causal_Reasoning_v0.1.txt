1.3 THE CENTRAL ARCHITECTURAL RULE
The governing axiom of Phase 1C.5c is:
    "A DIAGNOSIS IS A CAUSAL CLAIM EARNED BY EVIDENCE —
     NOT THE MOST CONVENIENT HYPOTHESIS,
     NOT THE MOST FAMILIAR EXPLANATION,
     AND NOT THE HIGHEST-RANKED CANDIDATE."

A second binding rule is:
    "MOST SUPPORTED HYPOTHESIS != DIAGNOSED CAUSE."

A hypothesis becomes a diagnosis only when available evidence justifies the corresponding causal claim at the stated level of specificity.

Tone Translator never selects a diagnosis merely because it is the "highest-ranked" candidate,
nor because downstream code is eager to apply an equalizer or swap an amplifier model.
A causal diagnosis must identify an electro-acoustic process whose causal relationship to the
observed audio phenomena is justified by rigorous empirical and physical evidence.

3.2 THE REMEDY FIREWALL (RF-1): DIAGNOSIS MUST NOT BECOME A REMEDY
The central boundary pathology in automated audio systems is the premature conflation of
diagnosis with remediation. When an AI engineer thinks "how do I fix this?" while still asking
"what caused this?", diagnostic objectivity is completely destroyed.

The Remedy Firewall mandates:
    A DIAGNOSIS IDENTIFIES THE PHYSICAL CAUSAL REALITY OF THE PROBLEM.
    IT NEVER PRESCRIBES, RECOMMENDS, OR SELECTS THE SOLUTION.

A valid diagnostic conclusion identifies what physical, electrical, or acoustic processes
produced the observed phenomena. How that problem should be addressed (whether by moving a
physical microphone, adjusting amplifier tone controls, inserting a notch filter, altering
performance technique, or accepting the sound as genre-authentic) is an intervention decision
belonging exclusively to Phase 1C.5d.

SECTION 4 — DIAGNOSIS SEMANTICS & EPISTEMIC TAXONOMY
===============================================================================

4.1 THE SIX CRITICAL COGNITIVE CATEGORIES
To ensure absolute semantic clarity across the reasoning chain, Tone Translator maintains
strict definitional boundaries among six distinct cognitive categories:

1. OBSERVATION:
   - What the evidence directly and objectively demonstrates.
   - Example: "A discrete spectral peak is measured at 3.8 kHz (+7.4 dB relative to 1 kHz)."

2. PHENOMENOLOGICAL INTERPRETATION:
   - How the observed signal manifests in musical and perceptual listening experience.
   - Example: "The guitar sound exhibits harsh, brittle, metallic upper-midrange bite."

3. CANDIDATE HYPOTHESIS:
   - A proposed physical, acoustic, or electrical mechanism at a specific signal locus that
     could explain one or more observations.
   - Example: "High cable capacitance loading passive pickup inductance produces an electrical
     resonance peak in the 3.5-4.0 kHz region."

4. CAUSAL DIAGNOSIS (PHASE 1C.5c OUTPUT):
   - An evidence-justified conclusion identifying the actual physical causal mechanism(s)
     responsible for the observed phenomena, structured by causal role, explanatory coverage,
     and accompanied by explicit residual uncertainty.
   - Example: "The primary origin of the 3.8 kHz harshness is confirmed as instrument cable
     capacitive loading; downstream preamp clipping acts as a material severity amplifier."

5. ENGINEERING REQUIREMENT (PHASE 1C.5d):
   - A target physical or electro-acoustic modification derived from the diagnosis that must
     be achieved to satisfy engineering intent.
   - Example: "Reduce electrical resonance peak at 3.8 kHz by at least 6 dB prior to preamp clipping."

6. INTERVENTION (PHASE 1C.5d):
   - The concrete physical, routing, or processing action selected to realize the requirement.
   - Example: "Insert a high-impedance buffer pedal immediately following the guitar output."

4.2 THE ANTI-RANKING PRINCIPLE: RANKING IS NOT DIAGNOSIS
A frequent failure mode in automated decision engines is sorting hypotheses by a heuristic
score and declaring the top candidate to be the "diagnosis".
Tone Translator explicitly repudiates this practice:
    SORTING HYPOTHESES DOES NOT EARN A CAUSAL DIAGNOSIS.

Why Ranking Fails:
  - The "top-ranked" hypothesis in an incomplete workspace may still be completely false.
  - A hypothesis may have high plausibility simply because it represents a common textbook case
    (familiarity bias), while the true mechanism is an unusual compound interaction.
  - Multiple mechanisms may be simultaneously operating in joint or enabling roles, which a
    linear ranking collapses into a false winner-take-all contest.
  - Moving to a diagnosis requires satisfying affirmative evidential criteria (Section 5),
    not merely outscoring alternative candidates in an arbitrary sort.

4.3 EXTENSIBLE CAUSAL ROLE TAXONOMY
Real-world electro-acoustic guitar systems rarely involve a single isolated fault.
Tone Translator adopts a baseline, extensible taxonomy of causal roles:

  1. `PRIMARY_CAUSE`:
     - The dominant physical mechanism whose operation is primarily responsible for the
       generation or fundamental character of the observed defect or sonic phenomenon.
  2. `SECONDARY_CONTRIBUTOR`:
     - A distinct physical mechanism that independently contributes to the observed symptom,
       but whose removal would leave a substantial portion of the phenomenon intact.
  3. `JOINT_CONTRIBUTOR`:
     - Mechanisms that operate concurrently, where each mechanism is necessary to produce
       the full magnitude or character of the observed acoustic result.
  4. `ENABLING_CONDITION`:
     - A physical or circuit state that does not directly generate the defect, but provides
       the operating environment required for another mechanism to manifest (e.g. high guitar
       volume pot setting enabling severe cable loading resonance).
  5. `SEVERITY_AMPLIFIER`:
     - A downstream stage that does not originate the feature, but materially magnifies its
       perceptual annoyance or acoustic severity (e.g. non-linear power-tube clipping compressing
       and squaring an upstream resonant peak).
  6. `CONTEXTUAL_CONTRIBUTOR`:
     - An environmental or playback factor (e.g. monitor boundary loading, room modes, headphone
     coloration) that colors user perception without existing in the recorded track.
  7. `UNRESOLVED_POTENTIAL_CONTRIBUTOR`:
     - A candidate mechanism that remains physically viable and consistent with evidence,
       but whose causal presence cannot be confirmed or eliminated with available telemetry.

SECTION 5 — CAUSAL EVIDENCE STANDARDS & SUFFICIENCY CRITERIA
================================================================================

5.1 THE EIGHT PROFESSIONAL CAUSAL EVIDENCE CLASSES
To transition a candidate hypothesis into a verified causal diagnosis, Tone Translator requires
affirmative evidential support. The AI Sound Engineer evaluates case evidence against eight
rigorous, non-interchangeable causal evidence classes:

1. DIRECT LOCUS ISOLATION:
   - Physical signal tap or stem isolation proving the artifact exists at, or enters upstream of,
     a specific signal boundary.
   - Example: A dry DI capture exhibiting a sharp 3.8 kHz resonance peak directly isolates the
     feature to the instrument/transduction stage, proving it does not originate in the amplifier.

2. NECESSARY-PREDICTION CONFIRMATION:
   - Confirmation of a specific physical prediction that MUST be true if the candidate mechanism
     is operating.
   - Example: If harshness is caused by on-axis microphone dust-cap beaming, moving the microphone
     1.5 inches off-axis toward the cone edge MUST produce a material attenuation (>3 dB) of the
     4-5 kHz energy peak.

3. CONTROLLED SUBSTITUTION / BYPASS TEST:
   - Physical or routing manipulation where only the suspected component is substituted or bypassed
     under identical performance and gain conditions.
   - Example: Swapping an unbuffered 25-foot guitar cable for a 6-foot low-capacitance cable, or
     bypassing an analog overdrive pedal in an FX loop.

4. VALID EXCLUSION OF MAJOR ALTERNATIVES:
   - Empirical elimination of competing plausible mechanisms using controlled, sensitive exclusion
     tests that control for plausible confounders.
   - Example: Confirming that high-frequency noise persists unchanged when the guitar volume pot is
     rolled to zero, validly excluding pickup RF transduction and cable microphonics.

5. CONVERGING INDEPENDENT EVIDENCE:
   - Multiple distinct evidence modalities (e.g. calibrated DSP measurement, physical rig metadata,
     and controlled audio stems) independently pointing to the same physical mechanism without
     mutual dependency.
   - Example: A measured 120 Hz ripple spike on FFT, paired with a reported ungrounded two-prong
     vintage amplifier supply, and hum disappearing on battery-powered DC supply test.

6. PHYSICAL CONSISTENCY ACROSS MULTIPLE OBSERVATIONS:
   - Mathematical and physical harmonic coherence across multiple observed phenomena.
   - Example: Symmetrical odd-harmonic generation (3rd, 5th, 7th) aligning precisely with the
     mathematical transfer function of push-pull power tube saturation at high output voltages.

7. CAUSAL TEMPORAL ORDERING:
   - Direct verification that the putative cause precedes the observed signal effect in time or
     signal routing topology.
   - Example: Measuring a discrete latency delay of 1.4 ms between microphone channels confirming
     acoustic propagation comb filtering rather than electrical DSP phase inversion.

8. REPLICATED CONTROLLED CAPTURE:
   - Consistency of the phenomenon across repeated captures under identical playing technique,
     demonstrating that the feature is a systematic property of the rig rather than a random
     performance anomaly.

5.2 SUFFICIENCY EVALUATION WITHOUT ARBITRARY SCALAR THRESHOLDS
A fatal pathology in naive engineering systems is calculating a pseudo-mathematical "confidence score"
(e.g. "Causal certainty: 82.4%") to decide whether evidence is sufficient.
Tone Translator permanently repudiates synthetic scalar certainty:
    CAUSAL SUFFICIENCY IS AN EVALUATION OF EXPLANATORY COMPLETENESS
    AND EVIDENTIAL CONVERGENCE, NOT AN ARBITRARY MATHEMATICAL SUM.

Professional Standards for Causal Resolution:
  - Tone Translator may transition to `CAUSAL_DIAGNOSIS_RESOLVED` only when:
    1. At least one primary physical mechanism is corroborated by high-directness evidence
       (such as Locus Isolation, Substitution, or Necessary-Prediction Confirmation);
    2. Major competing alternative explanations at the same locus have been either tested and
       weakened, or explicitly accounted for as unresolved secondary contributors;
    3. The identified mechanism physically accounts for the observed acoustic characteristics;
    4. Explanatory coverage and residual uncertainties are explicitly documented.
  - If evidence isolates the signal locus but cannot distinguish between competing internal
    circuit mechanisms (e.g. DI confirms pickup/cable origin, but cannot isolate pot resistance
    vs coil capacitance), Tone Translator must issue a `LOCUS_RESOLVED_MECHANISM_UNRESOLVED`
    diagnosis rather than guessing.

SECTION 6 — CAUSAL NARROWING ARCHITECTURE & ELIMINATION DISCIPLINE
================================================================================

6.1 THE NON-BINARY ELIMINATION INVARIANT
In classical deductive logic, disproving one premise in a disjunction (A OR B) proves the other.
In electro-acoustic sound engineering, this logic is routinely invalid because:
    THE REASONING WORKSPACE IS RARELY A PROVEN CLOSED SET.

The Non-Binary Elimination Invariant:
    FAILURE OF HYPOTHESIS A DOES NOT AUTOMATICALLY PROVE HYPOTHESIS B.

Operational Rules:
  - Eliminating on-axis mic beaming does NOT prove that preamp cold-clipping is the cause of harshness.
    The harshness could stem from speaker cone breakup, pickup resonance, an engaged bright cap,
    or a digital interface converter distortion.
  - A surviving hypothesis gains plausibility ONLY when:
    1. The competing hypotheses were explicitly shown to share the same evidential locus; AND
    2. The candidate space was thoroughly explored and justified as practically exhaustive; OR
    3. The surviving hypothesis receives its own direct affirmative corroboration.
  - A diagnosis must NEVER be declared solely by default ("Hypotheses 1, 2, and 3 failed, therefore
    Hypothesis 4 is the confirmed cause").

6.2 REAFFIRMING THE VALID EXCLUSION TEST STANDARD (FB-C3)
Phase 1C.5c fully inherits and enforces the Valid Exclusion Standard established in Phase 1C.5b:
    FALSIFICATION REQUIRES A VALID EXCLUSION TEST.

To eliminate or retire a candidate hypothesis from the active causal set:
  1. The test must inspect the specific physical variable predicted by the hypothesis;
  2. The measurement procedure must possess calibrated sensitivity capable of detecting the expected change;
  3. Plausible confounding variables (e.g. player velocity variation, unintentional knob shifts)
     must be controlled.
  4. If a test is inconclusive, confounded, or uncalibrated, the candidate hypothesis is WEAKENED,
     NOT FALSIFIED. It remains logged as an uneliminated secondary or residual possibility.

SECTION 7 — MULTI-CAUSE & COMPOUND DIAGNOSIS ARCHITECTURE
================================================================================

7.1 COMPOUND CAUSATION IN GUITAR RIG SYSTEMS
Guitar amplification systems are non-linear, multi-stage electro-acoustic networks.
Single-cause problems are the exception rather than the rule:
  - Harshness: Commonly an interaction between a resonant electrical peak in the guitar pickup/cable,
    non-linear harmonic multiplication in the preamp, and directional acoustic beaming from the speaker dust-cap.
  - Muddy / Flubby Low-End: Commonly an interaction between excessive bass gain in the pre-clipping
    stage, dynamic power-tube screen-grid sag compressing the attack, and monitor boundary loading in the room.
  - Excessive Noise: Commonly an interaction between electromagnetic hum induced in single-coil pickups
    and high thermal Johnson noise generated in high-gain cascaded preamp stages.

7.2 COMPOUND CAUSAL STRUCTURE DATA CONTRACT
To accurately represent real systems without forcing false single-cause simplification,
Tone Translator models compound causal structures through an extensible conceptual contract:

```typescript
interface CompoundCausalDiagnosis {
  diagnosis_id: string; // e.g. "DIAG-SCENARIO-B-01"
  primary_causal_mechanisms: CausalMechanismContribution[];
  secondary_contributors: CausalMechanismContribution[];
  enabling_conditions: EnablingConditionRecord[];
  severity_amplifiers: SeverityAmplifierRecord[];
  contextual_factors: ContextualFactorRecord[];
  interaction_mode: CompoundInteractionMode;
  explanatory_coverage: ExplanatoryCoverageAssessment;
  residual_uncertainty_dossier: ResidualUncertaintyDossier;
}

interface CausalMechanismContribution {
  mechanism_id: string;
  locus: StandardSignalLocus | string;
  physical_description: string;
  causal_role: CausalRole; // PRIMARY_CAUSE | SECONDARY_CONTRIBUTOR | JOINT_CONTRIBUTOR
  evidential_grounding: CausalEvidenceClass[];
  contribution_tier: QualitativeContributionTier; // DOMINANT | MATERIAL | SECONDARY | MINOR
}

enum CompoundInteractionMode {
  INDEPENDENT_SUPERPOSITION = "INDEPENDENT_SUPERPOSITION", // Mechanisms sum linearly (e.g. two noise sources)
  NON_LINEAR_MULTIPLICATION = "NON_LINEAR_MULTIPLICATION", // Upstream feature multiplied by downstream clipping
  CASCADED_EXACERBATION = "CASCADED_EXACERBATION",         // Origin at Locus A, severity amplified at Locus B
  THRESHOLD_TRIGGERED = "THRESHOLD_TRIGGERED",             // Feature manifests only when level crosses a threshold
  RESONANT_ACOUSTIC_COUPLING = "RESONANT_ACOUSTIC_COUPLING" // Cabinet baffle and room mode interaction
}
```

7.3 EXPLICIT REJECTION OF SINGLE-CAUSE BIAS
Tone Translator explicitly prohibits forcing a multi-cause system into a single "root cause"
when evidence indicates compound operation. Where two mechanisms are both necessary to produce
the full observed defect, they must be diagnosed as `JOINT_CONTRIBUTOR` elements.

SECTION 8 — CONCEPTUAL CAUSAL CHAIN REPRESENTATION
================================================================================

8.1 THE SEVEN-LINK CONCEPTUAL CAUSAL CHAIN
To provide absolute explainability and retrospective auditability (Constitutional Principle 20),
Tone Translator structures every causal diagnosis as a traceable conceptual chain:

    [1. ROOT PHYSICAL CONDITION]
              ↓
    [2. ELECTRO-ACOUSTIC MECHANISM]
              ↓
    [3. PRIMARY SIGNAL EFFECT]
              ↓
    [4. DOWNSTREAM STAGE INTERACTION]
              ↓
    [5. COMPOSITE MEASURED RESULT]
              ↓
    [6. PERCEPTUAL / PSYCHOACOUSTIC MANIFESTATION]
              ↓
    [7. USER REPORTED SYMPTOM]

Explanation of Links:
  1. Root Physical Condition: The physical equipment state, geometry, or environment (e.g. 20-foot unshielded cable, on-axis mic angle).
  2. Electro-Acoustic Mechanism: The physical law in operation (e.g. LC resonant tank circuit, directional acoustic wave beaming).
  3. Primary Signal Effect: The immediate voltage or wave modification (e.g. +6 dB peak at 3.8 kHz in electrical waveform).
  4. Downstream Stage Interaction: How subsequent stages react to the primary effect (e.g. preamp clipping generating odd harmonics from the 3.8 kHz peak).
  5. Composite Measured Result: The final objective acoustic capture properties (e.g. elevated 3.8 kHz and 11.4 kHz bins on FFT).
  6. Perceptual Manifestation: How a human ear interprets the composite sound (e.g. harsh, metallic, fatiguing bite).
  7. User Reported Symptom: The initial subjective client complaint (e.g. "My guitar sounds piercing and hurts my ears").

8.2 WORKED CAUSAL CHAIN DEMONSTRATION
Example: Scenario G (Harsh High-E String Ringing)
  - Link 1 (Root Physical Condition): Stratocaster bridge pickup loaded by vintage-spec 250k volume pot and 18-foot unbuffered instrument cable.
  - Link 2 (Electro-Acoustic Mechanism): High pickup coil inductance (2.8 H) interacting with cable capacitance (650 pF) forms an under-damped LC resonant circuit.
  - Link 3 (Primary Signal Effect): Resonant electrical boost peaking at 3.8 kHz (+6.2 dB) present on the dry DI signal.
  - Link 4 (Downstream Interaction): Preamp high-gain stage compresses dynamic envelope, holding the 3.8 kHz peak in continuous clipping saturation.
  - Link 5 (Composite Measured Result): Recorded track shows persistent +7.4 dB energy elevation at 3.8 kHz with dense intermodulation sidebands.
  - Link 6 (Perceptual Manifestation): Piercing metallic ringing that masks fundamental note body during solo passages.
  - Link 7 (User Reported Symptom): "My high-E string has a painful ice-pick ringing sound that won't go away."

8.3 CONCEPTUAL STATUS OF THE CAUSAL CHAIN
This causal chain is an architectural reasoning representation designed to enforce complete
traceability. Concrete database storage, JSON schemas, and graph database representations
remain explicitly DEFERRED to downstream implementation phases.

SECTION 9 — LOCUS RESOLUTION VS MECHANISM RESOLUTION
================================================================================

9.1 THE INDEPENDENCE OF LOCUS AND MECHANISM
In professional audio engineering, locating where an acoustic or electrical feature enters
the signal chain is a fundamentally distinct cognitive task from determining the exact
physical or electronic mechanism responsible:
    LOCUS ISOLATION != MECHANISM RESOLUTION.

Real-World Engineering Disconnects:
  - An engineer inspecting a Dry DI stem can confirm with absolute certainty that a 3.8 kHz
    resonant peak enters upstream of the amplifier (Locus: Instrument Electrical isolated).
    However, without component-level test gear, the engineer cannot definitively determine
    whether the peak is caused by pickup coil self-resonance, volume pot resistive loading,
    or cable capacitance (Exact Mechanism Unresolved).
  - An engineer testing a physical speaker cabinet with dual-channel FFT can confirm that a deep
    notch at 650 Hz originates at the cabinet acoustic stage (Locus: Cabinet Acoustic isolated).
    However, the engineer cannot immediately determine whether the notch is caused by internal
    standing wave cancellation, baffle wood compliance, or dust-cap acoustic phase cancellation
    (Exact Mechanism Unresolved).
  - Conversely, an engineer may understand the precise physical mechanism of power-tube screen grid
    clipping, but lack the telemetry to prove whether dynamic compression in a recorded track is
    occurring at the power stage, in an unrecorded compressor pedal, or in a DAW bus limiter
    (Mechanism Understood, Locus Unresolved).

9.2 THE THREE RESOLUTION STATES
To avoid false precision and maintain operational honesty, Tone Translator recognizes three
distinct resolution states:

  1. `LOCUS_RESOLVED_MECHANISM_RESOLVED`:
     - Both the signal chain boundary and the precise electro-acoustic process are corroborated
       by empirical evidence and necessary-prediction testing.
     - Example: "Feature enters at Instrument Electrical stage; confirmed as pickup cable loading
       resonance via short-cable substitution test."

  2. `LOCUS_RESOLVED_MECHANISM_UNRESOLVED`:
     - Empirical evidence isolates the feature to a specific signal boundary, but multiple internal
       physical mechanisms remain viable and indistinguishable under current telemetry.
     - Example: "Feature originates upstream of the amplifier in the Dry DI signal; whether it stems
       from pickup winding resonance, pot loading, or cable capacitance remains an unmeasured internal unknown."
     - Architectural Action: Tone Translator bounds the locus, states the viable candidate mechanisms,
       and halts diagnosis without guessing.

  3. `LOCUS_UNRESOLVED_MECHANISM_HYPOTHETICAL`:
     - A plausible physical mechanism is identified from general principles or user symptoms,
       but the signal locus cannot be verified due to missing stems or uncalibrated audio.
     - Architectural Action: Tone Translator designates the diagnosis as provisional or unverified,
       prohibiting high-confidence causal conclusions.

9.3 FORBIDDEN FALSE SPECIFICITY
Tone Translator is strictly barred from guessing specific electronic component faults when
only locus isolation has been achieved. If telemetry proves only that a problem enters at the
instrument stage, the diagnosis must state that the instrument locus is resolved while the
internal mechanism remains an open unknown.

SECTION 10 — CAUSAL CONTRIBUTION REASONING (WITHOUT FAKE PRECISION)
================================================================================

10.1 REPUDIATION OF FABRICATED CAUSAL PERCENTAGES
A widespread defect in pseudo-scientific automation is assigning precise numerical percentages
to causal contributions (e.g. "Pickup loading contributes 64%, preamp clipping contributes 28%,
speaker beaming contributes 8%").
In real-world sound engineering:
    THERE IS NO CALIBRATED TRANSFER FUNCTION CAPABLE OF
    LINEARLY APPORTIONING MULTI-STAGE HARMONIC INTERACTION.

Why Percentages Are Prohibited:
  - Acoustic and non-linear electrical stages interact synergistically. A +3 dB upstream resonant
    peak does not produce a simple arithmetic addition of downstream distortion; it changes the
    entire dynamic clipping threshold and intermodulation profile of the amplifier.
  - Inventing percentage values deceives the user with false precision and fabricates evidence
    that does not exist in the DSP telemetry (violating the Absolute Non-Fabrication Rule).
  - Quantitative decomposition is permitted ONLY where a mathematically exact, calibrated,
    linear physical decomposition method exists for the specific phenomenon (e.g. two linear
    independent uncorrelated noise sources with known calibrated RMS levels). In all other cases,
    percentage attribution is strictly prohibited.

10.2 THE EXTENSIBLE QUALITATIVE CONTRIBUTION TIERS
Tone Translator evaluates and communicates causal contribution using structured qualitative tiers:

  1. `DOMINANT`:
     - The primary driving mechanism behind the observed defect. Eliminating or mitigating this
       mechanism would resolve the fundamental character of the acoustic problem.
     - Evidential Requirement: Corroborated by high-directness evidence (locus isolation, substitution,
       or necessary prediction) showing that the defect largely tracks this mechanism.

  2. `MATERIAL`:
     - A substantial contributor that materially alters the severity, harmonic density, or spectral
       extent of the defect, but is not its sole origin.
     - Example: Preamp clipping saturating and multiplying an upstream pickup resonance peak.

  3. `SECONDARY`:
     - A noticeable but subsidiary contributor whose presence colors or worsens the defect,
       but whose total removal would leave the primary acoustic fault largely recognizable.
     - Example: Minor 120 Hz power supply ripple adding a slight background hum to an already
       severe 60 Hz single-coil pickup buzz.

  4. `ENABLING`:
     - A condition that creates the physical prerequisites for another mechanism to manifest,
       without directly generating the acoustic symptom itself.
     - Example: Wide-open tone pot allowing high-frequency cable resonance to reach the input grid.

  5. `PLAUSIBLE_UNRESOLVED`:
     - A mechanism that is physically consistent with all case data and may contribute to the
       defect, but whose actual contribution cannot be isolated or partitioned with available telemetry.

  6. `NOT_ESTABLISHED`:
     - A condition known to be present in the rig (e.g. vintage ungrounded bridge), but whose
       causal link to the observed acoustic complaint has not been demonstrated by evidence.

SECTION 11 — CAUSAL SUFFICIENCY & EXPLANATORY COVERAGE
================================================================================

11.1 EXPLANATORY COVERAGE ASSESSMENT
Identifying a real physical mechanism does not mean the entire engineering problem has been
explained. A fatal error is "premature diagnostic closure" — identifying one valid defect and
assuming all client complaints are thereby accounted for.

Tone Translator requires an explicit Explanatory Coverage Assessment answering four questions:
  1. Does the mechanism explain ALL measured objective anomalies, or only one frequency band?
  2. Does the mechanism explain the FULL acoustic severity, or only a minor baseline feature?
  3. Does the mechanism account for the user's subjective complaint, or does a gap remain?
  4. Does the mechanism explain the origin, the downstream amplification, or both?

11.2 THE THREE LEVELS OF EXPLANATORY COMPLETENESS
Every diagnosis must document its degree of explanatory completeness:

  1. `COMPLETE_EXPLANATION`:
     - The diagnosed mechanism(s) fully and adequately account for all observed objective anomalies,
       measured deltas, and user-reported perceptual complaints.
     - Example: A single-coil Stratocaster plugged into a high-gain amp with no gate; direct spectral
       inspection shows classic 60 Hz and odd-harmonic mains spikes matching single-coil pickup
       induction, with zero residual unaddressed noise bins.

  2. `PARTIAL_ORIGIN_EXPLANATION`:
     - The mechanism correctly explains where and how a signal feature originated, but does NOT
       alone explain why it is so severe or abrasive in the final output.
     - Example: A +3 dB pickup resonance at 3.8 kHz explains the spectral feature's entry into the rig,
       but does NOT explain the harsh +12 dB harmonic hash in the recorded track without accounting
       for downstream cascaded preamp clipping.

  3. `SUBSIDIARY_EXPLANATION`:
     - The mechanism explains a secondary or incidental anomaly, while the primary user complaint
       remains unaddressed.
     - Example: Diagnosing a slight 0.5 dB room reflection notch at 200 Hz while the user's central
       complaint is severe 4 kHz pick-attack fizz.
     - Architectural Rule: A subsidiary explanation must NEVER be presented as the primary diagnosis.

SECTION 12 — VALID EXCLUSION & HYPOTHESIS RETIREMENT
================================================================================

12.1 RETIREMENT CRITERIA IN CAUSAL DELIBERATION
During Phase 1C.5c deliberation, candidate hypotheses inherited from Phase 1C.5b are either
elevated to a diagnosis, retained as unresolved candidates, or formally retired.
A candidate hypothesis may be retired from the active causal set ONLY under four valid paths:

  1. Valid Exclusion Test (Principle 6 & FB-C3):
     - An empirical test disproves a necessary prediction of the hypothesis under verified sensitivity
       and controlled confounders (e.g. moving mic 1.5 inches off-axis produces zero change in a 4.2 kHz
       peak, validly eliminating on-axis dust-cap beaming as the primary cause).
  2. Direct Locus Disproof:
     - Inspection of clean multi-channel stems proves the feature is absent from the locus where the
       mechanism must operate (e.g. a feature absent from a pre-cabinet FX loop send cannot originate in the preamp).
  3. Evidential Supersession:
     - Direct, high-directness telemetry establishes a complete causal explanation that physically
       precludes the candidate mechanism.
  4. Scope Invalidation:
     - Clarified user intent confirms the phenomenon is an intentional artistic feature rather than a fault.

12.2 RETROSPECTIVE RETIREMENT AUDITABILITY (PRINCIPLE 20)
Retired hypotheses are NEVER silently deleted or purged from the record.
Every retired hypothesis must be preserved in the `RetiredHypothesisRegister` within the deliberation trace:
  - Documenting the exact hypothesis ID;
  - Specifying the retirement reason and the empirical test or evidence that justified it;
  - Ensuring an independent reviewer can inspect why an alternative explanation was set aside.

SECTION 13 — CONTRADICTION HANDLING & DIAGNOSIS UNDER CONFLICT
================================================================================

13.1 PRESERVATION OF EMPIRICAL CONTRADICTIONS
In automated systems, conflicting data is frequently smoothed over or silently averaged to
produce a neat, uninterrupted execution flow.
Tone Translator strictly repudiates silent conflict resolution:
    TONE TRANSLATOR NEVER AVERAGES OR COMPROMISES CONFLICTING EVIDENCE.

Conflict Invariants:
  - If a calibrated FFT measurement shows a severe +8 dB peak at 4 kHz, but critical listening
    reports the guitar as "dark and muffled", Tone Translator must NOT average the two into "neutral".
  - The conflict must be logged as an explicit `ConflictRecord` in the deliberation trace.
  - Causal hypotheses explaining the contradiction itself (e.g. severe ear fatigue, monitoring room
    nulls, or sub-bass masking of treble frequencies) must be elevated into active consideration.

13.2 LOAD-BEARING CONTRADICTIONS BLOCK CAUSAL RESOLUTION
When conflicting evidence impacts a load-bearing premise of the primary causal explanation:
  - Tone Translator is STRICTLY FORBIDDEN from declaring a resolved causal diagnosis.
  - The active reasoning session must transition to:
    `CONTRADICTORY_EVIDENCE_BLOCKS_DIAGNOSIS` or `DISCRIMINATING_EVIDENCE_REQUESTED`.
  - The deliberation record must clearly expose the contradictory evidence to the user,
    explain why the conflict prevents a defensible diagnosis, and specify the discriminating test
    required to resolve the contradiction.

SECTION 14 — ASSUMPTION SENSITIVITY & LOAD-BEARING RISK
================================================================================

14.1 EVALUATING DIAGNOSTIC DEPENDENCE ON ASSUMPTIONS
Every real-world engineering diagnosis relies to some degree on provisional assumptions
inherited from Phase 1C.5b (e.g. guitar volume pot setting, string gauge, unmeasured tube bias).
Tone Translator strictly forbids treating a conditional diagnosis as unconditional fact:
    THE STABILITY OF A DIAGNOSIS IS BOUNDED BY ITS LOAD-BEARING ASSUMPTIONS.

If a causal diagnosis relies on the assumption that a guitar's volume pot was fully open
(ensuring high pickup resonant Q), but the player actually had the pot rolled down to 6
(which flattens resonance and introduces series resistance), the entire causal diagnosis
is invalidated.

14.2 THE FOUR ASSUMPTION SENSITIVITY TIERS
Tone Translator evaluates every diagnosis against four explicit assumption sensitivity tiers:

  1. `ROBUST_TO_ASSUMPTION`:
     - The diagnosis holds true regardless of whether the assumption is valid or invalid.
     - Example: Diagnosing acoustic comb filtering from an on-axis microphone 2 inches off a hard
       reflective floor is robust to whether the guitar has a 250k or 500k volume pot.

  2. `CONDITIONAL_ON_ASSUMPTION`:
     - The diagnosis holds true only within the bounded validity range of the stated assumption,
       but failure of the assumption merely shifts the severity rather than reversing the cause.
     - Example: Diagnosing power-stage saturation where the exact onset threshold depends on
       assuming nominal domestic AC mains voltage (120V vs 112V brownout).

  3. `INVALIDATING_DEPENDENCY`:
     - If the assumption is false, the diagnosed causal mechanism is physically impossible or
       fundamentally misattributed.
     - Example: Diagnosing pickup cable capacitive loading on a guitar that is secretly equipped
       with active pickups and an onboard low-impedance preamp buffer.

  4. `LOAD_BEARING_REQUIRING_VERIFICATION`:
     - An assumption with high load-bearing risk that must be verified by user prompt or test
       before downstream Phase 1C.5d may commit to a high-friction or irreversible intervention.
     - Example: "Assuming the recorded audio represents the direct guitar-to-amp signal with no
       hidden DAW plugins on the input channel (Risk: LOAD_BEARING; requires verification)."

SECTION 15 — KNOWLEDGE GROUNDING & THE REFERENCE CASE FIREWALL
================================================================================

15.1 KNOWLEDGE GROUNDING BOUNDARIES (PHASE 1C.4 FIREWALL)
Tone Translator leverages the frozen Phase 1C.4 Knowledge Architecture to structure
causal models and verify physical plausibility. However, the Knowledge Firewall establishes:
    GOVERNED KNOWLEDGE PROVES WHAT IS PHYSICALLY POSSIBLE;
    ONLY CASE EVIDENCE PROVES WHAT ACTUALLY OCCURRED.

Strict Epistemic Invariants:
  - A peer-reviewed KnowledgeClaim (KC) documenting that cathode-biased EL84 tubes exhibit
    screen compression under transient peaks proves that this mechanism is physically viable.
  - A KnowledgeClaim does NOT prove that the current user's EL84 amplifier is currently compressing,
    nor that screen compression is the cause of their specific low-end complaint.
  - Case telemetry and empirical observations must independently demonstrate that the mechanism
    is active in the current case.
  - Familiarity bias is prohibited: an obscure, unindexed mechanism supported by empirical evidence
    outranks a famous textbook KnowledgeClaim that contradicts current telemetry.

15.2 THE REFERENCE CASE FIREWALL IN CAUSAL DIAGNOSIS
Phase 1C.5c strictly preserves the Reference Case Firewall established in Phase 1C.5b:
    HISTORICAL REFERENCE CASES ARE STRUCTURAL ANALOGIES.
    THEY NEVER ESTABLISH CURRENT-CASE DIAGNOSIS.

The "Brown Sound / Variac" Invariant Demonstration:
  - Case Telemetry: A user states: "I want that classic 1978 Sunset Sound lead guitar brown sound.
    I have a 100W Marshall Plexi reissue head and a 4x12 with Greenbacks." Audio analysis shows
    high crest factor (12.8 dB) and bright top-end bite.
  - Historical Archive: The knowledge base contains documented production notes from Sunset Sound (1978)
    where Eddie Van Halen ran a Marshall Super Lead with mains voltage dropped to 89V AC using an external Variac.
  - Prohibited Failure Mode: Declaring as a diagnosis: "The user's amplifier lacks power-tube sag
    because it is not running at 89V AC like Eddie Van Halen's 1978 rig."
  - Authoritative Diagnosis Discipline: The historical case suggests power-stage voltage sag and
    reactive load damping as candidate structural analogies. However, the current user's diagnosis
    is evaluated strictly against their own case evidence: "The user's amplifier operates at standard
    line voltage with comparatively high B+ / available power-stage headroom, keeping power tubes in
    a clean linear region; internal circuit voltages remain unmeasured and UNKNOWN."

SECTION 17 — DIAGNOSIS STATUS MODEL & QUALITATIVE UNCERTAINTY
================================================================================

17.1 EXTENSIBLE DIAGNOSIS STATUS MODEL
The diagnosis lifecycle state machine within Stage 08 is governed by an extensible status model:

  1. `CAUSAL_DIAGNOSIS_SUPPORTED`:
     - High-directness affirmative evidence confirms a primary causal mechanism; major competing
       alternatives are validly excluded or weakened; residual uncertainty is fully bounded.
  2. `PROVISIONAL_DIAGNOSIS`:
     - The diagnosed mechanism is the most plausible explanation consistent with case data,
       but relies on load-bearing unverified assumptions or uncalibrated audio telemetry.
  3. `COMPOUND_CAUSAL_DIAGNOSIS`:
     - Multiple distinct physical mechanisms across one or more signal loci jointly account
       for the observed phenomena (structured as Primary, Secondary, Joint, Enabling, or Amplifying).
  4. `LOCUS_RESOLVED_MECHANISM_UNRESOLVED`:
     - Empirical stems definitively isolate the entry locus (e.g. Dry DI precedes amp),
       but telemetry cannot distinguish between competing internal component mechanisms.
  5. `MULTIPLE_CAUSES_REMAIN_VIABLE`:
     - Evidence demonstrates that several distinct mechanisms are physically and acoustically
       viable, without discriminating evidence available to distinguish them.
  6. `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE_FOR_CAUSAL_RESOLUTION`:
     - Supplied telemetry is too poor, corrupted, or lossy to support any defensible causal claim;
       forces progression under high uncertainty or issues a discriminating evidence request.
  7. `CONTRADICTORY_EVIDENCE_BLOCKS_DIAGNOSIS`:
     - Direct, unresolvable conflict exists between objective DSP measurements and subjective
       perceptual reports, halting diagnostic resolution until discriminating testing occurs.
  8. `DIAGNOSIS_DEFERRED_PENDING_EVIDENCE`:
     - Active deliberation is formally paused in Stage 06A awaiting the execution of a studio
       discriminating test protocol.

17.2 QUALITATIVE UNCERTAINTY & THE UNCERTAINTY-SURVIVAL INVARIANT
In strict compliance with Constitutional Principle 15 and the derived invariant:
    "UNCERTAINTY MUST SURVIVE THE DECISION."

Resolving a diagnosis does NOT extinguish uncertainty:
  - Declaring a primary cause (e.g. microphone beaming) does not prove that the amplifier tone stack
    was perfectly nominal.
  - Every committed diagnosis dossier must carry its companion `ResidualUncertaintyDossier`,
    ensuring that downstream Phase 1C.5d respects unmeasured internal variables, secondary
    contributors, and operational boundaries.

SECTION 18 — DIAGNOSIS TRACEABILITY & AUDIT RECORD CONTRACT
================================================================================

18.1 RETROSPECTIVE AUDITABILITY ARCHITECTURE (PRINCIPLE 20)
In accordance with Constitutional Principle 20:
    "THE DELIBERATION RECORD MUST SUPPORT INDEPENDENT RETROSPECTIVE AUDIT."

An automated causal diagnosis is unacceptable if it emerges from a black box or hidden
chain-of-thought. An independent human recording engineer or technical auditor must be able
to inspect the completed case record and reconstruct every link in the causal derivation:

    CAUSAL DIAGNOSIS RECORD
        ↓ [Justified By]
    ACTIVE CANDIDATE HYPOTHESES (Phase 1C.5b)
        ↓ [Corroborated By]
    DISCRIMINATING TEST PROTOCOL OUTCOMES (Phase 1C.5b Stage 06A)
        ↓ [Grounded Upon]

SECTION 19 — RESIDUAL UNCERTAINTY DOSSIER AFTER DIAGNOSIS
================================================================================

19.1 STRUCTURE OF THE POST-DIAGNOSIS UNCERTAINTY DOSSIER
In naive automation, the declaration of a diagnosis marks the end of uncertainty.
In professional sound engineering, establishing a diagnosis reveals exactly what remains
unknown about the physical circuit and acoustic space.

Every committed `CausalDiagnosisRecord` carries a mandatory `PostDiagnosisUncertaintyDossier`:
  1. Unobserved Internal Circuit Variables:
     - Internal tube bias voltages, B+ plate sag dynamics, actual pot taper tolerances,
       and unmeasured pickup coil resistance.
  2. Unisolated Secondary Mechanisms:
     - Minor harmonic generation, subtle acoustic baffle resonances, or cabinet air-leak
       whistles that could not be isolated from the dominant primary cause.
  3. Measurement Limitations:
     - Acoustic room boundary reflections in the listening capture, lossy MP3 compression
       artifacts, or finite FFT window resolution boundaries.
  4. Operational Validity Bounds:
     - SPL ceilings, temperature ranges, or playing dynamic levels outside of which the
       diagnosed mechanism may behave differently (e.g. power-stage saturation ceases if
       the guitar volume is rolled down).

19.2 PREVENTING FALSE CLOSURE IN DOWNSTREAM PHASES
Phase 1C.5d (Intervention Selection) and Phase 1C.5e (Outcome Prediction) are bound by the
contents of this dossier:
  - Phase 1C.5d must NOT select an aggressive, irreversible intervention based on an unverified
    internal state documented in the uncertainty dossier.
  - Phase 1C.5e must incorporate the documented uncertainty bounds into its outcome prediction,
    warning the user if a proposed remedy addresses the primary cause but leaves a material
    secondary contributor untouched.

20.1 THE STAGE 08 TO STAGE 09 INTERFACE CONTRACT
Phase 1C.5c formally terminates when the active session reaches Stage 08:
    `CAUSAL_DIAGNOSIS_RESOLVED` (or graceful bounded abstention states).

Upon termination, Phase 1C.5c transmits the complete, immutable Causal Diagnosis Dossier
to Phase 1C.5d (Stage 09: `ENGINEERING_REQUIREMENTS_FORMULATION`).
The handoff package contains:
  1. Diagnosed Physical Causal Mechanism(s) and their verified signal loci;
  2. Conceptual Causal Chain tracing the problem from root physical condition to perceptual symptom;
  3. Qualitative Contribution Structure (Primary, Secondary, Joint, Enabling, Severity Amplifier);
  4. Explanatory Coverage Assessment (accounting for what is explained and what remains unresolved);
  5. Complete Post-Diagnosis Residual Uncertainty Dossier and Unknown Domain Register;
  6. Documented Assumption Dependencies and their load-bearing risk ratings;
  7. Auditable trace links connecting every causal claim directly to factual case evidence.

20.2 STRICT PROHIBITIONS ON THE HANDOFF CONTENT (THE REMEDY FIREWALL)
In strict preservation of the Remedy Firewall (RF-1), the handoff package transmitted
to Phase 1C.5d MUST NOT contain:
  - Proposed intervention actions (e.g. "Move the mic 2 inches off-axis");
  - Parametric EQ center frequencies, Q values, or gain cuts (e.g. "-4 dB cut at 3.8 kHz");
  - Dynamic processor recommendations or threshold settings;
  - Processor, pedal, amplifier, or cabinet substitution choices;
  - AmpliTube 5 gear IDs, module routings, or XML parameters;
  - Remedy trade-off ratings or intervention rankings.

Phase 1C.5c delivers an authoritative physical diagnosis of what is causally occurring.
How that reality is navigated to achieve the user's musical intent is the exclusive
charter of Phase 1C.5d.

SCENARIO A — STRONG SINGLE DOMINANT CAUSE (MIC DUST-CAP BEAMING)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. UPSTREAM PHASE 1C.5b INTAKE:
   - User Complaint: "My recorded distorted guitar tone has a piercing, brittle high-end fizz on chords."
   - DSP Observations: +6.4 dB elevation in the 4.0-5.0 kHz region on close-mic cab capture; Dry DI shows flat response.
   - Candidate Hypotheses from 1C.5b:
     * H1 (Transduction): Center-cone on-axis mic placement capturing directional dust-cap acoustic beaming.
     * H2 (Preamp): Preamp cold-clipper generating excessive upper harmonic distortion.
     * H3 (Speaker): Electro-mechanical speaker cone breakup in the 4.5 kHz zone.
   - Stage 06A Discriminating Test Outcome:
     * Action: Microphone moved 1.5 inches off-axis toward speaker cone edge; gain and distance held constant.
     * Result: 4.0-5.0 kHz energy attenuates by -5.2 dB; harsh fizz disappears from listening audition;
       overall dynamic crunch remains punchy and articulate.

2. CAUSAL EVIDENCE CLASSES EVALUATED:
   - Necessary-Prediction Confirmation: Moving the microphone off-axis confirmed the direct acoustic
     prediction required if dust-cap beaming was the cause.
   - Controlled Substitution/Manipulation: Changing only the physical angle/position altered the phenomenon.
   - Locus Isolation: Dry DI showed nominal upper-mids, isolating feature downstream of the instrument.

3. LOCUS VS MECHANISM RESOLUTION:
   - Resolution State: `LOCUS_RESOLVED_MECHANISM_RESOLVED`
   - Signal Locus: MICROPHONE_TRANSDUCTION (isolated via off-axis test).
   - Physical Mechanism: Directional high-frequency acoustic wave beaming from the rigid speaker dust-cap.

4. CAUSAL NARROWING & EXCLUSION:
   - Hypothesis H1 (Mic Beaming): Elevated to confirmed primary cause.
   - Hypothesis H2 (Preamp Cold Clipper): Weakened as primary explanation; preamp settings were unchanged
     during off-axis test, yet the acoustic defect was eliminated. Retained as minor baseline saturation.
   - Hypothesis H3 (Speaker Breakup): Weakened; physical cone breakup would radiate across wider angles
     and not attenuate by >5 dB with a 1.5-inch lateral shift.

5. CAUSAL ROLE & CONTRIBUTION STRUCTURE:
   - Primary Cause: On-axis microphone dust-cap acoustic beaming (`DOMINANT`).
   - Secondary Contributor: None identified.
   - Enabling Condition: High-gain amplifier providing upper-frequency harmonic energy for the mic to transduce.

6. CONCEPTUAL CAUSAL CHAIN:
   Rigid speaker dust-cap vibrates pistonically at 4.2 kHz
   → High-frequency sound waves beam in a narrow directional lobe perpendicular to the cone center
   → On-axis microphone capsule positioned directly in the beaming path captures elevated 4.2 kHz pressure
   → Captured audio exhibits +6.4 dB spectral peak relative to off-axis energy
   → Human listener experiences piercing, fatiguing high-frequency fizz.

7. EXPLANATORY COVERAGE ASSESSMENT:
   - Explanatory Completeness: `COMPLETE_EXPLANATION`.
   - The dust-cap beaming mechanism fully accounts for the measured +6.4 dB peak, the -5.2 dB off-axis delta,
     and the user's subjective description of high-end fizz.

8. ASSUMPTION SENSITIVITY:
   - Assumption: Microphone distance from grille cloth was maintained during off-axis movement (Risk: LOW).
   - Sensitivity Tier: `ROBUST_TO_ASSUMPTION` (5.2 dB attenuation is too large to be explained by minor distance drift).

9. RESULTING CAUSAL DIAGNOSIS:
   - Diagnosis Status: `CAUSAL_DIAGNOSIS_SUPPORTED`
   - Calibrated Assertion: "Evidence strongly supports on-axis microphone dust-cap beaming as the dominant
     cause of the 4.0-5.0 kHz fizz; preamp clipping and speaker breakup are weakened as primary explanations."

10. POST-DIAGNOSIS RESIDUAL UNCERTAINTY DOSSIER:
    - Unobserved Circuit Variables: Internal amplifier cathode bypass capacitor tolerances and tube bias states.
    - Operational Validity Bounds: Diagnosis applies specifically to close-microphone placement on this speaker cone.

11. WHAT TT IS NOT ENTITLED TO CONCLUDE (THE REMEDY FIREWALL):
    - TT is NOT entitled to prescribe: "Leave the mic at 1.5 inches off-axis and cut 4.5 kHz by 3 dB on your DAW EQ."
    - TT is NOT entitled to recommend buying a ribbon microphone or swapping speaker models.
    - STOP: Commits causal diagnosis dossier to trace; hands off to Phase 1C.5d.

SCENARIO B — COMPOUND CAUSATION (PICKUP RESONANCE + PREAMP CLIPPING)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. UPSTREAM PHASE 1C.5b INTAKE:
   - User Complaint: "My bridge humbucker rhythm tone has an unbearable metallic clank on heavy palm mutes."
   - DSP Observations: +7.2 dB resonance peak at 3.6 kHz on Dry DI; recorded processed track shows extreme
     intermodulation distortion and odd harmonics across 3.6 kHz, 7.2 kHz, and 10.8 kHz; crest factor is low (6.8 dB).
   - Candidate Hypotheses from 1C.5b:
     * H1 (Instrument Electrical): High cable capacitance interacting with pickup inductance producing resonant peak.
     * H2 (Preamplifier Stage): Cascaded preamp gain stages heavily overdriven, squaring the waveform.
     * H3 (Speaker): Voice coil rub or mechanical distortion.
   - Stage 06A Test Outcomes:
     * Short-Cable Test (Guitar directly into interface via 3-foot cable): Resonant peak shifts from 3.6 kHz to 6.2 kHz
       and drops in Q amplitude.
     * Preamp Gain Rollback Test (Gain lowered from 8 to 4 with original cable): 3.6 kHz peak remains on DI, but
       the harsh intermodulation sidebands and upper harmonics disappear; tone becomes clear but bright.

2. CAUSAL EVIDENCE CLASSES EVALUATED:
   - Controlled Substitution: Short-cable test confirmed electrical LC resonance mechanism.
   - Controlled Gain Manipulation: Preamp gain rollback confirmed non-linear harmonic multiplication.
   - Direct Locus Isolation: Dry DI proved the 3.6 kHz feature enters before the amplifier.

3. LOCUS VS MECHANISM RESOLUTION:
   - Resolution State: `LOCUS_RESOLVED_MECHANISM_RESOLVED` (Across two loci: Instrument Electrical and Preamp Stage).
   - Physical Mechanisms: Passive LC resonant tank circuit + non-linear multi-stage vacuum tube overdrive.

4. CAUSAL NARROWING & EXCLUSION:
   - Single-Cause Hypothesis (H1 alone): Excluded as a complete explanation; pickup resonance alone is merely bright,
     not harsh and clanky without preamp clipping.
   - Single-Cause Hypothesis (H2 alone): Excluded as a complete explanation; preamp clipping alone does not produce
     a selective 3.6 kHz emphasis without the resonant upstream boost.
   - Compound Causation Confirmed: H1 and H2 operate in compound interaction.
   - Hypothesis H3 (Voice Coil Rub): Falsified by valid exclusion; artifact disappeared on preamp rollback at high SPL.

5. CAUSAL ROLE & CONTRIBUTION STRUCTURE:
   - Primary Cause (Origin): Passive pickup inductance loaded by high cable capacitance (`DOMINANT` origin).
   - Severity Amplifier: Cascaded preamp high-gain clipping saturating and multiplying the peak (`MATERIAL` amplifier).
   - Interaction Mode: `NON_LINEAR_MULTIPLICATION` & `CASCADED_EXACERBATION`.

6. CONCEPTUAL CAUSAL CHAIN:
   High-inductance bridge pickup loaded by high-capacitance 20-foot instrument cable
   → Under-damped LC resonance creates +7.2 dB electrical voltage peak at 3.6 kHz in Dry DI
   → Overdriven preamp gain stages driven far past clipping threshold by the elevated 3.6 kHz peak
   → Non-linear distortion generates dense odd-harmonic series and intermodulation products
   → Captured audio exhibits harsh, dense spectral clank
   → Player experiences aggressive, unmusical metallic fatigue on palm-muted attacks.

7. EXPLANATORY COVERAGE ASSESSMENT:
   - Explanatory Completeness: `COMPLETE_EXPLANATION` (when modeled as compound interaction).
   - Explains both the origin of the 3.6 kHz spike and the dense harmonic distortion across higher bands.

8. ASSUMPTION SENSITIVITY:
   - Assumption: Guitar tone pot was on 10 during tracking (Risk: MODERATE; verified by user verbal confirmation).
   - Sensitivity Tier: `CONDITIONAL_ON_ASSUMPTION` (If tone pot were rolled down, resonance would be damped).

9. RESULTING CAUSAL DIAGNOSIS:
   - Diagnosis Status: `COMPOUND_CAUSAL_DIAGNOSIS`
   - Calibrated Assertion: "Causal contribution established for compound mechanism: pickup-cable electrical
     resonance at 3.6 kHz is the primary origin of the peak, while downstream preamp high-gain clipping
     acts as a material severity amplifier."

10. POST-DIAGNOSIS RESIDUAL UNCERTAINTY DOSSIER:
    - Unobserved Circuit Variables: Exact pickup coil DC resistance and cable capacitance per foot (unmetered).
    - Unisolated Secondary Mechanisms: Minor speaker cone stiffness contributing to upper-mid bite.

11. WHAT TT IS NOT ENTITLED TO CONCLUDE (THE REMEDY FIREWALL):
    - TT is NOT entitled to prescribe: "Replace your cable with a Mogami 10-foot and turn your Preamp Gain knob down to 5."
    - STOP: Transmits compound diagnosis to Phase 1C.5d.

22.2 DETAILED AUDIT TRACE OF CRITICAL ADVERSARIAL VECTORS

CHECK 1: HIGHEST-RANKED HYPOTHESIS AUTOMATICALLY BECOMES DIAGNOSIS
- Vulnerability: In heuristic AI systems, candidate hypotheses are assigned comparative scores,
  and the highest-scoring candidate is automatically crowned as the "diagnosis" even if its
  absolute evidential backing is weak, speculative, or unverified.
- Phase 1C.5c Enforcement: Section 4.2 establishes the Anti-Ranking Principle:
  "RANKING IS COMPARATIVE; DIAGNOSIS IS ABSOLUTE."
  A hypothesis with a comparative plausibility rank of 1 out of 5 remains merely an unverified
  hypothesis unless it satisfies the affirmative Causal Evidence Standards (Section 5.1).
  If no hypothesis earns causal resolution, the session enters `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE_FOR_CAUSAL_RESOLUTION`.

CHECK 2: MOST FAMILIAR MECHANISM WINS
- Vulnerability: Common studio folklore encourages default explanations (e.g. "Harshness is always
  4 kHz on a dynamic mic"; "Mud is always 250 Hz cabinet resonance").
- Phase 1C.5c Enforcement: Section 5.2 and Constitutional Principle 7 strictly forbid familiarity bias.
  Every diagnosis requires empirical telemetry demonstrating that the specific physical mechanism
  is active in the current case. Governed knowledge (Phase 1C.4) establishes physical plausibility
  but provides zero weight toward proving current-case occurrence.

CHECK 3: CORRELATION BECOMES CAUSATION
- Vulnerability: A user turns up the amplifier gain knob, and simultaneously harshness increases;
  a naive system concludes the amplifier gain stage generates the harshness.
- Phase 1C.5c Enforcement: Section 5.1 and Scenario D demonstrate that temporal or parameter correlation
  does not establish origin. A downstream gain increase will naturally amplify pre-existing upstream
  pickup resonance. Locus isolation (Class 1) and Controlled Substitution (Class 3) are mandatory
  to separate feature origin from downstream amplification.

CHECK 4: USER-STATED CAUSE BECOMES DIAGNOSIS
- Vulnerability: The user declares: "My tubes are blown, tell me how to fix them." An eager assistant
  adopts the user's causal claim without verification.
- Phase 1C.5c Enforcement: Section 4.2, Section 15.2, and Scenario G mandate that user statements are
  categorized strictly as Category 3 (User Subjective Report). Objective evidence (Dry DI capture)
  must independently verify or disprove the claim. In Scenario G, the user's assertion is formally
  disproven by direct DI locus isolation, protecting the user from unnecessary tube replacement.

CHECK 5: HISTORICAL REFERENCE BECOMES CURRENT-CASE DIAGNOSIS
- Vulnerability: The user requests the "1978 Van Halen Brown Sound"; an AI assumes the user's tone
  suffers from a lack of Variac voltage starvation or a Celestion Greenback speaker cone.
- Phase 1C.5c Enforcement: Section 15.2 and Scenario F strictly enforce the Reference Case Firewall.
  Documented historical rigs serve solely as contextual comparison targets. Diagnosis is confined
  exclusively to physical telemetry derived from the user's current audio captures.

CHECK 6: ONE POSITIVE TEST PROVES SOLE CAUSATION
- Vulnerability: A single test confirms that cabinet beaming is occurring; the system immediately
  declares cabinet beaming to be the sole cause of the tone problem, ignoring pickup resonance.
- Phase 1C.5c Enforcement: Section 7.1 and Section 11.2 require an Explanatory Coverage Assessment.
  A positive test proves contribution, but claiming sole cause requires proving that the mechanism
  accounts for all observed spectral, dynamic, and perceptual features within experimental error.

CHECK 7: ONE NEGATIVE TEST EXCLUDES WITHOUT SUFFICIENT SENSITIVITY
- Vulnerability: A quick FFT glance shows no 60 Hz spike, so power supply hum is dismissed, even though
  the FFT noise floor was -45 dBFS and the hum is audible at -52 dBFS.
- Phase 1C.5c Enforcement: Section 5.1 (Class 3) and Scenario K mandate calibrated negative evidence.
  A negative finding may only eliminate a candidate if the measurement procedure's bandwidth,
  resolution, and noise-floor margin are explicitly proven adequate to detect the feature.

CHECK 8: FAILURE OF A AUTOMATICALLY PROVES B
- Vulnerability: Binary fallacy where disproving Hypothesis A (e.g. mic placement) is taken as proof
  of Hypothesis B (preamp clipping) without affirmative testing of B.
- Phase 1C.5c Enforcement: Section 6.1 and Scenario H enforce Non-Binary Falsification. In open physical
  systems, the candidate space is rarely exhaustive. Eliminating A merely reduces the candidate pool;
  Hypothesis B must independently satisfy causal evidence hurdles.

CHECK 9: MULTIPLE CONTRIBUTORS FORCED INTO SINGLE-CAUSE EXPLANATION
- Vulnerability: Forcing an audio problem with multiple roots (e.g. pickup resonance + preamp distortion)
  into a single "root cause" box.
- Phase 1C.5c Enforcement: Section 7.1 and Scenario B establish compound causation as a first-class
  architectural construct. Causal roles (Primary, Secondary, Joint, Enabling, Exacerbating) allow
  accurate multi-stage modeling.

CHECK 10: CONTRIBUTION PERCENTAGES INVENTED
- Vulnerability: Fabricating pseudo-precise numbers (e.g. "65% cabinet beaming, 35% pickup Q").
- Phase 1C.5c Enforcement: Section 10.1 establishes the Non-Fabrication Rule for quantitative attribution.
  Unless calibrated multi-track decomposition or physical subtraction data exists, contribution
  must be expressed using defined qualitative tiers (`DOMINANT`, `MATERIAL`, `SECONDARY`, `MINOR`).

CHECK 11: LOCUS RESOLUTION MISTAKEN FOR MECHANISM RESOLUTION
- Vulnerability: Confirming that an artifact enters in the speaker cabinet and jumping to the conclusion
  that the speaker cone voice coil is warped.
- Phase 1C.5c Enforcement: Section 9.1 and Scenario C decouple locus from mechanism. The system
  explicitly records `LOCUS_RESOLVED_MECHANISM_UNRESOLVED`, preventing unwarranted mechanical claims.

CHECK 12: MECHANISM RESOLUTION MISTAKEN FOR EXPLANATORY COMPLETENESS
- Vulnerability: Confirming pickup resonance and assuming the entire tone issue is solved, while ignoring
  massive power-amp intermodulation distortion.
- Phase 1C.5c Enforcement: Section 11.1 enforces Explanatory Coverage checks across all observations.
  A supported mechanism is explicitly flagged as `PARTIAL_ORIGIN_ONLY` if downstream severity remains
  unaccounted for.

CHECK 13: DIAGNOSIS SILENTLY BECOMES INTERVENTION
- Vulnerability: Stating the diagnosis as: "The tone needs a 4 dB cut at 3.5 kHz with a high-pass filter."
- Phase 1C.5c Enforcement: Section 3.2 establishes the Remedy Firewall (RF-1). Causal diagnosis must
  characterize the physical phenomenon, never prescribe an equalizer setting or signal chain change.
  Stage 07/08 terminates before Phase 1C.5d Stage 09.

CHECK 14: KNOWLEDGECLAIM BECOMES CURRENT-CASE EVIDENCE
- Vulnerability: Citing a textbook claim that "JCM800s have bright caps" as proof that the user's amp
  has an engaged bright cap.
- Phase 1C.5c Enforcement: Section 15.1 enforces Knowledge Quarantine. KnowledgeClaims inform prior
  plausibility and candidate generation in Phase 1C.5b, but cannot serve as empirical evidence.

CHECK 15: MISSING EVIDENCE ASSUMED NOMINAL
- Vulnerability: Assuming that because tube bias was not measured, tube bias must be factory standard.
- Phase 1C.5c Enforcement: Principle 3 and Section 19.1 enforce that unmeasured parameters are logged
  in the `ResidualUncertaintyDossier` as unknown variables, never presumed nominal.

CHECK 16: CONTRADICTORY EVIDENCE SILENTLY AVERAGED
- Vulnerability: When DSP shows a bass cut of -6 dB but the user reports boomy bass, the system averages
  them to "nominal bass".
- Phase 1C.5c Enforcement: Section 13.1 and Scenario E require contradiction preservation. Conflicting
  evidence generates a load-bearing conflict record and halts causal resolution until resolved.

CHECK 17: LOAD-BEARING ASSUMPTION HIDDEN
- Vulnerability: Reaching a firm diagnosis that quietly assumes the guitar volume pot was at 10, without
  disclosing that if the pot was rolled off, the diagnosis is completely wrong.
- Phase 1C.5c Enforcement: Section 14.1 and Scenario I mandate explicit Assumption Sensitivity Tiers.
  Diagnoses dependent on unverified assumptions are marked `CONDITIONAL_ON_ASSUMPTION` with clear risk tags.

CHECK 18: RESIDUAL UNCERTAINTY ERASED AFTER DIAGNOSIS
- Vulnerability: Declaring 100% certainty and wiping out all notes about unmeasured secondary variables.
- Phase 1C.5c Enforcement: Constitutional Principle 21 and Section 19.1 mandate that every resolved
  diagnosis must be accompanied by an immutable `ResidualUncertaintyDossier`.

CHECK 19: PLATFORM LIMITATIONS DISTORT PHYSICAL DIAGNOSIS
- Vulnerability: Altering the diagnosis because AmpliTube 5 or a specific plugin lacks the controls
  to address the true physical cause.
- Phase 1C.5c Enforcement: Principle 15 and Section 2.1 enforce absolute platform neutrality. The
  physical diagnosis reflects electro-acoustic reality regardless of downstream software capabilities.

CHECK 20: CAUSAL TRACE CANNOT BE INDEPENDENTLY RECONSTRUCTED
- Vulnerability: Presenting a causal assertion without a recorded audit trail connecting it back to
  specific audio stems and test outputs.
- Phase 1C.5c Enforcement: Principle 20 and Section 18.1 mandate the `CausalDiagnosisRecord` data contract,
  enabling complete, independent retrospective audit from raw audio telemetry to final diagnosis.

CHECK 21: "MOST PLAUSIBLE" SILENTLY UPGRADED INTO "CONFIRMED"
- Vulnerability: A hypothesis is labeled "most plausible" during brainstorming and downstream stages
  treat it as an established, proven fact.
- Phase 1C.5c Enforcement: Section 4.2 and Section 16.1 enforce Calibrated Diagnostic Language. Plausible
  hypotheses remain strictly `PLAUSIBLE_UNRESOLVED` until empirical proof is recorded.

CHECK 22: VALIDLY WEAKENED HYPOTHESIS INCORRECTLY RETIRED
- Vulnerability: A test weakens a hypothesis, and the system deletes it completely, preventing recovery
  if a primary candidate is later refuted.
- Phase 1C.5c Enforcement: Section 12.2 mandates that weakened hypotheses are downgraded in status but
  retained in the candidate registry unless definitively excluded by a valid-exclusion test.

CHECK 23: SECONDARY CONTRIBUTOR INCORRECTLY LABELED PRIMARY
- Vulnerability: Blaming a slight room reflection for a severe comb-filtering issue caused by dual-mic
  phase cancellation.
- Phase 1C.5c Enforcement: Section 7.2 and Section 10.2 enforce clear causal hierarchies. Exacerbators
  and secondary factors cannot be elevated above the primary physical source.

CHECK 24: DOWNSTREAM SEVERITY AMPLIFIER INCORRECTLY LABELED FEATURE ORIGIN
- Vulnerability: Blaming an overdrive pedal for generating high-frequency harshness that was actually
  produced by single-coil pickup resonance and merely clipped by the pedal.
- Phase 1C.5c Enforcement: Section 8.1, Section 10.2, and Scenario D enforce causal chain differentiation
  between origin mechanisms and downstream severity amplifiers.

CHECK 25: EXPLANATORY COVERAGE OVERSTATED
- Vulnerability: Claiming a diagnosis fully explains a multi-faceted tone problem when it only accounts
  for one specific frequency anomaly.
- Phase 1C.5c Enforcement: Section 11.2 mandates explicit classification of Explanatory Coverage:
  `COMPLETE_EXPLANATION`, `PARTIAL_ORIGIN_ONLY`, `PARTIAL_SEVERITY_ONLY`, or `DEFICIENT`.

CHECK 26: PROVISIONAL DIAGNOSIS USED AS A EUPHEMISM FOR GUESS
- Vulnerability: Labeling speculative guesses as "provisional diagnoses" to bypass evidence hurdles.
- Phase 1C.5c Enforcement: Section 17.2 establishes strict qualifying criteria for provisional diagnoses:
  strong converging evidence, non-load-bearing residual alternatives, and explicit verification triggers.

CHECK 27: QUANTITATIVE PRECISION INFERRED FROM QUALITATIVE EVIDENCE
- Vulnerability: Taking a listening report of "a bit boomy" and turning it into a diagnosis of "+4.2 dB
  at 115 Hz with Q of 2.1".
- Phase 1C.5c Enforcement: Section 10.1 and Numerical Lineage rules bar inventing precision. Qualitative
  evidence produces qualitative causal characterizations.

CHECK 28: COMPOUND DIAGNOSIS REPRESENTED AS MUTUALLY EXCLUSIVE ALTERNATIVES
- Vulnerability: Presenting two co-occurring faults (e.g. dead strings + low pickup height) as an
  "either/or" choice rather than a compound system.
- Phase 1C.5c Enforcement: Section 7.1 and Phase 1C.5b pair-specific relationships explicitly model
  coexisting and synergistic causal mechanisms.

CHECK 29: STRONG EVIDENCE AT ONE LOCUS EXTRAPOLATED TO ANOTHER LOCUS
- Vulnerability: Discovering clipping in the preamp and assuming the power amp must also be clipping.
- Phase 1C.5c Enforcement: Section 9.2 enforces strict locus boundaries. Evidence gathered at one
  physical signal stage cannot be projected onto upstream or downstream stages.

CHECK 30: DIAGNOSIS REMAINS ACTIVE AFTER LOAD-BEARING ASSUMPTION IS FALSIFIED
- Vulnerability: An assumption upon which a diagnosis rests is disproven by new information, but the
  system continues to maintain the diagnosis.
- Phase 1C.5c Enforcement: Section 14.3 establishes the Assumption Invalidation Hook. Falsification of
  a load-bearing assumption immediately revokes the diagnosis, returning the session to Stage 07.

     Category 1 (Raw Evidence) != Category 2 (Factual Observation) !=
     Category 3 (User Report) != Category 4 (Phenomenological Interpretation) !=

   - Phase 1C.5b negative observations are consumed strictly within their documented

     a negative observation beyond its calibrated domain.

   - All numerical values carried into the causal diagnosis record retain their explicit
     lineage origin categories (Categories 1 to 9 per Phase 1C.5b Section 18.2).
     Phase 1C.5c introduces zero ungrounded scalar numbers or fabricated percentages.
