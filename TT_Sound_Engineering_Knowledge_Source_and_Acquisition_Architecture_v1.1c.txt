1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1.1 ARCHITECTURAL PURPOSE & STATUS
Phase 1C.4b-R establishes the corrected, authoritative source-selection, registration,
acquisition, structural extraction, atomic decomposition, provenance attribution,
normalization, and ingestion architecture for the Tone Translator (TT) Professional
Sound Engineering Knowledge System.

THIS SPECIFICATION DEFINES HOW KNOWLEDGE ENTERS THE SYSTEM.

It provides the engineering foundation to convert external technical literature,
experimental research, standards, manufacturer data, practitioner accounts, and
legacy code assets into verifiable, platform-independent, atomic candidate knowledge
units governed by the frozen Phase 1C.4a entity model.

Phase 1C.4b-R is governed by the core epistemological discipline:
    SOURCE VOLUME ≠ KNOWLEDGE QUALITY
    SISO: POOR SOURCE MATERIAL, FLAWED EXTRACTION, OR BROKEN PROVENANCE
    CONTAMINATES EVERY DOWNSTREAM LAYER REGARDLESS OF AGENT CAPABILITY.

The architecture is explicitly designed to produce traceable, provenance-backed,
bounded, reviewable, conflict-aware, uncertainty-preserving, and professionally
governed engineering knowledge. It reduces epistemic contamination; it does not
make ungrounded promises of universal or absolute truth.

1.2 SCOPE BOUNDARIES & INTEGRITY RULES
This artifact is an architectural specification:
  - NO external web research, literature scraping, or text harvesting was performed.
  - NO production knowledge library collections or vector databases were populated.
  - NO production application code, prompt templates, tests, or Firestore rules were modified.
  - NO runtime retrieval mechanisms (Phase 1C.4c) or conflict adjudication tools (Phase 1C.4d) were implemented.
  - NO Current TT knowledge migration (Phase 1C.4e) was executed.
  - NO runtime engineering reasoning loops (Phase 1C.5) or competency benchmark runners (Phase 1C.6) were created.

1.3 CORE ACQUISITION PIPELINE
The governing architecture enforces a ten-stage internal ingestion pipeline terminating
in a review-ready candidate package:
    SOURCE
      ↓
    REGISTER
      ↓
    ACQUIRE
      ↓
    EXTRACT
      ↓
    DECOMPOSE
      ↓
    NORMALISE
      ↓
    ATTRIBUTE
      ↓
    EVIDENCE ASSESS
      ↓
    CONFLICT / UNCERTAINTY IDENTIFY
      ↓
    PACKAGE & QUEUE (UNDER_REVIEW)

The internal acquisition pipeline outputs candidate packages in the frozen `UNDER_REVIEW`
lifecycle state. The candidate package is handed to Phase 1C.4d and is subject to the
applicable governance, evidence-sufficiency, review, conflict, and activation requirements
defined there. Canonical promotion to `VALIDATED_ACTIVE` status occurs only when formally
authorized by Phase 1C.4d governance.

4. ACQUISITION DESIGN PRINCIPLES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The acquisition architecture is governed by twenty inviolable design principles:

1. Knowledge Informs Reasoning — It Never Replaces It:
   Acquisition extracts physical principles, circuit relationships, boundaries, and trade-offs.
   It never extracts or generates prescriptive symptom-to-solution recipes.
2. Retrieval Is Not Acquisition:
   Acquisition is an offline, high-assurance vetting pipeline; retrieval is runtime
   indexing. Knowledge cannot be retrieved until it is acquired, attributed, and approved.
3. Documents Are Not Knowledge:
   A textbook, schematic, dataset, or paper is an artifact. Knowledge consists of normalized,
   atomic, verifiable propositions extracted from that artifact.
4. Extraction Is Not Validation:
   Accurately parsing a statement or extracting a data point verifies only what the source
   asserts, not whether the assertion is physically valid or operationally generalizable.
5. Repetition Is Not Corroboration:
   Multiple sources repeating an unverified assertion trace to a common ancestor.
   True corroboration requires independent methodology and diverse measurement conditions.
6. Popularity Is Not Authority:
   Studio folklore, internet consensus, and high view counts do not transform acoustic
   myths into engineering principles.
7. Manufacturer Origin Is Neither Automatic Truth Nor Disqualification:
   Datasheets, schematics, and measured responses provide invaluable physical facts,
   but must be separated from marketing prose and subjective promotional claims.
8. Practitioner Experience Is Evidence, Not Invariant Law:
   Documented master engineer practices provide crucial empirical insights and context,
   but must be bounded as contextual practices or empirical tendencies, not invariant laws.
9. AI Is an Extraction Agent, Not an Epistemic Source:
   LLMs have no autonomous authority. Model-generated assertions not grounded in the
   registered source are classified as unsupported additions and barred from canonical entry.
10. Current TT Is Legacy Source Material, Not Authority:
    Existing codebase rules enter as `UNASSESSED_LEGACY_SOURCE`. They must be atomically
    deconstructed, classified under the 3-axis taxonomy, and backed by appropriate provenance.
11. Runtime Outcomes Do Not Automatically Become Global Knowledge:
    Successful DSP presets or session feedback remain quarantined in session records. Only
    recurrent, controlled, plausibility-verified observations become `CandidateLesson` units.
12. Device-Specific Physical Facts Are Legitimate Knowledge:
    Measured physical properties of real commercial hardware (e.g. speaker cone resonance)
    are valid sound engineering facts when bound to a `DeviceSpecificityContext`.
13. Platform Implementation Details Are Contamination:
    Software GUIDs, XML parameter paths, DAW slot conventions, and exporter coordinates
    are strictly prohibited from the Knowledge Library.
14. Uncertainty Must Be Preserved:
    Approximations, measurement tolerances, and exploratory limits must be explicitly
    captured in `EpistemicValidation` and `OperationalBoundary` records.
15. Credible Conflicts Must Not Be Silently Resolved:
    Divergent engineering approaches or boundary disagreements between credible sources
    must be preserved in explicit `ConflictRecord` entities.
16. Provenance Must Survive Every Transformation:
    Every atomic claim, causal link, and boundary envelope must retain an unbroken link
    to an Exact Reconstructable Source Locator and documented extraction lineage.
17. Human Review Must Remain Reconstructable:
    Every promotion decision must be auditable: a reviewer must be able to inspect the
    source, extraction prompts, diffs, and evidence dossier to verify the chain of reasoning.
18. Canonical Knowledge Must Remain Inspectable and Auditable:
    All active knowledge is stored as human-readable, Git-tracked structured files (JSON/YAML)
    in canonical repositories.
19. Anti-Recipe Imperative Discipline:
    The ingestion linter unconditionally halts on ungrounded prescriptive generalisations and
    context-free workflow recipes ("always cut at 400 Hz", "never compress bass", "set Drive to 7").
    It does NOT operate as a blunt grammatical ban on imperative language. Legitimate normative
    procedural instructions—such as hardware calibration alignments (e.g. "align 0 VU to +4 dBu
    into 600 Ω"), laboratory measurement procedures, device operating limits, safety requirements,
    and standards compliance specifications—remain admissible when explicitly anchored to an
    `OperationalBoundary`, assigned an appropriate Knowledge Role (e.g. `PROFESSIONAL_CONVENTION`),
    and backed by verifiable technical provenance.
20. The Smallest Defensible Claim Prevails:
    A tightly bounded, specific claim that is demonstrably true under defined conditions is
    infinitely superior to an impressive, over-generalized claim that fails under edge cases.

5. SOURCE LANDSCAPE ARCHITECTURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The acquisition system recognizes eleven distinct source classes. To avoid a naive
scalar hierarchy, each class is evaluated along multi-dimensional operational axes:
source class influences epistemic assessment, but does not dictate epistemic truth.

5.1 CLASS A: FOUNDATIONAL SCIENTIFIC & ENGINEERING SOURCES
  - Description: Peer-reviewed textbooks, monographs, and academic treatises in physics,
    acoustics, electronics, wave mechanics, and signal processing.
  - Expected Strengths: Rigorous mathematical foundations, formal physical principles,
    reproducible derivations, clear boundary conditions.
  - Expected Limitations: Often abstract; may lack specific musical instrument context
    or guitar amplifier circuit idiosyncrasies.
  - Appropriate Uses: Grounding physical laws, wave equations, filter mathematics,
    and foundational circuit theorems.
  - Inappropriate Uses: Prescribing aesthetic musical mix balances or subjective tone goals.
  - Provenance Profile: High formal bibliographic stability (ISBN, DOI, publisher records).
  - Corroboration Expectations: High stability across established scientific literature.
  - Common Failure Modes: Applying idealized linear assumptions (e.g. small-signal analysis)
    to highly non-linear, overdriven guitar circuits.
  - Suitable Claim Types: `PHYSICAL_LAW`, `ESTABLISHED_ENGINEERING_PRINCIPLE`.
  - Unsuitable Claim Types: `CONTEXT_DEPENDENT_PRACTICE`, `ILLUSTRATIVE_EXAMPLE`.

5.2 CLASS B: PEER-REVIEWED RESEARCH LITERATURE
  - Description: Formal research papers published in indexed archival journals and
    conference proceedings (e.g. AES, ASA, IEEE).
  - Expected Strengths: Controlled empirical methodologies, statistical rigor, novel
    experimental validation, detailed error analyses.
  - Expected Limitations: Narrow operating envelopes; may test single synthetic signals
    rather than complex musical performances.
  - Appropriate Uses: Specialized empirical relationships (e.g. tube soft-clipping
    harmonic generation, speaker cone breakup modes, ear canal transfer functions).
  - Inappropriate Uses: Universalizing narrow laboratory findings across diverse production environments.
  - Provenance Profile: Authoritative archival citations (DOI, peer review records).
  - Corroboration Expectations: Verification against independent experimental replications.
  - Common Failure Modes: Prematurely accepting an uncorroborated single-study finding
    as established consensus.
  - Suitable Claim Types: `EMPIRICAL_RELATIONSHIP`, `HYPOTHETICAL_OR_COMPETING_MODEL`.
  - Unsuitable Claim Types: Universal workflow rules.

5.3 CLASS C: TECHNICAL STANDARDS & SPECIFICATIONS
  - Description: Formally ratified standards, recommendations, and reference alignments
    issued by international engineering bodies (e.g. AES, EBU, ITU-R, IEC, IEEE, ANSI).
  - Expected Strengths: Absolute clarity on reference calibrations, measurement methodologies,
    nominal electrical levels, and metering algorithms.
  - Expected Limitations: Purely definitional; standards define conventions and metrics,
    not creative engineering strategies.
  - Appropriate Uses: Defining calibration levels (0 VU, dBu, dBFS), nominal impedances,
    standard measurement bandwidths, and target loudness curves.
  - Inappropriate Uses: Treating a regional calibration convention as an immutable law of physics.
  - Provenance Profile: Formally ratified institutional documentation.
  - Corroboration Expectations: Reference to current vs superseded versions.
  - Common Failure Modes: Applying an audio broadcast standard (e.g. -23 LUFS) as a mandatory
    production constraint for an overdriven guitar amplifier pre-capture.
  - Suitable Claim Types: `PROFESSIONAL_CONVENTION`.
  - Unsuitable Claim Types: Causal models of acoustic phenomena.

5.4 CLASS D: PROFESSIONAL TEXTBOOKS & APPLIED TREATISES
  - Description: Comprehensive handbooks written by seasoned engineering authorities
    focusing on practical studio, acoustic, and circuit applications.
  - Expected Strengths: Strong bridge between fundamental theory and real-world studio
    practice; comprehensive coverage of system topology and equipment interactions.
  - Expected Limitations: May blend rigorous physics with anecdotal rules of thumb
    without explicit boundary markers.
  - Appropriate Uses: System signal flow, microphone deployment techniques, gain staging
    practices, acoustic boundary treatment.
  - Inappropriate Uses: Accepting colloquial rules of thumb as inviolable scientific truths.
  - Provenance Profile: Established technical publisher editions and revisions.
  - Corroboration Expectations: Cross-verification against underlying physics and manufacturer data.
  - Common Failure Modes: Generalizing a studio rule of thumb beyond its intended musical context.
  - Suitable Claim Types: `ESTABLISHED_ENGINEERING_PRINCIPLE`, `CONTEXT_DEPENDENT_PRACTICE`.
  - Unsuitable Claim Types: Unbounded universal assertions.

5.5 CLASS E: MANUFACTURER ENGINEERING MATERIAL
  - Description: Technical datasheets, schematics, engineering whitepapers, service manuals,
    and patents published by hardware/component manufacturers.
  - Expected Strengths: Authoritative data on the physical construction, component values,
    measured frequency response, and circuit topology of specific hardware.
  - Expected Limitations: Commercial interest; marketing narratives frequently intermixed with data.
  - Appropriate Uses: Device-specific behavior, circuit topologies, component tolerances,
    and published electrical specifications.
  - Inappropriate Uses: Marketing slogans, subjective timbre descriptions ("warm British roar").
  - Provenance Profile: Corporate engineering documents, schematic revisions, patent numbers.
  - Corroboration Expectations: Bench testing or independent measurement where feasible.
  - Common Failure Modes: Failing to filter out promotional exaggerations from objective specs.
  - Suitable Claim Types: `DEVICE_SPECIFIC_BEHAVIOUR`, `CIRCUIT_ELECTRONICS`.
  - Unsuitable Claim Types: Unbounded subjective assertions.

5.6 CLASS F: INDEPENDENT TECHNICAL MEASUREMENTS & TESTING
  - Description: Controlled, calibrated laboratory or bench measurements conducted by
    independent engineering institutions, research labs, or qualified technical testers.
  - Expected Strengths: Objective, unvarnished empirical data free from commercial marketing bias.
  - Expected Limitations: Heavily dependent on test rig calibration, room acoustics, and methodology.
  - Appropriate Uses: Validating or refuting manufacturer claims, quantifying non-linearities,
    measuring component tolerances and acoustic dispersion.
  - Inappropriate Uses: Discarding test methodology context, temperature, or measurement SPL.
  - Provenance Profile: Test protocol documentation, equipment calibration logs, raw data files.
  - Corroboration Expectations: Replicability across independent test environments.
  - Common Failure Modes: Treating a measurement taken at a single microphone position or single
    SPL as the universal behavior of the device.
  - Suitable Claim Types: `EMPIRICAL_RELATIONSHIP`, `DEVICE_SPECIFIC_BEHAVIOUR`.
  - Unsuitable Claim Types: Invariant theoretical proofs.

5.7 CLASS G: DOCUMENTED PROFESSIONAL PRACTICE
  - Description: In-depth, technically articulate interviews, masterclasses, and published
    case studies by master recording engineers, mastering engineers, and amplifier designers.
  - Expected Strengths: Deep insights into real-world trade-off balancing, psychoacoustic
    masking solutions, and context-dependent adjustments.
  - Expected Limitations: Subjective vocabulary; lack of formal scientific controls;
    practices are heavily tied to specific personal aesthetics and room environments.
  - Appropriate Uses: Capturing trade-off vectors, diagnostic cues, and contextual practices.
  - Inappropriate Uses: Extracting physical laws or universal dogmatic presets.
  - Provenance Profile: Published technical interviews, authorized masterclasses, transcribed papers.
  - Corroboration Expectations: Corroboration by underlying acoustics or multiple independent master practitioners.
  - Common Failure Modes: Confusing a creative personal signature technique with an engineering standard.
  - Suitable Claim Types: `CONTEXT_DEPENDENT_PRACTICE`, `ILLUSTRATIVE_EXAMPLE`.
  - Unsuitable Claim Types: `PHYSICAL_LAW`, universal invariant claims.

5.8 CLASS H: PRACTITIONER & TECHNICAL COMMUNITY KNOWLEDGE
  - Description: Technical discussions, repair logs, and empirical observations from
    curated professional audio engineering forums and builder communities (e.g. AES archives, GroupDIY).
  - Expected Strengths: Practical reporting of hardware quirks, component drift, failure modes,
    and field modification behaviors.
  - Expected Limitations: High noise-to-signal ratio; persistent repetition of urban legends;
    uncontrolled test environments; lack of rigorous attribution.
  - Appropriate Uses: Identifying candidate hypotheses for empirical validation; discovering
    real-world hardware edge cases.
  - Inappropriate Uses: Direct promotion to canonical knowledge without rigorous verification.
  - Provenance Profile: Forum threads, repair logs, community repository commits.
  - Corroboration Expectations: In accordance with Phase 1C.4a-DR Addendum 1, recover the best
    available underlying provenance and validate the claim to an evidence standard appropriate to
    its nature, scope, uncertainty, jurisdiction, and risk. Depending on the claim, appropriate
    corroboration may include authoritative technical literature, standards, manufacturer engineering
    documentation, independent measurements, multiple independent practitioner observations, or
    controlled empirical evidence. Primary literature and laboratory measurement are preferred where
    appropriate, but neither is a universal requirement; however, poorly supported practitioner
    or community claims must remain quarantined when evidence is insufficient for canonical promotion.
  - Common Failure Modes: Ingesting confirmation bias or forum groupthink as objective fact.
  - Suitable Claim Types: `HYPOTHETICAL_OR_COMPETING_MODEL`, `ENGINEERING_TENDENCY` (quarantined).
  - Unsuitable Claim Types: Direct promotion to canonical active status without appropriate corroboration and review.

5.9 CLASS I: CURRENT TT LEGACY MATERIAL
  - Description: The existing Tone Translator codebase, algorithms, tables, and heuristics.
  - Ingestion Classification: Formally registered with acquisition classification
    `UNASSESSED_LEGACY_SOURCE` and `source_rigor_tier: UNASSESSED`.
    `UNASSESSED_LEGACY_SOURCE` is an acquisition classification denoting unverified legacy code
    origin, NOT an assessed methodological rigor tier or epistemic truth ranking.
    Individual claims undergo provenance recovery and are assigned appropriate
    methodological rigor under Phase 1C.4e.
  - Expected Strengths: Contains valuable domain intuitions (e.g. microphone positioning
    heuristics, cabinet impedance pairings, pre-distortion filter concepts).
  - Expected Limitations: Entangled with deterministic code rules, keyword overrides,
    AmpliTube 5 GUIDs, and arbitrary numerical thresholds.
  - Appropriate Uses: Source of candidate concepts to be atomically deconstructed and classified.
  - Inappropriate Uses: Never automatically authoritative. Never cited as proof of truth.
  - Provenance Profile: Repository commit history, file paths, and function line numbers.
  - Corroboration Expectations: Best available underlying provenance appropriate to the claim type.
  - Common Failure Modes: Migrating code rules unchanged as "proven engineering principles."
  - Suitable Claim Types: Candidate propositions undergoing deconstruction in Phase 1C.4e.
  - Unsuitable Claim Types: Direct entry into `VALIDATED_ACTIVE` status.

5.10 CLASS J: TT EXPERIENTIAL & CANDIDATE LESSON MATERIAL
  - Description: System-generated runtime observations, closed-loop discrepancy analyses,
    and user session telemetry.
  - Ingestion Classification: Quarantined `CandidateLesson` records.
  - Expected Strengths: Direct insight into real-world algorithm performance and edge cases.
  - Expected Limitations: Case-specific; prone to localized overfitting and noise.
  - Appropriate Uses: Internal hypothesis generation and system refinement.
  - Inappropriate Uses: Cannot update canonical knowledge without passing the Evidence-Sufficiency Dossier.
  - Provenance Profile: Internal runtime execution logs and session traces.
  - Corroboration Expectations: Multi-factor evidence-sufficiency dossier.
  - Common Failure Modes: "Single good preset -> universal global rule."
  - Suitable Claim Types: Quarantined candidate lessons only.
  - Unsuitable Claim Types: Canonical active knowledge.

5.11 CLASS K: HISTORICAL ARCHIVAL & PATENT LITERATURE
  - Description: Original patents, historical technical reports, and vintage design notes.
  - Expected Strengths: Clear insight into original inventive intent and baseline derivations.
  - Expected Limitations: May describe obsolete technologies or components no longer manufactured.
  - Appropriate Uses: Documenting circuit topology origins and foundational patent claims.
  - Inappropriate Uses: Enforcing vintage design constraints on modern high-speed DSP or wide-bandwidth converters.
  - Provenance Profile: Patent office documentation, historical corporate laboratory archives.
  - Corroboration Expectations: Verification against modern circuit analysis.
  - Common Failure Modes: Treating a patent claim (which is legally protective) as an objective performance guarantee.
  - Suitable Claim Types: `ESTABLISHED_ENGINEERING_PRINCIPLE`, `CIRCUIT_ELECTRONICS`.
  - Unsuitable Claim Types: Modern production workflow standards.

6. SOURCE DISCOVERY VS SOURCE AUTHORITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

6.1 THE DISCOVERY-TO-AUTHORITY FIREWALL
A foundational failure mode in automated knowledge ingestion is conflating *discovering*
a source with *verifying* its authority. The acquisition architecture erects an impenetrable
firewall between these phases:

    +───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────+
    │                     DISCOVERY MECHANISMS                      │
    │  - Search Engine Queries       - LLM Parametric Memory        │
    │  - Literature Bibliographies   - Community Forum Links        │
    │  - Academic Citation Graphs    - YouTube / Podcast References │
    │  - Manufacturer Webpages       - Current TT Code Comments     │
    +───────────────────────────────┬───────────────────────────────+
                                    │ Identifies candidate artifact
                                    ▼
    +───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────+
    │                   DISCOVERY-TO-REGISTRATION                   │
    │                    VERIFICATION GATEWAY                       │
    │  Checks:                                                      │
    │  1. Does physical/digital artifact actually exist?            │
    │  2. Can stable bibliographic identity be established?         │
    │  3. Is source text/data accessible for direct audit?          │
    +───────────────────────────────┬───────────────────────────────+
                                    │ Registers SourceDocument
                                    ▼
    +───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────+
    │                     EPISTEMIC ASSESSMENT                      │
    │  - Source Rigor (Methodological) - Replicability & Controls     │
    │  - Domain Jurisdiction (J)     - Commercial Conflict Audit    │
    │  - Methodological Rigor        - Operational Boundary Check   │
    +───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────+

6.2 REJECTED EPISTEMIC SHORTCUTS
The acquisition engine unconditionally rejects the following arguments as proofs of authority:
  - "Gemini / GPT knows this principle": Model parametric memory is a discovery index,
    not a verifiable source. The model must point to a real, auditable source document.
  - "Many websites / blogs say this": Repetition without independent methodology is
    echo-chamber folklore.
  - "This is common studio wisdom": Intuitions must be physically formulated and bound.
  - "The manufacturer marketing page says this": Commercial claims require engineering
    schematic, datasheet, or bench measurement corroboration.
  - "Current TT already runs this code": Existing software heuristics are unvalidated legacy.

7. SOURCE REGISTRATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

7.1 REGISTRATION CONTRACT
Before any content can be parsed or claims extracted, the source must be registered as a
`SourceDocument` entity. The registration record guarantees persistent identity and auditability.

```json

  "title": "Acoustic Behavior of Loudspeaker Diaphragms at High Excursion",
  "authors": ["Dr. John E. Schmidt", "Robert M. Davis"],
  "publisher_or_institution": "Audio Engineering Society",
  "publication_date": "2004-11-15",
  "publication_year": 2004,
  "edition_or_version": "Vol. 52, Issue 11",
  "identifiers": {
    "doi": "10.17743/jaes.2004.0042",
    "isbn": null,
    "issn": "1549-4950",
    "url": "https://www.aes.org/e-lib/browse.cfm?elib=12984"
  },
  "source_class": "CLASS_B_PEER_REVIEWED_RESEARCH",
  "source_rigor_tier": "R2_CONTROLLED_EMPIRICAL",
  "domain_jurisdictions": {
    "9": "J_CORE",
    "10": "J_APPLIED",
```

8. COPYRIGHT, LICENSING & SOURCE PRESERVATION BOUNDARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

8.1 THE TWO-STORE INFORMATION GOVERNANCE ARCHITECTURE
To enable comprehensive engineering learning while maintaining rigorous information
governance and respecting legal/access constraints, TT establishes a two-store architecture:

1. Canonical Knowledge Library (`/knowledge/canonical/`):
   - Scope: Contains ONLY normalized, platform-independent engineering knowledge:
     `KnowledgeClaim`, `CausalModel`, `OperationalBoundary`, `ConflictRecord`.
   - Content: Human-readable declarative claims, mathematical AST/LaTeX formulas,
     typed numerical bounds, and minimal cited excerpts necessary for provenance.
   - Storage: Git-tracked, shared engineering repository.
   - Prohibitions: NEVER contains full book chapters, scraped web mirrors, scanned PDF
     bundles, or proprietary vendor code.

2. Source Preservation Archive (`/knowledge/archive/sources/`):
   - Scope: Secure, access-controlled offline storage holding raw source artifacts
     (PDFs, schematics, CSV measurement sweeps, vendor manual scans).
   - Content: Cryptographically sealed source files referenced by `SourceDocument.document_hash`.
   - Access: Restricted strictly to authorized human auditors and offline extraction pipelines.
   - Purpose: Enables complete provenance audit and extraction replayability without
     redistributing source material to runtime environments or end users.

8.2 LICENSING TYPOLOGY & JURISDICTION-AWARE GOVERNANCE
This architecture is an engineering information-governance design, not a universal legal
determination of fair use or fair dealing rights across jurisdictions. Every registered
source must record its explicit licensing status:
  - `PUBLIC_DOMAIN`: Unrestricted extraction and citation.
  - `OPEN_ACCESS_CC`: Free extraction, citation, and attribution under CC license terms.
  - `COMMERCIAL_ACQUIRED`: Lawfully acquired book, paper, or dataset. Extraction of
    underlying factual principles and data; full-text distribution prohibited; short
    cited excerpts retained solely for auditability.
  - `PROPRIETARY_CONFIDENTIAL`: Vendor NDA material. Extraction permitted only if
    expressly authorized; claims tagged with confidential operational flags.
  - `RESTRICTED_ACCESS`: Institutional or subscription access. Extraction captures facts
    and derivations; raw documents remain in the access-controlled archive.

9. PARSING & STRUCTURAL EXTRACTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

9.1 STRUCTURAL EXTRACTION REQUIREMENTS
Parsing converts raw source artifacts into structured extraction canvases while
preserving structural context. A successful parse does NOT equal knowledge validation.

The parser must capture:
  1. Exact Structural Hierarchy: Preserve chapter, section, subsection, and pagination.
  2. Mathematical Notation Fidelity: Isolate equations into exact LaTeX strings or
     MathML blocks. Preserve explicit variable definitions and operational ranges.
  3. Physical Units & SI Standardization: Extract original units exactly (e.g. inches,
     cm, mV, dBu, dBV) before any standardized SI conversion.
  4. Figure, Table & Schematic Identity: Extract table headers, column labels, figure
     captions, axis scaling (log vs linear), and schematic circuit reference designators.
  5. Optical Character Recognition (OCR) Audit: For scanned documents, record an
     OCR confidence indicator; low-confidence passages require human transcription verification.

9.2 EXTRACTION INTEGRITY CONTROLS
  - Corrupted Tables: If table rows or column alignments cannot be unambiguously parsed,
    the table must be quarantined.
  - Mathematical Syntax Validation: All extracted LaTeX formulas must pass AST syntax
    compilation before entering candidate extraction.
  - Unit Mismatches: If an extracted formula lacks explicit unit definitions for variables,
    it is flagged with `MISSING_VARIABLE_UNITS`.

10. ATOMIC KNOWLEDGE EXTRACTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

10.1 THE DE-SUMMARIZATION MANDATE
A critical failure mode of standard AI knowledge pipelines is "summarization."
Summarization discards nuance, blurs boundary conditions, and strips causal mechanisms
in favor of bland executive overviews.

The acquisition engine ENFORCES ATOMIC DECOMPOSITION:
  - Complex source passages must be decomposed into irreducible, independently
    verifiable engineering assertions.
  - Each extracted candidate must preserve:
      * The underlying physical/electrical mechanism.
      * The operating boundary and prerequisite conditions.
      * The magnitude, direction, and sensitivity of the change.
      * Associated collateral trade-offs.

10.2 ATOMIC EXTRACTION EXAMPLE
Consider an authoritative studio textbook passage discussing pre-distortion filtering:
"In heavy rock production, attenuating frequencies below 100 Hz on the rhythm guitar
signal before it hits an overdriven tube preamplifier dramatically tightens low-end
transient punch and prevents intermodulation flub, though excessive cutting makes the
instrument sound sterile and thin in sparse arrangements."

  - FLAWED / REJECTED EXTRACTION (Recipe Summary):
    "Cut frequencies below 100 Hz before high-gain distortion to get tight guitars."
    *Why Rejected*: Creates a dogmatic imperative; strips circuit mechanism (intermodulation);
    ignores arrangement context; hides collateral penalty (thin tone).

  - ACCEPTED ATOMIC EXTRACTION:
    * Proposition 1 (KnowledgeClaim): "Pre-distortion high-pass filtering reduces low-frequency
      intermodulation distortion products generated in non-linear clipping stages."
    * Proposition 2 (CausalModel): "Input: Sub-100 Hz attenuation. Mediating Stage: PREAMP_NONLINEAR.
      Acoustic Consequence: Decreases bass IMD, accelerates palm-mute decay envelope.
      Trade-off: Loss of fundamental low-end body; risks sterile timbre in sparse arrangements."
    * Proposition 3 (OperationalBoundary): "Prerequisites: Non-linear clipping stage THD > 10%;
      signal containing high-energy low-frequency transient fundamentals."

11.2 DECOMPOSITION TAXONOMY
The decomposition engine parses compound inputs into eleven distinct functional components:
  1. Physical Phenomenon: The observable wave or circuit event.
  2. Causal Mechanism: The mediating transfer function or transformation.
  3. Operating Boundary / Envelope: Quantitative parameter range of validity.
  4. Prerequisite System State: Mandatory upstream or environmental conditions.
  5. Confounding Variable: Independent factors that mimic or obscure the effect.
  6. Collateral Trade-off: Inevitable physical or perceptual penalty.
  7. Quantitative Metric: Specific numerical threshold or tolerance.
  8. Contextual Practice: Bounded methodology developed for specific genres/rooms.
  9. Subjective Aesthetic Descriptors: Qualitative terms (e.g. "warm", "muddy", "spongy").
     *Preserved in context alongside candidate acoustic correlates without forcing artificial 1:1 metrics.*
  10. Prescriptive Recommendation: Imperative guidance ("should", "must").
      *Must be stripped or converted into conditional trade-off options.*
  11. Known Exceptions / Invalidation Conditions: Scenarios where the principle fails.

13. NORMALISATION WITHOUT MEANING LOSS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

13.1 NORMALISATION RULES
Normalization maps extracted assertions into the frozen Phase 1C.4a entity model.
It must never alter the physical truth or strip necessary qualifications:

  1. Units Preservation & Standardisation:
     - Original units are preserved in `ClaimAttribution.reconstructable_locator`.
     - Normalized values are expressed in base SI units (Hz, m, s, V, Pa, dB SPL, dBFS).
  2. Observation vs Interpretation Separation:
     - Direct laboratory measurements are classified as `EMPIRICAL_RELATIONSHIP`.
     - Explanatory theoretical models are classified as `ESTABLISHED_ENGINEERING_PRINCIPLE`
       or `PHENOMENOLOGICAL_MODEL`.
  3. Correlation vs Causation Delineation:
     - Statistical associations without an identified transfer function are tagged as
       `ENGINEERING_TENDENCY` with explicit confounders.
     - Causal assertions require instantiating a `CausalModel` with an explicit mediating mechanism.
  4. Descriptive vs Prescriptive Decoupling:
     - Prescriptive statements ("use a notch filter") are rewritten as descriptive
       causal models ("a notch filter attenuates energy within bandwidth B, reducing
       resonance R while introducing phase shift P").
  5. Tendency vs Law Guard:
     - Empirical practices observed in specific studios must NEVER be promoted to `PHYSICAL_LAW`.
     - They must be assigned to Axis 2 `CONTEXT_DEPENDENT_PRACTICE`.

14. MULTI-SOURCE CORROBORATION & EVIDENCE DEPENDENCY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

14.1 THE CIRCULAR CITATION & ECHO-CHAMBER TRAP
Simple source counting is epistemologically invalid. If five modern recording blogs
and three forum posts all repeat that "cutting 400 Hz removes boxiness," but all eight
trace their lineage back to a single 1982 magazine article by one engineer, this represents
ONE historical observation point, not eight corroborating sources.

14.2 EVIDENCE DEPENDENCY GRAPH (EDG) & CLAIM-APPROPRIATE CORROBORATION
The acquisition engine constructs an Evidence Dependency Graph (EDG) for every concept:
  - Ancestry Nodes: The original theoretical derivation, experimental research, patent, standard, or empirical observation.
  - Derivative Nodes: Textbooks, articles, application notes, or manuals citing or repeating the ancestral source.
  - Claim-Appropriate Corroboration Principle:
    Corroboration is independent evidentiary support appropriate to the nature, scope, operational boundary, uncertainty, consequence, and risk of the claim.
    Corroboration factors are evaluated contextually, not as an inflexible universal checklist:
      1. Empirical & Measurement Claims: Corroborated by independent experimental setups, distinct test benches, separate physical measurements, or verified replication across relevant operating conditions.
      2. Theoretical & Mathematical Claims: Corroborated by separate mathematical derivations, proofs, or verified convergence of analytical models.
      3. Circuit & Hardware Claims: Corroborated by independent schematic verification, component bench measurements, or convergent manufacturer engineering documentation.
      4. Standards & Conventions: Corroborated by formal standards alignment (e.g. AES, IEC, IEEE, ITU), consensus industry reference manuals, or established calibration protocols.
      5. Practitioner & Operational Claims: Corroborated by convergent multi-practitioner observations across independent contexts, structured field testing, or subsequent controlled validation.
    Independence from Circular Citation (Universal Constraint):
      Multiple sources constitute true corroboration only when they possess genuine methodological independence:
      - They do not share circular citation ancestry where one source merely repeats another without independent verification.
      - Neither source relies upon the other as its sole epistemic authority.
      - False source diversity (e.g. multiple blog posts or tutorials echoing a single historical magazine quote) is mapped to its single root origin node in the EDG.

14.3 CONSENSUS UPGRADE RULES
  - When true independent corroboration is established across multiple independent sources
    with appropriate rigor and jurisdiction, `epistemic_validation.consensus_state` may be
    upgraded to `ESTABLISHED_CONSENSUS`.
  - If multiple sources cite a common ancestor without independent validation,
    `consensus_state` remains `PREDOMINANT_VIEW` with an explicit lineage note.
  - Primary literature is preferred where available, but consensus is evaluated by
    methodological independence and evidence convergence, not by source category alone.

15. SOURCE RIGOR, JURISDICTION & CLAIM QUALITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

15.1 INDEPENDENCE OF SOURCE RIGOR AND CLAIM QUALITY
The architecture maintains a strict distinction between the general standing of a
source and the validity of a specific extracted claim:
  - High-Rigor Source, Weak Claim: A foundational acoustics textbook (Class A) may make
    an offhand remark about electric guitar speaker distortion in a footnote.
    Because the author was operating outside their primary experimental focus, the
    applicable methodological rigor for that specific claim is R5 (Qualitative Observation)
    at J_PERIPHERAL jurisdiction. It cannot be treated as an authoritative engineering
    principle or physical law merely because of the textbook's general prestige.
  - Practitioner Source, Valid Observation: A veteran amplifier repair technician
    in an informal interview (Class G) observes that a specific screen resistor overheats
    under square-wave overdrive. While practitioner interviews typically provide qualitative
    observational accounts (R5), this specific observation identifies a measurable physical event.
    When independently corroborated on a calibrated test bench with documented oscilloscope sweeps
    (R2 controlled empirical methodology), the claim achieves `ESTABLISHED_ENGINEERING_PRINCIPLE`.

15.2 THE MULTI-DIMENSIONAL EVALUATION CONTRACT
Every claim and source is evaluated independently across orthogonal dimensions:
  1. `source_class`: Categorical taxonomy describing WHAT KIND OF PUBLICATION OR ARTIFACT THE SOURCE IS
     (Classes A through K: Foundational Textbooks, Peer-Reviewed Scientific Journals, Formal Technical
     Standards, Manufacturer Engineering Documentation, Manufacturer Product Documentation,
     Master Engineering Handbooks, Documented Practitioner Accounts, Community Audio Forums,
     Acoustic Simulation Models, Runtime Empirical Sessions, Historical Archive Repositories).
  2. `source_rigor_tier`: Evaluates the methodological and evidentiary characteristics
     exhibited by the source's data, derivations, or measurements:
       - R1_FORMAL_DERIVATION: Controlled mathematical, closed-form physical derivation,
         or formal consensus reference standards with rigorous theoretical validation.
       - R2_CONTROLLED_EMPIRICAL: Controlled laboratory experiments, calibrated instrumentation,
         documented error bounds, repeatable test-bench methodology, and empirical controls.
       - R3_STRUCTURED_ENGINEERING: Structured engineering documentation, circuit schematics,
         measured component parameters, calibrated device profiles, and bench test sweeps.
       - R4_SYSTEMATIC_PRACTICE: Systematic professional practice treatises, structured master
         engineering methodologies, and curated technical engineering handbooks.
       - R5_QUALITATIVE_OBSERVATION: Qualitative field observations, experiential studio accounts,
         uncalibrated practitioner interviews, or descriptive community observations.
       - UNASSESSED: Legacy assets whose methodology has not yet undergone provenance recovery.
     *Strict Independence Boundary (SOURCE CLASS ≠ SOURCE RIGOR)*:
     Source Class describes publication venue; Source Rigor evaluates evidentiary and
     methodological characteristics. They are orthogonal:
       - A Documented Practitioner Account (Class G) can exhibit R2 or R3 rigor if it contains
         calibrated oscilloscope measurements, documented signal traces, and controlled bench testing.
       - A Foundational Textbook (Class A) or Peer-Reviewed Paper (Class B) exhibits R5 observational
         rigor when making unverified offhand qualitative remarks or speculative commentary outside
         its experimental scope.
       - A Manufacturer Product Document (Class E) may contain high-rigor (R2/R3) measured frequency
         response curves alongside unverified (R5) promotional prose within the same publication.
     *Epistemic Basis Orthogonality (RIGOR ≠ EPISTEMIC BASIS)*:
     Source Rigor does NOT determine Epistemic Basis (`PHYSICAL_LAW`,
     `ESTABLISHED_ENGINEERING_PRINCIPLE`, `EMPIRICAL_RELATIONSHIP`, `ENGINEERING_TENDENCY`,
     `HYPOTHETICAL_OR_COMPETING_MODEL`). A high-rigor source does not automatically make an
     extracted proposition a physical law, nor does a lower-rigor source make an observation false.
  2. `domain_jurisdiction`: J_CORE, J_APPLIED, J_PERIPHERAL (evaluated per domain independently).
  3. `epistemic_basis`: Evaluates the nature of underlying truth across the independent 3-axis taxonomy.
  4. `evidence_strength`: THEORETICAL_PROOF, SYSTEMATIC_EMPIRICAL, CORROBORATED_PRACTICE, ANECDOTAL.
  5. `consensus_state`: ESTABLISHED_CONSENSUS, PREDOMINANT, DEBATED, OUTLIER.
  6. `replication_state`: INDEPENDENTLY_REPLICATED, EXPERIMENTALLY_VERIFIED, OBSERVATIONAL_ONLY.
  7. `boundary_certainty`: TIGHTLY_BOUNDED, PARTIALLY_BOUNDED, EXPLORATORY_LIMITS.
  8. `conflict_status`: UNCONTESTED, ACTIVE_COMPETING_CLAIM.

21. AI ROLE & ANTI-HALLUCINATION CONTROLS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

21.1 THE FIRST LAW OF AI ACQUISITION
    THE AI IS AN EXTRACTION AND STRUCTURAL ANALYSIS AGENT.
    THE AI IS NEVER AN EPISTEMIC SOURCE.

21.2 FIVE-LAYER ANTI-HALLUCINATION ARCHITECTURE
To prevent LLM parametric hallucinations from polluting the Knowledge Library:
  1. Source-Grounded Prompting: Extraction prompts strictly restrict candidate generation
     to content directly grounded in the supplied source artifact chunk.
  2. Reconstructable Locator Verification: Every generated `KnowledgeClaim` must link to an
     exact reconstructable source locator and be auditable against the source artifact.
  3. Unsupported Content Isolation: If an LLM introduces an acoustic concept not explicitly
     present in the source passage, the ingestion engine isolates it into an
     `unsupported_model_additions` field.
  4. Mandatory Quarantine of Unsupported Content: Claims containing unsupported model
     additions are quarantined and barred from review readiness.
  5. Zero Self-Authorizing Promotion: AI models cannot approve, sign off, or promote

23. ACQUISITION BATCH ARCHITECTURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

23.1 BOUNDED BATCH DESIGN
To ensure reviewability and prevent cognitive overload on human auditors, acquisition
is executed in Bounded Acquisition Batches:
  - Batch Scope: Restricted to a single Domain or tightly coupled Concept Cluster
    (e.g. "Domain 10: Pressure-Gradient Transducers & Proximity Effect").
  - Source Allocation: Bounded set of selected sources appropriate to the concept cluster.
  - Operational Sizing: Configurable batch sizing targets (e.g. initial operational
    guideline of ~10 to 30 candidate claims per review package) subject to operator tuning.
  - Reconstructability & Replayability Requirement: Every batch must preserve sufficient
    registered-source, workflow, tool, model, and configuration lineage to support historical
    reconstruction and rerunning under the applicable reproducibility guarantees defined in Section 29.
    Deterministic components (such as rule-based parsers, cryptographic hashing, and schema linters)
    can be reproduced exactly; model-assisted components support reconstructable execution lineage
    without promising bit-for-bit identical regeneration.

24. ACQUISITION QUALITY GATES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The internal acquisition pipeline enforces ten discrete quality gates (Gates 1 through 10)
terminating at Gate 10 in a review-ready candidate package in `UNDER_REVIEW` state.
Operational thresholds (such as OCR review triggers or batch sizes) are explicitly designated
as configurable operational parameters (Category B) rather than immutable architectural constants.

Gates 11 and 12 are retained below for end-to-end architectural visibility, but are
formally designated as DOWNSTREAM PHASE 1C.4d GOVERNANCE MILESTONES. They are not
executed by the Phase 1C.4b acquisition pipeline:

Gate 1: DISCOVERED
  - Entry Criteria: Source identified via literature review, bibliography, or citation index.
  - Verification: Basic bibliographic existence verified; duplication check passed.
  - Output: Candidate source record.

Gate 2: REGISTERED
  - Entry Criteria: Complete bibliographic metadata verified; persistent ID assigned.
  - Verification: Cryptographic hash generated; licensing status classified.
  - Output: Immutable `SourceDocument` entity.

Gate 3: ACQUIRED
  - Entry Criteria: Raw source file deposited in access-controlled preservation archive.
  - Verification: Document hash verified against registered `SourceDocument.document_hash`.
  - Output: Sealed raw artifact in archive.

Gate 4: PARSED
  - Entry Criteria: Text, mathematical formulas, structural hierarchy, and data extracted.
  - Verification: Locators mapped; LaTeX equations compiled; OCR quality reviewed against
    configurable review threshold (e.g. initial operational default <0.85 triggers manual review).
  - Output: Structured extraction canvas.

Gate 5: EXTRACTED
  - Entry Criteria: Atomic candidate claims, boundaries, and trade-offs extracted.
  - Verification: Source locator captured; de-summarization rules enforced.
  - Output: Candidate extraction objects.

Gate 6: ATTRIBUTED
  - Entry Criteria: Formal citation links instantiated.
  - Verification: `ClaimAttribution` links candidate claim to Exact Reconstructable Source Locator.
  - Output: Attributed claim records.

Gate 7: DECOMPOSED & NORMALISED
  - Entry Criteria: Compound claims split; units converted to SI; multi-axis taxonomy applied.
  - Verification: 3-axis taxonomy valid; anti-recipe linter passed; platform firewall passed.
  - Output: Normalized candidate `KnowledgeClaim`, `CausalModel`, `OperationalBoundary`.

Gate 8: EVIDENCE ASSESSED
  - Entry Criteria: Source Rigor (methodological characteristics) and Jurisdiction (J_CORE to J_PERIPHERAL) assigned.
  - Verification: `EpistemicValidation` factors populated; no scalar authority scores created.
  - Output: Epistemically classified candidate entities.

Gate 9: CONFLICT & LINEAGE AUDITED
  - Entry Criteria: Comparison against existing canonical library and parallel claims.
  - Verification: Evidence dependency graph checked for circular citations; conflicts flagged.
  - Output: Linked `ConflictRecord` or clean consensus classification.

Gate 10: REVIEW READY (Acquisition Pipeline Terminal Gate)
  - Entry Criteria: Ingestion linting passed; all required fields, boundaries, and locators populated.
  - Verification: Candidate package complete and viewable in audit workbench.
  - Output: Queued candidate package in frozen `UNDER_REVIEW` state.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DOWNSTREAM PHASE 1C.4d GOVERNANCE MILESTONES (EXTERNAL HANDOFF)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Gate 11: GOVERNANCE EVALUATED (Owned by Phase 1C.4d)
  - Entry Criteria: Candidate package in `UNDER_REVIEW` state evaluated under Phase 1C.4d governance requirements.
  - Verification: Formal `ReviewRecord` executed under Phase 1C.4d governance.
  - Output: Governance decision (e.g. `APPROVED`, `REJECTED`, or `CHANGES_REQUESTED`).

Gate 12: CANONICAL ACTIVATION (Owned by Phase 1C.4d)
  - Entry Criteria: Authorized for canonical promotion by Phase 1C.4d under its applicable governance requirements.
  - Verification: Phase 1C.4d lifecycle transition guard updates state to `VALIDATED_ACTIVE`.
  - Output: Immutable canonical knowledge unit eligible for runtime indexing in Phase 1C.4c.

25. ACQUISITION FAILURE MODES & MITIGATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The acquisition architecture provides explicit defenses against 22 distinct failure modes:

1. Hallucinated Citation: LLM invents non-existent author, book, or paper.
   - Mitigation: Bibliographic registration and cryptographic document hash verification at Gate 2.
2. Incorrect Edition / Outdated Revision: Citing an obsolete edition with known errors.
   - Mitigation: Registration requires edition/version tracking against known errata.
3. Outdated / Superseded Standard: Ingesting obsolete standards without context.
   - Mitigation: Standards records maintain `superseded_by` links; operational envelope specifies applicability.
4. Circular Citation Ancestry: Multiple secondary sources repeating one original source counted as independent.
   - Mitigation: Evidence Dependency Graph (Section 14) detects shared lineage and enforces single-origin counting.
5. Duplicated Evidence Lineage: Re-ingesting the same study via different reprints.
   - Mitigation: Cryptographic source hashing and DOI/identifier deduplication at Gate 1.
6. Missing Operational Context: Extracting a parameter without specifying conditions or boundary envelopes.
   - Mitigation: Mandatory `OperationalBoundary` attachment required at Gate 7.
7. Unit Conversion Error: Confusing dBu with dBV during normalization.
   - Mitigation: Automated unit transformation linter with static dimensional analysis.
8. OCR Corruption: Scanned text reading "400 Hz" as "4000 Hz" due to speckle.
   - Mitigation: Configurable OCR review threshold triggers human check; mathematical AST compilation.
9. Table / Figure Misinterpretation: Reversing axes or confusing logarithmic with linear plots.
   - Mitigation: Human verification for all graphical and tabular data digitizations.
10. Marketing Language Treated as Fact: Ingesting manufacturer puffery as physical specifications.
    - Mitigation: Class E ingestion firewall strips subjective adjectives and commercial superlatives.
11. Practitioner Anecdote Universalized: Treating a specific studio technique as an invariant physical law.
    - Mitigation: Multi-axis classification restricts practitioner accounts to Axis 2 Contextual Practices.
12. Model-Generated Content Presented as Source-Derived: LLM adding ungrounded assertions.
    - Mitigation: Reconstructable locator verification isolates model-added tokens into quarantined fields.
13. Correlation Presented as Causation: Ingesting an empirical correlation as a causal mechanism.
    - Mitigation: CausalModel requires explicit mediating mechanisms; correlations tagged as EngineeringTendency.
14. Device-Specific Behavior Universalized: Treating specific tone stack notches as universal guitar amp behavior.
    - Mitigation: Mandatory `DeviceSpecificityContext` tagging for all commercial hardware claims.
15. Historical Practice Presented as Current Standard: Ingesting vintage tape alignments as modern DAW standards.
    - Mitigation: Source class tagging and explicit historical context markers.
16. Measurement Without Reference Conditions: Ingesting an SPL measurement without distance or room specs.
    - Mitigation: Quantitative data contract requires mandatory `reference_conditions` object.
17. False Precision: Encoding "around 3 kHz" as "3000.00 Hz."
    - Mitigation: Numerical Typing Engine enforces `ILLUSTRATIVE_NOMINAL_VALUE` or `EMPIRICALLY_MEASURED_RANGE`.
18. Conflicting Sources Silently Merged: Ingestion pipeline averaging divergent recommendations.
    - Mitigation: Gate 9 conflict detector forces creation of `ConflictRecord`.
19. Source Summarization Losing Exceptions: Discarding "except on acoustic instruments" during extraction.
    - Mitigation: Atomic extraction rules require explicit capture of `known_exceptions`.
20. Current TT Heuristic Promoted Without Provenance: Promoting an existing code rule unchanged.
    - Mitigation: Current TT classified as `UNASSESSED_LEGACY_SOURCE`; cannot promote without recovering provenance.
21. Copyrighted Material Reposited in Canonical Library: Storing full textbook pages in Git.
    - Mitigation: Two-store boundary; canonical library contains only normalized claims and minimal cited excerpts.
22. Silent Recipe Generation: Ingesting an ungrounded imperative "always do X" recipe.
    - Mitigation: Ingestion anti-recipe linter halts on ungrounded prescriptive generalisations
      and symptom-to-setting recipes, while permitting bounded normative calibration, measurement,
      safety, and standards compliance procedures anchored to explicit `OperationalBoundary` envelopes.

26. SOURCE / CLAIM REJECTION & QUARANTINE POLICY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

26.1 TAXONOMY OF INGESTION DISPOSITIONS
The system strictly distinguishes terminal and intermediate dispositions:

  1. REJECT SOURCE:
     - Applied When: The document is fraudulent, commercial marketing with zero technical data,
       unidentifiable provenance, unrecoverable corruption, material integrity failure,
       synthetic material misrepresented as external source, or unlawful licensing conditions.
     - Result: `SourceDocument` marked `REJECTED`. No claims extracted.
  2. REJECT CLAIM:
     - Applied When: The source is legitimate, but the specific extracted proposition is
       contradicted or disproven by established physical laws or validated foundational
       engineering principles, or contains unresolvable internal errors.
     - Result: `KnowledgeClaim` marked `REJECTED`. Preserved in audit archive to prevent re-ingestion.
  3. QUARANTINE CLAIM:
     - Applied When: A claim has an identified source but lacks corroboration, contains
       unsupported model additions, has unverified boundaries, or represents an uncorroborated
       `CandidateLesson`.
     - Result: Moved to `/knowledge/quarantine/`. Invisible to production retrieval.
  4. RETAIN AS CONFLICTING EVIDENCE:
     - Applied When: Two credible sources make competing, irreconcilable claims under
       similar conditions.
     - Result: Both claims retained; bound to an active `ConflictRecord`.
  5. RETAIN AS HISTORICAL CONTEXT:
     - Applied When: A claim represents superseded historical engineering practice.
     - Result: Retained with `knowledge_role: PROFESSIONAL_CONVENTION`, explicitly bounded
       to vintage hardware contexts.
  6. REVIEW READY (PROMOTION CANDIDATE):
     - Applied When: Fully attributed via Exact Reconstructable Source Locator, normalized,
       boundary-complete, lineage-verified, and packaged for expert evaluation.
     - Result: Maintained in frozen `UNDER_REVIEW` state and queued for Phase 1C.4d governance
       evaluation. Transition to `VALIDATED_ACTIVE` is strictly reserved for Phase 1C.4d governance sign-off.

  - "Has a commercial device behavior been improperly universalized?"
  - "Is the mathematical derivation formally correct and complete?"
  - "Can a future auditor completely reconstruct how this knowledge was derived?"

29. AUDITABILITY & REPLAYABILITY SPECIFICATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

29.1 REPLAYABILITY DEFINITIONS
To maintain strict architectural honesty, Phase 1C.4b-R distinguishes between
deterministic components and model-assisted processes:
  - Replayability: The historical acquisition workflow can be reconstructed and rerun
    using preserved inputs, software versions, prompt templates, and configuration manifests.
  - Deterministic Reproducibility: Identical outputs are expected only for components
    explicitly designed and verified as deterministic (e.g. document hashing, schema validation,
    LaTeX AST compilation, unit conversion, and rule-based linters).
  - Model-Assisted Reproducibility: Extraction involving language models can be replayed
    and compared using identical prompts and parameters, but bit-for-bit output identity
    is not assumed across diverse runtimes unless guaranteed by the underlying engine.

29.2 REPROCESSING UPON UPSTREAM IMPROVEMENT
If an extraction rule, parser, or prompt template is updated:
  - The acquisition manifest allows the system to identify every `KnowledgeClaim`
    generated under the previous version.
  - Affected claims are marked `UNDER_REPROCESSING_REVIEW`.
  - The pipeline re-runs the extraction to generate proposed diffs for human expert review.

DECISION A: Canonical Relationship Between Stored Sources and Normalized Knowledge
  - Direction: Independent, Decoupled Relational Linkage.
  - Classification: UNCHANGED

    they do not constitute the runtime knowledge representation.

DECISION B: Physical Storage Architecture for Full Source Documents
  - Direction: Two-Store Information Governance Architecture.
  - Classification: CLARIFIED
  - Rationale: Full source artifacts (PDFs, scans, CSVs, schematics) reside in an access-controlled,
    offline Source Preservation Archive (`/knowledge/archive/sources/`). The Git-tracked
    canonical repository contains only normalized structured files with minimal cited excerpts
    necessary for provenance, respecting applicable copyright and access conditions.

DECISION C: Minimum Provenance Required for Promotion Eligibility
  - Direction: Unbroken Three-Point Reconstructable Anchor.
  - Classification: CORRECTED
  - Rationale: Replaced rigid text-only page/passage requirements with an Exact Reconstructable
    Source Locator supporting text, equations, schematics, tables, datasets, and standards.
    A claim must link to: (1) an immutable `SourceDocument` with cryptographic hash, (2) an
    Exact Reconstructable Source Locator, and (3) an evaluated Rigor/Jurisdiction classification.

DECISION D: Representation of Unavailable or Proprietary Sources
  - Direction: Sealed Bibliographic Provenance Stub.
  - Classification: UNCHANGED
  - Rationale: If a source becomes unavailable after initial extraction, its persistent
    metadata, SHA-256 hash, and exact locator anchor remain sealed in `SourceDocument` and
    `ClaimAttribution`, preserving full historical traceability without requiring live access.

DECISION E: Distinguishing Independent Corroboration from Duplicated Citation Lineage
  - Direction: Graph-Based Evidence Dependency Tracking (EDG).
  - Classification: CLARIFIED
  - Rationale: Multiple citations tracing to a common ancestral study are mapped as a
    single evidentiary root. Consensus upgrades require independent methodologies or separate
    empirical roots, without treating "primary" as synonymous with "true."

DECISION F: Representation of AI-Added Unsupported Content
  - Direction: Isolated Quarantined Additions Field.
  - Classification: UNCHANGED
  - Rationale: Any concept or parameter introduced by an LLM that cannot be grounded in
    the cited source evidence is isolated in an `unsupported_model_additions` field. The
    claim is automatically quarantined and blocked from review readiness.

DECISION G: Whole Source Rejection vs Individual Claim Rejection
  - Direction: Granular Epistemic Scoping with Expanded Source Rejection Criteria.
  - Classification: CORRECTED

    corruption, material integrity failure, synthetic misrepresentation, or fatal licensing
    barriers. Otherwise, legitimate sources with contested claims have individual claims
