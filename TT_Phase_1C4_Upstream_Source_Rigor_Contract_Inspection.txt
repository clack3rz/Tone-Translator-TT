EVIDENCE ITEM 8: Section 9 — Two-Dimensional Authority Matrix
Artifact: TT_Sound_Engineering_Knowledge_Architecture_v1.1.txt (Lines 648-672)
"9.1 THE TWO-DIMENSIONAL AUTHORITY MATRIX
Authority is determined by the intersection of Source Rigor and Domain Jurisdiction:

Dimension 1: Source Rigor (R-Tier)
  - R1: Formally Proven Physical / Mathematical Law (Peer-reviewed textbooks, IEEE/AES standards).
  - R2: Controlled Empirical & Scientific Engineering Research (AES papers, ASA laboratory data).
  - R3: Authoritative Technical & Manufacturer Documentation (Schematics, patents, engineering whitepapers).
  - R4: Systematic Professional Engineering Literature (Treatises by master recording engineers).
  - R5: Contextual Practitioner Accounts & Interviews (Documented artist/engineer interviews).

Dimension 2: Domain Jurisdiction (J-Tier)
  - J_CORE: The source operates within its primary scientific or technical discipline.
  - J_APPLIED: The source applies principles from an external discipline to its domain.
  - J_PERIPHERAL: The source makes informal remarks outside its primary expertise.

9.2 DISPENSING WITH SCALAR AUTHORITY SCORES
The architecture strictly prohibits combining R-Tier and J-Tier into a single scalar
score (e.g. "Authority = 8.7/10").
During retrieval and reasoning:
  - Queries filter by minimum Rigor and Jurisdiction requirements.
  - An R3/J_CORE manufacturer schematic for an amplifier circuit takes precedence
    over an R1/J_PERIPHERAL textbook comment on guitar amp tone stacks.
  - An R1/J_CORE acoustics textbook takes precedence over an R4/J_APPLIED recording
    engineer's handbook regarding wave diffraction physics."

"9.2 EVALUATION OF 1C.4a-R SECTION 17.1
In Phase 1C.4a-R v1.1 Section 17.1, the document states:
    "Knowledge embedded in Current TT's codebase is treated as source material with
     source_rigor_tier: R4_PROFESSIONAL_TREATISE and jurisdiction: J_APPLIED.
     It is NOT authoritative until it passes full deconstruction and governance."

Architectural Evaluation:
  - The four-stage deconstruction pipeline in Section 17.2 (Separation -> Physical
    Formulation -> Boundary Documentation -> Governance Review) is completely sound.
  - However, labeling Current TT itself as `R4_PROFESSIONAL_TREATISE` is an epistemic
    overclaim. Current TT is a software codebase containing unverified heuristics, not
    a published, peer-reviewed professional treatise (such as Huber, Rumsey, or Blencowe).
  - Assigning R4 to Current TT risks inflating the baseline authority of unprovenanced
    heuristics before their underlying literature roots are identified.

9.3 RESOLUTION: BOUNDED REVALIDATION ADDENDUM
In accordance with the review instruction and smallest-change principle:
  - An architectural rewrite of Phase 1C.4a is NOT required.
  - A formal Bounded Revalidation Addendum is established (Section 15, Addendum 1):
    Current TT codebase is formally designated as:
        `source_rigor_tier: UNASSESSED_LEGACY_SOURCE`
    When an atomic claim is extracted from Current TT:
      a) If its underlying physical principle is traced to authoritative primary
         literature (e.g. Blencowe, AES, Beranek), the resulting `ClaimAttribution`
         cites that primary source and receives the primary source's true Rigor Tier
         (R1 to R4). Current TT is recorded solely as the historical migration origin.
      b) If no primary literature source can be verified, the claim remains at
         `UNASSESSED_LEGACY_SOURCE` (or R5_PRACTITIONER_ACCOUNT if corroboration exists)
         and CANNOT be promoted to `VALIDATED_ACTIVE` without empirical validation."

SECTION 4 — SUMMARY OF ANSWERS TO EPISTEMIC QUESTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. source_rigor_tier is explicitly defined in frozen 1C.4a-R (SourceDocument field and claim query facet).
2. Canonical enum set: R1_PHYSICAL_LAW, R2_PEER_REVIEWED_RESEARCH, R3_MANUFACTURER_ENGINEERING,
   R4_PROFESSIONAL_TREATISE, R5_PRACTITIONER_ACCOUNT, plus UNASSESSED_LEGACY_SOURCE (from Addendum 1).
3. It represents an upstream composite classification/authority facet of a source document conflating
   publication format/genre with methodological caliber.
4. It is implicitly ordinal upstream (treated as a continuum, used for minimum rigor queries,
   precedence under equal jurisdiction, and warnings against inflating baseline authority),
   though cross-dimensional queries prohibit scalar collapse.
5. It directly maps source types/venues to R-tiers (parenthetical mappings in Section 9.1).
6. SOURCE TYPE != TRUTH is explicit (1C.4a-DR lines 230-233).
   SOURCE VENUE != RIGOR is not present upstream (upstream maps venue directly to rigor).
   RIGOR != EPISTEMIC BASIS is explicit (Rigor is on SourceDocument, Epistemic Basis is on KnowledgeClaim).
   JURISDICTION != RIGOR is explicit (orthogonal dimensions).
7. Concepts are decoupled across SourceDocument (source_rigor_tier, domain_jurisdictions) and
   KnowledgeClaim (epistemic_basis, epistemic_validation, lifecycle_state).
8. Source-level assessment is decoupled from claim-level assessment via relational normalization.
9. Inheritance of source rigor to claims is conditionally stated in 1C.4a-DR, but claims require
   independent epistemic validation and operational boundaries.
10. Addendum 1 reclassified Current TT as UNASSESSED_LEGACY_SOURCE, required primary literature recovery,
    and quarantined unprovenanced claims.
11. UNASSESSED_LEGACY_SOURCE serves multiple roles upstream: source_rigor_tier value, source classification,
    acquisition status, and claim quarantine standing.
12. Downstream phases may add orthogonal characterisation (e.g. methodological_mode) provided
    SourceDocument.source_rigor_tier and backward compatibility are strictly preserved.
