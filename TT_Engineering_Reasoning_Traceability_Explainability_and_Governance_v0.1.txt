EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
================================================================================
Phase 1C.5f establishes the comprehensive reasoning traceability, explainability,
and governance architecture for the Tone Translator (TT) AI Sound Engineer. Operating
strictly within the binding design fence established by TT_Phase_1C.5f_Design_Mandate.txt
and subordinate to the immutable frozen upstream baselines (Phases 1C.5a v0.3c through
1C.5e v0.1m), Phase 1C.5f addresses fourteen core architectural capability areas:

  1. TRACE IDENTITY & RUN LINEAGE: Defining immutable run, record, and relationship
     identity structures that link parent runs, child iteration cycles, and downstream
     artifacts across multi-session life cycles without UUID technology lock-in.
  2. EVIDENCE & RECORD PROVENANCE: Architecting a rigorous origin-classification
     taxonomy (user-supplied, measured, system-derived, human-engineer, reference,
     telemetry, illustrative, and external platform) to trace where every input originated.
  3. CROSS-STAGE DEPENDENCY TRACING: Constructing a traversable, acyclic dependency
     graph connecting the full frozen reasoning chain from Stage 01 Evidence to Stage 14 Review.
  4. RECORD IMMUTABILITY & VERSION GOVERNANCE: Enforcing strict sealing semantics,
     anti-rewrite protections, tombstoning, and non-destructive successor linking.
  5. DECISION EXPLAINABILITY ARCHITECTURE: Defining governed engineering explanations
     assembled strictly from authoritative records without requiring model chain-of-thought.
  6. UNCERTAINTY TRACEABILITY: Preserving surviving epistemic and aleatoric uncertainty
     across every transformation without artificial confidence amplification.
  7. EXECUTION & OUTCOME LINEAGE: Tracing intent through semantic design, platform translation,
     applied configuration, execution receipts, and actual outcome evidence.
  8. PARENT / CHILD RUN GOVERNANCE: Formalizing the boundary conditions under which child
     runs inherit historical records without automatically re-qualifying parent evidence.
  9. INDEPENDENT AUDIT RECONSTRUCTION: Enabling peer reviewers and external auditors to
     reconstruct the complete engineering rationale of any run offline without AI re-execution.
  10. CANDIDATE-LESSON GOVERNANCE: Establishing quarantine evaluation, conflict detection,
      and promotion gates for generalizable knowledge without performing Phase 1C.5g migration.
  11. INTEGRITY PROTECTION: Defining tamper-evident sealing, cryptographic hashing, and
      manifest verification requirements while maintaining strict technology neutrality.
  12. HUMAN REVIEW & OVERRIDE PROVENANCE: Preserving the distinct provenance of human
      engineering interventions, approvals, overrides, and escalations.
  13. FAILURE, MISSING-TRACE & CORRUPTION HANDLING: Defining deterministic degradation
      protocols when trace data is missing, corrupted, broken, or unverifiable.
  14. DOWNSTREAM INTERFACES: Delivering governance metadata to Phase 1C.5g (Knowledge
      Migration) and verification requirements to Phase 1C.5h (UAT & Verification).

SECTION 1 — PURPOSE, SCOPE & GOVERNANCE BOUNDARIES
================================================================================

1.1 PRIMARY PURPOSE
Phase 1C.5f establishes the authoritative engineering architecture required for the
complete Tone Translator (TT) AI Sound Engineer reasoning lifecycle to be:
  - fully traceable across all 14 lifecycle stages;
  - attributable to authoritative human, system, or acoustic origins;
  - provenance-aware across all input evidence and derived deliberations;
  - lineage-preserving across iterative cycles and parent-child run transitions;
  - integrity-governed against retroactive modification or accidental tampering;
  - explainable through governed engineering records rather than opaque AI introspections;
  - retrospectively auditable by independent peer reviewers without relying on model execution;
  - version-aware across all algorithm, knowledge, and platform evolutions;
  - historically non-destructive so that superseded or erroneous records remain preserved;
  - and suitable for controlled downstream governance in knowledge migration and testing.

Phase 1C.5f ensures that any decision made by the AI Sound Engineer can be independently
inspected, reconstructed, defended, or falsified by an external sound engineer or auditor.

1.2 THE GOVERNING CONSTITUTIONAL DISTINCTION
The core architectural boundary of Phase 1C.5f is established by the following rule:
  "PHASES 1C.5a THROUGH 1C.5e DEFINE HOW THE TT AI SOUND ENGINEER REASONS.
   PHASE 1C.5f DEFINES HOW THAT REASONING HISTORY CAN BE TRUSTED, UNDERSTOOD,
   RECONSTRUCTED AND GOVERNED."

Phase 1C.5f is a CROSS-CUTTING GOVERNANCE AND TRACEABILITY ARCHITECTURE.
It is NOT:
  - a new reasoning stage;
  - an alternative reasoning lifecycle running parallel to the frozen lifecycle;
  - an implementation phase (e.g. database setup, UI design, or DSP code);
  - a migration execution phase (owned by Phase 1C.5g);
  - an empirical testing or UAT execution phase (owned by Phase 1C.5h);
  - an AT5 gear mapping or execution layer (owned by Stage 12 operational platforms).

1.3 SCOPE BOUNDARIES & PROHIBITED OVERREACH
To protect architectural integrity, Phase 1C.5f operates under strict design prohibitions:
  1. NO REDEFINITION OF UPSTREAM PRINCIPLES: The 21 Principles of Sound Engineering
     Reasoning established in Phase 1C.5a v0.3c remain immutable constitutional law.
  2. NO NEW LIFECYCLE STAGES OR STATUSES: Phase 1C.5f shall NOT invent new lifecycle
     stages (the 14 stages of Phase 1C.5a are frozen) and shall NOT invent new lifecycle
     statuses (the 27 statuses of Phase 1C.5a are frozen). Governance conditions,
     record states, and audit dispositions are record-level and governance-level
     attributes, never lifecycle statuses.
  3. NO RETROACTIVE REWRITING: Sealed records are immutable historical artifacts.
     When subsequent evidence reveals an earlier diagnosis or decision was erroneous,
     the historical record remains intact, and a linked corrective successor or
     child record is instantiated.
  4. NO CONFUSION OF AUDITABILITY WITH REASONING CORRECTNESS: A flawlessly preserved,
     fully signed trace can document deeply flawed acoustic reasoning. Traceability
     proves what occurred; it does not prove the reasoning was sound.
  5. NO CONFUSION OF PROVENANCE WITH EVIDENTIAL VALIDITY: Correctly identifying that a
     measurement came from a calibrated RTA does not guarantee that the measurement
     was uncorrupted by acoustic comb filtering or mic placement error.
  6. NO EXPLAINABILITY BY MODEL INTROSPECTION: Engineering explainability is defined
     exclusively through governed engineering records, evidence bindings, and causal
     justifications. The internal scratchpad, token probabilities, or hidden
     chain-of-thought of large language models are prohibited as audit fixtures.
  7. NO ARTIFICIAL UNCERTAINTY REMOVAL: An explanation must never produce false
     confidence. Where uncertainty existed during deliberation, that uncertainty
     must be explicitly exposed in the trace and the explanation.
  8. NO PREMATURE TECHNOLOGY LOCK-IN: Phase 1C.5f is platform-neutral. It defines
     conceptual integrity, identity, and relational structures without mandating
     specific database engines, cloud services, hash algorithms, or serialization formats.

SECTION 3 — CONSTITUTIONAL FOUNDATION & THE 21 FROZEN REASONING PRINCIPLES
================================================================================

Phase 1C.5f strictly incorporates the 21 Principles of Sound Engineering Reasoning
from Phase 1C.5a v0.3c. Below is the authoritative mapping of each principle to its
specific Phase 1C.5f operational governance interpretation:

PRINCIPLE 1: Evidence Precedes Diagnosis.
  - Phase 1C.5f Interpretation: The trace graph must enforce strict temporal and causal
    lineage where evidence records have timestamps and dependency edges preceding
    diagnostic records. An audit must reject any run where diagnosis records exist
    without upstream evidence bindings.

PRINCIPLE 2: Observation Is Not Interpretation.
  - Phase 1C.5f Interpretation: Provenance architecture must strictly classify raw
    descriptive observations (e.g. measured spectral energy, dynamic envelope data)
    separately from inferential interpretations (e.g. psychoacoustic boxiness, harshness).
    The trace must prevent conflation between objective data and subjective inference.

PRINCIPLE 3: Missing Evidence Is Not Negative Evidence.
  - Phase 1C.5f Interpretation: When evidence is absent, the trace must record it
    explicitly as UNKNOWN or MISSING. The explainability layer is strictly forbidden
    from treating missing evidence as confirmation of absence or proof of health.

PRINCIPLE 4: A Symptom Does Not Identify Its Cause.
  - Phase 1C.5f Interpretation: Trace lineage must connect an observed acoustic symptom
    to a causal mechanism graph, not directly to an intervention. The audit trail must
    expose whether an intervention addressed the underlying root mechanism or merely
    treated an isolated symptom.

PRINCIPLE 5: Diagnosis Should Be Causal Where Evidence Permits.
  - Phase 1C.5f Interpretation: Diagnostic records in the trace must capture the causal
    hypothesis chain, mechanism pathways, and competing hypotheses. The explanation
    must present the causal rationale justifying why this diagnosis was chosen.

PRINCIPLE 6: Competing Hypotheses Survive Until Evidence Justifies Narrowing Them.
  - Phase 1C.5f Interpretation: The trace graph must preserve all competing hypotheses
    that were considered, along with their survival or exclusion statuses. An audit must
    be able to inspect excluded alternatives and verify the evidential basis of exclusion.

PRINCIPLE 7: Context Informs Reasoning but Does Not Prove Causation.
  - Phase 1C.5f Interpretation: Provenance records must explicitly identify context inputs
    (genre priors, track metadata, user intent) as contextual weighting factors rather
    than direct causal proof. Contextual assumptions must remain auditable.

PRINCIPLE 8: Engineering Intent Constrains the Solution.
  - Phase 1C.5f Interpretation: Engineering Requirement records establish non-negotiable
    preservation bounds in the trace. The explainability layer must verify that candidate
    solutions and actual outcomes were evaluated against these declared intent constraints.

PRINCIPLE 9: Generate Alternatives When the Problem Admits Alternatives.
  - Phase 1C.5f Interpretation: The lineage graph must record all candidate interventions
    deliberated in Stage 09. A decision record that failed to consider alternatives when
    the problem admitted them must be flagged during retrospective audit.

PRINCIPLE 10: Intervention Selection Follows Diagnosis.
  - Phase 1C.5f Interpretation: Trace dependency edges must link Candidate Intervention
    records directly to authorized Causal Diagnosis records. Disconnected interventions
    or "solution-first" actions represent constitutional governance violations.

PRINCIPLE 11: Intervention Should Occur at the Causally Appropriate Point.
  - Phase 1C.5f Interpretation: The trace must capture the locus of intervention (e.g.
    source transducer, pre-amp gain staging, EQ before compression, post-effects).
    The explanation must articulate why the chosen locus was causally appropriate.

PRINCIPLE 12: Predict Consequences Before Acting.
  - Phase 1C.5f Interpretation: The PredictedOutcomeRecord must be sealed in the trace
    BEFORE dispatch across the Stage 12 execution boundary. The timestamp and hash of
    the prediction record must demonstrably precede the execution receipt.

PRINCIPLE 13: Every Intervention Has Potential Trade-Offs.
  - Phase 1C.5f Interpretation: The trace must preserve the ConstraintAndTradeOffEvaluationRecord,
    documenting evaluated side effects, collateral degradation risks, and accepted costs.
    Explanations must explicitly state accepted trade-offs.

PRINCIPLE 14: Parsimony: Do Not Intervene Without Justified Engineering Purpose.
  - Phase 1C.5f Interpretation: Audit reconstruction must evaluate whether the minimal
    necessary intervention was selected. Unnecessary processing blocks or gratuitous
    parameter shifts must be highlighted in the audit summary.

PRINCIPLE 15: Platform Translation Operates Downstream of Engineering Reasoning.
  - Phase 1C.5f Interpretation: The trace must enforce a clean separation between the
    platform-neutral engineering decision (Phase 1C.5d Stage 11) and the target-platform
    parameter translation (Stage 12). Reasoning must be auditable independently of AT5 gear.

PRINCIPLE 16: Reject Unacceptable Platform Compromises.
  - Phase 1C.5f Interpretation: Where platform translation truncates or violates core
    preservation requirements, the trace must record the rejection gate (Status 22)
    and preserve the specific constraint violation report in the audit trail.

PRINCIPLE 17: Deterministic Code Validates Constraints; It Does Not Replace Judgement.
  - Phase 1C.5f Interpretation: Algorithmic checks (e.g. clipping detectors, LUFS bounds)
    must have their execution receipts preserved in the trace, but must be represented as
    constraint validators rather than replacements for acoustic evaluation.

PRINCIPLE 18: Evaluate Outcomes Honestly Without Circular Justification.
  - Phase 1C.5f Interpretation: The Outcome Evidence Firewall must be enforced in the trace.
    Actual outcome evidence records (Stage 13) must derive strictly from empirical post-action
    audio, never from pre-action predictions. Circular confirmation is a fatal audit failure.

PRINCIPLE 19: Capture Engineering Experience for Governed Review.
  - Phase 1C.5f Interpretation: Retrospective review records (Stage 14) and quarantined
    candidate lessons must be immutably recorded in the trace, enabling governed
    cross-session learning without premature or unverified knowledge promotion.

PRINCIPLE 20: The Deliberation Record Must Support Independent Retrospective Audit.
  - Phase 1C.5f Interpretation: This is the constitutional cornerstone of Phase 1C.5f.
    The deliberation record must contain all information necessary for an independent
    human expert to reconstruct the entire reasoning lifecycle without conversational AI.

PRINCIPLE 21: Where Evidence Is Missing, Seek Discriminating Evidence Rather Than Guessing.
  - Phase 1C.5f Interpretation: Where uncertainty prevents definitive diagnosis or decision,
    the trace must record the DiscriminatingEvidenceRequestRecord and the resulting
    information state rather than fabricating certainty.

SECTION 4 — TRACE IDENTITY, RUN LINEAGE & GRAPH TOPOLOGY
================================================================================

4.1 TRACE IDENTIFIER CONCEPTUAL SPECIFICATION
To ensure unambiguous reference across the multi-stage reasoning lifecycle, Phase 1C.5f
establishes conceptual requirements for stable identities.

TECHNOLOGY-NEUTRAL STANCE:
Phase 1C.5f does not mandate a specific UUID version (e.g. UUIDv4 vs UUIDv7) or binary
encoding. However, all implementations must satisfy the following architectural criteria:
  - Universally Unique: Identifiers must be globally unique across time, sessions, and platforms.
  - Immutable Association: An identifier assigned to a record, run, or relationship
    must never be reassigned, repurposed, or mutated.
  - Traceability: Given an identifier, a governance engine must be able to locate the
    associated record, its parent run, and its cryptographic fingerprint.

4.2 THE THREE LEVELS OF TRACE IDENTITY
Phase 1C.5f defines three distinct conceptual levels of identity:

1. RUN IDENTITY (`run_id`):
   - Represents a single execution cycle through the reasoning lifecycle.
   - A run begins with evidence intake (or child run instantiation) and terminates at
     a frozen terminal status (e.g. Status 18, 21, 27) or a review pause / iteration state
     (Status 25, 26).
   - Contains a stable run descriptor, session metadata, initialization timestamp,
     and parent run pointer (null for root runs).

2. RECORD IDENTITY (`record_id`):
   - Represents a discrete, sealed engineering artifact produced within a lifecycle stage.
   - Every record across all 14 stages possesses a unique, stable `record_id`.
   - Examples: `EvidenceRecord_id`, `ObservationRecord_id`, `CausalDiagnosisRecord_id`,
     `EngineeringRequirementRecord_id`, `EngineeringDecisionRecord_id`,
     `PredictedOutcomeRecord_id`, `ActualOutcomeEvidenceRecord_id`, `EngineeringReviewRecord_id`.

3. RELATIONSHIP / EDGE IDENTITY (`relationship_id` / `edge_id`):
   - Represents a directed causal, evidential, or governance dependency between two records.
   - Unambiguously defines the source record, target record, relationship semantic,
     and creation metadata.

4.3 PARENT-CHILD RUN LINEAGE & SUCCESSOR SHADOWING
When an engineering intervention requires refinement, alternative exploration, or upstream
re-diagnosis, Phase 1C.5e mandates the instantiation of a governed child run (Stage 14 -> Status 26).
Phase 1C.5f formalizes the lineage between parent and child runs:
  - Linear Lineage Pointer: Every child run must contain an explicit `parent_run_id`
    referencing the immediate predecessor run.
  - Lineage Root Pointer: Every child run retains a `root_run_id` pointing to the initial
    root problem session, enabling full traversal of the lineage chain.
  - Iteration Generation Index: An integer counter (`generation_index`) incremented with
    each child cycle (Root = 0, Child 1 = 1, Child 2 = 2, etc.).
  - Retrospective Immutability: Instantiating a child run does not reopen or mutate the
    parent run. The parent run remains permanently sealed.

4.4 TRACE GRAPH TOPOLOGY (DIRECTED ACYCLIC GRAPH)
The engineering deliberation within a single run is architecturally represented as a
Directed Acyclic Graph (DAG):
  - Nodes: Sealed engineering records produced at each lifecycle stage.
  - Edges: Directed dependencies representing evidential derivation, causal justification,
    constraint satisfaction, or retrospective audit links.
  - Acyclicity Rule: Within a single run, no record may depend on its own downstream output.
    Any cyclic feedback loop (iteration, refinement, return-to-diagnosis) is explicitly
    broken across runs by instantiating a new Child Run Node in the multi-run lineage tree.

```
   [Root Run DAG]
   (Evidence) -> (Observation) -> (Diagnosis) -> (Decision) -> (Outcome) -> (Review)
                                                                               |
                                                                [Status 26: Iteration]
                                                                               |
                                                                               v
   [Child Run 1 DAG]
   (Re-qualified Evidence) -> (Alternative Decision) -> (New Outcome) -> (Review)
```

SECTION 5 — EVIDENCE & RECORD PROVENANCE ARCHITECTURE
================================================================================

5.1 THE FUNDAMENTAL PROVENANCE QUESTION
The provenance architecture answers the foundational audit question:
  "WHERE DID THIS INFORMATION COME FROM, WHO SUPPLIED OR COMPUTED IT, AND UNDER
   WHAT BOUNDING ASSUMPTIONS WAS IT GENERATED?"

Provenance defines origin, chain of custody, and operational context.

5.2 THE EIGHT-TIER PROVENANCE TAXONOMY
Phase 1C.5f defines an authoritative origin taxonomy to classify every piece of data,
metric, assertion, or configuration ingested or generated by the system:

1. `ORIGIN_1_USER_SUPPLIED`:
   - Information directly provided by the human musician, producer, or mixing client.
   - Examples: Subjective aesthetic goals ("make it warmer"), reference track links,
     stylistic preferences, declarations of instrument type ("1960s Stratocaster").
   - Epistemic Status: Subjective intent or unverified prior belief. Informs requirements
     and context; does not constitute measured acoustic evidence (Principle 7).

2. `ORIGIN_2_MEASURED_EVIDENCE`:
   - Objective physical measurements obtained directly from input audio signals.
   - Examples: FFT power spectral density, short-term/integrated LUFS, peak-to-average
     crest factor, stereo inter-channel phase correlation, THD analysis.
   - Governance Rule: Must specify measurement methodology, calibration reference,
     window size, and sampling rate.

3. `ORIGIN_3_SYSTEM_DERIVED_OBSERVATION`:
   - Algorithmic extractions, feature detections, and deterministic pattern matches
     derived from measured evidence without high-level causal interpretation.
   - Examples: Detected resonant frequency peak at 680 Hz with Q=8.5; detected dynamic
     envelope overshoot of 4.2 dB in the 2 kHz to 4 kHz range.

4. `ORIGIN_4_SYSTEM_INTERPRETATION`:
   - High-level psychoacoustic, musical, or technical inferences formed by the AI engine.
   - Examples: Inferring that the 680 Hz resonance manifests as "hollow snare boxiness";
     hypothesizing that high-frequency harshness stems from clipping harmonics.
   - Governance Rule: Must remain strictly segregated from raw observations (Principle 2).

5. `ORIGIN_5_INHERITED_HISTORICAL_EVIDENCE`:
   - Evidence, measurements, or observations brought forward from a prior parent run.
   - Governance Rule: Inherited evidence is labeled with its historical run ID.
     It is NOT automatically valid for the current child run and must be re-qualified
     (Section 11).

6. `ORIGIN_6_HUMAN_ENGINEERING_INPUT`:
   - Direct engineering specifications, constraints, adjustments, or manual overrides
     supplied by a professional sound engineer.
   - Examples: A senior mixing engineer manually overriding a high-pass filter frequency,
     rejecting an EQ notch, or providing an explicit constraint bound.
   - Governance Rule: Must be preserved with distinct human provenance; must never be
     silently blended into AI-derived reasoning.

7. `ORIGIN_7_SCENARIO_ILLUSTRATIVE_DATA`:
   - Synthetic, pedagogical, or mock data utilized strictly for worked examples,
     system test harnesses, or architectural demonstrations.
   - Governance Rule: Must be prominently stamped with `[ORIGIN_7_SCENARIO_ILLUSTRATIVE_VALUE]`.
     Prohibited from being ingested as empirical production evidence.

8. `ORIGIN_8_EXTERNAL_PLATFORM_TELEMETRY`:
   - Operational telemetry, parameter readbacks, execution receipts, and error codes
     emitted by external DSP hardware, DAW hosts, or plugin frameworks (e.g. AmpliTube 5).
   - Examples: Preset load confirmations, buffer underrun notices, parameter clamping
     receipts, latency reports.

5.3 PROVENANCE RECORD INTEGRITY & ANTI-FABRICATION INVARIANT
  "WHERE PROVENANCE IS INCOMPLETE OR UNKNOWN, IT MUST BE RECORDED AS UNRESOLVED.
   THE ARCHITECTURE STRICTLY PROHIBITS INFERRING OR FABRICATING PROVENANCE WITHOUT EVIDENCE."

If a piece of audio arrives without calibration data, microphone metadata, or track
identity, the provenance record must state `provenance_status: "UNRESOLVED_INCOMPLETE"`.
The system must not fabricate plausible origins.

SECTION 6 — CROSS-STAGE REASONING DEPENDENCY TRACING
================================================================================

6.1 THE FOURTEEN-STAGE REASONING CHAIN
Phase 1C.5f maps the complete dependency path linking all 14 frozen stages of the
AI Sound Engineer reasoning lifecycle. Every stage produces one or more sealed records
that serve as directed dependency anchors for subsequent stages:

```
  [Stage 01: Evidence Intake]
          | (evidence_record_ref)
          v
  [Stage 02: Acoustic Observation]
          | (observation_record_ref)
          v
  [Stage 03: Psychoacoustic Interpretation]
          | (interpretation_record_ref)
          v
  [Stage 04: Hypothesis Formation]
          | (hypothesis_set_ref)
          v
  [Stage 05: Evidence Sufficiency Evaluation]
          | (sufficiency_evaluation_ref)
          v
  [Stage 06: Causal Reasoning & Mechanism Analysis]
          | (causal_graph_ref)
          v
  [Stage 07: Causal Diagnosis Authorization]
          | (causal_diagnosis_record_ref)
          v
  [Stage 08: Engineering Requirements Formulation]
          | (engineering_requirement_record_ref)
          v
  [Stage 09: Candidate Interventions Deliberation]
          | (candidate_interventions_set_ref)
          v
  [Stage 10: Constraint & Trade-Off Evaluation]
          | (tradeoff_evaluation_record_ref)
          v
  [Stage 11: Engineering Decision & Predicted Outcome]
          | (decision_record_ref, predicted_outcome_record_ref)
          v
  [Stage 12: Semantic Tone Design & Platform Execution Handoff]
          | (semantic_design_ref, platform_receipt_ref)
          v
  [Stage 13: Actual Outcome Evidence Ingestion]
          | (actual_outcome_evidence_record_ref)
          v
  [Stage 14: Retrospective Engineering Review]
            (engineering_review_record_ref)
```

6.2 FORMAL RELATIONSHIP EDGE SEMANTICS
To ensure rigorous cross-stage traversal, Phase 1C.5f defines standard relationship edge types:

  - `PRODUCED_FROM`: Indicates that Record B was derived by applying algorithmic analysis
    or reasoning to Record A (e.g. Stage 02 Observation `PRODUCED_FROM` Stage 01 Evidence).
  - `JUSTIFIED_BY`: Indicates that an inferential conclusion, diagnosis, or decision
    relies upon an upstream evidential or causal claim (e.g. Stage 07 Diagnosis `JUSTIFIED_BY`
    Stage 06 Causal Graph; Stage 11 Decision `JUSTIFIED_BY` Stage 10 Trade-Off Evaluation).
  - `CONSTRAINED_BY`: Indicates that an intervention or parameter allocation is bounded
    by an upstream requirement or preservation rule (e.g. Stage 09 Candidate `CONSTRAINED_BY`
    Stage 08 Engineering Requirement).
  - `PREDICTS_FOR`: Indicates that a predicted outcome specifies falsifiable expectations
    for an engineering decision (Stage 11 Predicted Outcome `PREDICTS_FOR` Stage 11 Decision).
  - `TRANSLATED_INTO`: Links platform-neutral semantic intent to target-platform parameter
    instructions (Stage 11 Decision `TRANSLATED_INTO` Stage 12 Execution Command).
  - `EVALUATED_AGAINST`: Links empirical post-action evidence to pre-action predictions
    and requirements across the Outcome Evidence Firewall (Stage 13 Actual Outcome
    `EVALUATED_AGAINST` Stage 11 Predicted Outcome and Stage 08 Requirement).
  - `AUDITED_BY`: Links the comprehensive deliberation trace of the run to the Stage 14
    retrospective engineering review (Run Trace `AUDITED_BY` Stage 14 Engineering Review).

6.3 TRACE TRAVERSAL & ORPHAN-RECORD DETECTION
An audit engine traversing the trace graph must verify that no record is an orphan:
  - Upstream Reachability: Starting at Stage 14 Review or Stage 11 Decision, backwards
    traversal must successfully reach the root Stage 01 Evidence and Stage 08 Requirements.
  - Forward Completeness: In completed runs, every authorized diagnosis must have
    corresponding downstream requirement and deliberation paths, or an explicit
    recorded disposition explaining why deliberation halted (e.g. Status 18 Justified No Change).
  - Broken Trace Detection: Any gap where an edge references a missing `record_id` constitutes
    a severe governance degradation (`GOVERNANCE_CONDITION_BROKEN_TRACE`).

SECTION 7 — RECORD IMMUTABILITY, SEALING & HISTORICAL PRESERVATION
================================================================================

7.1 THE SEALING PROTOCOL & ANTI-REWRITE ENFORCEMENT
Once an engineering stage completes its deliberation, its corresponding record is
FORMALLY SEALED.
  "A SEALED RECORD IS AN IMMUTABLE HISTORICAL ARTIFACT.
   IT MUST NEVER BE SILENTLY OVERWRITTEN, RETROACTIVELY EDITED, DELETED,
   OR MUTATED TO MAKE SUBSEQUENT REASONING APPEAR MORE ACCURATE OR SUCCESSFUL."

Sealing implies:
  - Cryptographic Fingerprinting: Generating a cryptographic content hash of the record
    upon stage completion.
  - State Locking: Marking the record as `RECORD_GOVERNANCE_STATE_SEALED`.
  - Mutation Prohibition: Any attempt by a database operation, API call, or model process
    to modify the contents of a sealed record must be rejected with a hard governance error.

7.2 HISTORICAL PRESERVATION OF FLAWED OR SUPERSEDED REASONING
In real-world sound engineering, diagnoses prove wrong, predictions fail, and initial
interventions cause unexpected side effects. Phase 1C.5f strictly forbids "sanitizing" history:
  - Preserving the Error: If Stage 13 outcome evidence reveals that an EQ intervention
    failed because the true cause was acoustic room modes rather than speaker resonance,
    the original faulty `CausalDiagnosisRecord` and `EngineeringDecisionRecord` MUST
    REMAIN EXACTLY AS THEY WERE RECORDED.
  - Corrective Successor Pointers: The subsequent correction is documented by instantiating
    a new record in a child run, or a corrective review record in Stage 14, which explicitly
    references the faulty predecessor record via a `SUPERSEDES_WITH_CORRECTION` edge.
  - Audit Trail Integrity: An auditor must be able to see:
      (1) Exactly what the system believed at time T1;
      (2) Why that belief was formed;
      (3) What evidence arrived at time T2 to disprove it;
      (4) How the system responded at time T3.

7.3 RECORD GOVERNANCE STATES VS LIFECYCLE STATUSES
A critical architectural boundary established in the Phase 1C.5f Design Mandate is:
  "RECORD GOVERNANCE STATES ARE NOT LIFECYCLE STATUSES."

Lifecycle statuses (Statuses 01 to 27) belong strictly to Phase 1C.5a v0.3c and define the
macro progress of the reasoning lifecycle (e.g. `STATUS 20: DECISION_SEALED_AWAITING_EXEC`,
`STATUS 24: OUTCOME_EVIDENCE_INGESTED`).

Record Governance States represent the localized lifecycle of individual record artifacts:
  - `RECORD_GOVERNANCE_STATE_DRAFT`: Record is currently being compiled within the active stage.
  - `RECORD_GOVERNANCE_STATE_SEALED`: Record is complete, hashed, and locked against modification.
  - `RECORD_GOVERNANCE_STATE_SUPERSEDED`: A successor record in a child run has updated this artifact.
  - `RECORD_GOVERNANCE_STATE_QUARANTINED`: Record is isolated pending governed review (e.g. candidate lessons).
  - `RECORD_GOVERNANCE_STATE_TOMBSTONED`: Record has been marked invalid due to verified data corruption,
    retaining historical headers while invalidating active operational use.

SECTION 8 — DECISION EXPLAINABILITY ARCHITECTURE
================================================================================

8.1 DEFINITION OF GOVERNED ENGINEERING EXPLAINABILITY
Phase 1C.5f defines explainability not as post-hoc generative storytelling or unrestricted
AI introspections, but as a GOVERNED ENGINEERING AUDIT TRAIL:
  "AN EXPLANATION IS A STRUCTURED, VERIFIABLE RECONSTRUCTION OF THE ENGINEERING
   JUSTIFICATION, DERIVED EXCLUSIVELY FROM SEALED EVIDENCE, DIAGNOSTIC MECHANISMS,
   EXPLICIT REQUIREMENTS, EVALUATED TRADE-OFFS, AND RECORDED OUTCOMES."

8.2 THE FIVE AUDITABLE EXPLANATION COMPONENT LAYERS
A complete Phase 1C.5f engineering explanation is assembled from five governed layers:

1. THE EVIDENTIAL LAYER:
   - What acoustic observations and measurements were present?
   - What was the gain calibration status and measurement integration window?
   - What evidence was missing or uncertain? (Explicitly citing Principle 3 & 21).

2. THE DIAGNOSTIC LAYER:
   - What physical or acoustic mechanisms were identified to explain the observations?
   - What competing hypotheses were considered, and on what evidential grounds were
     alternative hypotheses excluded or retained? (Principles 5 & 6).
   - What surviving diagnostic uncertainty remained?

3. THE REQUIREMENT & INTENT LAYER:
   - What specific engineering goals were formulated (attenuation targets, preservation
     bounds, perceptual criteria)? (Principle 8).
   - What non-negotiable constraints were active (e.g. transient crest factor, mono compatibility)?

4. THE DELIBERATION & TRADE-OFF LAYER:
   - Why was this candidate intervention selected over credible alternatives? (Principles 9, 10, 11).
   - What collateral consequences and trade-offs were predicted and consciously accepted? (Principle 13).
   - Was the intervention parsimonious and causally targeted? (Principle 14).

5. THE OUTCOME & REVIEW LAYER:
   - What was predicted before action was taken? (Principle 12).
   - What actually occurred post-execution, and did execution deviate from intent? (Principle 18).
   - How was causal attribution established, and what retrospective review conclusions were drawn?

8.3 PROHIBITION OF HIDDEN CHAIN-OF-THOUGHT AS AUDIT EVIDENCE
Modern foundation models generate internal reasoning scratchpads (chain-of-thought tokens).
Phase 1C.5f establishes an absolute constitutional firewall:
  "PRIVATE AI CHAIN-OF-THOUGHT TOKENS ARE UNGOVERNED, NON-DETERMINISTIC,
   AND PROHIBITED AS SUBSTITUTE FOR STRUCTURED DELIBERATION RECORDS."

Reasoning that does not map to a sealed record (such as an `ObservationRecord`,
`CausalDiagnosisRecord`, or `ConstraintAndTradeOffEvaluationRecord`) DOES NOT EXIST from
an audit perspective. If an AI system "thought" of a risk but failed to log it in the
sealed trade-off evaluation record, the risk was unconsidered from a governance perspective.

8.4 AUDIENCE-TARGETED EXPLANATION RECONSTRUCTION
A governed explanation engine can render the sealed audit trail into different views:
  - Technical Engineering View: Full acoustic parameters, Q-factors, FFT decibel deltas,
    mechanisms, and formal schema cross-references.
  - Musician / Producer View: Translated musical language (e.g. "reduced harshness around
    3 kHz while preserving vocal bite"), highlighting intent, accepted trade-offs, and listening checks.
  - Governance & Compliance View: Verification of timestamps, hash integrity, human override
    approvals, and constitutional checklist alignment.

In all views, the factual claims MUST BE BOUND TO THE SAME SEALED RECORDS.

SECTION 9 — UNCERTAINTY TRACEABILITY ACROSS THE LIFECYCLE
================================================================================

9.1 CONSTITUTIONAL INVARIANT: UNCERTAINTY MUST SURVIVE THE DECISION
Inherited from Phase 1C.5a v0.3c, the governing invariant stands as absolute law:
  "UNCERTAINTY MUST SURVIVE THE DECISION."

Traceability must preserve uncertainty rather than retrospectively smoothing it away.
A decision reached under bounded uncertainty does NOT retroactively make the diagnosis
certain. The trace must permanently record the surviving ambiguity.

9.2 THE UNCERTAINTY CASCADE ACROSS LIFECYCLE STAGES
Uncertainty must be tracked, classified, and propagated at every stage:

1. EVIDENCE UNCERTAINTY (Stages 01–03):
   - Measurement noise, room acoustic contamination, uncalibrated monitoring levels,
     or insufficient performance phrase duration.
   - Classification: `UNCERTAINTY_TYPE_MEASUREMENT_LIMITATION`.

2. DIAGNOSTIC UNCERTAINTY (Stages 04–07):
   - Multiple competing causal mechanisms explain the observed symptom equally well
     (e.g. 680 Hz snare ring caused by bottom head tuning vs top head resonance vs snare wires).
   - Classification: `UNCERTAINTY_TYPE_COMPETING_CAUSAL_MECHANISMS`.

3. PREDICTIVE UNCERTAINTY (Stages 08–11):
   - Non-linear interactions, platform approximation tolerances, or unknown downstream
     master bus processing that could alter the intervention's actual response.
   - Classification: `UNCERTAINTY_TYPE_PREDICTIVE_NONLINEARITY`.

4. OUTCOME & ATTRIBUTION UNCERTAINTY (Stages 13–14):
   - Performance variation between takes (e.g. drummer hit harder on take 2), or multi-track
     leakage masking the isolated result.
   - Classification: `UNCERTAINTY_TYPE_CONFOUNDED_OBSERVATION`.

9.3 PROHIBITION OF EXPLANATORY CONFIDENCE AMPLIFICATION
A core anti-pattern in automated systems is "confidence drift," where an explanation
sounds far more confident than the underlying data justifies.
  "AN EXPLANATION LAYER MUST NEVER UPGRADE AN UNCERTAIN INFERENCE
   INTO A CERTAIN FACT MERELY TO PRODUCE A FLUENT OR CONVINCING NARRATIVE."

If a notch filter was applied under `STATUS 19: PROCEED_BOUNDED_UNCERTAINTY`, the
explanation must explicitly state: "The intervention was applied to test the 680 Hz
resonance hypothesis while acknowledging surviving uncertainty regarding head interaction."

SECTION 10 — EXECUTION, PLATFORM TRANSLATION & OUTCOME LINEAGE
================================================================================

10.1 THE EIGHT-STEP EXECUTION BOUNDARY SEQUENCE IN TRACEABILITY
Phase 1C.5f preserves the clean separation across the eight-step operational execution
boundary established in Phase 1C.5a v0.3c (§§19–21) and Phase 1C.5e v0.1m (§4.1):

  STEP 1: Sealed Stage 11 Decision & Predicted Outcome (`Phase 1C.5d` records sealed).
  STEP 2: Pre-Action Consequence Elaboration & Simulation Lane (`Phase 1C.5e` pre-validation).
  STEP 3: Stage 12 Semantic Tone Design Layer & Handoff Boundary.
  STEP 4: Target Platform Parameter Translation (`Stage 12` Platform Translator).
  STEP 5: Pre-Execution Gate Verification (`Stage 12` Gate evaluation).
  STEP 6: Target Platform Audio Execution / Rendering (`AmpliTube 5` / DAW DSP rendering).
  STEP 7: Post-Execution Render / Preset Verification & Capture Buffer Intake.
  STEP 8: Execution Receipts, Telemetry & Outcome Evidence Intake (`Stage 13`).

10.2 INTENDED VS TRANSLATED VS APPLIED PARAMETER TRACEABILITY
To isolate whether an unexpected acoustic outcome was caused by poor engineering reasoning,
translation distortion, or platform execution failure, Phase 1C.5f mandates tracking three
distinct parameter layers in the execution lineage:

1. INTENDED PARAMETERS (Semantic Intent):
   - What the abstract engineering decision specified.
   - Example: Parametric notch at 680 Hz, Q=8.5, Gain=-9.0 dB; Pre-compression locus.
   - Source: `EngineeringDecisionRecord` (Phase 1C.5d).

2. TRANSLATED PARAMETERS (Platform Mapping):
   - How the platform translator mapped the abstract intent to concrete gear controls.
   - Example: AmpliTube 5 EQ 81 model: Band 3 selected at 700 Hz (stepped frequency grid),
     Gain knob set to position 2.1 (corresponding to -8.8 dB), Q switch set to "Narrow".
   - Source: `PlatformTranslationRecord` (Stage 12).

3. APPLIED PARAMETERS (Actual Rendered State):
   - What was actually executed on the DSP engine or DAW track, captured via readback telemetry.
   - Example: Readback confirms EQ 81 Band 3 at 700 Hz, -8.8 dB; but detects that a master
     limiter on the channel was accidentally engaged with +3 dB makeup gain.
   - Source: `ExecutionReceiptRecord` (Stage 12/13).

10.3 EXECUTION DEVIATION TAXONOMY
Where applied parameters diverge from intended parameters, Phase 1C.5f classifies the divergence:
  - `DEVIATION_TYPE_PLATFORM_GRID_APPROXIMATION`: Target hardware or plugin uses stepped
    frequency/gain pots that cannot hit continuous floating-point values exactly.
  - `DEVIATION_TYPE_TRANSLATOR_CLAMPING`: Target parameter exceeded the legal bounds of
    the model and was clamped at minimum/maximum.
  - `DEVIATION_TYPE_USER_MANUAL_INTERVENTION`: Human engineer tweaked a knob in the DAW
    interface after the automated patch was loaded.
  - `DEVIATION_TYPE_EXECUTION_ENGINE_FAULT`: DSP buffer drop, plugin crash, or silent failure.

10.4 OUTCOME LINEAGE & THE EVIDENCE FIREWALL
Once audio renders, post-intervention audio evidence is captured and ingested into Stage 13.
Phase 1C.5f verifies that:
  - The `ActualOutcomeEvidenceRecord` binds directly to the `ExecutionReceiptRecord` via an
    `OUTCOME_OF` edge.
  - The Outcome Evidence Firewall is enforced: outcome metrics are computed exclusively from
    the captured post-action audio buffer, never backfilled from `PredictedOutcomeRecord`.

SECTION 11 — PARENT / CHILD RUN GOVERNANCE & HISTORICAL INHERITANCE
================================================================================

11.1 THE PROBLEM OF EVIDENCE CONTAMINATION ACROSS RUNS
When an engineering intervention fails or requires iteration, a child run is launched.
A critical epistemic danger identified in Phase 1C.5e is EVIDENCE CONTAMINATION:
  "ASSUMING THAT BECAUSE AN ACOUSTIC PROPERTY WAS MEASURED IN THE PARENT RUN,
   IT REMAINS UNCHANGED AND DIRECTLY APPLICABLE IN THE CHILD RUN."

For example:
  - The parent run observed 680 Hz snare ring on Take 1.
  - An intervention was applied, but the drummer switched sticks or changed performance
    dynamics on Take 2.
  - The child run cannot blindly reuse the parent's baseline measurements without re-verifying
    their comparability.

11.2 GOVERNING RULES FOR CHILD-RUN HISTORICAL INHERITANCE
Phase 1C.5f formalizes four mandatory rules governing parent-child lineage:

RULE 1: HISTORICAL PRESERVATION OF PARENT RECORDS:
  The parent run remains permanently sealed. A child run NEVER edits, overwrites, or truncates
  parent records. The parent run retains its terminal or review pause status (e.g. Status 26).

RULE 2: NO AUTOMATIC EVIDENCE RE-QUALIFICATION:
  Parent evidence records imported into a child run are tagged as `ORIGIN_5_INHERITED_HISTORICAL_EVIDENCE`.
  They are treated as HISTORICAL REFERENCES, not current empirical ground truth, until explicitly
  re-qualified by the child run's Stage 01/05 evidence intake engine.

RULE 3: MANDATORY COMPARABILITY AUDIT:
  Before an inherited measurement is used to guide child-run decisions, a comparability check
  must be logged:
    - Did the audio source, musician performance, or recording take change?
    - Did intervening processing alter the track gain staging or frequency balance?
    - If comparability cannot be verified, fresh baseline evidence MUST be acquired.

RULE 4: EXPLICIT LINEAGE POINTERS:
  The child run's header must declare:
    - `parent_run_id`: The immediate prior run.
    - `root_run_id`: The originating session run.
    - `lineage_trigger_action`: The specific Layer B action from the parent run that
      authorized iteration (e.g. `action_bounded_refinement`, `action_alternate_intervention`,
      `action_full_reversion_to_checkpoint`, `action_return_to_diagnosis`).

SECTION 12 — INDEPENDENT RETROSPECTIVE AUDIT RECONSTRUCTION
================================================================================

12.1 THE INDEPENDENT AUDIT OBJECTIVE
A primary reason for Phase 1C.5f's existence is to satisfy Principle 20:
  "THE DELIBERATION RECORD MUST SUPPORT INDEPENDENT RETROSPECTIVE AUDIT."

An independent human sound engineer, technical auditor, or regulatory reviewer must be
able to audit any historical run:
  - WITHOUT access to the original generative AI model;
  - WITHOUT re-running complex non-deterministic LLM prompts;
  - SOLELY by inspecting the sealed deliberation records, evidence bindings, and execution receipts.

12.2 THE TEN AUDIT VERIFICATION CHECKS
An independent audit engine or human auditor must evaluate ten standard verification criteria:

1. EVIDENCE INTEGRITY: Was all ingested evidence properly calibrated, timestamped, and
   classified by provenance? Was missing evidence explicitly acknowledged?
2. OBSERVATION/INTERPRETATION PURITY: Were raw acoustic measurements kept unpolluted by
   interpretive biases?
3. CAUSAL JUSTIFICATION: Did the authorized diagnosis identify a plausible physical mechanism,
   and were competing hypotheses reasonably excluded on evidential grounds?
4. INTENT & CONSTRAINT COMPLIANCE: Did the formulated requirements accurately reflect user intent,
   and were non-negotiable preservation constraints respected?
5. ALTERNATIVE EXPLORATION: Were credible alternative interventions deliberated before selecting
   the primary action?
6. TRADE-OFF HONESTY: Were potential side effects, distortion risks, and collateral damages
   honestly identified and evaluated prior to execution?
7. PREDICTION SEALING TIMESTAMPS: Was the PredictedOutcomeRecord cryptographically sealed
   BEFORE the execution command was dispatched?
8. EXECUTION CONCORDANCE: Did actual execution match semantic intent, or were deviations
   properly detected and classified?
9. FIREWALL ADHERENCE: Was outcome evidence measured empirically from post-action audio without
   circular reliance on predictions?
10. UNCERTAINTY SURVIVAL: Did surviving epistemic uncertainty remain visible throughout the
    deliberation and in the final review record?

12.3 AUDIT DISPOSITIONS & VERDICTS
Upon completing an independent retrospective audit, the audit engine assigns an audit verdict:
  - `AUDIT_VERDICT_PASS_SOUND`: The run's deliberation was rigorous, well-evidenced, properly
    bounded, and fully compliant with the 21 Principles.
  - `AUDIT_VERDICT_PASS_DEFECT_NOTED`: Minor non-critical defects noted (e.g. imprecise Q
    estimation), but core causal reasoning and safety constraints remained intact.
  - `AUDIT_VERDICT_FAIL_UNJUSTIFIED_ACTION`: The intervention lacked evidential justification,
    ignored plausible competing hypotheses, or failed to evaluate obvious trade-offs.
  - `AUDIT_VERDICT_FAIL_FIREWALL_BREACH`: Pre-action predictions were falsely ingested as
    outcome evidence, or outcomes were circularly justified.
  - `AUDIT_VERDICT_FAIL_CORRUPTED_LINEAGE`: The trace graph contains missing records, broken edges,
    or tampered cryptographic hashes.

SECTION 13 — HUMAN REVIEW, COLLABORATION & OVERRIDE PROVENANCE
================================================================================

13.1 THE HUMAN-IN-THE-LOOP COEXISTENCE MODEL
Sound engineering is an artistic and technical discipline where human producers, mixing
engineers, and mastering professionals collaborate with automated systems. Phase 1C.5f
establishes strict governance rules for human interactions:
  "HUMAN INTERVENTIONS, MODIFICATIONS, AND OVERRIDES MUST BE PRESERVED AS
   DISTINCT, FIRST-CLASS PROVENANCE ARTIFACTS.
   THEY MUST NEVER BE SILENTLY ERASED, NOR MAY AI-GENERATED REASONING BE
   FALSELY ATTRIBUTED TO HUMAN AUTHORITY."

13.2 THE SIX FORMS OF HUMAN INTERACTION
Phase 1C.5f classifies six discrete types of human involvement:

1. `HUMAN_ACTION_EXPLICIT_APPROVAL`:
   - A human engineer reviews an AI-generated diagnosis, decision, or patch, and explicitly
     approves it for execution without modification.
   - Trace Record: `HumanReviewRecord` storing reviewer ID, timestamp, approval scope, and rationale.

2. `HUMAN_ACTION_PARAMETER_MODIFICATION`:
   - A human engineer accepts the AI's causal mechanism and strategy, but adjusts specific
     parameters (e.g. shifts EQ center frequency from 680 Hz to 710 Hz, or reduces compression ratio).
   - Trace Record: Documents both the AI proposal and the human modification, highlighting
     the delta and preserving the human's stated rationale.

3. `HUMAN_ACTION_FULL_OVERRIDE`:
   - A human engineer completely rejects the AI's proposed diagnosis or intervention and
     substitutes their own distinct strategy (e.g. AI suggested notch filtering; human engineer
     substitutes transient shaping or dynamic gating).
   - Governance Rule: The trace must record the AI proposal as REJECTED_BY_HUMAN and the human
     action as ACTIVE_HUMAN_DIRECTIVE. The AI must not take credit for the human's decision.

4. `HUMAN_ACTION_AESTHETIC_VETO`:
   - A human client or artist rejects an intervention on purely subjective aesthetic grounds
     ("I prefer the raw, un-notched sound; the ring gives it character"), overriding technical optimality.
   - Trace Record: Documented as an intentional aesthetic preference, preventing the system
     from treating the rejection as a technical diagnostic failure.

5. `HUMAN_ACTION_ESCALATION_REQUEST`:
   - The AI encounters ambiguity or safety risks that exceed its confidence bounds, pausing
     deliberation and requesting human expert guidance.
   - Trace Record: Logs the escalation trigger, surviving options, and the human expert's resolution.

6. `HUMAN_ACTION_POST_HOC_AUDIT_VERDICT`:
   - A peer reviewer audits a completed historical run and logs formal audit findings.

13.3 ANTI-COLLUSION & PROVENANCE PURITY INVARIANT
Under no circumstances may an automated system:
  - Falsely claim that a human engineer reviewed an intervention when no human was present.
  - Present an AI-generated diagnosis as "human approved" without an explicit digital signature/receipt.
  - Overwrite human manual tweaks in the DAW without explicit user confirmation.

SECTION 14 — CANDIDATE-LESSON GOVERNANCE, QUARANTINE & PROMOTION GATES
================================================================================

14.1 THE PROBLEM OF UNGOVERNED LEARNING & ONE-SHOT OVERFITTING
A severe hazard in automated AI engineering is "one-shot overfitting":
  "AN INTERVENTION SUCCEEDED ON ONE SPECIFIC SNARE DRUM TRACK,
   LEAD THE SYSTEM TO PREMATURELY CONCLUDE THAT ALL SNARE DRUMS REQUIRE THE SAME INTERVENTION."

Phase 1C.5e established that Stage 14 retrospective reviews may identify candidate lessons,
but mandates that ALL candidate lessons must be placed in a QUARANTINE REPOSITORY.
Phase 1C.5f defines the governance architecture governing this quarantine and the gates
required for potential promotion.

14.2 THE CANDIDATE-LESSON LIFECYCLE
A candidate lesson moves through four distinct governance states:

```
  [Stage 14 Review]
         |
         v
  [STATE: QUARANTINED] ----(Rejection Gate)----> [STATE: REJECTED / DISCARDED]
         |
         v (Accumulation & Conflict Verification)
  [STATE: CANDIDATE_FOR_PROMOTION]
         |
         v (Phase 1C.5g Governed Review Gate)
  [STATE: PROMOTED_TO_KNOWLEDGE_BASE]
```

14.3 THE SEVEN PROMOTION ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
Before a quarantined candidate lesson can even be proposed for promotion to the core
knowledge architecture (owned by Phase 1C.5g), Phase 1C.5f requires satisfying seven criteria:

1. REPEATABILITY: The lesson must have been observed across multiple distinct runs, takes,
   and recording environments (minimum statistical support).
2. CAUSAL PLAUSIBILITY: The lesson must be grounded in verified acoustic physics or psychoacoustics,
   not purely empirical correlation.
3. CLEAR APPLICABILITY SCOPE: The lesson must explicitly define its boundary conditions
   (e.g. "Applies only to wooden-shell snare drums recorded with dynamic microphones; invalid for sample-replaced tracks").
4. ABSENCE OF FALSIFYING INSTANCES: The quarantine engine must search historical traces
   for counter-examples where the same intervention produced harmful collateral damage.
5. NON-CONFLICT WITH GOVERNED KNOWLEDGE: The lesson must not contradict the 21 Principles
   or established Phase 1C.4 knowledge baselines.
6. EXPLICIT UNCERTAINTY BOUNDS: The lesson must specify its remaining epistemic limitations.
7. INDEPENDENT HUMAN SIGN-OFF: A qualified human sound engineering authority must review
   and approve the promotion.

14.4 DOWNSTREAM BOUNDARY TO PHASE 1C.5g
Phase 1C.5f owns the QUARANTINE GOVERNANCE, AUDIT LOGGING, AND ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA.
Phase 1C.5f DOES NOT perform the actual migration, remapping, or integration of knowledge
into the core knowledge graph. That migration is strictly owned by Phase 1C.5g.

SECTION 15 — TRACE INTEGRITY, TAMPER EVIDENCE & VERIFICATION
================================================================================

15.1 WHAT MUST BE INTEGRITY-PROTECTED
Phase 1C.5f defines what information requires tamper-evident protection:
  - Record Contents: The exact acoustic parameters, hypotheses, diagnoses, requirements,
    and decisions recorded during deliberation.
  - Creation Timestamps: Verification that predictions preceded execution and observations
    preceded diagnoses.
  - Dependency Edges: Protection against retroactively adding or removing dependency links.
  - Run Headers & Lineage: Protection against altering parent/child pointers or generation indices.
  - Provenance Classifications: Protection against upgrading synthetic or unverified data
    to measured status.

15.2 TECHNOLOGY-NEUTRAL INTEGRITY MECHANISMS
While remaining platform-neutral, Phase 1C.5f outlines standard architectural mechanisms
that implement these integrity requirements:
  - Cryptographic Content Hashing: Each sealed record generates a deterministic cryptographic
    digest (e.g. SHA-256 or BLAKE3) computed across its canonicalized fields.
  - Merkle Lineage Trees / Hash Chaining: Linking record hashes in sequential stages so that
    any retrospective edit alters all subsequent hashes in the chain.
  - Manifest Signing: At the conclusion of a run, a `RunManifestRecord` aggregates all record
    hashes and is digitally signed by the execution runtime.
  - Append-Only Storage Discipline: Underlying storage engines must enforce write-once,
    read-many (WORM) constraints on sealed trace tables or collections.

15.3 INTEGRITY FAILURE DETECTION
During retrospective audit, the audit engine re-calculates all content hashes and traverses
the hash chain. If a hash mismatch is detected, the run is immediately flagged with
`GOVERNANCE_CONDITION_INTEGRITY_VIOLATION`. The audit engine must identify the exact record
that was tampered with and invalidate all downstream derivations.

SECTION 16 — FAILURE, MISSING-TRACE & CORRUPTION DEGRADATION GOVERNANCE
================================================================================

16.1 DETERMINISTIC DEGRADATION WHEN TRACEABILITY FAILS
Real-world systems experience telemetry loss, network disconnects, disk corruption,
and incomplete inputs. Phase 1C.5f mandates that the AI Sound Engineer MUST NOT FAIL SILENTLY
or fabricate missing history:
  "WHEN TRACE INTEGRITY CANNOT BE ESTABLISHED, THE SYSTEM SHALL NOT FABRICATE
   MISSING RECORDS, INFER UNPROVEN PROVENANCE, OR CLAIM COMPLETE RECONSTRUCTION.
   THE SYSTEM MUST DEGRADE DETERMINISTICALLY AND EXPOSE THE UNCERTAINTY."

16.2 THE TEN GOVERNANCE DEGRADATION CONDITIONS
Phase 1C.5f defines ten distinct governance degradation conditions:

1. `GOVERNANCE_CONDITION_INCOMPLETE_TRACE`:
   - One or more non-critical intermediate records are missing from the trace graph.
   - Handling: Mark trace as INCOMPLETE. Audit may proceed on available records, but no
     conclusive claim of end-to-end soundness can be rendered.

2. `GOVERNANCE_CONDITION_UNRESOLVED_PROVENANCE`:
   - Audio or metadata arrived without clear source origin, mic configuration, or calibration.
   - Handling: Assign `ORIGIN_UNKNOWN`. Prohibit treating data as calibrated measured evidence.

3. `GOVERNANCE_CONDITION_UNVERIFIABLE_LINEAGE`:
   - A child run's `parent_run_id` points to a run that cannot be located in the repository.
   - Handling: Treat the child run as an orphaned root run; prohibit automatic inheritance
     of parent evidence.

4. `GOVERNANCE_CONDITION_INTEGRITY_VIOLATION`:
   - A record's cryptographic hash does not match its content, indicating tampering or corruption.
   - Handling: Immediate governance fault. Tombstone the corrupted record; bar all downstream
     records from active deployment.

5. `GOVERNANCE_CONDITION_CONFLICTING_LINEAGE`:
   - Two runs claim contradictory parent-child lineage or mutually exclusive ancestor states.
   - Handling: Flag as lineage collision. Freeze both runs pending human engineering review.

6. `GOVERNANCE_CONDITION_MISSING_DEPENDENCY`:
   - An authorized diagnosis exists, but the prerequisite causal graph or evidence record is missing.
   - Handling: Hard constitutional failure (Principle 1 breach). Invalidate the diagnosis.

7. `GOVERNANCE_CONDITION_UNEXPLAINED_MUTATION`:
   - Parameters applied to the DSP hardware do not match the sealed decision or platform
     translation, and no deviation receipt was logged.
   - Handling: Audit failure. Flag as unaccounted execution discrepancy.

8. `GOVERNANCE_CONDITION_PARTIAL_EXPLANATION`:
   - An explanation can only reconstruct a subset of the decision rationale due to missing
     deliberation links.
   - Handling: Explicitly label the explanation as partial; declare missing rationale components.

9. `GOVERNANCE_CONDITION_BROKEN_EXECUTION_CHAIN`:
   - Execution commands were dispatched, but no execution receipt or telemetry was returned.
   - Handling: Lifecycle pauses at `STATUS 23: EXECUTION_COMPLETED_AWAITING_OUT` or transitions
     to `STATUS 25: OUTCOME_UNEVALUABLE`. Prohibit manufacturing fake receipts.

10. `GOVERNANCE_CONDITION_FIREWALL_VIOLATION`:
    - Post-action outcome evaluation records contain direct references to predicted values
      treated as observed data.
    - Handling: Fatal audit violation (Principle 18 breach). Void the evaluation.

16.3 ANTI-FABRICATION PROTOCOL
In all failure modes, the system must enforce:
  - No synthetic gap-filling: The system must never use a generative LLM to "hallucinate what
    the system probably meant" when a record is lost.
  - Transparent error exposure: The audit report must prominently highlight the degradation
    condition and its exact location in the trace graph.

SECTION 17 — RECORD ARCHITECTURE & PROPOSED PHASE 1C.5f SCHEMAS

17.1 NON-NORMATIVE IMPLEMENTATION STANCE
To avoid premature technology lock-in, all schemas defined in this section are designated:
  `NON_NORMATIVE_ILLUSTRATIVE_SCHEMA`

They illustrate conceptual field structures, relationships, and data types required to
implement the architecture. Concrete production schemas (e.g. JSON-LD, Protocol Buffers,
SQL tables, or Firestore documents) may vary in syntax but MUST preserve these conceptual
fields and semantic boundaries.

17.2 PRIMARY PROPOSED SCHEMA 1: `TraceRunManifestRecord`
Captures the top-level execution container for a single lifecycle run:
  - `run_id`: Unique stable identifier for this execution cycle;
  - `session_id`: Broader project or mixing session container;
  - `parent_run_id`: Reference to parent run (null for root runs);
  - `root_run_id`: Reference to original root run;
  - `generation_index`: Integer counter indicating iteration depth;
  - `lifecycle_status`: Current frozen lifecycle status (Statuses 01 to 27);
  - `governance_state`: Overall trace governance state (`SEALED`, `ACTIVE`, `DEGRADED`);
  - `record_manifest`: Array of record references `[{ stage, record_id, record_type, content_hash }]`;
  - `edge_manifest`: Array of dependency edges `[{ edge_id, source_record_id, target_record_id, edge_type }]`;
  - `run_timestamp_initialized`: ISO 8601 initialization timestamp;
  - `run_timestamp_sealed`: ISO 8601 sealing timestamp;
  - `run_manifest_hash`: Cryptographic digest across the entire manifest;
  - `human_signoff_ref`: Reference to optional human approval record.

17.3 PRIMARY PROPOSED SCHEMA 2: `TraceRecordEnvelope`
Wraps every individual record across all 14 lifecycle stages:
  - `record_id`: Unique stable identifier for this artifact;
  - `run_id`: Reference to the containing run;
  - `stage_number`: Lifecycle stage (01 through 14);
  - `record_type`: Authoritative name (e.g. `CausalDiagnosisRecord`, `EngineeringDecisionRecord`);
  - `provenance_classification`: Origin classification (e.g. `ORIGIN_2_MEASURED_EVIDENCE`);
  - `governance_state`: `RECORD_GOVERNANCE_STATE_SEALED`, `DRAFT`, etc.;
  - `created_timestamp`: Precise creation timestamp;
  - `sealed_timestamp`: Timestamp when record was frozen;
  - `content_payload`: The verbatim stage-specific engineering record payload;
  - `content_hash`: Cryptographic digest of `content_payload`;
  - `predecessor_record_refs`: Array of input records directly consumed;
  - `uncertainty_profile`: Declared epistemic and aleatoric uncertainty attributes.

17.4 PRIMARY PROPOSED SCHEMA 3: `TraceRelationshipEdgeRecord`
Explicitly defines directed dependencies between records:
  - `edge_id`: Unique identifier for the relationship;
  - `run_id`: Reference to containing run;
  - `source_record_id`: Originating record;
  - `target_record_id`: Dependent downstream record;
  - `relationship_type`: Semantic edge type (`PRODUCED_FROM`, `JUSTIFIED_BY`, `CONSTRAINED_BY`, etc.);
  - `epistemic_dependency_weight`: Conceptual weight or necessity of the relationship;
  - `created_timestamp`: Timestamp when edge was instantiated.

17.5 PRIMARY PROPOSED SCHEMA 4: `GovernedExplanationRecord`
Captures the complete assembled engineering explanation for a decision or run:
  - `explanation_id`: Unique identifier;
  - `run_id`: Reference to audited run;
  - `decision_record_ref`: Reference to target `EngineeringDecisionRecord`;
  - `evidential_summary`: Structured reconstruction of input measurements and observations;
  - `diagnostic_justification`: Physical mechanisms, competing hypotheses, and exclusions;
  - `intent_alignment`: Mapping of selected action to declared requirements;
  - `tradeoff_disclosure`: Explicit statement of accepted collateral consequences;
  - `predicted_vs_actual_outcome`: Comparative audit across the Outcome Evidence Firewall;
  - `surviving_uncertainty`: Explicit declaration of uneliminated ambiguity;
  - `audience_view_type`: `VIEW_TECHNICAL_ENGINEERING`, `VIEW_MUSICIAN_PRODUCER`, etc.;
  - `audit_concordance_hash`: Hash linking explanation directly to underlying record hashes.

17.6 PRIMARY PROPOSED SCHEMA 5: `QuarantinedCandidateLessonRecord`
Captures generalizable insights isolated for governed review:
  - `candidate_lesson_id`: Unique identifier;
  - `originating_run_id`: Run where lesson was observed;
  - `originating_review_record_ref`: Stage 14 review record reference;
  - `acoustic_domain`: Target instrument, genre, or processing category;
  - `empirical_observation`: Observed intervention outcome correlation;
  - `proposed_causal_mechanism`: Acoustic physics rationale;
  - `applicability_boundary_conditions`: Strict operational scope and constraints;
  - `counter_examples_observed`: Historical records contradicting the lesson;
  - `quarantine_status`: `STATE_QUARANTINED`, `STATE_PROMOTION_CANDIDATE`, `STATE_REJECTED`;
  - `review_count`: Number of distinct runs evaluated;
  - `human_reviewer_signoff`: Sign-off by qualified engineering authority.

   - User subsequently switches execution platform to Universal Audio Teletronix LA-2A plugin.
2. TRACE SEPARATION:
   - Upstream records (Stages 01–11) remain 100% UNCHANGED and sealed (Principle 15).
   - New `PlatformTranslationRecord` instantiated for LA-2A (translating semantic ratio/attack
     to LA-2A Peak Reduction knob).
   - Lineage demonstrates that semantic engineering intent survived the platform migration.

SCENARIO 13: UNRESOLVED PROVENANCE IN THIRD-PARTY SAMPLE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. CONTEXT:
   - Client uploads drum track labeled "Kick Drum" downloaded from an unknown internet sample pack.

2. TRACE CAPTURE:
   - `EvidenceRecord` created with `provenance_classification: ORIGIN_UNKNOWN`.
   - `provenance_status: "UNRESOLVED_INCOMPLETE"`.
3. REASONING GOVERNANCE:
   - Diagnostic engine is prohibited from assuming acoustic microphone placement physics
     (e.g. cannot assume proximity effect from a cardioid mic).
   - Trace enforces that inferences remain bounded strictly to observed waveform properties.

1. CONTEXT:
   - Hardware DSP outboard processor executed an analog EQ change, but MIDI telemetry

2. TRACE GOVERNANCE:
   - Decision and post-action audio captured; intermediate hardware telemetry missing.
   - Explanation engine generates `GovernedExplanationRecord` with flag
     `GOVERNANCE_CONDITION_PARTIAL_EXPLANATION`.
   - Explanation provides full evidential and diagnostic justification, but explicitly notes:
     "Hardware execution telemetry was lost; applied parameters inferred from audio delta."

   - An archived run from an early development prototype lacks cryptographic hash manifests.

   - Audit engine assigns `GOVERNANCE_CONDITION_INTEGRITY_NOT_ESTABLISHED`.
   - System permits human inspection of records, but displays prominent warning:
     "Historical record tamper-evidence cannot be cryptographically verified."
   - Run cannot be used as authoritative ground truth for regression testing.

   - Producer input logged as `HumanInputRecord` (`ORIGIN_6_HUMAN_ENGINEERING_INPUT`).
   - New child run instantiated to evaluate the confounder with full historical linkage.

21.1 21-PRINCIPLE CONSTITUTIONAL ALIGNMENT AUDIT
External sound engineering and architectural peer reviewers must verify that Phase 1C.5f
remains in strict alignment with the 21 Principles of Sound Engineering Reasoning (v0.3c):

  [ ] Principle 1: Evidence Precedes Diagnosis — Trace graphs enforce temporal and dependency
      precedence of evidence over diagnosis.
  [ ] Principle 2: Observation Is Not Interpretation — Provenance strictly separates objective
      acoustic measurements from subjective psychoacoustic interpretations.
  [ ] Principle 3: Missing Evidence Is Not Negative Evidence — Missing trace data is recorded
      as UNKNOWN; treating absence of proof as proof of absence is prohibited.
  [ ] Principle 4: A Symptom Does Not Identify Its Cause — Traces connect symptoms to causal
      graphs, auditing root mechanism resolution.
  [ ] Principle 5: Diagnosis Should Be Causal Where Evidence Permits — Causal mechanisms and
      competing hypotheses are preserved in diagnostic traces.
  [ ] Principle 6: Competing Hypotheses Survive Until Evidence Justifies Narrowing Them — Excluded
      hypotheses remain inspectable with recorded evidential grounds.
  [ ] Principle 7: Context Informs Reasoning but Does Not Prove Causation — Context inputs are
      tagged as weighting priors, not deterministic causal proof.
  [ ] Principle 8: Engineering Intent Constrains the Solution — Requirements establish non-negotiable
      bounds audited in trade-off and outcome evaluation.
  [ ] Principle 9: Generate Alternatives When the Problem Admits Alternatives — Retrospective
      audit verifies that alternative interventions were deliberated.
  [ ] Principle 10: Intervention Selection Follows Diagnosis — Dependency edges link Candidate
      Interventions directly to authorized Causal Diagnoses.
  [ ] Principle 11: Intervention Should Occur at the Causally Appropriate Point — Trace captures
      processing locus and justifies causal placement.
  [ ] Principle 12: Predict Consequences Before Acting — PredictedOutcomeRecord is cryptographically
      sealed prior to execution handoff.
  [ ] Principle 13: Every Intervention Has Potential Trade-Offs — ConstraintAndTradeOffEvaluationRecord
      preserves accepted collateral costs.
  [ ] Principle 14: Parsimony: Do Not Intervene Without Justified Purpose — Retrospective review
      verifies minimal necessary processing.
  [ ] Principle 15: Platform Translation Operates Downstream of Engineering Reasoning — Semantic
      intent is decoupled from target DSP hardware mapping.
  [ ] Principle 16: Reject Unacceptable Platform Compromises — Rejection gate (Status 22) logs
      violations before audio execution.
  [ ] Principle 17: Deterministic Code Validates Constraints; It Does Not Replace Judgement —
      Algorithmic metrics are preserved as bounds validators.
  [ ] Principle 18: Evaluate Outcomes Honestly Without Circular Justification — Outcome Evidence
      Firewall is cryptographically enforced.
  [ ] Principle 19: Capture Engineering Experience for Governed Review — Quarantined candidate
      lessons are preserved for governed promotion.
  [ ] Principle 20: The Deliberation Record Must Support Independent Retrospective Audit — The core
      constitutional mandate of Phase 1C.5f is fulfilled.
  [ ] Principle 21: Where Evidence Is Missing, Seek Discriminating Evidence Rather Than Guessing —
      Information requests and surviving uncertainty are explicitly preserved.
