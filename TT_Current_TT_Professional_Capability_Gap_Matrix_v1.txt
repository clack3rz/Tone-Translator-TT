SECTION 1 — EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1.1 OBJECTIVE & MANDATE
Phase 1C.3b evaluates the current capabilities of Tone Translator (TT) against the
frozen, platform-independent Professional Sound Engineer Standards established in
Phase 1C.3a (/TT_Professional_Sound_Engineer_Standards_v1.txt).

Using the signed-off repository audit from Phase 1C.2
(/TT_Current_Sound_Engineer_Knowledge_Inventory_v1.txt) as the factual baseline,
this assessment measures what Current TT demonstrably knows, interprets,
diagnoses, decides, and evaluates across all 22 professional engineering domains.
It establishes the exact distance separating Current TT from the target
professional standard without lowering the benchmark or confusing deterministic
preset production with autonomous audio-engineering intelligence.

1.2 MACRO ASSESSMENT FINDINGS
The assessment confirms a stark, structural asymmetry between Current TT's
encoded knowledge/heuristics and its capacity for autonomous engineering reasoning:

  1. High Conceptual & Heuristic Encoding in Specific Modules:
     Current TT possesses mature, high-quality engineering rules in isolated
     subsystems. Most notably, its Semantic Microphone Placement Reasoning
     engine (/src/services/at5MicPlacementReasoning.ts) embodies exceptional
     transducer physics (proximity effect, off-axis rolloff, complementary dual-mic
     phase blending), scoring L4 in Knowledge and L3 in Decision-Making.
     Similarly, pre-distortion low-end tightening and functional EQ deduplication
     embody valid sound-engineering principles (L3/L4 Knowledge).

  2. Near-Total Absence of Diagnostic & Evidence-Driven Reasoning:
     Across almost all domains, Evidence Interpretation, Diagnosis, and Outcome
     Reasoning collapse to L0 (Absent) or L1 (Vocabulary). Current TT does not
     extract acoustic features from audio, does not formulate competing causal
     hypotheses, and does not evaluate why a sonic defect occurs before
     intervening.

  3. Deterministic Overrides Masquerading as AI Judgement:
     Critical tone decisions are not made by AI reasoning from evidence. Instead,
     deterministic TypeScript post-processors scan user text strings for keywords
     (e.g., "thrash", "metal", "Marshall") and force specific gear overrides
     (injecting OverScream, clamping noise gates to -45 dB / 120 ms, overwriting
     engineering notes). The system produces an engineering-like result through
     brute-force code rather than cognitive evaluation.

  4. Complete Disconnect of User Audio Inputs & Feature Schemas:
     While the UI accepts a "Your Recording" audio file, the runtime silently
     discards it before calling Gemini. Furthermore, all nine audio feature
     extraction fields on `ToneProfile` (spectral_centroid, dynamic_range_estimate,
     etc.) are explicitly wiped to `undefined`. Closed-loop tone comparison and
     iterative refinement are completely non-existent (L0).

  5. Pervasive Platform Contamination:
     Engineering concepts are deeply entangled with AmpliTube 5 implementation
     details. Gear rosters, parameter limits (e.g., forcing 2-band Parametric EQ
     restrictions), knob names, and XML attributes dictate what the system
     treats as "engineering knowledge."

1.3 SUMMARY DISPOSITION
Current TT operates as a sophisticated, rule-governed automated preset generator
paired with a multimodal LLM taxonomist. It is NOT yet an autonomous Sound Engineer.
However, it contains substantial high-value assets—specifically its acoustic
microphone reasoning rules, cabinet/speaker selection heuristics, and functional
EQ role taxonomy—that should be extracted and re-homed into the future
platform-independent architecture.

SECTION 2 — ASSESSMENT METHODOLOGY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

2.1 INDEPENDENT BENCHMARK DISCIPLINE
Under the Critical Independence Rule, this assessment holds Current TT strictly
accountable to the platform-independent standards authored in Phase 1C.3a.
The benchmark was frozen prior to this assessment. No target maturity has been
lowered to accommodate Current TT's shortcomings, nor has any domain been elevated
merely because Current TT happens to execute a deterministic script successfully.

2.2 MULTI-DIMENSIONAL DECOUPLING
To avoid misleading aggregate scores, each domain is assessed independently
across the five professional dimensions:
  1. Knowledge: Understanding physical, electrical, acoustic, and psychoacoustic mechanisms.
  2. Evidence Interpretation: Distinguishing measured/observed facts from inferences and assumptions.
  3. Diagnosis: Generating and ranking competing causal hypotheses from symptoms.
  4. Decision-Making: Balancing objectives, constraints, dependencies, and trade-offs.
  5. Outcome Reasoning: Predicting consequences, defining verification criteria, and revising hypotheses.

2.3 MANDATORY "WHY NOT NEXT LEVEL" GATE
To guarantee rigorous grading, every assigned score includes:
  - Documented evidence establishing why the level was awarded.
  - An explicit, mandatory explanation of why the next higher maturity level
    was denied based on missing capabilities.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 3 — EVIDENCE & SCORING RULES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

3.1 EVIDENCE PROVENANCE TAXONOMY
Every capability finding is tagged with its verified origin:
  - EXPLICIT_KNOWLEDGE: Principles deliberately encoded in prompts, schemas, or docs.
  - DETERMINISTIC_BEHAVIOUR: Hardcoded TypeScript logic making execution decisions.
  - STATIC_RECIPE: Fixed profiles or keyword-intercept signal chains.
  - MODEL_DELEGATED: Tasks requested from Gemini without application-level verification.
  - RUNTIME_DEMONSTRATED: Verified execution paths operating in the live app.
  - REPRESENTED_NOT_EXERCISED: Schemas or contracts that exist but are unpopulated.
  - PLATFORM_SPECIFIC: Knowledge coupled to AmpliTube 5.
  - ABSENT: Completely missing from repository evidence.
  - UNKNOWN: Indeterminate from static code inspection alone.

3.2 STRICT PROHIBITIONS APPLIED DURING SCORING
  - Do NOT confuse correct output with professional decision-making.
  - Do NOT confuse prompt instructions with demonstrated LLM competence.
  - Do NOT confuse deterministic keyword replacement with acoustic diagnosis.
  - Do NOT treat Phase 1B contracts (EvidenceRecord, EngineeringDecisionRecord)
    as live runtime reasoning (they remain unexercised in Current TT).
  - Do NOT calculate an average maturity score across dimensions or domains.

SECTION 7 — SYSTEMIC FINDINGS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The capability audit reveals eight systemic patterns that cut across all domains:

1. THE "AUDIO-BLIND" PARADOX:
   The user interface prominently features audio dropzones for Target Audio and
   Your Recording, giving the user the strong impression of an intelligent,
   listening AI. Under the hood, however, the user recording is dropped before
   execution, all nine audio feature fields on `ToneProfile` are explicitly set to
   `undefined`, and zero local audio DSP analysis is performed. Current TT is
   completely audio-blind in application code.

2. DETERMINISTIC OVERRIDES OF AI DECISIONS:
   Instead of using Gemini as an intelligent sound engineer, the application polices
   and overrides it with deterministic TypeScript logic. When Gemini generates a
   valid tone, downstream code inspects strings, deletes user pedals, forces
   OverScream, clamps gate parameters, and overwrites the engineering notes text.
   The AI is treated as an unreliable draft generator, while hardcoded code acts
   as the true engineer.

3. STATIC RECIPE HIJACKING:
   For key benchmark tones (AC/DC Malcolm Young, Pantera Cowboys From Hell,
   Metallica Kill 'Em All), Current TT bypasses AI generation entirely when
   validation recipes are active. These static recipes produce excellent presets,
   creating an illusion of high model intelligence that is actually static file playback.

4. PLATFORM-INDUCED MYOPIA:
   Current TT cannot think about sound engineering outside the catalog and
   constraints of AmpliTube 5. Prompts are filled with AT5 gear names, normalizers
   are built to handle AT5 GUIDs and XML slot bugs, and filter design is crippled
   by AT5's 2-band Parametric EQ limit. General sound engineering knowledge is
   almost entirely conflated with AT5 operational knowledge.

5. FEED-FORWARD OPEN-LOOP ARCHITECTURE:
   Current TT has no concept of outcome verification. Once it emits a preset, the
   execution lifecycle ends. It cannot listen to the result, compare it to the
   target, or adjust a single parameter based on performance. It lacks the most
   fundamental attribute of professional engineering: the iterative feedback loop.

6. ABSENCE OF HYPOTHESIS FORMATION:
   Current TT never formulates or tests causal hypotheses. It matches text
   keywords directly to gear (e.g., "thrash" -> OverScream; "metal" -> Darrell 100;
   "clean" -> compressor). It does not reason about causes, evidence, or alternatives.

7. COSMETIC CONFIDENCE & METADATA:
   The `confidence` output is an uncalibrated, cosmetic number. It is never used
   to trigger alternative processing branches, warn the user of ambiguity, or
   request additional information. The system projects false certainty on every run.

8. RIGID SERIAL TOPOLOGY LOCK-IN:
   The entire architecture assumes a single linear serial chain. It has no
   representational or cognitive capability to handle parallel paths, wet/dry
   routing, or dual-cabinet phase-aligned summing.

SECTION 10 — PLATFORM CONTAMINATION SUMMARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The following platform-specific elements currently contaminate upstream sound
engineering concerns and must be decoupled:

1. AMPLIFIER BADGES AS COGNITIVE CONCEPTS:
   Current TT forces the AI to select from a directory of AT5-specific amplifier
   names ("Brit 8000", "British Lead S100", "Darrell 100"). The Sound Engineer
   should instead specify abstract amplifier behaviors (e.g., "EL34 Class AB,
   high-gain 4-stage triode preamp, cathode-follower FMV tone stack, low damping factor").

2. PLATFORM HARDWARE LIMITATIONS DICTATING ENGINEERING PRINCIPLES:
   The MASTER_PROMPT explicitly tells Gemini: "AT5 Parametric EQ has ONLY 2 bands...
   never generate 3 or 4 bands." This is a pure platform limitation that has been
   allowed to infect the Sound Engineer's cognitive model.

3. KNOB RANGES & PARAMETER ARTIFACTS:
   The AI is forced to output parameters on AT5's 0.0 to 10.0 scale, rather than
   physical or perceptual engineering units (dB, Hz, ms, ratio).

4. SLOT ARCHITECTURE CONSTRAINTS:
   The concept of "Stomp vs. Rack" is an AmpliTube 5 UI convention, not a universal
   acoustic reality. The Sound Engineer should think in terms of Pre-Gain,
   Post-Preamp (Loop), and Post-Acoustic Capture.

     downstream Platform Translation layer.

     units, causal diagnosis, and closed-loop outcome evaluation.
