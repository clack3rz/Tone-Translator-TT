2. PURPOSE & ARCHITECTURAL MISSION

2.1 The Core Mission
The foundational purpose of Phase 1C.5h is to provide an independent, empirical, objective, perceptual, and operational governance firewall between migrated reasoning capabilities and live production guitar tone generation.

In the Tone Translator architecture:
  - Phase 1C.5a through 1C.5e established how an AI Sound Engineer thinks;
  - Phase 1C.5f established how that thinking is audited, traced, and governed;
  - Phase 1C.5g established how legacy heuristics and mappings are inventoried, remapped, and staged in shadow execution;
  - Phase 1C.5h establishes whether the resulting system actually works, sounds right, preserves playability, respects platform reality, avoids regressions, reverts reliably, and is fit for live musicians, audio engineers, and commercial production.

2.2 The Independence of Empirical Validation
Empirical validation is structurally independent from migration planning and code generation.
A migration package may be:
  - 100% syntactically valid against Phase 1C.5g schemas;
  - 100% compliant with the 14-stage reasoning lifecycle;
  - 100% provenanced and sealed under Phase 1C.5f integrity envelopes;
  - and yet be completely unmusical, muddy, harsh, phase-smeared, sluggish, or prone to crashing the target platform.

Phase 1C.5h exists to guarantee that architectural elegance is never mistaken for acoustic truth, and that no unverified or regressive capability ever reaches production.

4. CORE PHASE IDENTITY & GOVERNING AXIOMS

Phase 1C.5h is governed by seven foundational architectural axioms:

4.1 AXIOM 1: CONFORMANCE IS NOT SUFFICIENCY
`ARCHITECTURAL CONFORMANCE IS NECESSARY BUT NOT SUFFICIENT FOR PRODUCTION READINESS.`
`A SYSTEM MAY BE ARCHITECTURALLY CORRECT AND STILL FAIL EMPIRICAL VALIDATION.`
Schema validation proves that data fits the contract. Empirical validation proves that the tone does not degrade transient response, introduce unmusical resonances, destroy mix clarity, or crash the target platform.

4.2 AXIOM 2: LEGACY IS A BASELINE, NOT THE TRUTH
`LEGACY OUTPUT ≠ EXPECTED TRUTH.`
`LEGACY OUTPUT IS AN OPERATIONAL COMPARATOR AND REGRESSION BASELINE, NEVER UNQUESTIONED ACOUSTIC PERFECTION.`
Phase 1C.5h compares candidate output against legacy production output (PATH-PROD) to detect unintended acoustic regression. However, where legacy produced unmusical, harsh, or muffled tones due to crude heuristics, candidate divergence that improves musicality is an Intentional Architectural Correction, not a regression.

4.3 AXIOM 3: FIT-FOR-PURPOSE EVIDENCE
`VALIDATION EVIDENCE MUST BE FIT FOR THE QUESTION BEING EVALUATED.`
`WHAT CAN BE MEASURED SHOULD BE MEASURED. WHAT MUST BE HEARD SHOULD BE HEARD.`
Deterministic questions (schema validity, parameter clamp bounds, syntax, export structure) require objective computational proof; they do not require human listening panels. Perceptual questions (tone feel, pick attack, musical warmth, aesthetic balance, mix masking) require qualified human evaluation; scalar metrics cannot substitute for trained ears.

4.4 AXIOM 4: UNCERTAINTY SURVIVAL
`UNCERTAINTY MUST SURVIVE THE DECISION.`
Validation never forces false binary certainty. If evidence is incomplete, if listening panels disagree, if test fixtures exhibit unknown calibration variables, or if edge-case behavior is untested, the uncertainty is explicitly recorded in ValidationUncertaintyRecord and propagates directly into the AdoptionDecisionRecord.

4.5 AXIOM 5: SEPARATION OF AUTHORITY AND EXECUTION
`PHASE 1C.5h AUTHORIZES. PHASE 1C.5g EXECUTES GOVERNED ADOPTION.`
Phase 1C.5h grants, restricts, conditions, suspends, or withdraws Gate 8 authorization. Phase 1C.5h does not touch production routing tables, toggle traffic slices, or execute rollback commands. Governed operational execution belongs strictly to Phase 1C.5g.

4.6 AXIOM 6: STRICTLY SCOPED AUTHORIZATION
`GATE 8 AUTHORIZATION NEVER EXCEEDS THE EMPIRICALLY TESTED AND VALIDATED SCOPE.`
Authorizing an American High Gain amplifier model does not authorize British Plexi models. Authorizing a standard sampling rate export does not authorize untested rates. Gate 8 attaches exclusively to tested capabilities, gear families, platforms, and version bindings.

4.7 AXIOM 7: VERSION-BOUND VALIDITY
`A PASS DOES NOT AUTOMATICALLY TRANSFER TO A MATERIALLY CHANGED VERSION.`
Every validation finding is bound to the exact immutable version and integrity reference of the architecture, code, models, heuristics, fixtures, and target platform. Any material upstream or environment change triggers governed revalidation.

6. GOVERNING VALIDATION & READINESS INVARIANTS

6.1 The Ten Constitutional Design Mandate Invariants
In accordance with Section 8 of Phase 1C.5h Design Mandate v0.1d, the architecture enforces ten inviolable constitutional invariants:

INVARIANT 1: EMPIRICAL PRIMACY INVARIANT
Structural compliance, schema validity, and passing unit tests are necessary prerequisites, but NEVER constitute sufficient proof of validation. An asset or reasoning engine is validated if and only if it demonstrates acoustic excellence and sound reasoning under empirical audio testing.

INVARIANT 2: MULTI-DIMENSIONAL ACOUSTIC EVALUATION INVARIANT (ANTI-SCALAR COLLAPSE)
Acoustic quality, musical fidelity, and tone validity shall NEVER be collapsed into a single scalar score, average metric, or simplistic threshold. Evaluation must occur across orthogonal dimensions (spectral, dynamic, harmonic, phase, mix context, and playability feel), where failure in any critical dimension constitutes validation rejection.

INVARIANT 3: PRINCIPLE COMPLIANCE INVARIANT
Empirical success achieved via unprincipled shortcuts, hardcoded cheats, or violations of the frozen 21 Principles of Sound Engineering Reasoning is invalid. Every validated run must exhibit strict adherence to the 21 Principles across its full decision trace.

INVARIANT 4: GOLDEN FIXTURE IMMUTABILITY & INDEPENDENCE INVARIANT
The golden acoustic fixture library used for validation must be independently governed, versioned, and immutable. Test fixtures must never be used as training data, prompt engineering tuning sets, or heuristic fitment sets for the candidate engine.

INVARIANT 5: MATERIAL REGRESSION PROTECTION INVARIANT
The new architecture shall not introduce unresolved material regressions into commercial guitar tone generation capability, AT5 preset compatibility, or operational stability within the authorized production scope relative to the established baseline. Intentional architectural corrections and verified neutral divergences do not constitute regressions. Any unresolved material acoustic, functional, or operational regression within the production-critical path of the authorized scope unconditionally blocks Gate 8 authorization.

INVARIANT 6: GATE 8 EXCLUSIVITY & INDEPENDENCE INVARIANT
Gate 8 Production Cutover Authorization can be granted solely by Phase 1C.5h through an independently verifiable `AdoptionDecisionRecord`. Gate 8 is exclusive, explicit, non-bypassable, scoped, traceable, and subject to governed revalidation or withdrawal where material change or new evidence invalidates the authorization basis. Neither Phase 1C.5g, operational engineering, nor automated test scripts may self-authorize production cutover.

INVARIANT 7: FIT-FOR-PURPOSE EVIDENCE INVARIANT
VALIDATION EVIDENCE MUST BE FIT FOR THE QUESTION BEING EVALUATED.
In accordance with the Phase 1C.5h mandate statement: "WHAT CAN BE MEASURED SHOULD BE MEASURED. WHAT MUST BE HEARD SHOULD BE HEARD." Objective evidence is required where the validation question is objectively measurable; perceptual/human evidence is required where musical quality, feel, preference, usability, or other perceptual questions are materially at issue. Some validation activities may be purely deterministic or operational (and do not require a human listening panel, such as deterministic XML/schema/export validation or parameter boundary verification); some may be primarily perceptual; and some require mixed evidence. Deterministic metrics shall never substitute for perceptual evaluation where musical quality or feel is materially at issue, nor may subjective opinion bypass objective acoustic and platform compliance. Gate 8 readiness as a whole must reconcile all materially relevant evidence classes.

INVARIANT 8: ADVERSARIAL & EDGE-CASE SUFFICIENCY INVARIANT
Validation cannot be granted based solely on "happy-path" scenarios. Every candidate component, rule, and workflow must be rigorously evaluated against applicable pathological, degraded, noisy, clipped, ambiguous, and contradictory edge-case inputs within its operational domain to verify robust handling, graceful degradation, and explicit uncertainty preservation.

INVARIANT 9: REVERSIBILITY & ROLLBACK VERIFICATION INVARIANT
No component or path may receive Gate 8 Cutover Authorization without prior empirical verification that its operational rollback mechanism (PATH-ROLLBACK) executes reliably, cleanly, and within governed operational recovery objectives under simulated failure conditions.

INVARIANT 10: COMPLETE EVIDENTIAL LINEAGE INVARIANT
Every validation decision, score, audit result, and sign-off must possess an unbroken, tamper-evident Phase 1C.5f provenance trail linking the input audio fixtures, user prompts, reasoning traces, parameter outputs, rendered audio, and evaluator identities.

6.2 Derived Detailed Architecture Rules
To operationalize the ten constitutional invariants within downstream testing, Phase 1C.5h establishes four derived architectural rules:
  1. Conformance Non-Equivalence Rule: Mathematical proof of contract compliance does not establish acoustic validity, musical plausibility, or production safety (derived from Invariant 1);
  2. Legacy Non-Equivalence Rule: Divergence from legacy output must be investigated, but divergence that corrects a known legacy defect or enhances tone quality without regression is welcomed as an Intentional Architectural Correction (derived from Invariant 5);
  3. Governance & Evidential Independence Rule: VALIDATION AUTHORITY, EVIDENCE, CRITERIA, AND DECISION GOVERNANCE MUST BE INDEPENDENT FROM THE INTERESTS OR ASSUMPTIONS THAT PRODUCED OR MIGRATED THE CANDIDATE. One technical platform or organization may host both generation and validation, but validation evidence, policies, records, and authorization decisions must remain logically and governance-independent; self-approval without independent evidence and Gate 8 governance is prohibited (derived from Invariant 6);
  4. Continuous Production Protection Rule: Live production tone generation (PATH-PROD) must remain uninterrupted, operationally isolated, and immune to validation crashes and shadow-path anomalies at all times (derived from Invariant 9).

7.4 Governed Validation Workflow Activities & Checkpoints
Validation workflow activities in Phase 1C.5h are dependency-governed and fit-for-purpose checkpoints, NOT a universally mandatory linear pipeline.
Depending on the declared scope and assigned test profiles, certain activities execute in parallel, some are conditional, and purely deterministic subjects omit perceptual branches:

  Activity WF-01: Intake & Envelope Verification [MANDATORY GATEWAY]
    - Prerequisite: Sealed MigrationHandoffPackageRecord from Phase 1C.5g.
    - Action: Verifies tamper-evident integrity metadata and records intake entry.

  Activity WF-02: Scope & Version Binding [MANDATORY GATEWAY]
    - Prerequisite: Accepted intake.
    - Action: Establishes explicit boundaries and locks all dependency versions.

  Activity WF-03: Validation Readiness Assessment [MANDATORY GATEWAY]
    - Prerequisite: Completed binding.
    - Action: Verifies all seven frozen Phase 1C.5g Gates, test environments, and fixture availability.

  Activity WF-04: Test Profile & Dimension Selection [MANDATORY GATEWAY]
    - Prerequisite: Readiness confirmed.
    - Action: Selects orthogonal test profiles fit-for-purpose (Profile-01 through Profile-14).

  Activity WF-05: Fixture Corpus Association [CONDITIONAL BY PROFILE]
    - Prerequisite: Profiles requiring audio stems or benchmark presets.
    - Action: Binds uncontaminated golden fixtures with documented provenance.

  Activity WF-06: Deterministic & Schema Verification [BRANCH A]
    - Prerequisite: Profiles 01, 02.
    - Action: Executes automated syntax, schema, and parameter clamp assertions.

  Activity WF-07: Target-Platform Integrity Verification [BRANCH B]
    - Prerequisite: Profile 03.
    - Action: Verifies preset serialization, parameter IDs, and target-platform loadability.

  Activity WF-08: Objective Acoustic Signal Analysis [BRANCH C]
    - Prerequisite: Profiles 04, 05, 10.
    - Action: Measures spectral deltas, dynamic envelopes, harmonic distortion, and stability.

  Activity WF-09: Perceptual Expert Listening Evaluation [BRANCH D]
    - Prerequisite: Profile 06, 08; requires candidate to be acoustically/perceptually relevant.
    - Action: Convenes qualified expert listening panels using governed methodologies.

  Activity WF-10: Multi-Perspective User Acceptance Testing [BRANCH E]
    - Prerequisite: Profiles 07, 14; applies to user-facing workflows and playability.
    - Action: Executes task-oriented usability and musician playability evaluations.

  Activity WF-11: Differential Auditing & Regression Analysis [CONVERGENCE CHECKPOINT]
    - Prerequisite: Completion of assigned execution branches.
    - Action: Tripartite comparison classifying divergences into corrections, neutral, or regressions.

  Activity WF-12: Failure Attribution & Root-Layer Isolation [CONDITIONAL ON FAILURE]
    - Prerequisite: Execution anomalies detected.
    - Action: Isolates root cause across nine governed attribution classes.

  Activity WF-13: Evidence Reconciliation & Uncertainty Logging [CONVERGENCE CHECKPOINT]
    - Prerequisite: Evaluated evidence records.
    - Action: Identifies and preserves conflicts (metrics vs ears) and logs residual uncertainty.

  Activity WF-14: Pre-Cutover Rollback Verification [MANDATORY FOR GATE 8]
    - Prerequisite: Profile 11; required for all candidates seeking production cutover.
    - Action: Empirically verifies automated failover under simulated failure conditions.

  Activity WF-15: Production-Readiness Synthesis [MANDATORY GATEWAY]
    - Prerequisite: All assigned activities completed; rollback verified.
    - Action: Audits the Four Readiness Pillars and inspects for Hard Blockers.

  Activity WF-16: Gate 8 Cutover Adjudication [MANDATORY GATEWAY]
    - Prerequisite: ProductionReadinessAssessmentRecord generated.
    - Action: Issues scoped AdoptionDecisionRecord (AUTHORIZED | CONDITIONAL | REJECTED | BLOCKED).

  Activity WF-17: Post-Cutover Telemetry Governance [POST-AUTHORIZATION]
    - Prerequisite: Active production exposure.
    - Action: Evaluates real-time quality telemetry; issues Gate8ChangeRecord if stop criteria fire.

8. VALIDATION INTAKE ARCHITECTURE

8.1 The Validation Intake Interface
Phase 1C.5h initiates validation solely upon receipt of a formally sealed `MigrationHandoffPackageRecord` generated by Phase 1C.5g (Section 16.3 of Phase 1C.5g Architecture).

Validation cannot begin on ad-hoc code snippets, unversioned branches, uncommitted prompts, or unsealed migration candidate states.

8.2 Intake Verification Protocol
Upon receipt of the handoff package, Phase 1C.5h executes the following intake verification sequence:
  1. Envelope Integrity Verification: Verifies the Phase 1C.5f tamper-evident record integrity metadata of the handoff package;
  2. Provenance Audit: Confirms that the package was issued by an authorized Phase 1C.5g process and contains unbroken lineage back to legacy assets;
  3. Gate 1–7 Completion Inspection: Verifies that all seven frozen Phase 1C.5g Gates are recorded as COMPLETE in the handoff record:
      - Gate 1: Structural Mapping Complete
      - Gate 2: Architectural Compatibility Verified
      - Gate 3: Provenance & Lineage Sealed
      - Gate 4: Shadow Execution Exercised
      - Gate 5: Differential Analysis Documented
      - Gate 6: Rollback Path Operational
      - Gate 7: Handoff Package Assembled
  4. Non-Mutation Principle: Phase 1C.5h NEVER mutates, amends, or augments the received handoff package. The package is preserved as an immutable read-only intake artifact;
  5. Intake Record Generation: Produces a sealed `ValidationIntakeRecord` that establishes the official entrance of the candidate into Phase 1C.5h governance.

8.3 The ValidationIntakeRecord Schema
Each intake event generates an immutable record with the following required fields:
  - intake_id: Unique deterministic identifier identifying the intake event;
  - timestamp: ISO 8601 UTC timestamp of intake;
  - handoff_package_id: Reference to the source MigrationHandoffPackageRecord;
  - handoff_package_integrity_ref: Independently verifiable integrity reference of the handoff package;
  - migration_batch_id: Identifier of the Phase 1C.5g migration batch;
  - requested_by: Identity of the migration coordinator or pipeline;
  - declared_scope: Scope description as asserted by Phase 1C.5g;
  - frozen_upstream_versions: Map of upstream architecture version identifiers (1C.5a through 1C.5g);
  - target_platform_declared_version: Version of target platform declared in handoff;
  - intake_status: INTAKE_ACCEPTED | INTAKE_REJECTED;
  - rejection_reason: Optional diagnostic string if envelope or prerequisite gates fail;
  - record_integrity_metadata: Phase 1C.5h sealing metadata.


9. SCOPE & VERSION BINDING ARCHITECTURE

9.1 Validation Scope Governance
One of the most dangerous validation failure modes is "Scope Inflation" — assuming that because an American High Gain amplifier model was validated, the British Clean amplifier or Bass cabinet models are also valid.

Phase 1C.5h strictly requires that every validation activity be bound to an explicit `ValidationScopeRecord`.

9.2 Supported Scope Dimensions
A `ValidationScopeRecord` defines an unambiguous boundary across one or more of the following orthogonal dimensions:
  1. Capability Scope: Specific reasoning capabilities (e.g. causal acoustic diagnosis, cabinet filtering interventions);
  2. Subsystem Scope: Specific pipeline components (e.g. Tone Profile Matcher, Preset Exporter, Parameter Normalizer);
  3. Gear Family Scope: Explicit amplifier, cabinet, stompbox, or rack gear categories (e.g. high-gain lead amps, vintage clean combos, 4x12 speaker cabs);
  4. Migration Package Scope: The explicit set of remapped legacy heuristics within the handoff package;
  5. Platform Export Scope: Specific export target capabilities (e.g. preset serialization, parameter automation mapping);
  6. Genre / Musical Domain Scope: Explicit playing styles and reference tones (e.g. heavy rhythm, blues lead, jazz clean);
  7. Audio Format Scope: Sampling rates, bit depths, and channel configurations declared in the test profile.

9.3 The ValidationScopeRecord Schema
  - scope_id: Unique identifier;
  - intake_id: Reference to parent ValidationIntakeRecord;
  - primary_domain: GEAR_FAMILY | REASONING_CAPABILITY | PLATFORM_EXPORT | END_TO_END_WORKFLOW;
  - included_units: Array of explicitly included gear IDs, rule IDs, or workflow IDs;
  - excluded_units: Array of explicitly excluded or unverified adjacent units;
  - boundary_constraints: Textual and structural invariants defining the limits of validation;
  - scope_inflation_protection: Boolean flag enforcing that authorization cannot transfer outside `included_units`.

9.4 Validation Version Binding Governance
A validation result is only meaningful when tied to the exact bits, weights, rules, and host environments used during testing.
Phase 1C.5h enforces the cardinal rule:
`A PASS DOES NOT AUTOMATICALLY TRANSFER TO A MATERIALLY CHANGED VERSION.`

9.5 The ValidationVersionBindingRecord Schema
The `ValidationVersionBindingRecord` creates an immutable integrity lock on all dependencies:
  - binding_id: Unique identifier;
  - scope_id: Reference to ValidationScopeRecord;
  - architecture_version_matrix:
      - 1C.5a_version: "v0.3c" + integrity_ref;
      - 1C.5b_version: "v0.2f" + integrity_ref;
      - 1C.5c_version: "v0.1d" + integrity_ref;
      - 1C.5d_version: "v0.1l" + integrity_ref;
      - 1C.5e_version: "v0.1m" + integrity_ref;
      - 1C.5f_version: "v0.1j" + integrity_ref;
      - 1C.5g_version: "v0.1e" + integrity_ref;
      - 1C.5h_mandate_version: "v0.1d" + integrity_ref;
  - runtime_implementation_version: Commit hash, build tag, or package digest of execution codebase;
  - model_configuration_version: Checkpoint identifier, sampling parameters, prompt template digests;
  - deterministic_rules_version: Version digest of rule tables and parameter clamps;
  - gear_catalog_version: Version digest of target-platform gear definition catalog;
  - platform_exporter_version: Version digest of preset serialization logic;
  - fixture_library_version: Manifest digest of golden audio stems and test presets;
  - target_platform_runtime_version: Exact version and build of target platform host application;
  - host_environment_version: Operating environment, audio driver API, runtime dependencies;
  - version_integrity_reference: Composite integrity digest across all bound version references.

10.2 Mandatory Readiness Prerequisites

11. VALIDATION CASE ARCHITECTURE

11.1 The ValidationCase Concept
A `ValidationCase` represents one bounded, explicit, and falsifiable validation question.
It is the atomic unit of validation execution in Phase 1C.5h.

11.2 Structure of a ValidationCase
Each ValidationCase contains:
  1. Case Identity: Unique identifier and descriptive title;
  2. Validation Question: A precise natural-language and formal engineering question;
  3. Validation Subject: The exact component, rule, model, or workflow being questioned;
  4. Scoped Boundaries: The applicable gear, genre, or parameter subspace;
  5. Applicable Test Dimensions: The orthogonal dimensions required to answer the question;
  6. Required Evidence Classes: Objective, perceptual, operational, or mixed;
  7. Associated Fixture Set: Explicit golden stems, DI tracks, or reference presets;
  8. Evaluation Methodology: Concrete test procedure (e.g. Spectral Delta, Blind Listening, Fault Injection);
  9. Acceptance / Tolerance Criteria: Reference to version-bound threshold profile (`threshold_profile_ref` or `tolerance_profile_ref`; NEVER universal hardcoded numbers);
  10. Failure Consequence: What fails if this case fails (e.g. Scoped Gate 8 Block, Remand to 1C.5g);
  11. Expected Remand Destination: The governed upstream phase responsible if this case fails.

11.3 Illustrative Non-Normative Validation Cases
  - CASE-01 (Export Integrity): "Does the serialized preset for American High Gain Lead load into the target platform without GUI parameter errors or DSP instantiation failure?"
  - CASE-02 (Transient Response): "Does the candidate high-gain signal chain preserve palm-muted transient definition and low-end tightness relative to the applicable tolerance profile and golden reference stem?"
  - CASE-03 (Pick Attack Dynamics): "Does the candidate amplifier model respond dynamically across pick attack velocities without digital clipping or unmusical sag relative to dynamic profile tolerances?"
  - CASE-04 (Rollback Reversion): "When a simulated DSP fault occurs in PATH-SHADOW, does PATH-ROLLBACK restore PATH-PROD production tone generation within its governed operational recovery objective (governed by recovery_objective_ref) without audible corruption?"
  - CASE-05 (Harmonic Resonance): "Does the candidate cabinet mic-placement reasoning produce unnatural comb-filtering or hollow mid-range notches under off-axis microphone placement?"

13. TEST PROFILE ARCHITECTURE

13.1 Orthogonal Test Dimensions vs Universal Fixed Scorecards
Phase 1C.5h explicitly prohibits universal fixed scorecards where every component must run every test.
A deterministic XML preset serializer does not need a human listening panel. A subtle psychoacoustic tube-sag model cannot be certified solely by an FFT spectrum analyzer.

Test dimensions in Phase 1C.5h are ORTHOGONAL EVIDENCE DOMAINS.
Each validation subject is assigned a fit-for-purpose selection of Test Profiles based on its function, risk profile, declared scope, and failure modes.

13.2 The Fourteen Governed Test Profile Families
Phase 1C.5h establishes fourteen standardized, reusable Test Profile families:

  1. PROFILE-01: STRUCTURAL / SCHEMA PROFILE
     - Focus: Schema conformance, XML well-formedness, parameter bound clamping, datatype validation, enum validity.
     - Applicability: Serialization, parameter remapping, catalog imports, configuration.

  2. PROFILE-02: DETERMINISTIC PARAMETER PROFILE
     - Focus: Mathematical correctness, algorithmic transfer functions, linear/logarithmic conversions, parameter continuity.
     - Applicability: Deterministic transformation rules, gain-staging calculators, tone-stack math.

  3. PROFILE-03: TARGET-PLATFORM EXPORT PROFILE
     - Focus: Successful target platform preset import, parameter ID integrity, gear substitution legality, GUI rendering without error.
     - Applicability: Stage 12 platform translation, preset exporters, platform packaging.

  4. PROFILE-04: OBJECTIVE ACOUSTIC PROFILE
     - Focus: Multi-band spectral magnitude, harmonic distortion (THD), phase coherence, noise floor, frequency response deltas governed by tolerance profiles.
     - Applicability: Amp models, cab emulations, EQ modules, analog saturation algorithms.

  5. PROFILE-05: DYNAMIC RESPONSE PROFILE
     - Focus: Compression envelope, transient attack preservation, sustain decay curve, crest factor, velocity sensitivity relative to dynamic budgets.
     - Applicability: Overdrive/distortion stages, power-amp sag, compressor modules, gate logic.

  6. PROFILE-06: PERCEPTUAL LISTENING PROFILE
     - Focus: Expert human evaluation of tonal balance, musical warmth, harshness, presence, mid-range punch, listening fatigue.
     - Applicability: Complete guitar tones, cabinet IR selections, EQ curves, core sound engineering algorithms.

  7. PROFILE-07: PLAYABILITY PROFILE
     - Focus: Performing guitarist feel, pick response, latency within the declared latency budget (latency_budget_ref), dynamic interaction, cleanup on volume roll-off.
     - Applicability: Live guitar emulation, interactive DSP, real-time input conditioning.

  8. PROFILE-08: MIX-CONTEXT PROFILE
     - Focus: Tonal behavior in full multitrack mix, bass masking, vocal frequency separation, cymbal harshness interaction.
     - Applicability: Final mix presets, studio mastering chains, multi-mic blend reasoning.

  9. PROFILE-09: REGRESSION PROFILE
     - Focus: Differential tripartite comparison against PATH-PROD and reference benchmarks; isolation of genuine regressions.
     - Applicability: All migrated components replacing existing legacy production heuristics.

  10. PROFILE-10: OPERATIONAL STABILITY PROFILE
      - Focus: Host resource consumption, memory stability under continuous audio rendering, buffer underrun/overrun resistance, thread safety within operational budgets (operational_budget_ref).
      - Applicability: Runtime DSP engine, background shadow execution pipelines, export batch services.

  11. PROFILE-11: ROLLBACK PROFILE
      - Focus: Pre-cutover automated rollback execution under load, state recovery speed within governed recovery objectives (recovery_objective_ref), zero-downtime reversion to PATH-PROD.
      - Applicability: All production-cutover candidate deployments. Mandatory for Gate 8 consideration.

  12. PROFILE-12: ADVERSARIAL / EDGE-CASE PROFILE
      - Focus: Degraded inputs, extreme signal levels exceeding nominal full scale (ILLUSTRATIVE / NON-NORMATIVE: e.g. signals above 0 dBFS), extreme input impedances, contradictory user prompts, corrupt XML.
      - Applicability: All user-facing, platform-facing, or signal-intake modules.

  13. PROFILE-13: TRACEABILITY / GOVERNANCE PROFILE
      - Focus: Phase 1C.5f RunTrace completeness, tamper-evident record integrity metadata, causal reasoning step justification.
      - Applicability: All AI Sound Engineer reasoning runs; audit compliance verification.

  14. PROFILE-14: USER-WORKFLOW / UAT PROFILE
      - Focus: End-to-end task completion by real-world personas (Recording Engineer, Session Guitarist, Producer, Preset Architect).
      - Applicability: End-to-end Tone Translator user workflows, UI controls, preset discovery.

15. OBJECTIVE EVIDENCE ARCHITECTURE

15.1 The Role of Objective Evidence
Objective evidence provides mathematical, deterministic, and physical proof of system behavior.
It is mandatory where the validation question concerns:
  - schema validity;
  - parameter-bound clamping;
  - target-platform preset file serialization;
  - spectral response curves;
  - total harmonic distortion (THD) and harmonic distribution;
  - dynamic crest factor and attack envelope;
  - phase coherence across stereo and multi-mic configurations;
  - system latency, memory allocation, and CPU execution time;
  - error handling and exception logging under invalid input.

15.2 The Prohibition of Universal Numeric Freezing
Phase 1C.5h strictly adheres to Invariant 7 and the Design Mandate:
`NO UNIVERSAL NUMERIC THRESHOLDS ARE FROZEN AT THE MANDATE OR SPECIFICATION LEVEL.`

A universal rule such as fixing a single decibel delta or distortion limit across all guitar audio engineering is fundamentally flawed:
  - In an ultra-clean amplifier model, subtle mid-scoop divergences or fractional harmonic distortion increases may be severe defects;
  - In a heavily saturated high-gain fuzz-lead tone, significant harmonic shifts or high distortion percentages may be musically desirable and expected.

Therefore, objective acceptance thresholds in Phase 1C.5h are strictly:
  - declared within individual, version-bound Test Profiles;
  - scoped to specific gear families, pickup types, and musical genres;
  - calibrated against verified golden acoustic reference fixtures;
  - governed via profile references (`threshold_profile_ref` and `tolerance_profile_ref`).

15.3 The ObjectiveEvidenceRecord Schema
  - objective_evidence_id: Unique identifier;
  - execution_id: Reference to ValidationExecutionRecord;
  - metric_category:
      - STRUCTURAL_SCHEMA
      - PARAMETER_BOUNDS
      - EXPORT_INTEGRITY
      - SPECTRAL_ANALYSIS
      - DYNAMIC_ENVELOPE
      - HARMONIC_DISTORTION
      - PHASE_COHERENCE
      - OPERATIONAL_LATENCY_PERFORMANCE;
  - measured_parameters: Detailed key-value map of raw measurements;
  - applied_threshold_profile_ref: Versioned reference of the applicable tolerance rule;
  - tolerance_evaluation: MET | OUT_OF_TOLERANCE | BORDERLINE | NOT_APPLICABLE;
  - measured_delta: Numerical difference from baseline or reference;
  - objective_pass_fail: PASS | FAIL | INCONCLUSIVE;
  - computational_artifacts_retained: URIs/integrity references of spectral plots, audio renders, serialization dumps.


16. PERCEPTUAL EVIDENCE ARCHITECTURE

16.1 The Materiality of Perceptual Evidence
In guitar tone engineering, objective measurements alone can never certify tone quality.
A frequency response curve cannot measure whether a guitar tone feels "stiff" or "spongy" under the guitarist's pick. An FFT plot cannot determine whether high-end harmonic content sounds like "silky air" or "grating digital fizz."

Perceptual evidence is mandatory whenever:
  - musical quality, tone warmth, depth, or presence is at issue;
  - pick attack dynamics, instrument cleanup, and musician feel are evaluated;
  - psychoacoustic masking, mix separation, and stereo imaging are assessed;
  - competing tone interventions are judged for aesthetic preference.

16.2 Controlled, Flexible Listening Methodologies
Phase 1C.5h explicitly rejects listening-methodology dogmatism. No single evaluation methodology is universal. Phase 1C.5h supports a governed toolkit of eleven distinct perceptual methodologies:

  1. Sighted Expert Review: Qualitative evaluation by master sound engineers to rapidly identify gross defects, obvious phase errors, or inappropriate gain staging;
  2. A/B Testing: Direct sequential comparison between candidate and baseline to evaluate specific audible differences;
  3. Blind A/B Testing: Evaluator compares Tone X and Tone Y without knowing which is candidate or legacy, eliminating brand and model bias;
  4. ABX Testing: Evaluator identifies whether unknown Tone X matches Reference A or Candidate B; used to prove whether an audible difference genuinely exists;
  5. MUSHRA (MUlti Stimulus test with Hidden Reference and Anchor): Rigorous evaluation methodology comparing candidate against hidden reference, low-pass anchor, and legacy baseline;
  6. Paired Comparison: Forced-choice comparative preference testing across multiple tone variants;
  7. Reference Matching Evaluation: Scoring how accurately candidate reproduces the acoustic characteristics of an approved commercial record stem;
  8. Defect Detection Panel: Targeted listening specifically tasked with hunting for aliasing, digital clipping, unnatural gate chatter, or phase cancellation;
  9. Musical-Context Evaluation: Listening to the guitar track embedded inside a full multitrack mix (drums, bass, vocals, keys) to evaluate mix fit;
  10. Interactive Playability Testing: Hands-on evaluation by performing guitarists with real instruments to assess latency, pick response, touch sensitivity, and cleanup;
  11. Extended Listening Fatigue Test: Prolonged listening at calibrated studio monitoring levels (governed by listening_level_profile_ref) to detect fatiguing high-frequency resonances.

16.3 The PerceptualEvaluationRecord Schema
  - perceptual_evidence_id: Unique identifier;
  - execution_id: Reference to ValidationExecutionRecord;
  - evaluator_id: Anonymized unique identifier of the evaluator;
  - evaluator_role: MASTER_ENGINEER | SESSION_GUITARIST | PRODUCER | PLATFORM_SPECIALIST;
  - methodology_used: One of the 11 governed methodologies above;
  - listening_environment:
      - monitor_type: NEARFIELD_STUDIO_MONITORS | OPEN_BACK_HEADPHONES | IEM | GUITAR_CAB_FRFR;
      - calibrated_spl_profile_ref: Reference to calibrated listening level profile;
      - acoustic_treatment_level: TREATED_CONTROL_ROOM | UNTREATED | REFERENCE_HEADPHONE;
  - presented_stimuli_refs: Map of stimulus labels to audio integrity references;
  - perceptual_dimensions_evaluated:
      - tonal_balance (rating governed by rating_scale_profile_ref);
      - harmonic_warmth_vs_harshness (rating governed by rating_scale_profile_ref);
      - transient_punch_and_clarity (rating governed by rating_scale_profile_ref);
      - dynamic_touch_response (rating governed by rating_scale_profile_ref);
      - mix_presence_and_separation (rating governed by rating_scale_profile_ref);
      - listening_fatigue_absence (rating governed by rating_scale_profile_ref);
  - qualitative_observations: Structured text notes detailing specific acoustic phenomena;

  - evaluation_outcome [PHASE_1C5H_PROPOSED]: PERCEPTUAL_PASS | PERCEPTUAL_FAIL | PERCEPTUAL_INCONCLUSIVE.


17. USER ACCEPTANCE TESTING (UAT) ARCHITECTURE

17.1 Task-Oriented Acceptance vs Listening Tests
While listening tests evaluate audio signals, User Acceptance Testing (UAT) evaluates task-oriented workflows, usability, cognitive friction, and system fitness from the perspective of real-world audio professionals.

17.2 The Four Governed UAT Evaluator Personas
Phase 1C.5h defines four distinct, governed UAT evaluator personas:

  1. PERSONA 1: THE RECORDING / MIX ENGINEER
     - Focus: Workflow speed, mix context integration, phase coherence, headroom management, automation behavior, export precision.
     - Sample Task (ILLUSTRATIVE / NON-NORMATIVE): "Take a raw multitrack DI guitar, prompt Tone Translator for a 90s Grunge rhythm tone, export preset to target platform, and blend into mix."
     - Acceptance Criteria: Tone sits in mix without extreme corrective EQ, preset loads immediately, level matches studio standards defined by calibration_profile_ref.

  2. PERSONA 2: THE PERFORMING / SESSION GUITARIST
     - Focus: Real-time playability, latency within declared latency budget (latency_budget_ref), dynamic feel, volume pot cleanup, pick attack sensitivity, absence of unnatural gating.
     - Sample Task (ILLUSTRATIVE / NON-NORMATIVE): "Plug guitar directly into input, play dynamic blues lead transitioning from soft thumbing to heavy aggressive picking."
     - Acceptance Criteria: Amp cleans up naturally on low volume; dynamic touch response preserved without digital glitching or unnatural sustain decay.

  3. PERSONA 3: THE CREATIVE PRODUCER / SONGWRITER
     - Focus: Intuitive semantic translation, aesthetic inspiration, genre authenticity, variety of valid alternatives presented.
     - Sample Task (ILLUSTRATIVE / NON-NORMATIVE): "Request an 'ethereal shoegaze shimmer lead with dark vintage body' and evaluate relevance of generated signal chains."
     - Acceptance Criteria: System accurately translates subjective musical intent without requiring deep DSP parameter tweaking.

  4. PERSONA 4: THE TONE-PLATFORM / PRESET ARCHITECT
     - Focus: Target-platform compliance, gear licensing safety, XML structure, preset organization, parameter normalization.
     - Sample Task (ILLUSTRATIVE / NON-NORMATIVE): "Verify that generated preset conforms to target platform parameter manifests and contains no illegal gear identifiers."
     - Acceptance Criteria: Zero XML parsing errors, zero missing gear warnings, parameter values within valid platform boundaries (parameter_bounds_ref).

18. GOLDEN ACOUSTIC FIXTURE LIBRARY & BENCHMARK STEM MANAGEMENT

18.1 The Golden Fixture Concept
A `ValidationFixtureRecord` represents a curated, immutable, version-controlled audio stem, DI performance, or reference preset used as a standardized benchmark for validation.

Phase 1C.5h enforces the cardinal rule:
`NO FIXTURE OR FIXTURE FAMILY BECOMES UNIVERSAL GROUND TRUTH OUTSIDE ITS DECLARED VALIDATION SCOPE.`

18.2 The Eight Governed Fixture Classes
Phase 1C.5h establishes eight distinct fixture classes:
  1. DI Performance Fixtures: Clean, unamplified guitar direct-injection recordings captured with calibrated high-impedance (Hi-Z) DI hardware, representing varied playing techniques (palm muting, sweep picking, fingerstyle, funk strums, heavy downstrokes);
  2. Reference Tone Fixtures: Multi-track stems and isolated guitar tracks from acclaimed commercial recordings, representing gold-standard studio production tones;
  3. Approved Preset Fixtures: Known-good, human-designed target-platform presets that have previously achieved commercial success and engineering approval;
  4. Rendered Platform Benchmark Fixtures: Audio files pre-rendered through verified physical amplifiers and official platform hardware profiles to test algorithmic parity;
  5. Synthetic Test Signal Fixtures: Mathematically pure test tones (sine sweeps across the audible spectrum, pink noise, dual-tone IMD signals, impulse clicks, square waves) to evaluate transfer functions, frequency response, and phase linearity;
  6. Multitrack Context Fixtures: Complete rhythm section stems (drums, bass, vocals) designed to evaluate guitar masking and mix clarity;
  7. Adversarial & Pathological Fixtures: Deliberately corrupted or extreme audio stems (signals with extreme digital clipping above nominal full scale, high hum, DC offsets, high noise floor, impedance-mismatched transducers);
  8. Workflow & Prompt Fixtures: Standardized suites of natural language user prompts and tone requests designed to evaluate semantic reasoning and translation robustness.

19. MULTI-DIMENSIONAL ACOUSTIC EVALUATION MODEL

19.1 The Failure of Scalar Audio Metrics
Traditional audio software testing often relies on scalar metrics like Signal-to-Noise Ratio (SNR), Mean Squared Error (MSE), or simple RMS delta.
In guitar tone engineering, scalar metrics fail catastrophically:
  - An output signal phase-inverted 180 degrees sounds identical to the human ear in isolation, but has 100% MSE difference from the original;
  - A tone with an aggressive, musical high-gain saturation profile may have a huge spectral divergence from a clean reference, but represent the exact intended sound engineering outcome;
  - A tone that matches a simple frequency curve can suffer from severe digital aliasing or intermodulation distortion that ruins musicality.

19.2 The Seven Orthogonal Acoustic Evaluation Dimensions
Phase 1C.5h evaluates audio across seven mutually orthogonal dimensions:

  1. Spectral Balance Dimension: Frequency distribution across sub-bass, bass, low-mids, mid-range, upper-mids, and treble/air bands;
  2. Dynamic Envelope Dimension: Transient attack speed, compression ratio under peak picking, sustain decay curve, and crest factor;
  3. Harmonic Texture Dimension: Total harmonic distortion (THD), harmonic order distribution (warm/even vs aggressive/odd ratios), and intermodulation distortion (IMD);
  4. Spatial & Phase Coherence Dimension: Stereo field width, phase correlation between dual microphones, comb-filtering absence, and mono-compatibility;
  5. Temporal & Latency Dimension: Algorithmic processing delay, buffer latency, phase smearing, and transient response time;
  6. Playability & Dynamic Feel Dimension: Instrument cleanup on volume roll-off, touch sensitivity, pick velocity response, and natural acoustic bloom;
  7. Mix Context & Masking Dimension: Vocal frequency pocket preservation, kick/bass separation, cymbal harshness avoidance, and intelligible presence in full production.


20. REGRESSION DETECTION & DIFFERENTIAL AUDITING ARCHITECTURE

20.1 Tripartite Differential Comparison
Phase 1C.5h evaluates candidate migrations using a rigorous Tripartite Comparison:
  - Path A: Candidate Migration Output (PATH-SHADOW);
  - Path B: Legacy Production Output (PATH-PROD);
  - Path C: Golden Studio Reference / Human Benchmark (FIXTURE-REF).

Comparing candidate solely against legacy (Path A vs Path B) is flawed because legacy often contains known acoustic bugs, harshness, or unmusical heuristics.
Comparing candidate solely against golden reference (Path A vs Path C) is flawed because it ignores operational continuity and may mask subtle regressions in working production features.

Tripartite comparison allows Phase 1C.5h to distinguish between improvements, neutral differences, and genuine regressions.

20.2 The Four Regression Classification Categories
Every detected divergence between candidate output and baseline output MUST be classified into exactly one of four governed categories:

  1. INTENTIONAL ARCHITECTURAL CORRECTION [PHASE_1C5H_PROPOSED]
     - Definition: Candidate diverges from PATH-PROD, but the divergence fixes a documented legacy defect, removes unmusical harshness, or aligns with the 21 Principles of Sound Engineering Reasoning without degrading user expectations.
     - Gate 8 Impact: Does NOT block Gate 8. Documented as a verified architectural improvement.

  2. NEUTRAL DIVERGENCE [PHASE_1C5H_PROPOSED]
     - Definition: Candidate differs subtly from PATH-PROD (e.g. minor micro-dB shifts, harmless harmonic variation), but listening panels and UAT judge neither output to be superior or degraded.
     - Gate 8 Impact: Does NOT block Gate 8. Logged in audit trail.

  3. GENUINE REGRESSION [PHASE_1C5H_PROPOSED]
     - Definition: Candidate performs worse than PATH-PROD in a production-critical acoustic, functional, export, or operational dimension (e.g. lost low-end definition, introduced clipping, broken preset loading, unacceptable latency increase, audible aliasing).
     - Gate 8 Impact: CRITICAL BLOCKER. Invariant 5 applies: ANY unresolved material genuine regression within scope blocks Gate 8.

  4. UNRESOLVED DIVERGENCE [PHASE_1C5H_PROPOSED]
     - Definition: Candidate differs materially from PATH-PROD, but evidence is inconclusive as to whether the divergence is an improvement or a regression (e.g. listening panel is split, or metrics indicate a change whose musical impact is unknown).
     - Gate 8 Impact: BLOCKS Gate 8 until resolved by targeted retest or formal engineering trade-off review.

20.3 The RegressionAssessmentRecord Schema
  - regression_id: Unique identifier;
  - package_id: Reference to parent ValidationPackage;
  - comparison_target: Legacy PATH-PROD vs Candidate PATH-SHADOW;
  - divergence_detected: Boolean;
  - divergence_magnitude: Quantitative summary of differences relative to tolerance profiles;
  - classification:
      - INTENTIONAL_ARCHITECTURAL_CORRECTION
      - NEUTRAL_DIVERGENCE
      - GENUINE_REGRESSION
      - UNRESOLVED_DIVERGENCE;

  - regression_domain: ACOUSTIC | FUNCTIONAL | EXPORT | OPERATIONAL | PLAYABILITY;
  - detailed_justification: Engineering rationale for classification;
  - blocks_gate_8: Boolean (True where an unresolved material Genuine Regression or unresolved material divergence blocks the affected authorization scope under governed regression rules).

21. FAILURE ATTRIBUTION ARCHITECTURE

21.1 The Necessity of Root-Layer Attribution
When a validation test fails, speculative or premature blame corrupts engineering feedback loops:
  - If a test crashes because the test runner ran out of host memory, blaming the AI reasoning model wastes engineering effort;
  - If an export fails because the target platform updated its schema, blaming the Phase 1C.5g migration mapping is incorrect;
  - If a tone sounds muffled because the test fixture DI track was recorded with depleted active pickup batteries, blaming the tone EQ logic is disastrous.

Phase 1C.5h enforces strict, evidence-supported Failure Attribution across eight explicit architectural layers plus UNKNOWN.

21.2 The Nine Governed Failure Attribution Classes
Phase 1C.5h categorizes failures into:

  1. VALIDATION_ENVIRONMENT
     - Definition: Failure caused by the test harness, OS configuration, audio driver dropouts, test runner memory exhaustion, or network timeouts.
     - Remand: Validation Infrastructure Engineering.

  2. TEST_FIXTURE
     - Definition: Failure caused by a corrupt, miscalibrated, clipped, or contaminated golden audio stem, DI track, or reference preset.
     - Remand: Golden Fixture Library Curation Team.

  3. MIGRATION_MAPPING
     - Definition: Failure caused by an incorrect heuristic remapping, erroneous parameter transformation, or schema mismatch generated by Phase 1C.5g.
     - Remand: Phase 1C.5g Knowledge Migration Team.

  4. RUNTIME_IMPLEMENTATION
     - Definition: Failure caused by bugs, crashes, null pointers, buffer overflows, or race conditions in the runtime execution codebase.
     - Remand: Software Engineering / Runtime Core Team.

  5. REASONING
     - Definition: Failure caused by flawed AI sound engineering cognition (Stages 01–14), contradictory hypotheses, ungrounded causal diagnosis, or unmusical intervention selection.
     - Remand: Upstream Reasoning Governance (Phases 1C.5a through 1C.5e).

  6. PLATFORM_TRANSLATION
     - Definition: Failure caused by incorrect translation from abstract sound engineering requirements to target-platform parameters in Stage 12 (Semantic Tone Design Handoff / Platform Translation).
     - Remand: Stage 12 Platform Translation Governance.

  7. TARGET_PLATFORM
     - Definition: Failure caused by a bug, limitation, regression, or undocumented behavior inside the external target platform application itself.
     - Remand: Platform Integration & Vendor Compatibility Team.

  8. OPERATIONAL_INFRASTRUCTURE
     - Definition: Failure caused by host hardware, cluster scheduling, database connection dropouts, or external storage degradation.
     - Remand: Operations & SRE Team.

  9. UNKNOWN
     - Definition: Failure whose root cause cannot be established by available evidence.
     - Rule: Phase 1C.5h forbids speculative attribution. When cause is uncertain, it is marked UNKNOWN and triggers targeted forensic isolation.

22. EVIDENCE CONFLICT & UNCERTAINTY ARCHITECTURE

22.1 The Principle of Conflict Preservation
In complex sound engineering validation, evidence frequently conflicts:
  - An objective frequency response metric indicates a treble boost exceeding standard profile tolerances, but expert listening panels enthusiastically rate the tone as open and cutting through the mix;
  - An objective parameter check passes completely, but guitarists report that the preset feels unresponsive to dynamic pick nuances;
  - Expert recording engineers listening to the same preset report divergent aesthetic assessments across monitoring environments.

In traditional QA systems, such conflicts are often hidden by calculating an arbitrary average or overriding qualitative feedback.
Phase 1C.5h strictly prohibits averaging conflicting evidence into artificial certainty.
Conflicts must be explicitly modeled in `EvidenceConflictRecord` and preserved for governed engineering resolution.

23. VALIDATION OUTCOME ARCHITECTURE

23.1 Validation Outcomes vs Reasoning Lifecycle Statuses
To prevent architectural contamination, Phase 1C.5h explicitly separates its validation outcomes from the 27 immutable reasoning lifecycle statuses of Phase 1C.5a.

The 27 lifecycle statuses (e.g. `STATUS_EVIDENCE_ACQUIRED`, `STATUS_DIAGNOSIS_COMMITTED`, `STATUS_INTERVENTION_FINALIZED`, `STATUS_RUN_COMPLETE`) govern the internal cognition of an AI Sound Engineer during a run.
They are NOT validation verdicts on migration packages.

23.2 The Four Governed Validation Outcome Classes
Phase 1C.5h defines four distinct, version-bound validation outcome classes (marked `PHASE_1C5H_PROPOSED`):

  1. PASS [PHASE_1C5H_PROPOSED]
     - Definition: All applicable assigned test profiles and validation criteria are satisfied; no unresolved material genuine regressions or hard blockers remain within the evaluated scope; applicable rollback requirements are satisfied; and residual uncertainty remains within governed acceptable bounds for the requested scope.
     - Gate 8 Impact: Eligible to proceed to Gate 8 adjudication. PASS does not itself authorize production cutover.

  2. FAIL [PHASE_1C5H_PROPOSED]
     - Definition: Candidate exhibited one or more verified material genuine regressions, structural export failures, unmusical artifacts, or failed rollback verification.
     - Gate 8 Impact: Disqualified from Gate 8. Triggers defect remand to appropriate upstream phase.

  3. BLOCKED / NOT EVALUABLE [PHASE_1C5H_PROPOSED]
     - Definition: Candidate cannot be evaluated due to missing fixtures, broken test environment, unverified intake envelopes, or unresolved upstream blockers.
     - Gate 8 Impact: Disqualified from Gate 8. Testing halted until blockers resolved.

  4. CONDITIONAL / REQUIRES FOLLOW-UP [PHASE_1C5H_PROPOSED]
     - Definition: Candidate passes core functional and acoustic criteria, but minor non-critical anomalies, limited fixture coverage, or residual uncertainties require restricted deployment scope and mandatory production monitoring.
     - Gate 8 Impact: Eligible for Conditional Gate 8 Authorization only (Section 27).

24. RETEST & REVALIDATION ARCHITECTURE

24.1 Revalidation Governance
A common flaw in complex software systems is the "Stale Pass" — a component passes testing, but a subsequent tweak to an adjacent prompt, a compiler upgrade, a gear catalog update, or a target-platform patch silently breaks its output without anyone retesting.

Phase 1C.5h enforces:
`A PASS DOES NOT AUTOMATICALLY TRANSFER TO A MATERIALLY CHANGED VERSION.`
`EVERY MATERIAL CHANGE TO AN AUTHORIZED CANDIDATE OR ITS ENVIRONMENT MANDATES REVALIDATION.`

24.2 Revalidation Triggers
Phase 1C.5h identifies ten mandatory Revalidation Triggers:
  1. TRIGGER-01: Upstream reasoning architecture or prompt template update;
  2. TRIGGER-02: Deterministic heuristic rule, parameter clamp, or table update;
  3. TRIGGER-03: Gear definition catalog or gear alias update;
  4. TRIGGER-04: Target-platform parameter manifest or exporter serialization update;
  5. TRIGGER-05: Target-platform version update or host OS runtime update;
  6. TRIGGER-06: Golden Acoustic Fixture update, recalibration, or contamination invalidation;
  7. TRIGGER-07: Runtime execution engine, audio driver, or DSP core codebase update;
  8. TRIGGER-08: PATH-ROLLBACK mechanism or operational deployment logic update;
  9. TRIGGER-09: Approval and promotion of a new Candidate Lesson affecting the subject domain;
  10. TRIGGER-10: Production quality telemetry reporting a significant anomaly or defect spike.

25. PRODUCTION READINESS ASSESSMENT ARCHITECTURE

25.1 Pre-Gate-8 Synthesis
The `ProductionReadinessAssessmentRecord` represents the comprehensive, pre-decision synthesis where all evidence classes, test profiles, regression analyses, and operational checks are evaluated in concert before presenting the candidate for Gate 8 adjudication.

Phase 1C.5h enforces:
`PRODUCTION READINESS IS A MULTI-PILLAR SYNTHESIS, NEVER A SINGLE SYNTHETIC SCALAR SCORE.`

25.2 The Four Pillars of Production Readiness
Phase 1C.5h synthesizes readiness across four orthogonal pillars:

  PILLAR 1: ACOUSTIC & MUSICAL READINESS
    - Objective signal measurements within tolerance profile;
    - Expert listening panel approval across tonal balance, dynamics, warmth, and punch;
    - Musician playability confirmed (touch response, cleanup, latency budget);
    - Absence of audible defects (harshness, aliasing, comb-filtering, unnatural sag).

  PILLAR 2: REASONING & GOVERNANCE READINESS
    - 100% compliance with the 14-stage reasoning lifecycle and 21 Principles;
    - Full causal explainability and unbroken Phase 1C.5f RunTrace provenance;
    - Zero ungrounded hypotheses or fabricated acoustic interventions;
    - Candidate Lessons are governed through the inherited Phase 1C.5e/f quarantine and Knowledge Governance process.

  PILLAR 3: PLATFORM & OPERATIONAL READINESS
    - Target-platform preset export verified and loadable without errors;
    - Legal gear parameter identifiers and valid parameter boundary mapping;
    - Runtime resource consumption and latency within established operational budgets;
    - Stability proven under continuous audio rendering stress.

  PILLAR 4: ROLLBACK & PRODUCTION-SAFETY READINESS
    - PATH-PROD live production tone generation completely isolated and protected;
    - No unresolved material genuine regressions within the requested authorization scope;
    - Pre-cutover automated rollback (PATH-ROLLBACK) empirically verified under load within recovery objectives;
    - Real-time quality telemetry stop-criteria fully configured.

25.3 Hard Blockers to Gate 8 Consideration
The presence of ANY of the following conditions constitutes an absolute Hard Blocker that disqualifies the candidate from Gate 8 consideration:
  1. Any unresolved material genuine regression within the requested authorization scope;
  2. Any target-platform preset export failure, crash, or serialization corruption;
  3. Failure or unverified state of automated rollback (PATH-ROLLBACK);
  4. Missing, broken, or unverifiable Phase 1C.5f provenance/traceability records;
  5. Contaminated, miscalibrated, or missing golden test fixtures;
  6. Requested authorization scope broader than the actually tested scope;
  7. Unresolved critical uncertainty regarding production tone safety;
  8. Unresolved upstream architectural violation (Phases 1C.5a through 1C.5g).

27. CONDITIONAL AUTHORIZATION ARCHITECTURE

27.1 The Role of Conditional Authorization
Conditional Authorization allows Phase 1C.5h to authorize bounded production exposure (e.g. internal testing, restricted opt-in evaluations) for candidates that pass all core acoustic and functional tests but possess minor non-critical gaps (e.g. limited fixture coverage on rare pickup types, minor workflow friction).

27.2 Absolute Non-Waivable Invariants
Phase 1C.5h strictly forbids using Conditional Authorization as a loophole to bypass critical engineering rigor.
Conditional Authorization CAN NEVER BE GRANTED if any of the following exist:
  - Any unresolved material genuine regression in guitar tone or dynamics;
  - Any broken target-platform export or preset corruption;
  - Any failure or unverified state of automated rollback;
  - Any broken or missing Phase 1C.5f audit provenance;
  - Any violation of the 21 Principles of Sound Engineering Reasoning;
  - Any critical unresolved uncertainty that could crash production or degrade live audio.

27.3 Governance Constraints on Conditional Authorization
Phase 1C.5h defines authorization governance constraints, NOT runtime rollout mechanics (which belong exclusively to Phase 1C.5g). Every `AdoptionDecisionRecord` with status `CONDITIONALLY_AUTHORIZED` must specify:
  1. authorized_scope: Explicitly narrowed scope of permissible cutover;
  2. authorization_constraints: Declared governance bounds on deployment;
  3. excluded_scope: Explicit list of sub-models or parameter spaces excluded from cutover;
  4. required_monitoring: Real-time telemetry monitoring rules and stop-criteria;
  5. review_requirement: Mandatory governance review trigger or validity condition;
  6. follow_up_evidence_requirement: Specific empirical data that must be submitted to achieve full authorization;
  7. validity_condition_ref: Reference to the formal condition contract.


28. GATE 8 SUSPENSION, RESTRICTION & WITHDRAWAL ARCHITECTURE

28.1 Post-Cutover Governance
Gate 8 authorization is NOT an irrevocable, permanent grant of production cutover.
If real-world production conditions degrade, if live musicians report regressions not caught in the lab, or if target platforms update and invalidate prior assumptions, Phase 1C.5h possesses the full constitutional authority to modify or revoke Gate 8 authorization.

28.2 Governance State Modifications
Phase 1C.5h governs Gate 8 authorization through four lifecycle modifications:

  1. SUSPENSION (Temporary Stop)
     - Cause: Real-time telemetry detects an anomaly spike exceeding the monitoring threshold profile, or a major upstream dependency is modified.
     - Action: Gate 8 status becomes `SUSPENDED`. Phase 1C.5g is notified that authorization is suspended; Phase 1C.5g executes the required adoption response (halting slicing and reverting traffic to PATH-PROD). Authorization remains suspended pending revalidation.

  2. RESTRICTION (Scope Narrowing)
     - Cause: A defect is discovered affecting a specific subset of authorized gear.
     - Action: Gate 8 status becomes `RESTRICTED`. The authorized scope is surgically narrowed to exclude the defective gear while preserving cutover for the unaffected scope.

  3. WITHDRAWAL (Authorization Revocation)
     - Cause: A critical unresolved material regression, structural defect, unresolvable platform failure, or other evidence invalidates the basis of the active Gate 8 authorization for the affected scope.
     - Action: Gate 8 status becomes `WITHDRAWN` for the affected authorization record and scope. Phase 1C.5g executes the governed operational rollback response. The withdrawn authorization record is permanently closed and cannot be reactivated or reinstated.
     - Future Eligibility: Withdrawal of an authorization does NOT permanently prohibit the underlying capability from future validation. A corrected or materially revised candidate may re-enter Phase 1C.5h only through a new governed validation package, applicable empirical validation/revalidation, Production Readiness Assessment, and a new Gate 8 adjudication. Any resulting authorization MUST be issued through a new AdoptionDecisionRecord. WITHDRAWAL PERMANENTLY CLOSES THE AUTHORIZATION RECORD. IT DOES NOT PERMANENTLY BAN THE CAPABILITY. A WITHDRAWN AUTHORIZATION IS NEVER REINSTATED. A REMEDIATED CANDIDATE REQUIRES A NEW GATE 8 DECISION.

  4. SUPERSEDED (Version Upgrade)
     - Cause: A newer, revalidated version of the package achieves full Gate 8 authorization.
     - Action: The prior decision record status becomes `SUPERSEDED`.

28.3 The Gate8ChangeRecord Schema
Modifications to Gate 8 authorization are NEVER performed by mutating historical records.
They are recorded via an immutable, tamper-evident `Gate8ChangeRecord`:
  - change_id: Unique identifier;
  - decision_id: Reference to original AdoptionDecisionRecord;
  - previous_governance_status: ACTIVE | SUSPENDED | RESTRICTED;
  - new_governance_status: SUSPENDED | RESTRICTED | WITHDRAWN | SUPERSEDED;
  - change_reason: Detailed technical explanation of the modification;
  - trigger_source: TELEMETRY_STOP_CRITERIA | HUMAN_AUDIT | REVALIDATION_FAILURE | DOWNSTREAM_DEFECT;
  - governance_effect: SUSPEND_AUTHORIZATION | NARROW_SCOPE | REVOKE_AUTHORIZATION | SUPERSEDE_RECORD;
  - affected_authorization_scope: Explicit scope impacted by the change;
  - required_adoption_response: ADOPTION_HALTED | SCOPE_NARROWED | ROLLBACK_REQUIRED | NO_OPERATIONAL_CHANGE;
  - revalidation_required: Boolean;
  - change_timestamp: ISO 8601 UTC timestamp;
  - authorizing_official: Identity/role of governance authority;
  - record_integrity_metadata: Verifiable sealing metadata.

29. DEFECT REMAND ARCHITECTURE

29.1 Governed Remand Routing
When a candidate component fails empirical validation, Phase 1C.5h does NOT attempt to fix code, tweak prompts, adjust migration mappings, or redesign target-platform translation tables.
Doing so would violate separation of concerns and bypass upstream architectural governance.

Instead, Phase 1C.5h packages the failure evidence into a governed `DefectRemandPackage` and routes it to the authoritative upstream owner based on its verified Failure Attribution.

29.2 Remand Routing Destination Matrix
  - Attribution: MIGRATION_MAPPING
    -> Destination: Phase 1C.5g (Knowledge Migration, Remapping & Controlled Adoption)
    -> Action: Phase 1C.5g reviews remapping heuristics, parameter transformation tables, and schema translation logic.

  - Attribution: REASONING
    -> Destination: Upstream Reasoning Governance (Phases 1C.5a through 1C.5e)
    -> Action: Reviewed by reasoning architects across Stages 01–14; prompts, causal graphs, or hypothesis evaluation models are investigated.

  - Attribution: PLATFORM_TRANSLATION
    -> Destination: Stage 12 Platform Translation Governance
    -> Action: Stage 12 translation planners re-examine target-platform abstraction mappings and parameter normalization curves.

  - Attribution: RUNTIME_IMPLEMENTATION
    -> Destination: Software Engineering / Runtime Core Team
    -> Action: Code-level bug remediation (DSP buffer handling, memory allocation, multi-threading race conditions).

  - Attribution: TEST_FIXTURE
    -> Destination: Golden Acoustic Fixture Curation Team
    -> Action: Fixture recalibration, audio re-capture, or contamination quarantine.

  - Attribution: VALIDATION_ENVIRONMENT
    -> Destination: Validation Infrastructure & CI/CD Engineering
    -> Action: Test runner resource expansion, audio driver updates, host container stabilization.

  - Attribution: TARGET_PLATFORM
    -> Destination: Platform Integration & Vendor Compatibility Team
    -> Action: Investigation of external platform updates, vendor defect reporting, or compatibility shim implementation.

  - Attribution: OPERATIONAL_INFRASTRUCTURE
    -> Destination: Operations / SRE Team
    -> Action: Cluster scheduling, hardware allocation, operational latency optimization.

  - Attribution: UNKNOWN
    -> Destination: Joint Diagnostic Working Group
    -> Action: Forensic isolation and reproduction testing until root-layer attribution is established.

30. CANDIDATE LESSON BOUNDARY & GOVERNANCE

30.1 The Cardinal Rule of Candidate Lessons
During empirical validation, test failures and acoustic evaluations often reveal profound sound engineering insights.
However, Phase 1C.5h enforces the strict constitutional fence:
`VALIDATION EVIDENCE MAY PRODUCE A CANDIDATE LESSON ONLY WHEN THE EVIDENCE SUPPORTS A POTENTIALLY GENERALIZABLE ENGINEERING INSIGHT REQUIRING GOVERNED REVIEW.`
`STAGE 14 ENGINEERING REVIEW MAY CONTRIBUTE REVIEW EVIDENCE RELEVANT TO A CANDIDATE LESSON, BUT STAGE 14 IS NOT THE CANDIDATE LESSON QUARANTINE, KNOWLEDGE GOVERNANCE, OR PROMOTION AUTHORITY.`
`PHASE 1C.5h MAY FORMULATE CANDIDATE LESSON EVIDENCE. IT MAY NOT PROMOTE IT.`
Candidate Lessons identified during Phase 1C.5h validation are remanded into the inherited Candidate Lesson quarantine and Knowledge Governance process defined by frozen Phase 1C.5e and Phase 1C.5f.

30.2 Governed Candidate Lesson Lifecycle
When a potentially generalizable insight is identified:
  1. FORMULATION: Phase 1C.5h may formulate a Candidate Lesson record/evidence package linked to the supporting validation evidence, provenance, uncertainty, affected scope, and relevant Engineering Review evidence where applicable;
  2. GOVERNED QUARANTINE HANDOFF: The Candidate Lesson enters the inherited Candidate Lesson quarantine process defined by frozen Phase 1C.5e and Phase 1C.5f;
  3. INDEPENDENT KNOWLEDGE GOVERNANCE REVIEW: Independent Knowledge Governance evaluates the Candidate Lesson under the inherited governance process;
  4. PROMOTION / REJECTION / CONTINUED QUARANTINE: Promotion, rejection, or continued quarantine remains outside Phase 1C.5h authority;
  5. REVALIDATION CONSEQUENCE: If an approved Candidate Lesson materially alters reasoning or output, affected Phase 1C.5h validation evidence and Gate 8 authorization are subject to governed revalidation (Trigger 09).


31. PRODUCTION PROTECTION ARCHITECTURE

31.1 Continuous Production Isolation (PATH-PROD)
Phase 1C.5h guarantees that live musician tone generation (PATH-PROD) remains completely protected from test failures, experimental model crashes, and shadow execution load.

31.2 Production Safety Invariants
  1. Strict Execution Isolation: Validation test harnesses, automated audio rendering, and listening panels execute exclusively in isolated test sandbox environments or against PATH-SHADOW. They NEVER execute in live production audio processing threads;
  2. Zero Implicit Cutover: Gate 8 cutover is never granted by default, timeout, or absence of errors. Cutover requires an explicit, tamper-evident, verifiable `AdoptionDecisionRecord`;
  3. Execution Boundary Demarcation: Phase 1C.5h grants authorization, but does NOT execute production routing. The physical promotion of traffic from PATH-PROD to PATH-ADOPT is executed exclusively by Phase 1C.5g under strict operational safeguards;
  4. Operational Recovery Verification: No candidate may be promoted without a verified, automated rollback path (PATH-ROLLBACK) proven capable of restoring pure PATH-PROD within the governed recovery objective (governed by recovery_objective_ref) without service interruption.


32. TRACEABILITY & EXPLAINABILITY ARCHITECTURE

32.1 Phase 1C.5f Traceability Integration
Every validation record, test execution, evidence measurement, and authorization decision generated under Phase 1C.5h integrates seamlessly into the Phase 1C.5f Traceability, Explainability & Governance Architecture.

32.2 Audit Lineage & Explainability
Phase 1C.5h enforces:
  - Stable Identifiers: Every validation entity has an immutable, globally unique deterministic identifier;
  - RunTrace Linkage: Validation executions reference the Phase 1C.5f RunTrace ID of the underlying reasoning runs;
  - Tamper-Evident Record Integrity: All intake records, evidence records, failure records, and adoption decision records are wrapped in Phase 1C.5f integrity envelopes with verifiable integrity metadata;
  - Governance Explainability: If a musician or sound engineer asks, "Why was this guitar tone chosen, and who certified that it sounds right?", the system provides an unbroken, auditable chain of evidence:
      Customer Prompt -> Stages 01–14 Reasoning Trace -> Migration Remapping -> Validation Cases -> Golden Fixtures -> Listening Panel Scores -> Rollback Verification -> AdoptionDecisionRecord.

    - Key Fields: change_id, decision_id, previous_governance_status, new_governance_status (SUSPENDED | RESTRICTED | WITHDRAWN | SUPERSEDED), change_reason, trigger_source, governance_effect, affected_authorization_scope, required_adoption_response, revalidation_required, change_timestamp, authorizing_official, record_integrity_metadata.


34. RELATIONSHIP EDGE MODEL

34.1 Phase 1C.5f Compatible Lineage Graph
Phase 1C.5h defines fourteen governed relationship edges connecting validation entities into an immutable, directed lineage graph fully compatible with Phase 1C.5f:

  1. VALIDATES (ValidationCase -> MigrationCandidate | ReasoningComponent)
     - Asserts that a validation case evaluates a specific subject.

  2. TESTED_WITH (ValidationExecutionRecord -> ValidationFixtureRecord)
     - Identifies the exact golden audio fixture used during execution.

  3. BOUND_TO (ValidationCase | ValidationPackage -> ValidationVersionBindingRecord)
     - Locks validation entities to exact version references.

  4. EVIDENCED_BY (ValidationCase | ValidationOutcomeRecord -> ObjectiveEvidenceRecord | PerceptualEvaluationRecord)
     - Links validation evaluations to observed physical or human evidence.

  5. COMPARED_AGAINST (RegressionAssessmentRecord -> LegacyOutput | GoldenReference)
     - Establishes the baseline against which differential analysis was performed.

  6. EVALUATED_BY (PerceptualEvaluationRecord | UATEvaluationRecord -> EvaluatorPersona)
     - Attaches expert listener or UAT persona attribution to evaluation records.

  7. CONFLICTS_WITH (ObjectiveEvidenceRecord <-> PerceptualEvaluationRecord)
     - Explicitly links competing evidence records across an unresolved discrepancy.

  8. UNCERTAIN_OF (ValidationOutcomeRecord -> ValidationUncertaintyRecord)
     - Attaches explicit residual doubts or unverified domains to an outcome.

  9. SUPERSEDES_VALIDATION (ValidationExecutionRecord -> ValidationExecutionRecord)
     - Links a retest run to the prior failed or invalidated run it replaces.

  10. INVALIDATED_BY (ValidationOutcomeRecord | AdoptionDecisionRecord -> RevalidationTriggerRecord)
      - Identifies the material change trigger that rendered prior evidence stale.

  11. REQUIRES_RETEST (RevalidationTriggerRecord -> RetestPlanRecord)
      - Directs the generation of a governed retest plan following a material change.

  12. AUTHORIZES_SCOPE (AdoptionDecisionRecord -> ValidationScopeRecord)
      - Confirms that Gate 8 authorization attaches strictly to the validated scope.

  13. REMANDS_TO (ValidationFailureRecord -> GovernanceEntity)
      - Routes an attributed defect package to the responsible upstream phase.

  14. DERIVED_FROM_HANDOFF (ValidationIntakeRecord -> MigrationHandoffPackageRecord)
      - Connects Phase 1C.5h intake to the sealed Phase 1C.5g handoff package.

35. THE TWENTY REQUIRED VALIDATION FAILURE & DEGRADATION CONDITIONS

Phase 1C.5h fully operationalizes all twenty failure and degradation conditions established in Design Mandate v0.1d. Each condition is defined with its detection point, affected records, immediate governance action, Gate 8 consequence, retest rule, failure attribution, and remand path:

Condition 01: Invalid Test Fixture
  - Detection Point: Activity WF-05 / Fixture Integrity Audit.
  - Affected Record(s): ValidationFixtureRecord, ValidationReadinessAssessmentRecord.
  - Immediate Governance Action: Mark fixture INVALIDATED; halt testing dependent on fixture; generate ValidationFailureRecord.
  - Gate 8 Consequence: Blocks Gate 8 for all candidate scopes relying on the invalid fixture.
  - Retest / Revalidation Rule: Requires replacement with a verified fixture and complete retest of affected cases.
  - Attribution Category: TEST_FIXTURE.
  - Remand Path: Golden Acoustic Fixture Curation Team.

Condition 02: Fixture Provenance Missing
  - Detection Point: Activity WF-05 / Fixture Provenance Inspection.
  - Affected Record(s): ValidationFixtureRecord, ValidationReadinessAssessmentRecord.
  - Immediate Governance Action: Quarantine fixture; refuse execution of dependent ValidationCases.
  - Gate 8 Consequence: Blocks Gate 8 (unverifiable provenance violates Invariant 4 and Invariant 10).
  - Retest / Revalidation Rule: Re-establish documented capture chain of custody or replace fixture.
  - Attribution Category: TEST_FIXTURE.
  - Remand Path: Golden Acoustic Fixture Curation Team.

Condition 03: Calibration Mismatch
  - Detection Point: Activity WF-05 / Input Signal Calibration Check.
  - Affected Record(s): ValidationExecutionRecord, ObjectiveEvidenceRecord.
  - Immediate Governance Action: Abort execution; log calibration offset relative to calibration_profile_ref.
  - Gate 8 Consequence: Blocks Gate 8 until calibrated re-run is executed.
  - Retest / Revalidation Rule: Re-align digital gain staging to declared reference level and re-run.
  - Attribution Category: TEST_FIXTURE or VALIDATION_ENVIRONMENT.
  - Remand Path: Fixture Team / Validation Infrastructure.

Condition 04: Non-Reproducible Result
  - Detection Point: Activity WF-08 or WF-09 / Multi-Run Determinism Audit.
  - Affected Record(s): ValidationExecutionRecord, ValidationFailureRecord.
  - Immediate Governance Action: Flag candidate as non-deterministic; log execution environment discrepancies.
  - Gate 8 Consequence: Blocks Gate 8 (production audio must be reliably reproducible).
  - Retest / Revalidation Rule: Isolate pseudo-random seeds, uninitialized DSP buffers, or thread concurrency races.
  - Attribution Category: RUNTIME_IMPLEMENTATION or REASONING.
  - Remand Path: Software Engineering Core Team.

Condition 05: Objective Metrics Pass but Listening Fails
  - Detection Point: Activity WF-13 / Evidence Reconciliation.
  - Affected Record(s): EvidenceConflictRecord, PerceptualEvaluationRecord, ValidationOutcomeRecord.
  - Immediate Governance Action: Prohibit objective scores from overriding human listening; log acoustic dissonance; trigger targeted listening review.
  - Gate 8 Consequence: Blocks Gate 8 (Axiom 3 / Invariant 7: What must be heard must be heard).
  - Retest / Revalidation Rule: Remand to upstream reasoning to adjust acoustic requirements or candidate interventions; re-run listening panel.
  - Attribution Category: REASONING.
  - Remand Path: Upstream Reasoning Governance (Phases 1C.5a through 1C.5e).

Condition 06: Listening Passes but Deterministic / Export Validation Fails
  - Detection Point: Activity WF-13 / Evidence Reconciliation.
  - Affected Record(s): EvidenceConflictRecord, ObjectiveEvidenceRecord, ValidationOutcomeRecord.
  - Immediate Governance Action: Prohibit subjective enthusiasm from excusing structural defect; log parameter boundary or serialization failure.
  - Gate 8 Consequence: Blocks Gate 8 (broken presets cannot be deployed to users).
  - Retest / Revalidation Rule: Fix export serialization or parameter clamping; re-run deterministic suite.
  - Attribution Category: PLATFORM_TRANSLATION or RUNTIME_IMPLEMENTATION.
  - Remand Path: Stage 12 Platform Translation Governance.

Condition 07: PATH-PROD and PATH-SHADOW Not Comparable
  - Detection Point: Activity WF-11 / Tripartite Comparator Calibration.
  - Affected Record(s): RegressionAssessmentRecord, ValidationReadinessAssessmentRecord.
  - Immediate Governance Action: Halt differential auditing; log comparability divergence (e.g. mismatched input buffers or gain staging).
  - Gate 8 Consequence: Blocks Gate 8 (regression detection is invalid without calibrated comparability).
  - Retest / Revalidation Rule: Align input buffers, sampling rates, and normalization across production and shadow paths.
  - Attribution Category: VALIDATION_ENVIRONMENT or MIGRATION_MAPPING.
  - Remand Path: Phase 1C.5g Coexistence Infrastructure.

Condition 08: Unexplained Material Divergence
  - Detection Point: Activity WF-11 / Differential Auditing.
  - Affected Record(s): RegressionAssessmentRecord, ValidationUncertaintyRecord.
  - Immediate Governance Action: Classify as UNRESOLVED_DIVERGENCE; freeze candidate cutover.
  - Gate 8 Consequence: Blocks Gate 8 until divergence is resolved as either an Intentional Architectural Correction or a Neutral Divergence where evidence supports that classification.
  - Retest / Revalidation Rule: Convene Master Sound Engineer review to determine acoustic root cause and determine whether divergence represents an Intentional Architectural Correction, a Neutral Divergence, or a Genuine Regression.
  - Attribution Category: REASONING or MIGRATION_MAPPING.
  - Remand Path: Phase 1C.5g / Upstream Reasoning.

Condition 09: Export Succeeds but Target-Platform Playback Fails
  - Detection Point: Activity WF-07 / Target-Platform Integrity Verification.
  - Affected Record(s): ObjectiveEvidenceRecord, ValidationFailureRecord.
  - Immediate Governance Action: Log platform DSP instantiation failure, preset parse crash, or missing gear error.
  - Gate 8 Consequence: Absolute Hard Blocker for Gate 8.
  - Retest / Revalidation Rule: Remand export logic to Stage 12; verify against actual target platform binaries.
  - Attribution Category: PLATFORM_TRANSLATION or TARGET_PLATFORM.
  - Remand Path: Stage 12 Platform Translation Governance.

Condition 10: Acoustic Result Passes but Runtime Is Unstable
  - Detection Point: Activity WF-08 / Operational Stability Profile.
  - Affected Record(s): ObjectiveEvidenceRecord, ValidationFailureRecord.
  - Immediate Governance Action: Log resource leak, CPU execution budget breach (operational_budget_ref), or buffer underrun under load.
  - Gate 8 Consequence: Absolute Hard Blocker for Gate 8 (production audio cannot stutter or crash).
  - Retest / Revalidation Rule: Optimize DSP execution loops, memory allocations, or threading model.
  - Attribution Category: RUNTIME_IMPLEMENTATION.
  - Remand Path: Software Engineering Core Team.

Condition 11: Rollback Validation Fails
  - Detection Point: Activity WF-14 / Pre-Cutover Rollback Verification.
  - Affected Record(s): ProductionReadinessAssessmentRecord, ValidationFailureRecord.
  - Immediate Governance Action: Log rollback failure (e.g. reversion time exceeding recovery_objective_ref, audio corruption, state inconsistency).
  - Gate 8 Consequence: Absolute Hard Blocker for Gate 8 (Invariant 9: Reversibility Assurance is non-negotiable).
  - Retest / Revalidation Rule: Remand rollback harness to Phase 1C.5g; re-verify automated reversion under live traffic simulation.
  - Attribution Category: OPERATIONAL_INFRASTRUCTURE or MIGRATION_MAPPING.
  - Remand Path: Phase 1C.5g Coexistence Team.

Condition 12: Undocumented Test-Environment Dependency
  - Detection Point: Activity WF-02 / Version Binding Audit.
  - Affected Record(s): ValidationVersionBindingRecord, ValidationReadinessAssessmentRecord.
  - Immediate Governance Action: Identify undeclared dynamic library, local driver patch, or unversioned environment variable.
  - Gate 8 Consequence: Blocks Gate 8 until all dependencies are explicitly codified and locked.
  - Retest / Revalidation Rule: Codify dependency into environment manifest; update ValidationVersionBindingRecord.
  - Attribution Category: VALIDATION_ENVIRONMENT.
  - Remand Path: Validation Infrastructure Team.

Condition 13: Expert Reviewers Materially Disagree
  - Detection Point: Activity WF-13 / Evidence Reconciliation.
  - Affected Record(s): EvidenceConflictRecord, PerceptualEvaluationRecord.
  - Immediate Governance Action: Log reviewer split; forbid averaging divergent scores.
  - Gate 8 Consequence: Prevents unqualified PASS; forces CONDITIONAL or BLOCKED status.
  - Retest / Revalidation Rule: Convene blind consensus panel; analyze whether disagreement stems from differing monitoring or aesthetic expectations.
  - Attribution Category: REASONING or UNKNOWN.
  - Remand Path: Upstream Reasoning / Knowledge Governance.

Condition 14: UAT Result Inconclusive
  - Detection Point: Activity WF-10 / UAT Adjudication.
  - Affected Record(s): UATEvaluationRecord, ValidationOutcomeRecord.
  - Immediate Governance Action: Log task confusion or ambiguous user feedback; mark outcome INCONCLUSIVE.
  - Gate 8 Consequence: Blocks Gate 8 until workflow clarity is established.
  - Retest / Revalidation Rule: Refine user interaction design or semantic translation; re-run UAT with fresh evaluator persona.
  - Attribution Category: REASONING or RUNTIME_IMPLEMENTATION.
  - Remand Path: Product Design / Upstream Reasoning.

Condition 15: Critical Domain Remains Untested
  - Detection Point: Activity WF-03 / Scope Coverage Audit.
  - Affected Record(s): ValidationScopeRecord, ValidationUncertaintyRecord.
  - Immediate Governance Action: Identify missing fixture coverage for declared critical gear or playing styles.
  - Gate 8 Consequence: Limits authorized scope strictly to tested domain; blocks broader deployment.
  - Retest / Revalidation Rule: Acquire and validate missing fixtures; execute assigned test profiles.
  - Attribution Category: TEST_FIXTURE.
  - Remand Path: Golden Fixture Library Curation Team.

Condition 16: Material Implementation Change Occurs After PASS
  - Detection Point: Activity WF-02 / Post-Pass Dependency Integrity Monitor.
  - Affected Record(s): RevalidationTriggerRecord, AdoptionDecisionRecord.
  - Immediate Governance Action: Generate RevalidationTriggerRecord; instantly SUSPEND active Gate 8 authorization.
  - Gate 8 Consequence: Gate 8 status transitions to SUSPENDED.
  - Retest / Revalidation Rule: Issue RetestPlanRecord; execute targeted revalidation of all impacted test cases.
  - Attribution Category: RUNTIME_IMPLEMENTATION, REASONING, or PLATFORM_TRANSLATION.
  - Remand Path: Author of the change.

Condition 17: Test Evidence Corrupted or Unverifiable
  - Detection Point: Activity WF-01 or WF-12 / Integrity Envelope Verification.
  - Affected Record(s): ValidationExecutionRecord, ObjectiveEvidenceRecord.
  - Immediate Governance Action: Invalidate corrupt record; generate integrity alert.
  - Gate 8 Consequence: Absolute Hard Blocker for Gate 8 (unverifiable evidence cannot support cutover).
  - Retest / Revalidation Rule: Investigate storage or transmission corruption; re-run test harness to generate fresh sealed record.
  - Attribution Category: VALIDATION_ENVIRONMENT or OPERATIONAL_INFRASTRUCTURE.
  - Remand Path: Infrastructure / Traceability Team.

Condition 18: Target-Platform Update Invalidates Prior Evidence
  - Detection Point: Activity WF-02 / Target-Platform Version Monitor.
  - Affected Record(s): RevalidationTriggerRecord, AdoptionDecisionRecord.
  - Immediate Governance Action: Issue Trigger 05; mark prior export and rendering evidence STALE; suspend active cutover.
  - Gate 8 Consequence: Gate 8 SUSPENDED pending compatibility verification against new platform build.
  - Retest / Revalidation Rule: Re-test all export and rendering cases against the updated platform version.
  - Attribution Category: TARGET_PLATFORM.
  - Remand Path: Platform Integration Team.

Condition 19: Gate 8 Requested With Unresolved Critical Uncertainty
  - Detection Point: Activity WF-15 / Production Readiness Synthesis.
  - Affected Record(s): ValidationUncertaintyRecord, ProductionReadinessAssessmentRecord.
  - Immediate Governance Action: Identify critical unresolved risk (e.g. unknown stability under continuous streaming); reject Gate 8 petition.
  - Gate 8 Consequence: Absolute Hard Blocker for Gate 8.
  - Retest / Revalidation Rule: Execute targeted stress tests to resolve the specific uncertainty domain.
  - Attribution Category: UNKNOWN or RUNTIME_IMPLEMENTATION.
  - Remand Path: Testing Working Group.

Condition 20: Requested Production Authorization Scope Exceeds Tested Scope
  - Detection Point: Activity WF-02 & WF-16 / Scope Alignment Audit.
  - Affected Record(s): ValidationScopeRecord, AdoptionDecisionRecord.
  - Immediate Governance Action: Trigger Scope Inflation Protection; refuse global authorization; restrict authorized scope strictly to tested boundary.
  - Gate 8 Consequence: Global authorization REJECTED; scoped authorization permitted ONLY for tested subset.
  - Retest / Revalidation Rule: Submit separate handoff packages and validation suites for adjacent untested gear.
  - Attribution Category: MIGRATION_MAPPING.
  - Remand Path: Phase 1C.5g Migration Planning.


36. THE TWENTY ARCHITECTURAL STRESS SCENARIOS

Phase 1C.5h demonstrates robust, non-bypassable governance through twenty architectural walkthroughs corresponding to the scenarios established in Design Mandate v0.1d. These walkthroughs model the architecture's structural response to hypothetical conditions without presenting fabricated empirical observations:

Scenario 01: Architecture Passes Structural Checks but Sounds Worse
  - Context / Hypothetical Condition: A migrated high-gain rhythm candidate passes structural schema validation and parameter bound checks, but listening evaluations report that the tone is thin, harsh, and deficient in body compared to legacy baseline.
  - Applicable Test Profile(s): PROFILE-01 (Structural/Schema), PROFILE-04 (Objective Acoustic), PROFILE-06 (Perceptual Listening).
  - Evidence Generated: ObjectiveEvidenceRecord (Schema) indicates passing syntax; PerceptualEvaluationRecord indicates failure across tonal balance and warmth dimensions; EvidenceConflictRecord is created.
  - Governing Record(s): ValidationExecutionRecord, ObjectiveEvidenceRecord, PerceptualEvaluationRecord, EvidenceConflictRecord, ValidationOutcomeRecord.
  - Failure Attribution: REASONING (upstream reasoning generated requirements that over-attenuated low-frequency warmth).
  - Validation Outcome: FAIL. Invariant 1 and Invariant 7 govern: structural compliance cannot override acoustic failure.
  - Gate 8 Consequence: Architectural decision path routes to authorization denial or conditional remand; Gate 8 consideration blocked pending resolution of acoustic defects.
  - Remand / Retest Path: DefectRemandPackage routed to Upstream Reasoning Governance (Phases 1C.5a–e).
  - Preserved Uncertainty: None; the acoustic defect is verified by panel consensus.

Scenario 02: Candidate Sounds Better than Legacy but Differs Significantly
  - Context / Hypothetical Condition: A candidate lead preset diverges significantly in spectral response from legacy PATH-PROD, but listening evaluations judge the candidate to be acoustically superior, finding that legacy suffered from harsh resonance.
  - Applicable Test Profile(s): PROFILE-04 (Objective Acoustic), PROFILE-06 (Perceptual Listening), PROFILE-09 (Regression Profile).
  - Evidence Generated: Objective differential detected; PerceptualEvaluationRecord confirms superior musical warmth and absence of fatigue; RegressionAssessmentRecord classifies divergence as INTENTIONAL_ARCHITECTURAL_CORRECTION.
  - Governing Record(s): RegressionAssessmentRecord, PerceptualEvaluationRecord, ValidationOutcomeRecord.
  - Failure Attribution: None (improves upon legacy baseline).
  - Validation Outcome: PASS for the evaluated scope. Invariant 5 and Axiom 2 govern: intentional correction of legacy flaws is not a regression.
  - Gate 8 Consequence: Architectural decision path routes to Gate 8 consideration for tested scope if all other gates and readiness criteria are satisfied.
  - Remand / Retest Path: None required.
  - Preserved Uncertainty: User expectation adjustments documented in version notes.

Scenario 03: Export Fails Despite Good Acoustic Reasoning
  - Context / Hypothetical Condition: Cognitive sound engineering reasoning across Stages 01–11 establishes a platform-neutral engineering decision and prediction for the required tone behavior. Stage 12 (Semantic Tone Design Handoff / Platform Translation) translates that semantic tone design into target-platform gear, parameters, microphone configuration, and preset structure, but the serialized preset fails to load because Stage 12 emits an invalid target-platform parameter identifier.
  - Applicable Test Profile(s): PROFILE-03 (Target-Platform Export Profile).
  - Evidence Generated: ObjectiveEvidenceRecord (Export) indicates failure due to platform load rejection; ValidationFailureRecord generated.
  - Governing Record(s): ObjectiveEvidenceRecord, ValidationFailureRecord, ValidationOutcomeRecord.
  - Failure Attribution: PLATFORM_TRANSLATION (Stage 12 parameter mapping defect).
  - Validation Outcome: FAIL. Invariant 1 governs: reasoning elegance cannot excuse platform export failure.
  - Gate 8 Consequence: Architectural decision path identifies Hard Blocker; routes to authorization denial and defect remand pending verified platform translation fix.
  - Remand / Retest Path: DefectRemandPackage routed to Stage 12 Platform Translation Governance.
  - Preserved Uncertainty: None.

Scenario 04: Spectra Similar but Perceptually Different
  - Context / Hypothetical Condition: Candidate and legacy clean presets produce closely aligned spectral magnitude curves, but performing guitarists report that the candidate feels stiff and dynamic response is compressed under pick attack.
  - Applicable Test Profile(s): PROFILE-04 (Objective Acoustic), PROFILE-05 (Dynamic Response), PROFILE-07 (Playability).
  - Evidence Generated: Objective spectral analysis indicates match; dynamic crest factor and attack envelope tests indicate transient suppression; PerceptualEvaluationRecord reports touch insensitivity.
  - Governing Record(s): ObjectiveEvidenceRecord, PerceptualEvaluationRecord, EvidenceConflictRecord, ValidationOutcomeRecord.
  - Failure Attribution: REASONING (compression-related constraints and trade-offs were evaluated in Stage 10, but the Stage 11 Engineering Decision & Prediction committed to an excessively compressed response that degraded dynamic feel).
  - Validation Outcome: FAIL. Invariant 2 governs: scalar or spectral similarity cannot mask dynamic envelope failure.
  - Gate 8 Consequence: Architectural decision path routes to authorization denial and remand to upstream reasoning pending dynamic resolution.
  - Remand / Retest Path: DefectRemandPackage routed to Upstream Reasoning (Phase 1C.5d).
  - Preserved Uncertainty: None.

Scenario 05: Reference Mismatch Caused by Player / Performance
  - Context / Hypothetical Condition: A candidate blues tone fails an objective match against an approved commercial reference stem. Forensic investigation reveals the fixture DI stem was captured with mismatched pickup types and playing technique relative to the reference stem.
  - Applicable Test Profile(s): PROFILE-04 (Objective Acoustic), PROFILE-06 (Perceptual Listening).
  - Evidence Generated: Objective match fails; evaluator notes identify pickup impedance and dynamic playing mismatch in the test fixture.
  - Governing Record(s): ValidationFixtureRecord, ValidationFailureRecord, ValidationReadinessAssessmentRecord.
  - Failure Attribution: TEST_FIXTURE (inappropriate fixture applied to validation case).
  - Validation Outcome: BLOCKED_NOT_EVALUABLE.
  - Gate 8 Consequence: Architectural decision path suspends evaluation / holds Gate 8 in BLOCKED state pending application of a calibrated fixture matching declared instrument scope.
  - Remand / Retest Path: Remanded to Golden Fixture Curation Team to associate calibrated reference stem.
  - Preserved Uncertainty: Candidate tone quality under appropriate pickups remains unverified.

Scenario 06: High-Gain Passes but Clean Fails
  - Context / Hypothetical Condition: A migration handoff package covering an amplifier family passes all objective and perceptual tests on high-gain channels, but clean channel heuristics exhibit crossover distortion.
  - Applicable Test Profile(s): Full test battery across clean and high-gain cases.
  - Evidence Generated: High-gain cases produce PASS; clean cases produce FAIL with ValidationFailureRecord.
  - Governing Record(s): ValidationPackage, ValidationOutcomeRecord, AdoptionDecisionRecord.
  - Failure Attribution: MIGRATION_MAPPING (clean bias parameter remapping defect).
  - Validation Outcome: CONDITIONAL (Clean blocked; High-Gain passed).
  - Gate 8 Consequence: Architectural decision path restricts Gate 8 consideration strictly to high-gain scope if partitioned; routes clean scope to authorization denial and remand.
  - Remand / Retest Path: DefectRemandPackage for clean channel routed to Phase 1C.5g.
  - Preserved Uncertainty: None; scopes are explicitly segregated.

Scenario 07: Reviewers Disagree
  - Context / Hypothetical Condition: A candidate modern metal rhythm tone is evaluated by an expert listening panel, resulting in split assessments regarding whether the mid-frequency scoop is aesthetically appropriate or excessive for dense mixes.
  - Applicable Test Profile(s): PROFILE-06 (Perceptual Listening), PROFILE-08 (Mix Context).
  - Evidence Generated: PerceptualEvaluationRecords record divergent assessments; EvidenceConflictRecord is created.
  - Governing Record(s): PerceptualEvaluationRecord, EvidenceConflictRecord, ValidationUncertaintyRecord, ValidationOutcomeRecord.
  - Failure Attribution: REASONING (aesthetic trade-off between modern scoop and mix presence).
  - Validation Outcome: CONDITIONAL / REQUIRES FOLLOW-UP. Averaging scores is prohibited.
  - Gate 8 Consequence: Architectural decision path blocks full authorization; routes to bounded conditional authorization evaluation or additional discriminating listening.
  - Remand / Retest Path: Formulated as candidate lesson remanded into inherited Phase 1C.5e/f quarantine and escalated to Knowledge Governance for review on genre-specific equalization rules.
  - Preserved Uncertainty: Aesthetic variance across listener demographics logged in ValidationUncertaintyRecord.

Scenario 08: Metrics Disagree with Listening
  - Context / Hypothetical Condition: Objective harmonic analysis shows harmonic distortion exceeding nominal clean-profile thresholds on an edge-of-breakup blues preset, but listening panels enthusiastically praise the musical warmth and touch sensitivity.
  - Applicable Test Profile(s): PROFILE-04 (Objective Acoustic), PROFILE-06 (Perceptual Listening), PROFILE-07 (Playability).
  - Evidence Generated: ObjectiveEvidenceRecord indicates distortion threshold breach; harmonic order analysis reveals distortion is predominantly even-order; PerceptualEvaluationRecord reports passing musicality; EvidenceConflictRecord created.
  - Governing Record(s): ObjectiveEvidenceRecord, PerceptualEvaluationRecord, EvidenceConflictRecord, ValidationOutcomeRecord.
  - Failure Attribution: None (intentional musical trade-off).
  - Validation Outcome: Routes to governed review to determine whether harmonic content represents intentional musical trade-off under applicable tolerance profile or requires tolerance profile review.
  - Gate 8 Consequence: Architectural decision path permits Gate 8 consideration only if governed review establishes intentional musicality without unresolved regression.
  - Remand / Retest Path: None.
  - Preserved Uncertainty: None.

Scenario 09: Edge-Case Crashes New Path but Legacy Survives
  - Context / Hypothetical Condition: An adversarial test feeds an audio stem with extreme digital clipping and DC offset into both paths. Legacy PATH-PROD clamps the signal stably; candidate PATH-SHADOW encounters an unhandled exception in internal DSP math and halts execution.
  - Applicable Test Profile(s): PROFILE-12 (Adversarial / Edge-Case Profile).
  - Evidence Generated: ValidationExecutionRecord indicates execution aborted due to unhandled exception; ValidationFailureRecord generated.
  - Governing Record(s): ValidationExecutionRecord, ValidationFailureRecord, ValidationOutcomeRecord.
  - Failure Attribution: RUNTIME_IMPLEMENTATION (missing boundary sanitization in DSP intake).
  - Validation Outcome: FAIL. Invariant 8 governs: adversarial edge cases must be handled gracefully.
  - Gate 8 Consequence: Architectural decision path identifies Hard Blocker; routes to authorization denial and remand pending runtime exception handling fix.
  - Remand / Retest Path: DefectRemandPackage routed to Runtime Software Engineering.
  - Preserved Uncertainty: None.

Scenario 10: Legacy Fails and Candidate Succeeds
  - Context / Hypothetical Condition: A complex multi-mic cabinet preset in legacy PATH-PROD exhibits severe comb-filtering and phase cancellation due to static delay offsets. Candidate PATH-SHADOW applies causal diagnosis to align mic distances, producing rich, phase-coherent output.
  - Applicable Test Profile(s): PROFILE-04 (Phase Coherence), PROFILE-06 (Perceptual Listening), PROFILE-09 (Regression Profile).
  - Evidence Generated: Legacy differential indicates phase cancellation; candidate indicates phase coherence and expert listening approval; RegressionAssessmentRecord classifies result as INTENTIONAL_ARCHITECTURAL_CORRECTION.
  - Governing Record(s): RegressionAssessmentRecord, PerceptualEvaluationRecord, ValidationOutcomeRecord.
  - Failure Attribution: None.
  - Validation Outcome: PASS.
  - Gate 8 Consequence: Architectural decision path routes to Gate 8 eligibility for tested scope where improvement over legacy baseline is verified.
  - Remand / Retest Path: None.
  - Preserved Uncertainty: None.

Scenario 11: Rollback Simulation Fails
  - Context / Hypothetical Condition: A candidate package passes all objective and perceptual tests. During pre-cutover rollback verification under simulated failure, automated reversion fails to restore PATH-PROD within the declared operational recovery objective (recovery_objective_ref).
  - Applicable Test Profile(s): PROFILE-11 (Rollback Profile).
  - Evidence Generated: ObjectiveEvidenceRecord (Rollback) indicates failure to meet recovery objective; ValidationFailureRecord generated.
  - Governing Record(s): ObjectiveEvidenceRecord, ValidationFailureRecord, ProductionReadinessAssessmentRecord.
  - Failure Attribution: OPERATIONAL_INFRASTRUCTURE or MIGRATION_MAPPING.
  - Validation Outcome: FAIL. Invariant 9 governs: reversibility is mandatory for Gate 8 consideration.
  - Gate 8 Consequence: Architectural decision path identifies Hard Blocker under Invariant 9; routes to authorization denial pending verified deterministic reversibility.

37. TECHNOLOGY NEUTRALITY REQUIREMENTS

37.1 Architecture Over Implementation
This architectural specification establishes the data requirements, governing invariants, evaluation workflows, and decision rules of Phase 1C.5h while remaining strictly technology-neutral.
It does NOT mandate:
  - any specific programming language (TypeScript, Python, C++, Rust);
  - any specific database engine (PostgreSQL, SQLite, DynamoDB, MongoDB);
  - any specific test runner or CI/CD system (Jest, PyTest, GitHub Actions, Jenkins);
  - any specific DSP library, audio engine host, or DAW environment;
  - any specific cloud vendor, serverless platform, or container runtime;
  - any specific cryptographic hashing or digital signature algorithm (integrity requirements are technology-neutral, specifying verifiable tamper-evident properties rather than mandating specific hashing algorithms, signature schemes, or encryption standards);
  - any specific machine-learning model provider or LLM hosting infrastructure.

37.2 Status of Target-Platform References (AmpliTube 5)
References to IK Multimedia AmpliTube 5 (AT5), .at5p preset structures, and AT5 parameter manifests are retained throughout this specification solely because AmpliTube 5 is the active commercial target platform of Tone Translator.
These references are illustrative of target-platform verification requirements and do NOT bind future Tone Translator architectures to AmpliTube 5 if additional target platforms are supported downstream.

41. ARCHITECTURAL SELF-CHALLENGE

Before final status declaration, the architecture is subjected to fifteen mandatory boundary self-challenge questions:

1. Can a structurally correct system still fail acoustically?
   - Answer: YES. Modeled in Section 4.1 (Axiom 1) and Stress Scenario 01. Schema conformance only proves data formatting; empirical listening can and will reject structurally valid tones that sound unmusical.

2. Can a sonically good result still fail deterministic / platform validation?
   - Answer: YES. Modeled in Section 15.1 and Stress Scenario 03. A tone that sounds magnificent in simulation but crashes the platform parser is a Hard Blocker and cannot cut over.

3. Can legacy be wrong?
   - Answer: YES. Modeled in Section 4.2 (Axiom 2) and Stress Scenario 02. Legacy heuristics with harsh resonances or phase bugs are operational baselines, not truth. Divergence fixing them is an Intentional Architectural Correction.

4. Can human evaluators disagree?
   - Answer: YES. Modeled in Section 16.3, Section 22.1, and Stress Scenario 07. Disagreements are preserved in EvidenceConflictRecord; averaging scores is strictly prohibited.

5. Can metrics disagree with listening?
   - Answer: YES. Modeled in Section 15.2, Section 16.1, and Stress Scenario 08. Musical harmonic distortion may violate raw thresholds but be praised by listeners; governed trade-off resolution is supported.

6. Can a PASS become stale?
   - Answer: YES. Modeled in Section 24.1, Section 24.2, and Stress Scenarios 12, 13, 16. Upstream prompt changes, platform patches, or fixture recalibrations trigger RevalidationTriggers that invalidate prior PASS outcomes.

7. Can Gate 8 be scoped?
   - Answer: YES. Modeled in Section 4.6, Section 9, Section 26.4, and Stress Scenario 06. Authorization attaches strictly to tested gear families and capabilities; adjacent untested gear is excluded.

8. Can Gate 8 be revoked?
   - Answer: YES. Modeled in Section 28, Section 39 Table 14, and Stress Scenario 19. Gate8ChangeRecord allows suspension, restriction, or permanent withdrawal if real-world anomalies arise.

9. Can evidence remain unresolved?
   - Answer: YES. Modeled in Section 4.4 (Axiom 4), Section 20.2, and Section 22.3. UNRESOLVED_DIVERGENCE and ValidationUncertaintyRecord ensure doubt survives without forced false binary certainty.

10. Can validation stop without forcing PASS / FAIL?
    - Answer: YES. Modeled in Section 23.2. BLOCKED_NOT_EVALUABLE and CONDITIONAL outcome classes allow testing to halt or proceed with declared limitations when evidence is incomplete.

11. Can production remain protected while validation fails?
    - Answer: YES. Modeled in Section 6.2, Section 31, and Stress Scenario 09. PATH-PROD executes in complete operational isolation from test harnesses; shadow crashes cannot impact live musicians.

12. Can Phase 1C.5h detect a migration problem without assigning a migration disposition?
    - Answer: YES. Modeled in Section 5.2 and Section 29.2. Phase 1C.5h attributes failures to MIGRATION_MAPPING and remands evidence to Phase 1C.5g; Phase 1C.5h NEVER assigns RETAIN, REMAP, or QUARANTINE.

13. Can Phase 1C.5h suspend authorization without directly executing rollback?
    - Answer: YES. Modeled in Section 4.5, Section 5.2, and Section 28. Phase 1C.5h issues Gate8ChangeRecord modifying authorization governance state; Phase 1C.5g determines and executes the operational rollback action.

14. Can candidate lessons remain quarantined?
    - Answer: YES. Modeled in Section 30. Candidate lessons identified during Phase 1C.5h validation are remanded into inherited Candidate Lesson quarantine under Phase 1C.5e/f governance; Phase 1C.5h cannot unilaterally promote them.

15. Can test evidence be invalidated by fixture, platform, or version change?
    - Answer: YES. Modeled in Section 18.4, Section 24.2, and Stress Scenarios 12, 13, and 16. Recalibrations, vendor updates, or knowledge base expansions generate RevalidationTriggers that mark prior evidence STALE.
