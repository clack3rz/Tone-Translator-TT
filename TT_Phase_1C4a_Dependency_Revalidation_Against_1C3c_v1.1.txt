3. REASON FOR DEPENDENCY REVALIDATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

3.1 SEQUENCING ANOMALY
In the project lifecycle, Phase 1C.4a-R v1.1 was prepared and frozen based on Phase 1C.3c v1.
However, Phase 1C.3c v1 contained several critical defects that required a corrective
re-architecture (Phase 1C.3c-R v1.1), including:
  1. An artificial two-tier "Junior vs Professional" maturity benchmark that improperly
     reduced 9 core domains to Target L4 instead of the authoritative Phase 1C.3a L5 target.
  2. Aggregation of independent competency dimensions into pseudo-precise composite scores.
  3. Presentation of the seven-step educational cycle in language that could be mistaken
     for runtime reasoning architecture.
  4. Dogmatic single "root-cause" assertions rather than evidence-supported competing
     working hypotheses in underdetermined systems.
  5. Unbounded technical claims regarding 0 VU digital alignment, harmonic odd/even
     polarities, negative-feedback presence circuits, and microphone delay calculations.
  6. Unclear treatment of Current TT, which was previously conflated with authoritative
     treatises rather than deconstructed legacy source material.

3.2 REVALIDATION MANDATE
Because Phase 1C.4a forms the foundational data architecture for the entire Tone Translator
Knowledge System, it is critical to confirm that Phase 1C.4a-R v1.1:
  - Did not adopt or bake in the defects of Phase 1C.3c v1.
  - Fully supports the rigorous standards of the frozen Phase 1C.3c-R v1.1.
  - Can cleanly ingest the curriculum requirements without structural refactoring.
  - Accurately respects the boundary between knowledge storage and runtime reasoning.

6. KNOWLEDGE / EVIDENCE / REASONING BOUNDARY REVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

6.1 KNOWLEDGE VS CAPABILITY (EDUCATION VS TOOLING)
Phase 1C.3c-R Section 4 and Section 21 strictly distinguish between:
  - Knowledge / Education: Understanding acoustic physics, electrical circuits, and
    psychoacoustic curves.
  - Runtime Evidence & Tooling: External software services that objectively measure
    audio signals (FFT centroid, crest factor, correlation, attack slopes) and
    execute closed-loop delta comparisons.

Phase 1C.4a-R Section 4.1 establishes an identical tri-partite architectural division:
  1. Sound Engineering Knowledge Library: Stores durable physical principles, boundaries,
     and causal models.
  2. Runtime Evidence & DSP Tooling: Extracts objective signal metrics.
  3. AI Sound Engineer Reasoning Engine: Performs cognitive diagnosis and trade-off evaluation.
Phase 1C.4a does not pretend that knowledge provides missing DSP perception, closed-loop
audio comparison, or stateful checkpoint lineage.

6.2 KNOWLEDGE VS REASONING (SCAFFOLD VS RUNTIME ENGINE)
Phase 1C.3c-R Section 1.3 and Section 5 explicitly classify the seven-step engineering
method (Evidence -> Hypothesis -> Alternatives -> Trade-offs -> Decision -> Predict ->
Verify) as an EDUCATIONAL AND COMPETENCY-EVALUATION SCAFFOLD ONLY, not the runtime
reasoning architecture (which belongs to Phase 1C.5).

Phase 1C.4a-R Section 3 (Principle 1) and Section 4.3 enforce that:
  "Knowledge Informs Reasoning — It Never Replaces It. Knowledge provides principles,
   causal models, boundaries, and trade-offs. The cognitive agent must diagnose,
   evaluate competing causes, and design solutions. Knowledge never prescribes
   fixed symptom-to-fix recipes."
Phase 1C.4a-R does not encode the future runtime reasoning engine, execution graph,
or inference loop into the Knowledge Library. It provides the structured declarative
data that the future Phase 1C.5 reasoning engine will query.

VERDICT: FULLY COMPATIBLE (NO CONFLICT).

7. EPISTEMIC & PROVENANCE COMPATIBILITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

7.1 MULTI-FACTOR EVIDENCE ASSESSMENT
Phase 1C.3c-R Section 20 mandates a multi-factor evidence assessment framework where
source category alone never determines absolute truth. Factors include source rigor,
methodological validity, empirical reproducibility, peer review, domain jurisdiction,
and operational context.

Phase 1C.4a-R Sections 6, 8, 9, and 10 provide an exact architectural match:
  - Source Rigor Tier: Five-tier continuum (`R1_PHYSICAL_LAW` to `R5_PRACTITIONER_ACCOUNT`).
  - Domain Jurisdiction: Three-tier classification (`J_CORE`, `J_APPLIED`, `J_PERIPHERAL`)
    evaluated on a per-domain basis.
  - Elimination of Scalar Authority: Section 9.2 strictly prohibits collapsing rigor and
    jurisdiction into a composite score (e.g. "8.7/10"). Queries filter and sort along
    orthogonal dimensions.
  - Epistemic Validation: Replaces arbitrary confidence floats (e.g. 0.92) with five
    structured qualitative factors:
      * `evidence_strength`: THEORETICAL_PROOF, SYSTEMATIC_EMPIRICAL, CORROBORATED_PRACTICE, ANECDOTAL
      * `consensus_state`: ESTABLISHED_CONSENSUS, PREDOMINANT, DEBATED, OUTLIER
      * `replication_state`: INDEPENDENTLY_REPLICATED, EXPERIMENTALLY_VERIFIED, OBSERVATIONAL_ONLY
      * `conflict_status`: UNCONTESTED, ACTIVE_COMPETING_CLAIM
      * `boundary_certainty`: TIGHTLY_BOUNDED, PARTIAL_ENVELOPE, UNEXPLORED_LIMITS

7.2 PRESERVATION OF UNCERTAINTY & UNDERDETERMINATION
Phase 1C.3c-R emphasizes that audio systems are frequently underdetermined (multiple
circuit or acoustic faults can produce identical acoustic symptoms).
Phase 1C.4a-R directly supports underdetermined reasoning by:
  - Forbidding 1:1 symptom-to-cause mappings.
  - Mandating `OperationalBoundary.confounding_variables` to list phenomena that mimic
    the symptom.
  - Mandating `ConflictRecord` to store coexisting competing explanations without forced
    merging or premature resolution.

VERDICT: FULLY COMPATIBLE (NO CONFLICT).

8. TECHNICAL KNOWLEDGE BOUNDARY REVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Phase 1C.3c-R corrected several technical overclaims and over-generalizations present in
earlier curriculum drafts. The review confirmed that Phase 1C.4a-R v1.1 correctly accommodates
these bounded engineering realities:

1. Digital 0 VU Calibration:
   - 1C.3c-R Finding: 0 VU calibration is facility- and standard-dependent (-18 dBFS AES/EBU,
     -20 dBFS SMPTE, -14 dBFS legacy/broadcast).
   - 1C.4a-R Support: Section 12 establishes `Type 3: CALIBRATION_STANDARD`, explicitly
     treating calibration levels as reference alignments with specified operating contexts
     rather than universal immutable physical laws.

2. Harmonic Spectra Non-Triviality:
   - 1C.3c-R Finding: Harmonics cannot be simplified to "even = warm, odd = harsh." Perceived
     harshness depends on harmonic order, spectral envelope, masking thresholds, and decay.
   - 1C.4a-R Support: `CausalModel` models harmonic series as multi-order vectors across
     the frequency domain (`HARMONIC_SERIES`) with specific observable metrics (e.g.
     "High-Order Odd Harmonic Ratio") rather than simplistic binary warmth labels.

3. Presence / Negative-Feedback Circuitry:
   - 1C.3c-R Finding: Presence circuits function by altering global negative feedback in the
     power amplifier; they do not function when negative feedback is absent (e.g. cathode-biased
     Class A / VOX AC30 topologies).
   - 1C.4a-R Support: `OperationalBoundary.prerequisites` mandates listing circuit prerequisites
     (e.g. "Global negative feedback loop must be present in power stage topology").

4. Microphone Delay Calculations:
   - 1C.3c-R Finding: Acoustic delay calculations based on speed of sound (e.g. 1 ms per foot)
     are approximate, temperature-dependent, and affected by room boundaries.
   - 1C.4a-R Support: `KnowledgeClaim.formal_derivation_or_math` enforces declarative physical
     formulas ($c = 331.3 \sqrt{1 + T/273.15}$), while `OperationalBoundary` specifies
     atmospheric and boundary constraints.

5. Proximity Effect Acoustics:
   - 1C.3c-R Finding: Proximity effect depends strictly on pressure-gradient transducer
     acoustics and distance to spherical wavefronts; pure pressure (true omni) mics have zero
     proximity effect.
   - 1C.4a-R Support: Section 21 Example A explicitly models this exact principle, its
     formal mathematical derivation, its near-field spherical envelope, and its known
     exception (pure pressure transducers).

6. Parsimony as Heuristic, Not Physical Law:
   - 1C.3c-R Finding: Parsimony (Occam's razor) is a cognitive heuristic for prioritizing
     hypotheses, not a physical law that guarantees a single root cause.
   - 1C.4a-R Support: Classified under Axis 2 as `PROFESSIONAL_CONVENTION` or reasoning
     scaffold, barred from Axis 1 `PHYSICAL_LAW`.

VERDICT: FULLY COMPATIBLE (NO CONFLICT).

9. CURRENT TT LEGACY KNOWLEDGE REVALIDATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

9.1 CURRICULUM REQUIREMENT (PHASE 1C.3c-R)
Phase 1C.3c-R Section 3.3, Section 20, and Section 24 explicitly treat Current TT as:
    "LEGACY MATERIAL TO BE DECONSTRUCTED AND RE-VALIDATED."
Current TT is an unvalidated repository of heuristics, keyword overrides, hardcoded

authoritative sound engineering knowledge.

9.2 EVALUATION OF 1C.4a-R SECTION 17.1
In Phase 1C.4a-R v1.1 Section 17.1, the document states:
    "Knowledge embedded in Current TT's codebase is treated as source material with
     source_rigor_tier: R4_PROFESSIONAL_TREATISE and jurisdiction: J_APPLIED.
     It is NOT authoritative until it passes full deconstruction and governance."

Architectural Evaluation:
  - The four-stage deconstruction pipeline in Section 17.2 (Separation -> Physical
    Formulation -> Boundary Documentation -> Governance Review) is completely sound.
  - However, labeling Current TT itself as `R4_PROFESSIONAL_TREATISE` is an epistemic
    overclaim. Current TT is a software codebase containing unverified heuristics, not
    a published, peer-reviewed professional treatise (such as Huber, Rumsey, or Blencowe).
  - Assigning R4 to Current TT risks inflating the baseline authority of unprovenanced
    heuristics before their underlying literature roots are identified.

9.3 RESOLUTION: BOUNDED REVALIDATION ADDENDUM
In accordance with the review instruction and smallest-change principle:
  - An architectural rewrite of Phase 1C.4a is NOT required.
  - A formal Bounded Revalidation Addendum is established (Section 15, Addendum 1):
    Current TT codebase is formally designated as:
        `source_rigor_tier: UNASSESSED_LEGACY_SOURCE`
    When an atomic claim is extracted from Current TT:
      a) If its underlying physical principle is traced to authoritative primary
         literature (e.g. Blencowe, AES, Beranek), the resulting `ClaimAttribution`
         cites that primary source and receives the primary source's true Rigor Tier
         (R1 to R4). Current TT is recorded solely as the historical migration origin.
      b) If no primary literature source can be verified, the claim remains at
         `UNASSESSED_LEGACY_SOURCE` (or R5_PRACTITIONER_ACCOUNT if corroboration exists)
         and CANNOT be promoted to `VALIDATED_ACTIVE` without empirical validation.

VERDICT: COMPATIBLE WITH BOUNDED ADDENDUM (WORDING / CLARIFICATION).

13. FROZEN 1C.4a DESIGN DECISION REVALIDATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

All twelve frozen design decisions from Phase 1C.4a-R v1.1 were re-evaluated against
Phase 1C.3c-R v1.1:

Decision 1: Canonical knowledge stored as human-readable, Git-tracked JSON/YAML.
  - Status: UNCHANGED
  - Reason: Git-tracked declarative files provide transparent diffability, auditability,
    and version control, satisfying curriculum provenance requirements.

Decision 2: Runtime indexes are generated/disposable derivatives.
  - Status: UNCHANGED
  - Reason: Preserves strict decoupling between immutable canonical source knowledge
    and optimized runtime search representations.

Decision 3: Runtime retrieval technology remains deferred to Phase 1C.4c.
  - Status: UNCHANGED
  - Reason: Retrieval algorithms (dense vector vs sparse BM25 vs relational graph)
    depend on knowledge volume and retrieval benchmarks defined in 1C.4c.

Decision 4: Review governance is risk- and evidence-dependent.
  - Status: UNCHANGED
  - Reason: The three-tier model (Tier A Dual Senior + Triad, Tier B Single Senior,
    Tier C Single Technical) balances high assurance for physics with operational
    efficiency for contextual examples.

Decision 5: Current TT extraction uses atomic claim/model decomposition.
  - Status: UNCHANGED (WITH ADDENDUM 1)
  - Reason: Atomic decomposition is essential to separate physical acoustics from
    heuristic code rules. Addendum 1 clarifies that Current TT is an unassessed legacy
    source whose claims inherit primary literature rigor.

Decision 6: Mathematical knowledge is declarative.
  - Status: UNCHANGED
  - Reason: Declarative LaTeX and AST representations ensure human auditability, cross-platform
    stability, and absence of hidden execution side-effects.

Decision 7: Executable mathematical evaluation remains outside canonical knowledge.
  - Status: UNCHANGED
  - Reason: Dynamic formula execution belongs to deterministic validation services or
    runtime evidence tooling, not static knowledge storage.

Decision 8: Knowledge architecture uses normalized entity model.
  - Status: UNCHANGED (WITH ADDENDUM 2)
  - Reason: The 8-entity model (SourceDocument, ClaimAttribution, KnowledgeClaim,
    CausalModel, OperationalBoundary, ConflictRecord, ReviewRecord, CandidateLesson)
    cleanly decouples provenance, boundaries, governance, and runtime quarantine.
    Addendum 2 clarifies that session-level run reviews reside in downstream phases.

Decision 9: Knowledge classification remains multi-axis rather than mutually exclusive.
  - Status: UNCHANGED
  - Reason: The three orthogonal axes (Epistemic Basis, Knowledge Role, Physical Scope)
    prevent artificial classification bottlenecks.

Decision 10: Device-specific physical knowledge is allowed when bounded.
  - Status: UNCHANGED
  - Reason: Allows legitimate engineering facts (e.g. Celestion V30 resonance) via
    `DeviceSpecificityContext` without universalizing them into general acoustic laws.

Decision 11: Platform implementation leakage is prohibited.
  - Status: UNCHANGED
  - Reason: Software model IDs, GUIDs, XML paths, and GUI coordinates remain strictly
    firewalled from general sound engineering knowledge.

Decision 12: Experiential learning requires evidence sufficiency and review before promotion.
  - Status: UNCHANGED
  - Reason: The multi-factor Evidence-Sufficiency Dossier prevents noisy runtime outcomes
    from polluting canonical knowledge.
