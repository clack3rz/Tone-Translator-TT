1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1.1 ARCHITECTURAL PURPOSE & STATUS
Phase 1C.4b defines the authoritative source-selection, acquisition, extraction,
provenance, normalisation, and ingestion architecture for the Tone Translator (TT)
Professional Sound Engineering Knowledge System.

THIS PHASE DEFINES HOW KNOWLEDGE ENTERS THE SYSTEM.

It establishes the rigorous engineering pipeline that transforms raw external
documents, research literature, technical standards, manufacturer specifications,
and legacy materials into verifiable, platform-independent, atomic candidate
engineering knowledge units governed by the frozen Phase 1C.4a entity model.

Phase 1C.4b operates under strict architectural discipline:
    SOURCE VOLUME ≠ KNOWLEDGE QUALITY
    SISO: POOR SOURCE MATERIAL, POOR EXTRACTION, OR POOR PROVENANCE
    CONTAMINATES EVERY DOWNSTREAM LAYER REGARDLESS OF AGENT CAPABILITY.

1.2 SCOPE BOUNDARIES & INTEGRITY RULES
This report is a pure architecture and specification document:
  - NO external web research, literature scraping, or text harvesting is performed.
  - NO production knowledge library collections or vector databases are populated.
  - NO production code, prompt templates, tests, or Firestore rules are modified.
  - NO runtime retrieval mechanisms (Phase 1C.4c) or conflict adjudication tools (Phase 1C.4d) are implemented.
  - NO Current TT knowledge migration (Phase 1C.4e) is executed.
  - NO runtime engineering reasoning loops (Phase 1C.5) or competency benchmark runners (Phase 1C.6) are created.

1.3 CORE ACQUISITION PIPELINE
The governing architecture enforces a unidirectional eleven-stage ingestion pipeline:
    SOURCE
      ↓
    REGISTER
      ↓
    ACQUIRE
      ↓
    EXTRACT
      ↓
    DECOMPOSE
      ↓
    NORMALISE
      ↓
    ATTRIBUTE
      ↓
    EVIDENCE ASSESS
      ↓
    CONFLICT / UNCERTAINTY IDENTIFY
      ↓
    REVIEW
      ↓
    ACCEPT / QUARANTINE / REJECT
      ↓
    PROMOTION ELIGIBILITY

Canonical promotion to `VALIDATED_ACTIVE` status is strictly gated by human expert
review and adversarial validation under the frozen Phase 1C.4a governance tiers.

4. ACQUISITION DESIGN PRINCIPLES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The acquisition architecture is governed by twenty inviolable design principles:

1. Knowledge Informs Reasoning — It Never Replaces It:
   Acquisition extracts physical laws, circuit relationships, boundaries, and trade-offs.
   It never extracts or generates prescriptive symptom-to-solution recipes.
2. Retrieval Is Not Acquisition:
   Acquisition is an offline, high-assurance vetting pipeline; retrieval is runtime
   indexing. Knowledge cannot be retrieved until it is acquired, attributed, and approved.
3. Documents Are Not Knowledge:
   A textbook or paper is a physical artifact. Knowledge consists of normalized,
   atomic, verifiable propositions extracted from that artifact.
4. Extraction Is Not Validation:
   Accurately parsing a statement from a document verifies only what the author wrote,
   not whether the statement is physically true or universally applicable.
5. Repetition Is Not Corroboration:
   Multiple sources repeating an unverified assertion trace to a common ancestor.
   True corroboration requires independent methodology and diverse measurement conditions.
6. Popularity Is Not Authority:
   Studio folklore, internet forum consensus, and high YouTube view counts do not
   transform acoustic myths into engineering principles.
7. Manufacturer Origin Is Neither Truth Nor Disqualification:
   Datasheets, schematics, and measured responses provide invaluable physical facts,
   but must be rigorously firewalled from marketing prose and subjective claims.
8. Practitioner Experience Is Evidence, Not Physical Law:
   Documented master engineer practices provide crucial empirical insights and context,
   but must be bounded as contextual practices or empirical tendencies, not invariant laws.
9. AI Is an Extraction Agent, Not an Epistemic Source:
   LLMs have no autonomous authority. Model-generated assertions not grounded in the
   registered source are classified as unsupported additions and barred from canonical entry.
10. Current TT Is Legacy Source Material, Not Authority:
    Existing codebase rules enter as `UNASSESSED_LEGACY_SOURCE`. They must be deconstructed
    into first-principles models and validated against authoritative literature.
11. Runtime Outcomes Do Not Automatically Become Global Knowledge:
    Successful DSP presets or user feedback remain quarantined in session records. Only
    recurrent, controlled, plausibility-verified observations become `CandidateLesson` units.
12. Device-Specific Physical Facts Are Legitimate Knowledge:
    Measured physical properties of real commercial hardware (e.g. speaker cone resonance)
    are valid sound engineering facts when bound to a `DeviceSpecificityContext`.
13. Platform Implementation Details Are Contamination:
    Software GUIDs, XML parameter paths, DAW slot conventions, and exporter coordinates
    are strictly prohibited from the Knowledge Library.
14. Uncertainty Must Be Preserved:
    Approximations, measurement tolerances, and exploratory limits must be explicitly
    captured in `EpistemicValidation` and `OperationalBoundary` records.
15. Credible Conflicts Must Not Be Silently Resolved:
    Divergent engineering approaches or boundary disagreements between authoritative sources
    must be preserved in explicit `ConflictRecord` entities.
16. Provenance Must Survive Every Transformation:
    Every atomic claim, causal link, and boundary envelope must retain an unbroken link
    to its exact source passage, page number, and extraction lineage.
17. Human Review Must Remain Reconstructable:
    Every promotion decision must be auditable: a reviewer must be able to inspect the
    source, extraction prompts, diffs, and evidence dossier to verify the chain of reasoning.
18. Canonical Knowledge Must Remain Inspectable and Auditable:
    All active knowledge is stored as human-readable, Git-tracked structured files (JSON/YAML)
    in canonical repositories.
19. Anti-Recipe Imperative Discipline:
    The ingestion linter unconditionally rejects any candidate claim containing prescriptive
    absolutes ("always cut at 400 Hz", "mandatory drive setting").
20. The Smallest Defensible Claim Prevails:
    A tightly bounded, specific claim that is demonstrably true under defined conditions is
    infinitely superior to an impressive, over-generalized claim that fails under edge cases.

5. SOURCE LANDSCAPE ARCHITECTURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The acquisition system recognizes eleven distinct source classes. To avoid false
hierarchies, each class is evaluated along multi-dimensional operational axes:

5.1 CLASS A: FOUNDATIONAL SCIENTIFIC & ENGINEERING SOURCES
  - Description: Peer-reviewed textbooks, monographs, and academic treatises in physics,
    acoustics, electronics, wave mechanics, and signal processing.
  - Examples: Beranek & Mellow (*Acoustics*), Morse & Ingard (*Theoretical Acoustics*),
    Oppenheim & Schafer (*Discrete-Time Signal Processing*), Blencowe (*Valve Amplifiers*).
  - Expected Strengths: Rigorous mathematical foundations, formal physical laws,
    reproducible derivations, clear boundary conditions.
  - Expected Weaknesses: Often abstract; may lack specific musical instrument context
    or guitar amplifier circuit idiosyncrasies.
  - Appropriate Uses: Grounding invariant physical laws, wave equations, filter math,
    and foundational circuit theorems.
  - Inappropriate Uses: Prescribing aesthetic musical mix balances or subjective tone goals.
  - Provenance Quality: Exceptional (ISBN, DOI, academic publisher peer review).
  - Corroboration Expectations: Highly stable; textbook consensus represents established law.
  - Common Failure Modes: Applying idealized linear assumptions (e.g. small-signal analysis)
    to highly non-linear, overdriven guitar circuits.
  - Suitable Claim Types: `PHYSICAL_LAW`, `ESTABLISHED_ENGINEERING_PRINCIPLE`.
  - Unsuitable Claim Types: `CONTEXT_DEPENDENT_PRACTICE`, `ILLUSTRATIVE_EXAMPLE`.

5.2 CLASS B: PEER-REVIEWED RESEARCH LITERATURE
  - Description: Formal research papers published in indexed archival journals and
    conference proceedings.
  - Examples: Journal of the Audio Engineering Society (JAES), Acoustical Society of
    America (JASA), IEEE Transactions on Audio, Speech, and Language Processing.
  - Expected Strengths: Controlled empirical methodologies, statistical rigor, novel
    experimental validation, detailed error analyses.
  - Expected Weaknesses: Narrow operating envelopes; may test single synthetic signals
    rather than complex musical performances.
  - Appropriate Uses: Specialized empirical relationships (e.g. tube soft-clipping
    harmonic generation, speaker cone breakup modes, ear canal transfer functions).
  - Inappropriate Uses: Universalizing narrow laboratory findings across diverse production environments.
  - Provenance Quality: Exceptional (DOI, peer review records, institutional affiliations).
  - Corroboration Expectations: Independent replication by external research teams.
  - Common Failure Modes: Prematurely accepting an uncorroborated single-study finding
    as established consensus.
  - Suitable Claim Types: `EMPIRICAL_RELATIONSHIP`, `HYPOTHETICAL_OR_COMPETING_MODEL`.
  - Unsuitable Claim Types: Universal workflow rules.

5.3 CLASS C: TECHNICAL STANDARDS & SPECIFICATIONS
  - Description: Formally ratified standards, recommendations, and reference alignments
    issued by international engineering bodies.
  - Examples: AES (e.g. AES17, AES2), EBU (e.g. R128), ITU-R (e.g. BS.1770), IEC, IEEE, ANSI.
  - Expected Strengths: Absolute clarity on reference calibrations, measurement methodologies,
    nominal electrical levels, and metering algorithms.
  - Expected Weaknesses: Purely definitional; standards define conventions and metrics,
    not creative engineering strategies.
  - Appropriate Uses: Defining calibration levels (0 VU, dBu, dBFS), nominal impedances,
    standard measurement bandwidths, and target loudness curves.
  - Inappropriate Uses: Treating a regional calibration standard as an immutable law of physics.
  - Provenance Quality: Authoritative and legally binding within specified jurisdictions.
  - Corroboration Expectations: Institutional ratification.
  - Common Failure Modes: Applying an audio broadcast standard (e.g. -23 LUFS) as a mandatory
    production constraint for an overdriven guitar amplifier pre-capture.
  - Suitable Claim Types: `PROFESSIONAL_CONVENTION`, `CALIBRATION_STANDARD`.
  - Unsuitable Claim Types: Causal models of acoustic phenomena.

5.4 CLASS D: PROFESSIONAL TEXTBOOKS & APPLIED TREATISES
  - Description: Comprehensive handbooks written by seasoned engineering authorities
    focusing on practical studio, acoustic, and circuit applications.
  - Examples: Huber & Runstein (*Modern Recording Techniques*), Rumsey & McCormick
    (*Sound and Recording*), Everest & Pohlmann (*Master Handbook of Acoustics*), Ballou.
  - Expected Strengths: Strong bridge between fundamental theory and real-world studio
    practice; comprehensive coverage of system topology and equipment interactions.
  - Expected Weaknesses: May blend rigorous physics with anecdotal rules of thumb
    without explicit boundary markers.
  - Appropriate Uses: System signal flow, microphone deployment techniques, gain staging
    practices, acoustic boundary treatment.
  - Inappropriate Uses: Accepting colloquial rules of thumb as inviolable scientific truths.
  - Provenance Quality: High (reputable technical publishers, multiple revised editions).
  - Corroboration Expectations: Cross-verification against Class A physics and Class E datasheets.
  - Common Failure Modes: Generalizing a studio rule of thumb beyond its intended musical context.
  - Suitable Claim Types: `ESTABLISHED_ENGINEERING_PRINCIPLE`, `CONTEXT_DEPENDENT_PRACTICE`.
  - Unsuitable Claim Types: Unbounded universal assertions.

5.5 CLASS E: MANUFACTURER ENGINEERING MATERIAL
  - Description: Technical datasheets, schematics, engineering whitepapers, service manuals,
    and patents published by hardware/component manufacturers.
  - Examples: Shure microphone polar plots, Celestion loudspeaker impedance curves,
    Mullard/RCA receiving tube manuals, Jensen transformer whitepapers.
  - Expected Strengths: Authoritative data on the physical construction, component values,
    measured frequency response, and circuit topology of specific hardware.
  - Expected Weaknesses: Potential commercial bias; marketing narratives intermixed with data.
  - Appropriate Uses: Device-specific behavior, circuit topologies, component tolerances,
    and published electrical specifications.
  - Inappropriate Uses: Marketing slogans, subjective timbre descriptions ("warm British roar").
  - Provenance Quality: High for technical data; low for marketing prose.
  - Corroboration Expectations: Independent laboratory verification (Class F).
  - Common Failure Modes: Failing to filter out promotional exaggerations from objective specs.
  - Suitable Claim Types: `DEVICE_SPECIFIC_BEHAVIOUR`, `CIRCUIT_ELECTRONICS`.
  - Unsuitable Claim Types: Unbounded subjective assertions.

5.6 CLASS F: INDEPENDENT TECHNICAL MEASUREMENTS & TESTING
  - Description: Controlled, calibrated laboratory or bench measurements conducted by
    independent engineering institutions or accredited third-party labs.
  - Examples: Calibrated anechoic chamber loudspeaker polar sweeps, Audio Precision
    analyzer bench sweeps of amplifier total harmonic distortion (THD) vs power.
  - Expected Strengths: Objective, unvarnished empirical data free from commercial marketing bias.
  - Expected Weaknesses: Depends heavily on test rig calibration, room acoustics, and methodology.
  - Appropriate Uses: Validating or refuting manufacturer claims, quantifying non-linearities,
    measuring component tolerances and dispersion.
  - Inappropriate Uses: Discarding test methodology context or environmental temperature/SPL.
  - Provenance Quality: Very High when measurement methodology and raw files are published.
  - Corroboration Expectations: Independent replication with equivalent measurement equipment.
  - Common Failure Modes: Treating a measurement taken at a single microphone position or single
    SPL as the universal behavior of the device.
  - Suitable Claim Types: `EMPIRICAL_RELATIONSHIP`, `DEVICE_SPECIFIC_BEHAVIOUR`.
  - Unsuitable Claim Types: Invariant theoretical proofs.

5.7 CLASS G: DOCUMENTED PROFESSIONAL PRACTICE
  - Description: In-depth, technically articulate interviews, masterclasses, and published
    case studies by master recording engineers, mastering engineers, and amplifier designers.
  - Examples: Documented technical interviews with George Massenburg, Rupert Neve,
    Al Schmitt, Bruce Swedien, Soldano, Bogner.
  - Expected Strengths: Deep, highly nuanced insights into real-world trade-off balancing,
    psychoacoustic masking solutions, and context-dependent adjustments.
  - Expected Weaknesses: Strongly subjective vocabulary; lack of formal scientific controls;
    practices are heavily tied to specific personal aesthetics and room environments.
  - Appropriate Uses: Capturing trade-off vectors, diagnostic cues, and contextual practices.
  - Inappropriate Uses: Extracting physical laws or universal dogmatic presets.
  - Provenance Quality: Moderate (depends on publication credibility and direct quotation).
  - Corroboration Expectations: Must be corroborated by underlying acoustic physics or
    multiple independent master practitioners.
  - Common Failure Modes: Confusing a creative personal signature technique with an engineering standard.
  - Suitable Claim Types: `CONTEXT_DEPENDENT_PRACTICE`, `ILLUSTRATIVE_EXAMPLE`.
  - Unsuitable Claim Types: `PHYSICAL_LAW`, `HARD_ENGINEERING_BOUNDARY`.

5.8 CLASS H: PRACTITIONER & TECHNICAL COMMUNITY KNOWLEDGE
  - Description: Technical discussions, repair logs, and empirical observations from
    curated professional audio engineering forums and communities.
  - Examples: AES forum archives, GroupDIY, Gearspace (vetted sub-forums), Amp Garage.
  - Expected Strengths: Unfiltered reporting of practical hardware failure modes, circuit
    mods, component drift, and edge-case behaviors.
  - Expected Weaknesses: High noise-to-signal ratio; persistent repetition of urban legends;
    uncontrolled test environments; lack of rigorous attribution.
  - Appropriate Uses: Identifying candidate hypotheses for empirical validation; discovering
    real-world hardware quirks.
  - Inappropriate Uses: Direct promotion to canonical knowledge without rigorous verification.
  - Provenance Quality: Low to Moderate.
  - Corroboration Expectations: Mandatory corroboration by Class A, B, E, or F sources.
  - Common Failure Modes: Ingesting confirmation bias or forum groupthink as objective fact.
  - Suitable Claim Types: `HYPOTHETICAL_OR_COMPETING_MODEL`, `ENGINEERING_TENDENCY` (quarantined).
  - Unsuitable Claim Types: Canonical active claims without independent testing.

5.9 CLASS I: CURRENT TT LEGACY MATERIAL
  - Description: The existing Tone Translator codebase, algorithms, tables, and heuristics.
  - Ingestion Classification: Formally classified as `UNASSESSED_LEGACY_SOURCE`.
  - Expected Strengths: Contains valuable domain intuitions (e.g. microphone positioning
    heuristics, cabinet impedance pairings, pre-distortion filter concepts).
  - Expected Weaknesses: Entangled with deterministic code rules, keyword overrides,
    AmpliTube 5 GUIDs, and arbitrary numerical thresholds.
  - Appropriate Uses: Source of candidate concepts to be deconstructed and physically reformulated.
  - Inappropriate Uses: Never automatically authoritative. Never cited as proof of truth.
  - Provenance Quality: Unassessed until primary literature roots are recovered.
  - Corroboration Expectations: Must locate underlying primary literature (Classes A-E).
  - Common Failure Modes: Migrating code rules unchanged as "proven engineering principles."
  - Suitable Claim Types: Candidate units undergoing deconstruction in Phase 1C.4e.
  - Unsuitable Claim Types: Direct entry into `VALIDATED_ACTIVE` status.

5.10 CLASS J: TT EXPERIENTIAL & CANDIDATE LESSON MATERIAL
  - Description: System-generated runtime observations, closed-loop discrepancy analyses,
    and user session telemetry.
  - Ingestion Classification: Quarantined `CandidateLesson` records.
  - Expected Strengths: Direct insight into real-world algorithm performance and edge cases.
  - Expected Weaknesses: Case-specific; prone to localized overfitting and noise.
  - Appropriate Uses: Internal hypothesis generation and system refinement.
  - Inappropriate Uses: Cannot update canonical knowledge without passing the Evidence-Sufficiency Dossier.
  - Provenance Quality: Internal runtime execution logs.
  - Corroboration Expectations: Multi-factor dossier (7 distinct criteria).
  - Common Failure Modes: "Single good preset -> universal global rule."
  - Suitable Claim Types: Quarantined candidate lessons only.
  - Unsuitable Claim Types: Canonical active knowledge.

5.11 CLASS K: HISTORICAL ARCHIVAL & PATENT LITERATURE
  - Description: Original patents, historical technical reports, and vintage design notes.
  - Examples: US Patents for Baxandall tone control, Western Electric telephone lab reports,
    original Blumlein stereo patents.
  - Expected Strengths: Crystal-clear insight into original inventive intent and baseline derivations.
  - Expected Weaknesses: May describe obsolete technologies or components no longer manufactured.
  - Appropriate Uses: Documenting circuit topology origins and foundational patent claims.
  - Inappropriate Uses: Enforcing vintage design constraints on modern high-speed DSP or wide-bandwidth converters.
  - Provenance Quality: High (patent office documentation).
  - Corroboration Expectations: Verification against modern circuit analysis.
  - Common Failure Modes: Treating a patent claim (which is legally protective) as an objective performance guarantee.
  - Suitable Claim Types: `ESTABLISHED_ENGINEERING_PRINCIPLE`, `CIRCUIT_ELECTRONICS`.
  - Unsuitable Claim Types: Modern production workflow standards.

6. SOURCE DISCOVERY VS SOURCE AUTHORITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

6.1 THE DISCOVERY-TO-AUTHORITY FIREWALL
A foundational failure mode in automated knowledge ingestion is conflating *discovering*
a source with *verifying* its authority. The acquisition architecture erects an impenetrable
firewall between these phases:

    +───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────+
    │                     DISCOVERY MECHANISMS                      │
    │  - Search Engine Queries       - LLM Parametric Memory        │
    │  - Literature Bibliographies   - Community Forum Links        │
    │  - Academic Citation Graphs    - YouTube / Podcast References │
    │  - Manufacturer Webpages       - Current TT Code Comments     │
    +───────────────────────────────┬───────────────────────────────+
                                    │ Identifies candidate artifact
                                    ▼
    +───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────+
    │                   DISCOVERY-TO-REGISTRATION                   │
    │                    VERIFICATION GATEWAY                       │
    │  Checks:                                                      │
    │  1. Does physical/digital artifact actually exist?            │
    │  2. Can stable bibliographic identity be established?         │
    │  3. Is primary text/data accessible for direct audit?         │
    +───────────────────────────────┬───────────────────────────────+
                                    │ Registers SourceDocument
                                    ▼
    +───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────+
    │                     EPISTEMIC ASSESSMENT                      │
    │  - Source Rigor (R1-R5)        - Replicability & Controls     │
    │  - Domain Jurisdiction (J)     - Commercial Conflict Audit    │
    │  - Methodological Rigor        - Operational Boundary Check   │
    +───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────+

6.2 REJECTED EPISTEMIC SHORTCUTS
The acquisition engine unconditionally rejects the following arguments as proofs of authority:
  - "Gemini / GPT knows this principle": Model parametric memory is a discovery index,
    not a verifiable source. The model must point to a real, auditable source document.
  - "Many websites / blogs say this": Repetition without independent methodology is
    echo-chamber folklore.
  - "This is common studio wisdom": Intuitions must be physically formulated and bound.
  - "The manufacturer marketing page says this": Commercial claims require engineering
    schematic, datasheet, or bench measurement corroboration.
  - "Current TT already runs this code": Existing software heuristics are unvalidated legacy.

7. SOURCE REGISTRATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

7.1 REGISTRATION CONTRACT
Before any text can be parsed or claims extracted, the source must be registered as a
`SourceDocument` entity. The registration record guarantees persistent identity and auditability.

```json
{
  "source_id": "SRC-AES-JAES-V52-P1024",
  "title": "Acoustic Behavior of Loudspeaker Diaphragms at High Excursion",
  "authors": ["Dr. John E. Schmidt", "Robert M. Davis"],
  "publisher_or_institution": "Audio Engineering Society",
  "publication_date": "2004-11-15",
  "publication_year": 2004,
  "edition_or_version": "Vol. 52, Issue 11",
  "identifiers": {
    "doi": "10.17743/jaes.2004.0042",
    "isbn": null,
    "issn": "1549-4950",
    "url": "https://www.aes.org/e-lib/browse.cfm?elib=12984"
  },
  "source_class": "CLASS_B_PEER_REVIEWED_RESEARCH",
  "source_rigor_tier": "R2_PEER_REVIEWED_RESEARCH",
  "domain_jurisdictions": {
    "9": "J_CORE",
    "10": "J_APPLIED",
    "4": "J_PERIPHERAL"
  },
  "original_format": "PDF",
  "language": "en",
  "licensing_status": "ACADEMIC_RESTRICTED_ACCESS",
  "document_hash": {
    "algorithm": "SHA-256",
    "value": "b7f9a882e341c5921f00843d1a89c92bfa76e4210d3b6641e7790114f0923a1c"
  },
  "acquisition_metadata": {
    "acquired_by": "Specialist-04",

    "acquisition_method": "INSTITUTIONAL_SUBSCRIPTION_DOWNLOAD",
    "known_errata": [],
    "superseded_by": null
  }

```

7.2 INCOMPLETE METADATA HANDLING
  - Mandatory Fields: `source_id`, `title`, `authors` (or institutional author), `source_class`,
    `source_rigor_tier`, `domain_jurisdictions`, `licensing_status`, `document_hash`.
  - If publication year is missing or uncertain, it must be flagged as `YEAR_UNKNOWN`
    and investigated; it cannot be guessed.
  - If a DOI/ISBN is unavailable, an immutable SHA-256 hash of the canonical input file
    serves as the permanent unique digital identifier.
  - Under no circumstances may missing bibliographic metadata be hallucinated or synthetically generated.

8. COPYRIGHT, LICENSING & SOURCE PRESERVATION BOUNDARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

8.1 THE TWO-STORE ARCHITECTURE
To enable comprehensive engineering learning while strictly avoiding unauthorized
reproduction or copyright infringement, TT establishes a strict two-store boundary:

1. Canonical Knowledge Library (`/knowledge/canonical/`):
   - Scope: Contains ONLY normalized, platform-independent engineering knowledge:
     `KnowledgeClaim`, `CausalModel`, `OperationalBoundary`, `ConflictRecord`.
   - Content: Human-readable declarative claims, mathematical AST/LaTeX formulas,
     typed numerical bounds, and short supporting quotations (under fair-use doctrine).
   - Storage: Git-tracked, public/shared internal engineering repository.
   - Prohibitions: NEVER contains full book chapters, scraped web mirrors, scanned PDF
     bundles, or proprietary vendor code.

2. Source Preservation Archive (`/knowledge/archive/sources/`):
   - Scope: Secure, access-controlled offline storage holding raw source artifacts
     (PDFs, schematics, CSV measurement sweeps, vendor manual scans).
   - Content: Cryptographically sealed source files referenced by `SourceDocument.document_hash`.
   - Access: Restricted strictly to human auditors and offline extraction pipelines.
   - Purpose: Enables complete provenance audit and extraction replayability without
     distributing copyrighted material to runtime environments or end users.

8.2 LICENSING TYPOLOGY & OPERATIONAL SAFEGUARDS
Every registered source must record its `licensing_status`:
  - `PUBLIC_DOMAIN`: Unrestricted extraction and citation.
  - `OPEN_ACCESS_CC`: Free extraction, citation, and attribution under CC license terms.
  - `COMMERCIAL_PURCHASED`: Lawfully purchased book/paper. Permitted for internal
    extraction of uncopyrightable physical facts and principles; verbatim full-text
    distribution prohibited. Short cited excerpts permitted for attribution.
  - `PROPRIETARY_CONFIDENTIAL`: Vendor NDA material. Extraction permitted only if
    expressly authorized; claims tagged with confidential operational flags.
  - `RESTRICTED_ACCESS`: Sources behind institutional paywalls where individual access
    was obtained. Extraction captures facts and derivations; raw document remains in
    the access-controlled archive.

9. PARSING & STRUCTURAL EXTRACTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

9.1 STRUCTURAL EXTRACTION REQUIREMENTS
Parsing converts raw source artifacts into structured extraction canvases while
preserving structural context. A successful parse does NOT equal knowledge validation.

The parser must capture:
  1. Exact Pagination & Section Hierarchy: Preserve Chapter, Section, Subsection, and Page numbers.
  2. Mathematical Notation Fidelity: Isolate equations into exact LaTeX strings or
     MathML blocks. Preserve explicit variable definitions and operational ranges.
  3. Physical Units & SI Standardization: Extract original units exactly (e.g. inches,
     cm, mV, dBu, dBV) before any standardized SI conversion.
  4. Figure & Table Coordinate Identity: Extract table headers, column labels, figure
     captions, and axis scaling (log vs linear).
  5. Optical Character Recognition (OCR) Audit: For scanned documents, record an
     `ocr_confidence_score` (0.0 to 1.0) and flag low-confidence passages (<0.85)
     for mandatory human transcription check.

9.2 EXTRACTION INTEGRITY CONTROLS
  - Corrupted Tables: If table rows or column alignments cannot be unambiguously parsed,
    the table must be quarantined.
  - Mathematical Syntax Validation: All extracted LaTeX formulas must pass AST syntax
    compilation before entering candidate extraction.
  - Unit Mismatches: If an extracted formula lacks explicit unit definitions for variables,
    it is flagged with `MISSING_VARIABLE_UNITS`.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
10. ATOMIC KNOWLEDGE EXTRACTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

10.1 THE DE-SUMMARIZATION MANDATE
A critical failure mode of standard AI knowledge pipelines is "summarization."
Summarization discards nuance, blurs boundary conditions, and strips causal mechanisms
in favor of bland executive overviews.

The acquisition engine ENFORCES ATOMIC DECOMPOSITION:
  - Complex source passages must be decomposed into irreducible, independently
    testable engineering assertions.
  - Each extracted candidate must preserve:
      * The underlying physical/electrical mechanism.
      * The operating boundary and prerequisite conditions.
      * The magnitude, direction, and sensitivity of the change.
      * Associated collateral trade-offs.

10.2 ANTI-RECIPE EXTRACTION SAFEGUARD
Consider the following authoritative studio textbook passage:
"In heavy rock production, attenuating frequencies below 100 Hz on the rhythm guitar
signal before it hits an overdriven tube preamplifier dramatically tightens low-end
transient punch and prevents intermodulation flub, though excessive cutting makes the
instrument sound sterile and thin in sparse arrangements."

  - FLAWED / REJECTED EXTRACTION (Recipe Summary):
    "Cut frequencies below 100 Hz before high-gain distortion to get tight guitars."
    *Why Rejected*: Creates a dogmatic imperative; strips circuit mechanism (intermodulation);
    ignores arrangement context; hides collateral penalty (thin tone).

  - ACCEPTED ATOMIC EXTRACTION:
    * Proposition 1 (KnowledgeClaim): "Pre-distortion high-pass filtering reduces low-frequency
      intermodulation distortion products generated in non-linear clipping stages."
    * Proposition 2 (CausalModel): "Input: Sub-100 Hz attenuation. Mediating Stage: PREAMP_NONLINEAR.
      Acoustic Consequence: Decreases bass IMD, accelerates palm-mute decay envelope.
      Trade-off: Loss of fundamental low-end body; risks sterile timbre in sparse arrangements."
    * Proposition 3 (OperationalBoundary): "Prerequisites: Non-linear clipping stage THD > 10%;
      signal containing high-energy low-frequency transient fundamentals."

11. CLAIM DECOMPOSITION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

11.1 COMPOUND PROPOSITION SPLITTING RULES
Natural technical language frequently bundles multiple distinct assertion types into
a single sentence. The acquisition pipeline enforces rigorous decomposition rules:

```
+─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────+
| COMPOUND CLAIM:                                                             |

+──────────────────────────────────────┬──────────────────────────────────────+

     ┌─────────────────────────────────┼─────────────────────────────────┐
     ▼                                 ▼                                 ▼
+───────────────────────────+ +───────────────────────────+ +───────────────────────────+
| 1. PHYSICAL MECHANISM     | | 2. OPERATIONAL BOUNDARY   | | 3. PRESCRIPTIVE RECIPE    |

| DISPOSITION:              | | DISPOSITION:              | | DISPOSITION:              |

+───────────────────────────+ +───────────────────────────+ +───────────────────────────+
```

11.2 DECOMPOSITION TAXONOMY
The decomposition engine parses compound inputs into eleven distinct functional components:
  1. Physical Phenomenon: The observable wave or circuit event.
  2. Causal Mechanism: The mediating transfer function or transformation.
  3. Operating Boundary / Envelope: Quantitative parameter range of validity.
  4. Prerequisite System State: Mandatory upstream or environmental conditions.
  5. Confounding Variable: Independent factors that mimic or obscure the effect.
  6. Collateral Trade-off: Inevitable physical or perceptual penalty.
  7. Quantitative Metric: Specific numerical threshold or tolerance.
  8. Contextual Practice: Bounded methodology developed for specific genres/rooms.
  9. Subjective Aesthetic Valuation: Qualitative descriptors (e.g. "warm", "brittle").
     *Must be re-anchored to psychoacoustic metrics (e.g. low-frequency ratio, crest factor).*
  10. Prescriptive Recommendation: Imperative guidance ("should", "must").
      *Must be stripped or converted into conditional trade-off options.*
  11. Known Exceptions / Invalidation Conditions: Scenarios where the principle fails.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
13. NORMALISATION WITHOUT MEANING LOSS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

13.1 NORMALISATION RULES
Normalization maps extracted assertions into the frozen Phase 1C.4a entity model.
It must never alter the physical truth or strip necessary qualifications:

  1. Units Preservation & Standardisation:
     - Original units are preserved in `ClaimAttribution.extracted_passage`.
     - Normalized values are expressed in base SI units (Hz, m, s, V, Pa, dB SPL, dBFS).
  2. Observation vs Interpretation Separation:
     - Direct laboratory measurements are classified as `EMPIRICAL_RELATIONSHIP`.
     - Explanatory theoretical models are classified as `ESTABLISHED_ENGINEERING_PRINCIPLE`
       or `PHENOMENOLOGICAL_MODEL`.
  3. Correlation vs Causation Delineation:
     - Statistical associations without an identified transfer function are tagged as
       `ENGINEERING_TENDENCY` with explicit confounders.
     - Causal assertions require instantiating a `CausalModel` with an explicit mediating mechanism.
  4. Descriptive vs Prescriptive Decoupling:
     - All prescriptive statements ("use a notch filter") are rewritten as descriptive
       causal models ("a notch filter attenuates energy within bandwidth B, reducing
       resonance R while introducing phase shift P").
  5. Tendency vs Law Guard:
     - Empirical practices observed in specific studios must NEVER be promoted to `PHYSICAL_LAW`.
     - They must be assigned to Axis 2 `CONTEXT_DEPENDENT_PRACTICE`.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
14. MULTI-SOURCE CORROBORATION & EVIDENCE DEPENDENCY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

14.1 THE CIRCULAR CITATION & ECHO-CHAMBER TRAP
Simple source counting is epistemologically invalid. If five modern recording blogs
and three forum posts all repeat that "cutting 400 Hz removes boxiness," but all eight
trace their lineage back to a single 1982 magazine article by one engineer, this represents
ONE historical data point, not eight corroborating sources.

14.2 EVIDENCE DEPENDENCY GRAPH (EDG)
The acquisition engine constructs an Evidence Dependency Graph for every concept:
  - Primary Ancestry Nodes: The original theoretical derivation or laboratory experiment.
  - Derivative Nodes: Textbooks, articles, or manuals citing the primary source.
  - True Corroboration Criteria: Two sources constitute true corroboration if and only if:
      1. They employ independent experimental setups or mathematical derivations.
      2. They test across diverse input stimuli (different guitars, speakers, rooms).
      3. They do not share identical corporate or commercial lineage.
      4. Neither source relies on the other as its sole authority.

14.3 CONSENSUS UPGRADE RULES
  - When true independent corroboration is established across >= 2 independent R1/R2
    sources, `epistemic_validation.consensus_state` is upgraded to `ESTABLISHED_CONSENSUS`.
  - If multiple sources cite a common ancestor without independent validation,
    `consensus_state` remains `PREDOMINANT_VIEW` with an explicit lineage note.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
15. SOURCE RIGOR, JURISDICTION & CLAIM QUALITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

15.1 INDEPENDENCE OF SOURCE RIGOR AND CLAIM QUALITY
The architecture maintains a strict distinction between the general standing of a
source and the validity of a specific extracted claim:
  - High-Rigor Source, Weak Claim: A world-renowned acoustics textbook (R1) may make
    an offhand, untested remark about electric guitar speaker distortion in a footnote.
    Because the author was operating outside their primary experimental focus, the
    jurisdiction is `J_PERIPHERAL`, and the claim cannot be treated as physical law.
  - Practitioner Source, Valid Observation: A veteran amplifier repair technician
    in an informal interview (R5) observes that a specific screen resistor overheats
    under square-wave overdrive. While the source is R5, the observation reflects a
    measurable physical event. When corroborated by Ohm's Law and bench measurements,
    the claim achieves `ESTABLISHED_ENGINEERING_PRINCIPLE`.

15.2 THE MULTI-DIMENSIONAL EVALUATION CONTRACT
Every claim is evaluated independently across:
  1. `source_rigor_tier`: R1 (Law) to R5 (Practitioner).
  2. `domain_jurisdiction`: J_CORE, J_APPLIED, J_PERIPHERAL.
  3. `evidence_strength`: THEORETICAL_PROOF, SYSTEMATIC_EMPIRICAL, CORROBORATED_PRACTICE, ANECDOTAL.
  4. `consensus_state`: ESTABLISHED_CONSENSUS, PREDOMINANT, DEBATED, OUTLIER.
  5. `replication_state`: INDEPENDENTLY_REPLICATED, EXPERIMENTALLY_VERIFIED, OBSERVATIONAL_ONLY.
  6. `boundary_certainty`: TIGHTLY_BOUNDED, PARTIALLY_BOUNDED, EXPLORATORY_LIMITS.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
16. CONFLICT DETECTION DURING ACQUISITION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

16.1 CONFLICT DETECTION PROTOCOL
During ingestion, candidate claims are compared against existing canonical knowledge
and parallel candidates within the same domain. When divergent claims emerge, the
acquisition system NEVER discards or averages them.

Acquisition-time conflict detection identifies:
  1. Direct Contradictions: Incompatible statements regarding physical facts
     (e.g. Source A: "Transformer saturation generates purely even harmonics";
     Source B: "Transformer magnetic hysteresis produces predominantly third-order odd harmonics").
  2. Boundary Divergences: Differing quantitative recommendations caused by differing
     test conditions (e.g. Source A recommends 5 cm mic distance for low SPL; Source B
     recommends 30 cm for 130 dB SPL guitar cabs).
  3. Methodological Divergences: Competing engineering strategies achieving similar goals
     (e.g. acoustic off-axis mic positioning vs electrical post-clipping equalization).

16.2 ACQUISITION-TO-CONFLICT-RECORD HANDOFF
When a conflict is detected:
  - Both claims are tagged with `epistemic_validation.conflict_status = ACTIVE_COMPETING_CLAIM`.
  - A candidate `ConflictRecord` is generated with `conflict_nature` and an initial
    divergence analysis.
  - The conflict package is queued for expert adjudication in Phase 1C.4d.

21. AI ROLE & ANTI-HALLUCINATION CONTROLS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

21.1 THE FIRST LAW OF AI ACQUISITION
    THE AI IS AN EXTRACTION AND STRUCTURAL ANALYSIS AGENT.
    THE AI IS NEVER AN EPISTEMIC SOURCE.

21.2 FIVE-LAYER ANTI-HALLUCINATION ARCHITECTURE
To prevent LLM parametric hallucinations from polluting the Knowledge Library:

  1. Source-Grounded Prompting: Extraction prompts strictly restrict candidate generation
     to verbatim or strictly derived content within the supplied text chunk.
  2. Bidirectional Passage Matching: Every generated `KnowledgeClaim` must be verified
     by a deterministic string-containment or semantic alignment check against the
     `ClaimAttribution.extracted_passage`.
  3. Unsupported Content Flagging: If an LLM introduces an acoustic concept not explicitly
     present in the source passage (e.g. inferring a specific transformer core material
     not mentioned in the text), the ingestion engine isolates it into an
     `unsupported_model_additions` field.
  4. Mandatory Quarantine of Unsupported Content: Claims containing unsupported model
     additions are quarantined and barred from review readiness.
  5. Zero Self-Authorizing Promotion: AI models cannot approve, sign off, or promote
     claims to `VALIDATED_ACTIVE`. Governance promotion is exclusively human.

24. ACQUISITION QUALITY GATES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The acquisition pipeline enforces twelve discrete quality gates:

Gate 1: DISCOVERED
  - Entry Criteria: Source identified via literature review, bibliography, or citation index.
  - Verification: Basic bibliographic existence verified; duplication check passed.
  - Output: Candidate source record.

Gate 2: REGISTERED
  - Entry Criteria: Complete bibliographic metadata verified; persistent ID assigned.
  - Verification: Cryptographic hash generated; licensing status classified.
  - Output: Immutable `SourceDocument` entity.

Gate 3: ACQUIRED
  - Entry Criteria: Raw source file deposited in access-controlled preservation archive.
  - Verification: Document hash verified against registered `SourceDocument.document_hash`.
  - Output: Sealed raw artifact in archive.

Gate 4: PARSED
  - Entry Criteria: Text, mathematical formulas, and structural hierarchy extracted.
  - Verification: Pagination mapped; LaTeX equations compiled; OCR confidence >= 0.85.
  - Output: Structured extraction canvas.

Gate 5: EXTRACTED
  - Entry Criteria: Atomic candidate claims, boundaries, and trade-offs extracted.
  - Verification: Verbatim source passage captured; de-summarization rules enforced.
  - Output: Candidate extraction objects.

Gate 6: ATTRIBUTED
  - Entry Criteria: Formal citation links instantiated.
  - Verification: `ClaimAttribution` links candidate claim to exact source page and paragraph.
  - Output: Attributed claim records.

Gate 7: DECOMPOSED & NORMALISED
  - Entry Criteria: Compound claims split; units converted to SI; multi-axis taxonomy applied.
  - Verification: 3-axis taxonomy valid; anti-recipe linter passed; platform firewall passed.
  - Output: Normalized candidate `KnowledgeClaim`, `CausalModel`, `OperationalBoundary`.

Gate 8: EVIDENCE ASSESSED
  - Entry Criteria: Source Rigor (R1-R5) and Jurisdiction (J_CORE to J_PERIPHERAL) assigned.
  - Verification: `EpistemicValidation` factors populated; no scalar authority scores created.
  - Output: Epistemically classified candidate entities.

Gate 9: CONFLICT & LINEAGE AUDITED
  - Entry Criteria: Comparison against existing canonical library and parallel claims.
  - Verification: Evidence dependency graph checked for circular citations; conflicts flagged.
  - Output: Linked `ConflictRecord` or clean consensus classification.

Gate 10: REVIEW READY
  - Entry Criteria: Ingestion linting passed; all required fields and boundaries populated.
  - Verification: Candidate package complete and viewable in audit UI.
  - Output: Queued candidate package in `UNDER_REVIEW` state.

Gate 11: GOVERNANCE EVALUATED
  - Entry Criteria: Evaluated by qualified human expert(s) under Phase 1C.4a governance tiers.
  - Verification: Formal `ReviewRecord` signed with reviewer ID, role, and findings.
  - Output: `APPROVED`, `REJECTED`, or `CHANGES_REQUESTED`.

Gate 12: PROMOTION ELIGIBLE
  - Entry Criteria: All governance approvals, adversarial triad checks, and seals completed.
  - Verification: Lifecycle state updated to `VALIDATED_ACTIVE`.
  - Output: Immutable canonical knowledge unit eligible for runtime indexing in Phase 1C.4c.

25. ACQUISITION FAILURE MODES & MITIGATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The acquisition architecture provides explicit, automated defenses against 22 distinct
failure modes:

1. Hallucinated Citation: LLM invents non-existent author, book, or paper.
   - Mitigation: Automated DOI/ISBN resolver and cryptographic document hash verification
     at Gate 2. Claims citing unverified sources are immediately rejected.
2. Incorrect Edition / Outdated Revision: Citing an obsolete first edition with known errors.
   - Mitigation: Bibliographic registration requires edition verification against publisher errata.
3. Outdated / Superseded Standard: Ingesting obsolete broadcast standards (e.g. 1970s DIN).
   - Mitigation: Standards records maintain `superseded_by` links; active status restricted
     to current standards.
4. Circular Citation Ancestry: Multiple secondary blogs citing one original source treated
   as independent corroborations.
   - Mitigation: Evidence Dependency Graph (Section 14) detects shared lineage and enforces
     single-origin counting.
5. Duplicated Evidence Lineage: Re-ingesting the same study via different conference reprints.
   - Mitigation: Cryptographic source hashing and DOI deduplication at Gate 1.
6. Missing Operational Context: Extracting a filter slope without specifying Q or corner frequency.
   - Mitigation: Gate 7 schema enforcement requires mandatory `OperationalBoundary` attachment.
7. Unit Conversion Error: Confusing dBu (0.775 V) with dBV (1.0 V) during normalization.
   - Mitigation: Automated unit transformation linter with static dimensional analysis.
8. OCR Corruption: Scanned text reading "400 Hz" as "4000 Hz" due to speckle.
   - Mitigation: OCR confidence gating (<0.85 requires human transcription check); mathematical
     formula AST compilation.
9. Table / Figure Misinterpretation: Reversing X/Y axes or confusing logarithmic with linear plots.
   - Mitigation: Dual human review for all graphical/tabular digitizations.
10. Marketing Language Treated as Fact: Ingesting manufacturer puffery as physical specifications.
    - Mitigation: Class E ingestion firewall strips subjective adjectives and commercial claims.
11. Practitioner Anecdote Universalized: Treating a rock engineer's mic position as an acoustic law.
    - Mitigation: Multi-axis classification restricts practitioner accounts to Axis 2 Contextual
      Practices; barred from Axis 1 Physical Laws.
12. Model-Generated Content Presented as Source-Derived: LLM adding ungrounded assertions.
    - Mitigation: Bidirectional passage containment checker isolates model-added tokens to
      quarantined fields.
13. Correlation Presented as Causation: Ingesting an empirical correlation as a causal mechanism.
    - Mitigation: CausalModel requires explicit physical mediating mechanisms; statistical
      correlations restricted to EngineeringTendency.
14. Device-Specific Behavior Universalized: Treating Marshall tone stack frequency notches
    as universal guitar amp behavior.
    - Mitigation: Mandatory `DeviceSpecificityContext` tagging for all commercial hardware claims.
15. Historical Practice Presented as Current Standard: Ingesting 1960s German radio broadcast
    tape calibrations as modern DAW standards.
    - Mitigation: `source_class` tagging and explicit historical context markers.
16. Measurement Without Reference Conditions: Ingesting an SPL measurement without distance or room specs.
    - Mitigation: Quantitative data contract requires mandatory `reference_conditions` object.
17. False Precision: Encoding "around 3 kHz" as "3000.00 Hz."
    - Mitigation: Numerical Typing Engine enforces `ILLUSTRATIVE_NOMINAL_VALUE` or
      `EMPIRICALLY_MEASURED_RANGE` with soft boundaries.
18. Conflicting Sources Silently Merged: Ingestion pipeline averaging divergent frequency recommendations.
    - Mitigation: Gate 9 conflict detector forces creation of `ConflictRecord`.
19. Source Summarization Losing Exceptions: Discarding "except on acoustic instruments" during extraction.

22. Silent Recipe Generation: Ingesting an imperative "always do X" rule.

26. REJECTION / QUARANTINE POLICY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

26.1 TAXONOMY OF INGESTION DISPOSITIONS
The system strictly distinguishes five terminal and intermediate dispositions:

  1. REJECT SOURCE:
     - Applied When: The document is fraudulent, commercial marketing puffery with zero
       technical data, plagiarized, unidentifiable, or corrupted beyond recovery.
     - Result: `SourceDocument` marked `REJECTED`. No claims extracted.
  2. REJECT CLAIM:
     - Applied When: The source is legitimate, but the specific extracted proposition is
       factually disproven by higher-tier physical laws (e.g. author claims proximity
       effect occurs in pure omnidirectional pressure microphones).
     - Result: `KnowledgeClaim` marked `REJECTED`. Preserved in audit archive to prevent re-ingestion.
  3. QUARANTINE CLAIM:
     - Applied When: A claim has an identified source but lacks corroboration, contains
       unsupported model additions, has unverified boundaries, or represents an uncorroborated
       `CandidateLesson`.
     - Result: Moved to `/knowledge/quarantine/`. Invisible to production retrieval.
  4. RETAIN AS CONFLICTING EVIDENCE:
     - Applied When: Two high-rigor sources make competing, irreconcilable claims under
       similar conditions.
     - Result: Both claims retained; bound to an active `ConflictRecord`.
  5. RETAIN AS HISTORICAL CONTEXT:
     - Applied When: A claim represents superseded historical engineering practice
       (e.g. vintage tape biasing techniques).
     - Result: Retained with `knowledge_role: PROFESSIONAL_CONVENTION`, explicitly bounded
       to vintage hardware contexts.
  6. PROMOTION ELIGIBLE:
     - Applied When: Fully attributed, normalized, boundary-complete, verified against
       circular lineage, and approved by qualified human expert review.
     - Result: Transitioned to `VALIDATED_ACTIVE`.

33. DECISIONS REQUIRING EXPLICIT SIGN-OFF
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The fifteen core architectural decisions (A through O) are definitively resolved:

DECISION A: Canonical Relationship Between Stored Sources and Normalized Knowledge
  - Resolved Direction: Independent, Decoupled Relational Linkage.
  - Rationale: Source documents provide verifiable historical provenance, but the canonical
    knowledge base consists strictly of normalized, platform-independent entities
    (`KnowledgeClaim`, `CausalModel`, `OperationalBoundary`). Sources are not knowledge;
    they validate knowledge.

DECISION B: Physical Storage Architecture for Full Source Documents
  - Resolved Direction: Two-Store Hybrid Architecture.
  - Rationale: Full source documents (PDFs, scans, CSVs) reside in an access-controlled,
    offline Source Preservation Archive (`/knowledge/archive/sources/`). The Git-tracked
    canonical repository contains only normalized structured files with short fair-use
    passages, protecting copyright and keeping runtime knowledge lightweight.

DECISION C: Minimum Provenance Required for Promotion Eligibility
  - Resolved Direction: Unbroken Three-Point Provenance Anchor.
  - Rationale: A claim must link to: (1) an immutable `SourceDocument` with cryptographic hash,
    (2) a specific `ClaimAttribution` with exact location and quoted passage, and (3) an
    authoritative Rigor/Jurisdiction classification. Claims failing this anchor cannot advance.

DECISION D: Representation of Unavailable or Proprietary Sources
  - Resolved Direction: Sealed Bibliographic Provenance Stub.
  - Rationale: If a source becomes unavailable after initial extraction, its persistent
    metadata, SHA-256 hash, and extracted passage remain sealed in `SourceDocument` and
    `ClaimAttribution`. The claim retains full historical traceability without requiring
    continuous live external access.

DECISION E: Distinguishing Independent Corroboration from Duplicated Citation Lineage
  - Resolved Direction: Graph-Based Evidence Dependency Tracking (EDG).
  - Rationale: Multiple citations tracing to a common ancestral study are mapped as a
    single evidentiary root with derivative branches. Consensus upgrades require independent
    methodologies or separate primary experimental roots.

DECISION F: Representation of AI-Added Unsupported Content
  - Resolved Direction: Isolated Quarantined Additions Field.
  - Rationale: Any concept or parameter introduced by an LLM that cannot be grounded in
    the cited source passage is isolated in an `unsupported_model_additions` field. The
    claim is automatically quarantined and blocked from review readiness.

DECISION G: Whole Source Rejection vs Individual Claim Rejection
  - Resolved Direction: Granular Epistemic Scoping.
  - Rationale: A source is rejected entirely only for fraud, commercial bias with zero data,
    or total unreliability. Otherwise, the source is registered, and flawed or out-of-jurisdiction
    claims are rejected or bounded individually, preserving valid physical facts.

DECISION H: Acquisition Workflow States vs Frozen Lifecycle States
  - Resolved Direction: Two-Tier Decoupled State Hierarchy.
  - Rationale: Acquisition states (`DISCOVERED`, `REGISTERED`, `PARSED`, `EXTRACTED`,
    `NORMALISED`, `REVIEW_READY`) govern the offline ingestion workflow. The canonical
    lifecycle states (`CAPTURED`, `EXTRACTED`, `NORMALIZED`, `UNDER_REVIEW`, `VALIDATED_ACTIVE`,
    `DEPRECATED`) defined in Phase 1C.4a remain authoritative for canonical knowledge entities.

DECISION I: Provenance-Break Severity Blocking Promotion
  - Resolved Direction: Absolute Zero-Tolerance Gate.
  - Rationale: Any missing source ID, absent page/section reference, divergent document hash,
    or text discrepancy between claim and source passage constitutes a fatal provenance break,
    unconditionally blocking promotion to `VALIDATED_ACTIVE`.

DECISION J: Ingestion Pipeline Entry for Current TT Legacy Material
  - Resolved Direction: Unassessed Legacy Deconstruction Pipeline.
  - Rationale: Current TT enters as `UNASSESSED_LEGACY_SOURCE` per Revalidation Addendum 1.
    Claims are stripped of platform IDs, reformulated into first-principles physics, and
    traced to primary literature before achieving active status.

DECISION K: Re-Entry Pipeline for Quarantined Candidate Lessons
  - Resolved Direction: Evidence-Sufficiency Dossier Re-Ingestion.
  - Rationale: Runtime lessons cannot bypass acquisition rigor. When a `CandidateLesson`
    satisfies its 7-point dossier, it enters the normal acquisition pipeline as a candidate
    empirical claim requiring human expert review and adversarial triad validation.

DECISION L: Acquisition Prioritisation Strategy for Curriculum Build
  - Resolved Direction: Cross-Domain Foundational Wave Strategy.
  - Rationale: Wave 1 prioritizes core electrical, acoustic, and saturation physics (Domains 1, 2,
    3, 4, 10) that unlock multiple downstream disciplines, avoiding ad-hoc or domain-linear ingestion.

DECISION M: Retention of Acquisition Artifacts for Replayability
  - Resolved Direction: Full Ingestion Lineage Snapshot.
  - Rationale: The system retains the raw source hash, parser git commit, extraction prompt
    template, normalization schema, and `KnowledgeAcquisitionManifest` to guarantee 100%
    deterministic replayability.

DECISION N: Source Material Permitted for Discovery but Barred from Authority
  - Resolved Direction: Discovery-Only Classification.
  - Rationale: Web search results, YouTube videos, podcast transcripts, unvetted forum threads,
    and LLM parametric memory may be used to discover sources, but can NEVER serve as authoritative
    citations without independent, verifiable primary documentation.

DECISION O: Safeguards Preventing Canonical Library from Becoming Copyrighted Reproduction
  - Resolved Direction: Fair-Use Normalization Firewall.
  - Rationale: Raw source texts are quarantined in the offline archive. Canonical records store
    only declarative engineering claims, mathematical ASTs, operational boundaries, and
    short, illustrative quotation stubs legally permitted under fair-use doctrine.
