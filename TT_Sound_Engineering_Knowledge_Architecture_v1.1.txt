SECTION 1 — EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1.1 ARCHITECTURAL PURPOSE & STATUS
Phase 1C.4a-R establishes the corrected, authoritative, platform-independent Sound
Engineering Knowledge Architecture for the future Tone Translator (TT) AI Sound
Engineer. It serves as the official v1.1 Freeze Candidate.

Following comprehensive architectural review of v1, the core paradigm was accepted:
    KNOWLEDGE INFORMS REASONING.
    KNOWLEDGE DOES NOT REPLACE REASONING.

However, formal architectural freeze was conditioned upon eight specific design
corrections, normalization refactorings, and the definitive resolution of the four
Phase 1C.4a open architectural decisions. This v1.1 document fully resolves those
conditions without altering the underlying accepted paradigm.

1.2 SCOPE BOUNDARIES & INTEGRITY RULES
This report operates under strict read-only design constraints:
  - No application code, prompt templates, tests, or Firestore rules are modified.
  - No external knowledge, textbooks, papers, or websites have been scraped or ingested.
  - No production knowledge base collections or runtime databases have been created.
  - No runtime DSP feature extraction or retrieval (RAG) code has been implemented.
  - No Current TT re-scoring is performed.
  - Phase 1C.4b is NOT started.

All technical schemas and worked examples presented herein are formal structural
contracts and UNVERIFIED SCHEMA ILLUSTRATIONS. They are not authoritative sound
engineering facts and are NOT eligible for production retrieval.

1.3 SUMMARY OF MAJOR REFINEMENTS IN v1.1
  1. False-Precision Confidence Elimination: Removed arbitrary scalar confidence
     floats (e.g. 0.88, 0.92); replaced with a multi-factor Epistemic Validation
     descriptor (evidence strength, consensus, replication, boundary certainty).
  2. Multi-Dimensional Authority Preservation: Eliminated the composite scalar
     `authority_score`; preserved Source Rigor (R1-R5) and Domain Jurisdiction
     (J_CORE, J_APPLIED, J_PERIPHERAL) as independent, queryable dimensions.
  3. Relational Normalization: Normalized monolithic attribution into three distinct
     entities: `SourceDocument`, `ClaimAttribution`, and `ReviewRecord`.
  4. Multi-Axis Epistemic Classification: Replaced the mutually exclusive 8-class
     enum with an orthogonal 3-axis taxonomy (Epistemic Basis, Role/Usage, Scope).
  5. Refined Commercial Device Boundary: Decoupled physical commercial equipment
     behavior (legitimate sound engineering knowledge) from platform implementation
     leakage (proprietary IDs, XML paths, GUI coordinates).
  6. Evidence-Sufficiency Quarantine: Removed the arbitrary "50-session" rule for
     candidate lessons; established an objective multi-factor evidence framework.
  7. Risk-Dependent Governance: Replaced universal two-reviewer quorums with a
     risk-proportional governance model balancing high assurance with operational
     feasibility.
  8. Unverified Example Labeling: Explicitly labeled all worked examples as
     unverified schema illustrations using dedicated placeholder identifiers.
  9. Authoritative Roadmap Alignment: Corrected the Phase 1C.4 development sequence
     (1C.4a to 1C.4f) to ensure no premature knowledge population occurs.

SECTION 2 — PHASE 1C.4a REVIEW CORRECTIONS APPLIED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The eight required review corrections are recorded below with their original issues,
architectural solutions, affected entities, and resulting design principles.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Correction 1: Remove False-Precision Claim Confidence
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Original Issue:
  Phase 1C.4a included `confidence_score: Float (0.0 - 1.0)` with pseudo-precise
  values (0.88, 0.90, 0.92). The system possessed no measurement methodology to
  substantiate that one claim is 0.92 confident while another is 0.88.
Architectural Correction:
  Removed the scalar float entirely. Introduced a structured `EpistemicValidation`
  contract comprising five qualitative categorical factors: `evidence_strength`
  (THEORETICAL_PROOF, SYSTEMATIC_EMPIRICAL, CORROBORATED_PRACTICE, ANECDOTAL),
  `consensus_state` (ESTABLISHED_CONSENSUS, PREDOMINANT, DEBATED, OUTLIER),
  `replication_state` (INDEPENDENTLY_REPLICATED, EXPERIMENTALLY_VERIFIED,
  OBSERVATIONAL_ONLY), `conflict_status` (UNCONTESTED, ACTIVE_COMPETING_CLAIM),
  and `boundary_certainty` (TIGHTLY_BOUNDED, PARTIAL_ENVELOPE, UNEXPLORED_LIMITS).
  Distinguished claim-level confidence from runtime contextual applicability.
Affected Entities/Sections:
  `KnowledgeClaim` entity, Section 6, Section 7, Section 8, Section 22.
Resulting Design Principle:
  Epistemic validity is multi-factorial and qualitative; pseudo-precise numerical
  probability values without formal Bayesian priors represent false certainty.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Correction 2: Do Not Collapse Epistemic Authority into One Score
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Original Issue:
  Phase 1C.4a preserved the 2D Rigor × Jurisdiction matrix in theory, but introduced
  `authority_score` in retrieval metadata, creating the risk that retrieval would
  reduce authority to a scalar sort (e.g. Claim A score > Claim B score -> Claim A wins).
Architectural Correction:
  Eliminated `authority_score`. Source Rigor (R1 to R5) and Domain Jurisdiction
  (J_CORE, J_APPLIED, J_PERIPHERAL) remain separate, independently queryable facets
  in retrieval metadata. Conflict resolution and ranking remain contextual and
  evidence-sensitive rather than reducible to a single number.
Affected Entities/Sections:
  `SourceDocument`, `KnowledgeClaim`, Section 10, Section 16, Section 22.
Resulting Design Principle:
  Authority is structural and relational; collapsing multi-dimensional rigor and
  domain relevance into a single scalar destroys epistemological nuance.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Correction 3: Normalize Source Identity, Claim Attribution, and Review
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Original Issue:
  The `SourceAttribution` entity conflated document bibliographic identity, exact
  claim passage citations, and human governance sign-offs into a single object,
  causing severe data duplication when multiple claims cite the same publication.
Architectural Correction:
  Normalized into three decoupled entities:
  - `SourceDocument`: Persistent bibliographic identity and document hash.
  - `ClaimAttribution`: The specific relationship between a `KnowledgeClaim` and a
    `SourceDocument`, including page, quotation, support type (SUPPORTS, QUALIFIES,
    CONTRADICTS, ILLUSTRATES), and extraction lineage.
  - `ReviewRecord`: Independent governance audit entity tracking review purpose,
    reviewer role, decision, findings, and applicable standard.
Affected Entities/Sections:
  Section 6 (Entity Model), Section 7, Section 8, Section 9, Section 17, Section 22.
Resulting Design Principle:
  Provenance data, citation relationships, and governance events operate on distinct
  lifecycles and must be independently addressable and auditable.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Correction 4: Revisit Mutually Exclusive Knowledge Classes
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Original Issue:
  Phase 1C.4a enforced eight mutually exclusive classes, yet its own examples combined
  labels (e.g. `PHYSICAL_LAW / ESTABLISHED_PRINCIPLE` or `EMPIRICAL / ILLUSTRATIVE`).
  This mixed physical basis, usage role, and technical scope into one enum.
Architectural Correction:
  Replaced the single flat enum with an orthogonal 3-axis classification taxonomy:
  - Axis 1 (Epistemic Basis): Physical Law, Established Engineering Principle,
    Empirical Relationship, Engineering Tendency, Hypothetical/Competing Model.
  - Axis 2 (Knowledge Role): Fundamental Theory, Phenomenological Model, Context-
    Dependent Practice, Professional Convention, Illustrative Example.
  - Axis 3 (Physical Scope): Transducer Acoustics, Circuit Electronics, Wave
    Mechanics, Psychoacoustics, System Topology, Device-Specific Behavior.
Affected Entities/Sections:
  `KnowledgeClaim` entity, Section 6, Section 7, Section 22.
Resulting Design Principle:
  Classification must mirror multi-dimensional physical and operational reality
  rather than forcing entities into artificial, mutually exclusive buckets.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Correction 5: Refine Platform-Independence & Commercial Device Boundary
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Original Issue:
  The v1 platform contamination firewall blocked all commercial brand names. This was
  overly broad: legitimate sound engineering knowledge frequently concerns the measured
  or documented physical behavior of real commercial equipment (e.g. Celestion V30
  resonance, Neumann U87 polar dispersion, Marshall JCM800 tone stack topology).
Architectural Correction:
  Redefined the firewall to target IMPLEMENTATION LEAKAGE (proprietary IDs, GUIDs,
  XML paths, DAW slot rules, exporter structures). Introduced `DeviceSpecificityContext`
  within `OperationalBoundary` so that commercial physical behavior is explicitly
  tagged as device-specific, preventing it from silently becoming a universal rule.
Affected Entities/Sections:
  Section 4, Section 6, Section 11, Section 14, Section 21, Section 22.
Resulting Design Principle:
  Documented physical behavior of commercial hardware is valid engineering knowledge
  when properly bounded; software platform implementation details are contamination.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Correction 6: Remove the Arbitrary "50 Session" Promotion Threshold
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Original Issue:
  Phase 1C.4a mandated "at least 50 independent corroborating sessions" before a
  runtime candidate lesson could exit quarantine. This threshold was ungrounded in
  statistical measurement science.
Architectural Correction:
  Removed the 50-session rule. Replaced it with an Evidence-Sufficiency Framework
  evaluating: (a) independent observation count, (b) variance across diverse input
  sources, (c) controlled vs uncontrolled conditions, (d) causal plausibility,
  (e) corroboration by existing authoritative literature, and (f) adversarial triad
  stress testing.
Affected Entities/Sections:
  `CandidateLesson` entity, Section 17, Section 19, Section 22.
Resulting Design Principle:
  Experiential promotion requires qualitative evidence sufficiency and causal
  plausibility across diverse conditions, not an arbitrary numerical counter.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Correction 7: Make Governance Rigorous but Operationally Realistic
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Original Issue:
  Requiring two independent senior audio engineering experts for every entry in the
  entire library was operationally unrealistic and conflated low-risk contextual
  notes with high-risk foundational physics.
Architectural Correction:
  Designed a Risk- and Evidence-Dependent Governance Model with three distinct tiers:
  - High-Risk / Foundational: Mandatory dual independent review + adversarial triad.
  - Moderate-Risk / Empirical: Single expert review + automated boundary linter.
  - Low-Risk / Contextual & Illustrative: Single technical reviewer + provenance check.
  Formally distinguished source verification, technical review, adversarial validation,
  and governance promotion.
Affected Entities/Sections:
  `ReviewRecord` entity, Section 15, Section 17, Section 23.
Resulting Design Principle:
  Governance assurance must be proportional to epistemic risk, generalization scope,
  and downstream behavioral impact.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Correction 8: Worked Examples Are Unverified Schema Illustrations
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Original Issue:
  Phase 1C.4a presented technical worked examples with realistic parameters, formulas,
  fake reviewer signatures, and `VALIDATED_ACTIVE` states, creating a dangerous semantic
  inconsistency in an unpopulated, read-only design phase.
Architectural Correction:
  Explicitly designated all worked examples as UNVERIFIED SCHEMA ILLUSTRATIONS.
  Replaced all production IDs and states with illustrative placeholders (e.g.
  `ILLUSTRATIVE-KC-001`, `PLACEHOLDER-SRC-001`) and assigned lifecycle state
  `UNVERIFIED_SCHEMA_ILLUSTRATION`. Added prominent warning banners stating they
  are not authoritative and not eligible for production retrieval.
Affected Entities/Sections:
  Section 6, Section 22 (all worked examples).
Resulting Design Principle:
  Design artifacts must maintain strict epistemic honesty; structural illustrations
  must never masquerade as validated domain knowledge.

SECTION 3 — GOVERNING ARCHITECTURAL PRINCIPLES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Sound Engineering Knowledge Architecture is governed by twelve core principles:

1. Knowledge Informs Reasoning — It Never Replaces It:
   Knowledge provides principles, causal models, boundaries, and trade-offs. The
   cognitive agent must diagnose, evaluate competing causes, and design solutions.
   Knowledge never prescribes fixed symptom-to-fix recipes.

2. Principles Over Heuristics:
   Every knowledge claim must represent underlying physical, electrical, or
   psychoacoustic reality. Heuristics are accepted only as context-dependent
   practices tied to specific operating envelopes.

3. Atomic Claim Granularity:
   Composite source documents are decomposed into irreducible, independently
   verifiable claims. Each claim maintains its own provenance, boundary, and review.

4. Platform Independence:
   The knowledge library is expressed entirely in SI units, circuit topography, and
   acoustic parameters. Platform-specific target adapters exist solely downstream.

5. Unbroken Provenance:
   Every claim must trace directly to a verified source document, specific page/section,
   and documented extraction lineage.

6. Asymmetric, Multi-Dimensional Authority:
   Authority is evaluated across Source Rigor and Domain Jurisdiction independently.
   No single scalar authority score is permitted.

7. Explicit Operational Boundaries:
   Every claim must define its valid envelope, prerequisites, known exceptions,
   confounders, and failure modes. Unbounded universal claims are prohibited.

8. Preservation of Credible Conflict:
   Disagreements between authoritative sources are preserved via explicit Conflict
   Records. The system never silently discards or averages competing evidence.

9. Strict Typing of Numerical Values:
   Numbers are strictly typed into seven classes (constants, boundaries, standards,
   ranges, recommendations, illustrative values, platform parameters) to prevent
   illustrative figures from becoming normative constraints.

10. Decoupled Commercial Device Knowledge:
    Measured physical behavior of real commercial equipment is valid engineering
    knowledge when tagged with device-specificity context; software implementation
    details (IDs, XML, GUI coords) are barred as contamination.

11. Risk-Dependent Human Governance:
    Knowledge claims are promoted by qualified human experts through a tiered review
    process proportional to epistemic risk and generalization scope.

12. Experiential Quarantine:
    Closed-loop runtime learnings are quarantined as candidate lessons and require
    multi-condition evidence sufficiency before review and promotion.

SECTION 4 — KNOWLEDGE VS REASONING BOUNDARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

4.1 THE TRI-PARTITE ARCHITECTURAL DIVISION
The architecture strictly delineates responsibilities among three subsystems:
  1. Sound Engineering Knowledge Library: The durable repository of physical laws,
     circuit models, acoustic radiation physics, psychoacoustic curves, operational
     boundaries, and source attributions.
  2. Runtime Evidence & DSP Tooling: External software tools that measure the physical
     audio signal (spectral centroid, harmonic distribution, crest factor, attack
     slopes, phase correlation) and present objective measurements.
  3. AI Sound Engineer Reasoning Engine: The cognitive agent that ingests evidence,
     retrieves relevant causal models, forms competing hypotheses, diagnoses root
     causes, balances trade-offs, and outputs semantic tone designs.

4.2 THE DOWNSTREAM TRANSLATION BOUNDARY
The output of the Sound Engineer is a platform-independent `SemanticToneDesign`
(Phase 1B). Downstream translation (e.g. into AmpliTube 5 XML, Helix presets, or
analog patch sheets) is handled exclusively by the `PlatformTranslationPlan`.
General sound engineering knowledge must never contain target platform artifacts.

SECTION 5 — KNOWLEDGE CLASSIFICATION TAXONOMY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Phase 1C.4a-R establishes an orthogonal Three-Axis Classification Taxonomy,
eliminating the flaws of flat, mutually exclusive enums. Every `KnowledgeClaim`
must be indexed across all three axes:

5.1 AXIS 1: EPISTEMIC BASIS (The Nature of the Underlying Truth)
  - `PHYSICAL_LAW`: Invariant physical or mathematical theorems (e.g. Maxwell's
    equations, wave superposition, conservation of energy, Nyquist theorem).
  - `ESTABLISHED_ENGINEERING_PRINCIPLE`: Widely accepted, scientifically verified
    engineering theories (e.g. velocity-gradient proximity effect, triode cathode-
    bypass frequency roll-off, loudspeaker reactive impedance).
  - `EMPIRICAL_RELATIONSHIP`: Systematically measured laboratory correlations
    (e.g. Fletcher-Munson equal-loudness curves, speaker cone breakup frequency
    distribution, Bark scale critical bandwidths).
  - `ENGINEERING_TENDENCY`: Observed behavioral correlations in complex systems that
    hold true under common conditions but have known exceptions (e.g. pre-distortion
    high-pass filtering tends to increase transient definition).
  - `HYPOTHETICAL_OR_COMPETING_MODEL`: Plausible physical or circuit explanations
    where multiple competing models coexist without universal consensus.

5.2 AXIS 2: KNOWLEDGE ROLE & USAGE (How the Knowledge Functions in Practice)
  - `FUNDAMENTAL_THEORY`: Foundational grounding that explains underlying causes;
    informs understanding rather than direct adjustment.
  - `PHENOMENOLOGICAL_MODEL`: Operational model connecting observable inputs to
    acoustic consequences (e.g. how tube bias changes harmonic spectrum).
  - `CONTEXT_DEPENDENT_PRACTICE`: Systematic methodology developed for specific
    acoustic or musical contexts (e.g. high-passing rhythm guitars in a dense mix).
  - `PROFESSIONAL_CONVENTION`: Industry agreements, operating calibrations, and
    workflow standards (e.g. 0 VU = +4 dBu = -18 dBFS, EBU R128 loudness).
  - `ILLUSTRATIVE_EXAMPLE`: Concrete historical or laboratory example illuminating
    a principle. Must never be treated as a normative goal.

5.3 AXIS 3: TARGET PHYSICAL SCOPE (The Physical Domain of the Phenomenon)
  - `TRANSDUCER_PHYSICS`: Microphones, guitar pickups, loudspeakers.
  - `CIRCUIT_ELECTRONICS`: Tubes, transistors, tone stacks, power supplies, filters.
  - `WAVE_ACOUSTICS`: Room reflections, cabinet diffraction, phase cancellation.
  - `PSYCHOACOUSTICS`: Auditory perception, masking, loudness curves, localization.
  - `SYSTEM_TOPOLOGY`: Signal routing order, gain staging, commutativity.
  - `DEVICE_SPECIFIC_BEHAVIOUR`: Documented physical behavior of a specific real
    commercial device (e.g. SM57 off-axis response curve).

6.2 NORMALIZED ENTITY SPECIFICATIONS

Entity 1: `SourceDocument`
  Represents the persistent bibliographic identity of a source.
  - `source_id`: String (e.g. `SRC-BOOK-BLENCOWE-2014`)
  - `title`: String
  - `authors`: Array of String
  - `publisher_or_institution`: String
  - `publication_year`: Integer
  - `edition_or_version`: Optional String
  - `isbn_or_doi`: Optional String
  - `source_rigor_tier`: Enum (`R1_PHYSICAL_LAW`, `R2_PEER_REVIEWED_RESEARCH`,
    `R3_MANUFACTURER_ENGINEERING`, `R4_PROFESSIONAL_TREATISE`, `R5_PRACTITIONER_ACCOUNT`)
  - `domain_jurisdictions`: Map of DomainID -> Enum (`J_CORE`, `J_APPLIED`, `J_PERIPHERAL`)
  - `document_hash`: String (SHA-256 of canonical text or file)

Entity 2: `ClaimAttribution`
  Represents the formal citation link between a claim and a source document.
  - `attribution_id`: String (e.g. `ATTR-KC1001-BLENCOWE-P45`)
  - `claim_id`: String
  - `source_id`: String
  - `location_in_source`: String (Chapter, Section, Page, Equation/Figure number)
  - `support_type`: Enum (`SUPPORTS`, `QUALIFIES`, `CONTRADICTS`, `ILLUSTRATES`)
  - `extracted_passage`: String (Direct quotation or mathematical expression)
  - `extraction_method`: Enum (`MANUAL_EXPERT`, `SUPERVISED_SYNTHESIS`)
  - `extraction_timestamp`: Timestamp

Entity 3: `KnowledgeClaim`
  The fundamental atomic unit of sound engineering knowledge.
  - `claim_id`: String (e.g. `KC-D10-PROXIMITY-001`)
  - `domain_id`: Integer (1 to 22, per Phase 1C.3a)
  - `concept_name`: String
  - `epistemic_basis`: Enum (from Axis 1: `PHYSICAL_LAW`, `ESTABLISHED_ENGINEERING_PRINCIPLE`,
    `EMPIRICAL_RELATIONSHIP`, `ENGINEERING_TENDENCY`, `HYPOTHETICAL_OR_COMPETING_MODEL`)
  - `knowledge_role`: Enum (from Axis 2: `FUNDAMENTAL_THEORY`, `PHENOMENOLOGICAL_MODEL`,
    `CONTEXT_DEPENDENT_PRACTICE`, `PROFESSIONAL_CONVENTION`, `ILLUSTRATIVE_EXAMPLE`)
  - `physical_scope`: Enum (from Axis 3: `TRANSDUCER_PHYSICS`, `CIRCUIT_ELECTRONICS`,
    `WAVE_ACOUSTICS`, `PSYCHOACOUSTICS`, `SYSTEM_TOPOLOGY`, `DEVICE_SPECIFIC_BEHAVIOUR`)
  - `statement`: String (Declarative, platform-independent assertion)
  - `formal_derivation_or_math`: Optional Object:
    - `notation_format`: Enum (`LATEX_DECLARATIVE`, `STRUCTURED_MATH_AST`)
    - `formula_expression`: String
  - `epistemic_validation`: Object (Structured validation replacing scalar confidence):
    - `evidence_strength`: Enum (`THEORETICAL_PROOF`, `SYSTEMATIC_EMPIRICAL`,
      `CORROBORATED_PRACTICE`, `ANECDOTAL_OBSERVATION`)
    - `consensus_state`: Enum (`ESTABLISHED_CONSENSUS`, `PREDOMINANT_VIEW`,
      `DEBATED_VIEW`, `OUTLIER_HYPOTHESIS`)
    - `replication_state`: Enum (`INDEPENDENTLY_REPLICATED`, `LABORATORY_VERIFIED`,
      `OBSERVATIONAL_UNREPLICATED`)
    - `conflict_status`: Enum (`UNCONTESTED`, `ACTIVE_COMPETING_CLAIM`)
    - `boundary_certainty`: Enum (`TIGHTLY_BOUNDED`, `PARTIALLY_BOUNDED`,
      `EXPLORATORY_LIMITS`)
  - `lifecycle_state`: Enum (`CAPTURED`, `EXTRACTED`, `NORMALIZED`, `UNDER_REVIEW`,
    `VALIDATED_ACTIVE`, `DEPRECATED`, `SUPERSEDED`, `REJECTED`,
    `UNVERIFIED_SCHEMA_ILLUSTRATION`)
  - `version`: Integer
  - `superseded_by`: Optional String

Entity 4: `CausalModel`
  Dynamic input-stimulus to acoustic-consequence networks.
  - `model_id`: String (e.g. `CM-D03-PRE_CLIPPING_LF-001`)
  - `model_name`: String
  - `input_stimulus`: Object (Parameter name, physical domain, metric unit)
  - `mediating_stage`: Enum (`SOURCE`, `FRONT_END`, `PREAMP_NONLINEAR`, `POWER_STAGE`,
    `TRANSDUCER`, `ACOUSTIC_SPACE`, `CAPTURE`, `POST_PROCESSING`)
  - `mediating_mechanism`: String (Physical or circuit transformation)
  - `acoustic_consequences`: Array of Objects:
    - `consequence_description`: String
    - `observable_domain`: Enum (`FREQUENCY`, `DYNAMICS`, `HARMONIC_SERIES`, `PHASE`, `SPATIAL`)
    - `metric_indicator`: String (e.g. "Spectral Centroid", "Crest Factor")
    - `direction_of_change`: Enum (`INCREASE`, `DECREASE`, `SHIFT`, `SPREAD`, `COMPRESS`)
  - `trade_off_vectors`: Array of Objects:
    - `acoustic_benefit`: String
    - `collateral_penalty`: String
    - `mitigation_options`: Array of String
  - `coupled_domain_ids`: Array of Integer

Entity 5: `OperationalBoundary`
  Explicit validity envelopes, prerequisites, and confounders.
  - `boundary_id`: String (e.g. `OB-D10-PROXIMITY-NEARFIELD`)
  - `valid_physical_envelope`: String (Quantitative operating range)
  - `prerequisites`: Array of String (Mandatory system states)
  - `confounding_variables`: Array of String (Factors that mimic or mask phenomenon)
  - `known_exceptions`: Array of String (Scenarios where principle fails)
  - `failure_mode_description`: String (Acoustic result if boundary is exceeded)
  - `device_specificity_context`: Optional Object:
    - `is_device_specific`: Boolean
    - `commercial_device_identifier`: String (e.g. "Celestion Vintage 30")

SECTION 7 — KNOWLEDGECLAIM GRANULARITY & ATOMICITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

7.1 THE ATOMICITY STANDARD
A knowledge claim is atomic if and only if it represents an irreducible assertion
that cannot be split into independent propositions without losing physical meaning.

7.2 DECOMPOSITION AUDIT
Consider an authoritative source discussing guitar loudspeaker high-frequency response:
"The 12-inch guitar loudspeaker produces highly directional acoustic beaming above
2 kHz due to cone diameter matching acoustic wavelength, while the central dust cap
exhibits aggressive resonance peaks around 4 kHz to 5 kHz that are smoothed when
listening 45 degrees off-axis."

If ingested as a single unit, retrieval cannot separate wave diffraction beaming
from dust-cap cone resonance or off-axis microphone acoustic integration.

Atomic Decomposition:
  - Claim A: "Acoustic beaming occurs when acoustic wavelength becomes comparable to
    or smaller than loudspeaker diaphragm diameter, narrowing directional dispersion
    angle as frequency increases." [Physical Law / Wave Acoustics]
  - Claim B: "Guitar loudspeaker dust caps contribute high-frequency resonant peaks
    typically between 3.5 kHz and 5.5 kHz owing to structural stiffness transitions
    at the voice-coil junction." [Empirical Relationship / Transducer Physics]
  - Claim C: "Positioning a capture microphone 45 degrees off the central acoustic
    axis reduces measured high-frequency energy above 4 kHz due to diaphragm acoustic
    phase integration and speaker directional beaming." [Established Principle / Wave Acoustics]

Each claim cites the same source document via distinct `ClaimAttribution` records,
attaches to distinct `OperationalBoundary` envelopes, and can be revised independently.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 8 — PROVENANCE & ATTRIBUTION ARCHITECTURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

8.1 UNBROKEN CHAIN OF ATTRIBUTION
Provenance guarantees that no claim exists without verified historical origins:
  1. Primary Document Registration: `SourceDocument` created with cryptographic hash.
  2. Claim Extraction: Specialist extracts verbatim passage into `ClaimAttribution`.
  3. Relational Linking: `KnowledgeClaim` links to `ClaimAttribution` array.
  4. Cryptographic Seal: At `VALIDATED_ACTIVE` state, a SHA-256 seal is generated over
     `(claim_statement + boundary_envelope + source_id + reviewer_signatures)`.

8.2 MULTI-SOURCE ATTRIBUTION & CONSENSUS
When multiple independent sources support a single principle:
  - Each source is registered in `SourceDocument`.
  - A separate `ClaimAttribution` is instantiated for each source citing its page/passage.
  - The `KnowledgeClaim` references all attribution IDs in its `attribution_ids` array.
  - The claim's `consensus_state` is upgraded to `ESTABLISHED_CONSENSUS`.
  - If a source qualifies or restricts the claim, its `support_type` is marked `QUALIFIES`,
    and an accompanying `OperationalBoundary` is attached.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 9 — SOURCE AUTHORITY & EPISTEMIC RIGOR MODEL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

9.1 THE TWO-DIMENSIONAL AUTHORITY MATRIX
Authority is determined by the intersection of Source Rigor and Domain Jurisdiction:

Dimension 1: Source Rigor (R-Tier)
  - R1: Formally Proven Physical / Mathematical Law (Peer-reviewed textbooks, IEEE/AES standards).
  - R2: Controlled Empirical & Scientific Engineering Research (AES papers, ASA laboratory data).
  - R3: Authoritative Technical & Manufacturer Documentation (Schematics, patents, engineering whitepapers).
  - R4: Systematic Professional Engineering Literature (Treatises by master recording engineers).
  - R5: Contextual Practitioner Accounts & Interviews (Documented artist/engineer interviews).

Dimension 2: Domain Jurisdiction (J-Tier)
  - J_CORE: The source operates within its primary scientific or technical discipline.
  - J_APPLIED: The source applies principles from an external discipline to its domain.
  - J_PERIPHERAL: The source makes informal remarks outside its primary expertise.

9.2 DISPENSING WITH SCALAR AUTHORITY SCORES
The architecture strictly prohibits combining R-Tier and J-Tier into a single scalar
score (e.g. "Authority = 8.7/10").
During retrieval and reasoning:
  - Queries filter by minimum Rigor and Jurisdiction requirements.
  - An R3/J_CORE manufacturer schematic for an amplifier circuit takes precedence
    over an R1/J_PERIPHERAL textbook comment on guitar amp tone stacks.
  - An R1/J_CORE acoustics textbook takes precedence over an R4/J_APPLIED recording
    engineer's handbook regarding wave diffraction physics.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 10 — CONDITIONAL KNOWLEDGE & BOUNDARY MODEL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

10.1 MANDATORY BOUNDARY ATTACHMENT
Every `KnowledgeClaim` must be bound to at least one `OperationalBoundary` record.
Unbounded, universal assertions are automatically rejected during ingestion linting.

10.2 BOUNDARY STRUCTURE
The operational boundary defines four required vectors:
  1. Valid Envelope: Quantitative limits within which the claim is valid
     (e.g. "Signal Level: -18 dBFS to -6 dBFS; Distance: 1 cm to 15 cm").
  2. System Prerequisites: Mandatory architectural or physical configurations
     (e.g. "Loudspeaker mounted in infinite baffle or closed enclosure").
  3. Confounders & Mimics: Physical phenomena that mimic the symptoms of this claim
     (e.g. "Acoustic proximity bass boost mimics electrical low-shelf EQ boost;
     speaker cone breakup harmonics mimic preamp diode clipping").
  4. Failure Modes: Acoustic consequences when boundary is violated
     (e.g. "Applying this technique to clean guitar causes severe transient collapse").


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 11 — CONFLICT & CONTRADICTION MODEL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

11.1 PRESERVATION OF COMPETING PERSPECTIVES
When authoritative sources disagree, the system instantiates a `ConflictRecord`.
Disagreements are not hidden, averaged, or arbitrarily resolved.

11.2 CONFLICT CATEGORIES
  - `BOUNDARY_DIVERGENCE`: Both claims are correct within different operational envelopes
    (e.g. Source A recommends 10 cm mic distance for low SPL; Source B recommends 30 cm
    for high SPL).
  - `METHODOLOGICAL_DIFFERENCE`: Alternative engineering approaches to the same goal
    (e.g. Acoustic filtering via off-axis mic angle vs electrical post-clipping EQ).
  - `DIRECT_CONTRADICTION`: Incompatible claims requiring controlled empirical testing
    (e.g. Disputed claims regarding vacuum tube heater hum coupling mechanisms).

11.3 REASONING UNDER CONFLICT
When the AI Sound Engineer retrieves a claim linked to an active `ConflictRecord`,
the retrieval payload includes BOTH perspectives and their boundary conditions.
The Sound Engineer's `EngineeringDecisionRecord` must document which rationale was
selected and why it fits the task constraints.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 12 — NUMERICAL KNOWLEDGE HANDLING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Every numerical figure within the knowledge library must be explicitly typed into
one of seven classes:

Type 1: PHYSICAL_CONSTANT
  - Invariant mathematical and physical constants (e.g. Speed of sound c = 343.2 m/s).
  - Enforced as immutable exact value.

Type 2: HARD_ENGINEERING_BOUNDARY
  - Inviolable format or circuit ceilings (e.g. 0.0 dBFS unquantized ceiling; 100% plate dissipation).
  - Enforced as absolute constraint.

Type 3: CALIBRATION_STANDARD
  - Standardized reference operating levels (e.g. +4 dBu = 1.228 V RMS; 0 VU = -18 dBFS).
  - Enforced as reference alignment.

Type 4: EMPIRICALLY_MEASURED_RANGE
  - Statistically verified distributions from controlled laboratory measurements
    (e.g. Celestion V30 resonance Fs = 75 Hz +/- 5 Hz).
  - Expressed as `[min, mean, max]` with standard deviation.

Type 5: CONTEXTUAL_RECOMMENDED_RANGE
  - Industry guidelines bounded by context (e.g. "High-pass rhythm guitar between 80 Hz and 120 Hz").
  - Expressed as an interval with soft edges `[soft_min, typical_min, typical_max, soft_max]`.

Type 6: ILLUSTRATIVE_NOMINAL_VALUE
  - Concrete numbers cited in case studies solely to demonstrate a calculation.
  - MUST BE TAGGED AS NON-NORMATIVE. Prohibited from serving as default values or limits.

Type 7: PLATFORM_PARAMETER_MAPPING
  - Target software parameter scales (e.g. AT5 knob 0.0 to 10.0).
  - STRICTLY PROHIBITED FROM THE SOUND ENGINEERING KNOWLEDGE LIBRARY.

SECTION 13 — COMMERCIAL EQUIPMENT & PLATFORM INDEPENDENCE BOUNDARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

13.1 COMMERCIAL HARDWARE KNOWLEDGE IS NOT PLATFORM CONTAMINATION
A critical correction in v1.1 is distinguishing physical commercial hardware knowledge
from software platform implementation leakage:
  - Commercial Hardware Knowledge: The physical, measured behavior of real-world
    microphones, tubes, speakers, and amplifiers (e.g. Shure SM57 frequency response,
    Celestion Greenback breakup, Fender 5F6-A tone stack impedance). This is legitimate
    sound engineering knowledge.
  - Platform Implementation Leakage: Proprietary digital modeling parameters, GUIDs,
    XML tags, DAW track bounds, and exporter formats. This is contamination.

13.2 THE DEVICE-SPECIFICITY CONTEXT CONTRACT
Whenever physical knowledge concerns a specific commercial device, it must attach
a `DeviceSpecificityContext` within its `OperationalBoundary`:
  - `is_device_specific`: true
  - `commercial_device_identifier`: "Celestion Vintage 30"
  - `generalizability`: `MODEL_SPECIFIC_EMPIRICAL` (or `FAMILY_REPRESENTATIVE`)
This tag prevents the system from universalizing a device-specific behavior into a
general acoustic law.

13.3 IMPLEMENTATION LEAKAGE FIREWALL
Automated validation checks continuously reject knowledge units containing:
  - Proprietary software IDs (e.g. AT5 model integers, Helix GUIDs).
  - Software GUI coordinates or normalized 0.0-1.0 plugin parameter indices.
  - XML/JSON schema structures specific to downstream export targets.

SECTION 14 — CROSS-DOMAIN RELATIONSHIP MODEL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

14.1 DIRECTED CAUSAL COUPLING
Engineering problems are fundamentally cross-domain. Every `CausalModel` registers
its cross-domain links:
  - `UPSTREAM_SENSITIVITY`: Domains that supply input stimuli to this model
    (e.g. Domain 3 Distortion is sensitive to Domain 1 Pickup Output and Domain 2 Gain).
  - `DOWNSTREAM_CONSEQUENCE`: Domains altered by the output of this model
    (e.g. Domain 9 Speaker Breakup alters harmonic content entering Domain 10 Mic).
  - `COLLATERAL_IMPACT`: Domains that incur side-effects when this model is adjusted
    (e.g. Domain 6 Noise floor rises when Domain 8 Master Volume is increased).

14.2 CROSS-DOMAIN RETRIEVAL EXPANSION
When the Sound Engineer searches for causal mechanisms related to a symptom (e.g.
"boomy low end"), the retrieval engine traverses directed graph edges to present
hypotheses spanning Source, Electrical, Transducer, and Acoustic domains.

SECTION 16 — KNOWLEDGE LIFECYCLE & RISK-DEPENDENT GOVERNANCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

16.1 RISK- AND EVIDENCE-DEPENDENT GOVERNANCE
Governance requirements are strictly proportional to epistemic risk:

Tier A: High-Risk / Foundational Claims
  - Scope: Physical laws, circuit safety boundaries, broad general principles.
  - Governance Requirement: Source Verification + Dual Independent Senior Review +
    Adversarial Triad Validation + Governance Lead Approval.

Tier B: Moderate-Risk / Empirical & Device Claims
  - Scope: Empirical relationships, device-specific behaviors, standard engineering tendencies.
  - Governance Requirement: Source Verification + Single Senior Review + Automated
    Boundary/Linter Pass + Counterfactual Test.

Tier C: Low-Risk / Contextual & Illustrative Claims
  - Scope: Context-dependent practices, professional conventions, illustrative examples.
  - Governance Requirement: Source Verification + Single Technical Reviewer Sign-Off.

16.2 DISTINCT GOVERNANCE PHASES
The lifecycle strictly separates four distinct activities:
  1. Source Verification: Confirming the bibliographic existence, edition, and exact
     quoted passage in the primary document.
  2. Technical Review: Evaluating the physical accuracy, mathematical derivation,
     and operational boundaries of the extracted claim.
  3. Adversarial Validation: Executing counterfactual test triads to ensure the claim
     does not induce recipe-like dogmatic behavior.
  4. Governance Promotion: Official cryptographic signing and state transition to
     `VALIDATED_ACTIVE`.

SECTION 20 — SYSTEM INTEGRITY & FAILURE PROTECTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The architecture implements ten active structural protections:
  1. Cryptographic Provenance Seals: Active claims are sealed with SHA-256 hashes
     over claim text, boundaries, and reviewer IDs.
  2. Anti-Recipe Imperative Linter: Ingestion reject claims containing prescriptive
     imperatives ("always use", "mandatory setting", "set drive to 10").
  3. Platform Leakage Firewall: Automated rejection of software-specific IDs, GUIDs,
     XML paths, and normalized GUI coordinate parameters.
  4. Boundary Enforcement Guard: Rejection of any claim lacking an explicit
     `OperationalBoundary` record.
  5. Multi-Axis Classification Validator: Rejection of claims not indexed across all
     three classification axes.
  6. Numerical Type Enforcer: Validation that every number belongs to Types 1-6.
  7. Experiential Quarantine Isolation: Physical separation of candidate lessons
     from production retrieval indices.
  8. Adversarial Counterfactual Triad Gate: Ingestion testing against three cases with
     identical symptoms but different root causes.
  9. Lifecycle State Guard: Runtime retrieval restricted exclusively to `VALIDATED_ACTIVE`.
  10. Strict Audit Immutability: Rejection and deprecation events are recorded with
      immutable audit logs.

SECTION 22 — RESOLVED ARCHITECTURAL DECISIONS (SECTION 21 AUDIT)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The four outstanding architectural decisions from Phase 1C.4a Section 21 are
definitively resolved:

DECISION 1 — STORAGE & CANONICAL PERSISTENCE STRATEGY
  Resolved Direction: Hybrid Canonical-Source Architecture.
  - Canonical Storage: Human-readable, Git-tracked, diffable, reviewable source
    files (structured JSON / YAML) organized under `/src/knowledge/canonical/`.
  - Runtime Derivatives: Generated/compiled indexed representations (e.g. SQLite,
    in-memory search trees, vector embeddings) built as disposable, rebuildable
    derivatives during build time.
  - Final Selection of Runtime Technology: Deferred to Phase 1C.4c (Retrieval
    Architecture). Canonical knowledge is durable; runtime indexes are disposable.

DECISION 2 — REVIEW QUORUM & GOVERNANCE MODEL
  Resolved Direction: Risk- and Evidence-Dependent Governance Model.
  - Replaced universal two-reviewer requirement with a three-tier proportional quorum:
    * Tier A (Foundational / Safety / Broad Scope): Dual Senior Review + Adversarial Triad.
    * Tier B (Empirical / Device-Specific): Single Senior Review + Automated Linter.
    * Tier C (Contextual / Illustrative): Single Technical Review + Provenance Check.
  - Human expert promotion authority remains mandatory across all tiers.

DECISION 3 — CURRENT TT DECONSTRUCTION GRANULARITY
  Resolved Direction: Atomic Claim & Model Decomposition.
  - Current TT microphone, cabinet, and routing knowledge will be decomposed into
    as many atomic claims and reusable causal models as provenance and atomicity
    dictate (rejecting arbitrary target counters like "exactly 15 units").

DECISION 4 — MATHEMATICAL REPRESENTATION STANDARD
  Resolved Direction: Declarative Structured Notation.
  - Knowledge Library maintains declarative mathematical notation (LaTeX strings
    and structured mathematical AST blocks).
  - Executable evaluation is decoupled and resides in external deterministic validation
    tooling. Arbitrary executable TypeScript lambdas are prohibited inside canonical
    knowledge records.
